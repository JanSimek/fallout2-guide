# Karma and Reputation

Fallout 2 tracks three separate things: a global **karma** number, a hidden **reputation**
per town, and a set of named **special reputations** that behave like badges.

None of them matter as much as you would expect, and the numbers are stark. Of RPU's 1,537
scripts, **337 work out a reaction to you and only 49 do anything with the answer**. The rest
compute it and throw it away.

Karma feeds that reaction at a rate **each critter sets for itself**. The script multiplies your
karma by that critter's `Karma_Perception` and divides by 1000, and a critter only leaves the
neutral band at 25. Most of them barely notice: **159 scripts use the lowest real setting, worth
one point per 100 karma**, which is the 2,500 karma it then takes to move them a single step.
That is why there is usually no practical difference between 500 karma and 1500 — both are worth
+5 and +15 on a scale that does nothing until 25.

The exceptions are worth knowing, because they are the people whose opinion you can actually
change. Thirty scripts set it lower still and effectively ignore karma altogether; about seventy
set it higher, and at the top of the range a critter reads your karma ten times more sharply, so
250 karma moves it a step rather than 2,500. The Karma Beacon perk doubles whatever that
critter's rate happens to be.

The **karma titles below are almost decorative**. Nothing in the game checks for Holy Warrior,
Guardian, Defender, Wanderer, Betrayer, Scourge or Demon at all. What people actually react to
are three badges earned by deeds rather than by score: **Slaver** (74 scripts), **Childkiller**
(23) and **Berserker** (21).

## Karma titles

Every 250 karma in either direction earns a new title:

| Karma | Title |
| --- | --- |
| +2000 and above | Savior of the Damned |
| +1500 | Guardian of the Wastes |
| +1000 | Shield of Hope |
| +500 | Defender |
| 0 | Wanderer |
| −500 | Betrayer |
| −1000 | Sword of Despair |
| −1500 | Scourge of the Wastes |
| −2000 and below | Demon Spawn |

Playing as a shameless opportunist tends to land you at high positive karma anyway, simply
because the game contains far more good deeds than bad ones.

:::note
RPU restores each karma level's unique icon on the character sheet, as originally intended,
along with the cut **Virgin** title and the original Childkiller portrait.
<Vanilla>The unmodded game shows a generic icon for every karma level and never displays the
Virgin title.</Vanilla>
:::

## Town reputation

Each settlement tracks a hidden number rendered as: **Idolized, Liked, Accepted, Neutral,
Antipathy, Hated, Vilified.**

You arrive Neutral almost everywhere. The exceptions:

- **Arroyo** — Idolized. It is your home.
- **Klamath** — Accepted, if your Charisma is 6 or higher.
- **Vault City** — Antipathy. They dislike outsiders on principle.

Not every town can be brought to Idolized. The Ghost Farm caps at Neutral no matter how
thoroughly you save everyone in it.

## Special reputations

| Reputation | How you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **Berserker** | Kill significantly more good people than bad |
| **Champion** | Kill significantly more bad people than good, and not be a Childkiller |
| **Childkiller** | Kill a child, for any reason, including by accident |
| **Gigolo** | Have sex frequently — or once, extremely well |
| **Grave Digger** | Dig up any grave other than Anna's or the two legitimate ones at Golgotha |
| **Made Man** | Join one of the New Reno families |
| **Married** | Marry Miria or Davin in Modoc |
| **Porn Star** | Shoot a film at the Golden Globes |
| **Prizefighter** | Win the New Reno boxing title |
| **Separated** | Get divorced, or have your spouse killed or sold |
| **Sexpert** | Have sex ten times |
| **Slaver** | Join Metzger's guild in the Den |

Only **Prizefighter** has a mechanical effect (+1 to hit in unarmed combat).

## Childkiller: the details

This is the one reputation that will actively ruin a playthrough. Bounty hunters start
appearing on the world map, several merchants refuse to deal with you, and some quest
lines close.

The important quirk: **you get the label after one child, but nearly every script in the
game checks for two or more.** So a single accidental kill marks your character sheet
without triggering most of the consequences.

:::caution
Three children increment the counter **twice** because of a scripting redundancy, making you
a "real" Childkiller instantly: **Jonny in Modoc**, the **Slag children**, and **Curtis in
Vault City**. Be careful with area-effect weapons anywhere near them.
:::

:::note
If a *companion* kills a child, you do not get the reputation.
:::

## Radiation

Radiation is a minor system. You pick it up from fruit, golden geckos, centaurs and aliens
in random encounters, and from standing around in the Gecko reactor or the Toxic Caves.

The radiation icon appears at **66 rem**. Some time after absorbing a dose you take a hit
whose severity depends on your total rem count *and* how much you just absorbed:

| Rem | Message | HR | HP | ST | PE | EN | CH | IN | AG |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| >0 | "very nauseous" | | | | | | | | |
| >150 | "slightly fatigued" | | | −1 | | | | | |
| >300 | "vomiting does not stop" | −3 | | −1 | | | | | −1 |
| >450 | "hair is falling out" | −5 | −5 | −2 | | −1 | | | −2 |
| >600 | "skin is falling off" | −10 | −15 | −4 | −3 | −3 | −3 | −1 | −5 |
| >1000 | "intense agony" | −10 | −20 | −6 | −5 | −5 | −5 | −3 | −6 |

Penalties clear after **7 days**, signalled by a second identical message. There is no
permanent damage.

:::danger
You die instantly if any of these stats — Charisma included — is reduced to 0 or below. The
check happens only at the moment of reduction, so a well-timed chem can keep you alive.
:::

In practice you would have to work hard to exceed 300 rem in a normal playthrough. Carry
Rad-X if you are going into the reactor; RadAway afterwards is rarely necessary.
