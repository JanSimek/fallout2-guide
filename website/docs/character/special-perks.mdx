# Special Perks

These do not cost a perk slot. They are handed out by quests, doctors and one very unlucky
dog, and they appear alongside your normal perks on the character sheet.

## Implants — Vault City

The most valuable set in the game, and the most expensive. Learn about implants from the
**medical database in the Vault City vault**, then ask **"Doctor" Andrew** in the Courtyard
about them. **Doc Johnson** in Redding and **Dr. Fung** in San Francisco fit the same four.

**Each operation destroys a suit of Combat Armor.** That is four suits for the full set, and it is
a hard requirement everywhere — without one in your inventory all three doctors will discuss
implants at length and never offer to operate. The game spends the worst suit it can find, taking
plain Combat Armor before the Mark II and the Mark II before Brotherhood Combat Armor. Each
enhancement additionally requires the basic implant of the same kind first, and permanently costs
you **1 Charisma**.

| Implant | Effect | Time | CH |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dermal Impact Armor** | +5% DR vs normal and explosion | 2 days | — |
| **Dermal Impact Assault Enhancement** | +5% more (10% total) | 5 days | −1 |
| **Phoenix Armor Implants** | +5% DR vs plasma, laser and fire | 3 days | — |
| **Phoenix Assault Enhancement** | +5% more (10% total) | 6 days | −1 |

Prices differ by town, and Vault City's are the only ones you can haggle down — with **CH 6 or
Barter 75%**:

| Implant | Vault City | Redding | San Francisco |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dermal Impact Armor** | $7,000 → **$5,000** | $5,000 | $5,500 |
| **Dermal Impact Assault** | $40,000 → **$30,000** | $30,000 | $33,000 |
| **Phoenix Armor Implants** | $10,000 → **$8,000** | $8,000 | $8,500 |
| **Phoenix Assault** | $50,000 → **$40,000** | $48,000 | $49,000 |

So **haggle in Vault City and buy everything there** — the discount matches Redding on three of the
four and beats it by $8,000 on the Phoenix Assault. Without the Charisma or the Barter, go to
Redding instead. San Francisco is never cheapest, but its Phoenix Assault takes **four days**
rather than six.

:::tip
Take the two basic implants as soon as you can afford them. Hold off on the enhancements until
late in the game — the Charisma loss can cost you a companion slot, and by the time you have
$90,000 spare you are nearly finished anyway.
:::

:::caution[Recruit first]
The Charisma loss from the enhancements is permanent. Pick up every companion you want, and
finish any Charisma-gated dialogue, before you buy them.
:::

## Vault City

| Perk | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Vault City Inoculations** | Dr. Troy. +10% poison and radiation resistance. Free. Get it. |
| **Vault City Training** | Dr. Troy. +5% First Aid and Doctor. Free. |
| **Autodoc Raised Hit Points** / **II** | Dr. Andrew's Auto-Doc, on the second ride. **+2** max HP, or **+4** for the II version — which is what Luck 10 gets. |
| **Autodoc Lowered Hit Points** / **II** | The same Auto-Doc on a *third* ride. **−2**, or **−4** at Luck 1. |
| **Alcohol Raised Hit Points** / **II** | Drinking contest in the Parlor Room. **+2** max HP, or **+4** for the II. |
| **Alcohol Lowered Hit Points** / **II** | The same contest, going badly. **−2**, or **−4** for the II. |

The alcohol and Auto-Doc perks are gambles. Save first.

## Jinxed

The **pariah dog** — a special random encounter — will follow you until you accept it, and
gives you Jinxed as a **perk**, not a trait. Everyone around you, including you, starts fumbling
critically. It is a curse, not a reward. Leave the map before it attaches itself, or kill it —
its death takes the perk straight back off you.

## Stat gains during play

Not perks, but worth listing alongside them:

- **Vault City surgery** (Dr. Troy, once you have a good standing): +1 to one of ST, PE, CH
  or IN. Evil characters usually cannot get the Intelligence version.
- **Mirrored Shades**: +1 Charisma while worn. Found in New Reno; the fastest way to a
  bigger party.
- **Blue Memory Module** (Vault 13 / brain alignment): +1 Intelligence.
- **Zeta Scan / weird brain alignments**: +1 IN and +1 LK, **or** +2 LK. Mutually exclusive.
- **Miss Kitty date** (New Reno): +1 Agility, awarded after the Oil Rig.
  <Vanilla>The date has no mechanical reward in the unmodded game.</Vanilla>
- **Power Armor**: +3 Strength while worn. **Advanced Power Armor**: +4.
