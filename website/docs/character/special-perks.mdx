# Special Perks

These do not cost a perk slot. They are handed out by quests, doctors and one very unlucky
dog, and they appear alongside your normal perks on the character sheet.

## Implants — Vault City

The most valuable set in the game. Learn about implants by talking to **Dr. Troy** in Vault
City, then buy them from a qualified doctor in **Vault City, Redding or San Francisco**. They
are expensive and permanent.

| Perk | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Dermal Impact Armor** | +5% damage resistance, +4% AC |
| **Dermal Impact Assault Enhancement** | +10% damage resistance, +8% AC (replaces the above) |
| **Phoenix Armor Implants** | +5% laser/fire/plasma/electrical resistance |
| **Phoenix Assault Enhancement** | +10% energy resistances (replaces the above) |

Take the assault-grade versions if you can afford them. Dermal Impact is the more broadly
useful of the two lines.

## Vault City

| Perk | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Vault City Inoculations** | Dr. Troy. +10% poison and radiation resistance. Free. Get it. |
| **Vault City Training** | Dr. Troy. +5% First Aid and Doctor. Free. |
| **Autodoc Raised Hit Points** | Dr. Andrew's Auto-Doc. +2 max HP. |
| **Autodoc Lowered Hit Points** | The same Auto-Doc, going badly. −2 max HP. |
| **Alcohol Raised Hit Points** / **II** | Drinking contest in the Parlor Room. +2 max HP each. |
| **Alcohol Lowered Hit Points** / **II** | The same contest, going badly. −2 max HP each. |

The alcohol and Auto-Doc perks are gambles. Save first.

## Jinxed

The **pariah dog** — a special random encounter — will follow you until you accept it, and
gives you the Jinxed trait permanently. Everyone around you, including you, starts fumbling
critically. It is a curse, not a reward. Kill the dog or leave the map.

## Stat gains during play

Not perks, but worth listing alongside them:

- **Vault City surgery** (Dr. Troy, once you have a good standing): +1 to one of ST, PE, CH
  or IN. Evil characters usually cannot get the Intelligence version.
- **Mirrored Shades**: +1 Charisma while worn. Found in New Reno; the fastest way to a
  bigger party.
- **Blue Memory Module** (Vault 13 / brain alignment): +1 Intelligence.
- **Zeta Scan / weird brain alignments**: +1 IN and +1 LK, **or** +2 LK. Mutually exclusive.
- **Miss Kitty date** (New Reno): +1 Agility, awarded after the Oil Rig.
  <Vanilla>The date has no mechanical reward in the unmodded game.</Vanilla>
- **Power Armor**: +3 Strength while worn. **Advanced Power Armor**: +4.
