# Character Creation

Fallout 2 gives you one screen at the start of the game and then holds you to it for the
next eighty hours. You pick seven SPECIAL statistics, up to two traits, and three tagged
skills. Almost everything else — perks, skill points, equipment — accumulates as you play.

The good news is that Fallout 2 is far more forgiving than its reputation suggests. You earn
a lot of experience, skills climb quickly, and stats can be raised during play. A merely
sensible character will finish the game comfortably. The bad news is that a handful of
specific mistakes will make the first fifteen hours miserable, and this section exists to
help you avoid them.

## The short version

If you want to stop reading and start playing:

- **SPECIAL:** ST 5, PE 6, EN 4, CH 4, IN 8, AG 8, LK 5
- **Traits:** Gifted and Small Frame
- **Tag skills:** Small Guns, Speech, Lockpick

That character can talk its way through most of the game, shoot its way through the rest,
and open everything it finds. See [Suggested Builds](/character/builds) for alternatives.

## What matters, in order

1. **Intelligence.** It gates conversation options across the entire game, and it sets your
   skill points per level. Below 4 you cannot hold a normal conversation at all — see
   [Low Intelligence](/systems/low-intelligence) if that appeals to you.
2. **Agility.** It sets Action Points, which is how much you get to do in a combat round.
   It is the hardest stat to raise later.
3. **Your primary combat skill.** Pick one and commit. Small Guns carries you furthest.
4. **Perception 5+.** Purely so you can take the Awareness perk at level 3, which shows you
   every enemy's health and weapon for the rest of the game.

Everything else is negotiable.

## Stats can be raised during play

Do not treat your starting numbers as final. Over a full playthrough you can gain:

| Stat | Maximum gain | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | +6 | Gain Strength perk, Vault City surgery, Power Armor (+3) / Advanced (+4) |
| Perception | +2 | Gain Perception perk, Vault City surgery |
| Endurance | +1 | Gain Endurance perk |
| Charisma | +3 | Gain Charisma perk, Vault City surgery, Mirrored Shades |
| Intelligence | +3 | Gain Intelligence perk, Vault City surgery, Blue Memory Module |
| Agility | +1 | Gain Agility perk |
| Luck | +3 | Gain Luck perk, Zeta Scan / Memory Module outcomes |

:::note
No stat can be raised above 10 in practice. Setting Strength to 10 at creation wastes
several character points, because armour alone will carry you most of the way there.
Evil characters normally miss out on the Intelligence bonus from Vault City surgery.
:::

## Gender

Gender has a small effect and no mechanical penalty. It changes who will sleep with you,
which matters for a handful of quests:

- **Male characters** can donate genetic material in Vault City and date Phyllis, and can
  work as a comedian at the Shark Club in New Reno. Only male characters can box.
- **Female characters** can sleep with Metzger, Lil' Jesus and T-Ray for favours, can be
  hired by Miss Kitty to be sent to the Stables, and can dance at the Shark Club.
  <Vanilla>The comedian and dancer jobs at the Shark Club are RPU additions; the original
  game has no paid entertainment work there.</Vanilla>

Neither is better. Pick whichever you want to look at.

## Skip the premade characters

Narg, Chitsa and Mingan exist so you can start the game in ten seconds. They are all built
badly — Narg tags Throwing, Chitsa tags Barter, Mingan takes Skilled — and none of them is
close to what you would make yourself. Spend the two minutes.
