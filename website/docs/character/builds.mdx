# Suggested Builds

A note before the list: **do not build a theme character.** Tagging Science and Repair and
then taking "tech perks", or tagging Sneak and Steal and taking "ninja perks", sounds
appealing and plays badly. Fallout 2's skill system is generous enough that a generalist
gets to do all of it anyway, and the specialist just arrives at the same place with fewer
tools.

What every viable character needs is the same three things:

1. Decent **Intelligence** — for dialogue and skill points.
2. Decent **Speech** — because that is where the quests are.
3. **One efficient way to kill things.**

Everything after that is preference.

The "developed" stats below assume Advanced Power Armor (+4 ST), the Vault 13 memory module
(+1 IN), the NCR Zeta Scan (+2 LK), and any Gain Foo perks listed in the progression.

---

## Team Gunfighter

Bright, sociable, brings three friends. The most comfortable way to play the game.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Starting** | ST 5, PE 6, EN 4, CH 6, IN 7, AG 7, LK 6 |
| **Developed** | ST 10, PE 7, EN 4, CH 6, IN 8, AG 8, LK 8 |
| **Traits** | Small Frame, Fast Shot |
| **Tags** | Small Guns, Lockpick, Speech |

**Perks:** 3 Awareness · 6 Quick Pockets · 9 Better Criticals · 12 Living Anatomy ·
15 Bonus Rate of Fire · 18 Gain Agility · 21 Action Boy · 24 Sniper

Charisma 6 gives you three companions. Pick up Sulik in Klamath, Vic in the Den and Cassidy
in Vault City and you have a competent posse by the end of the first act. You will need a
Mentat or Jet to meet Sniper's Agility requirement at level 24.

Late game, switching to Big Guns pairs well with Fast Shot.

## Solo Sniper

Charisma 1, no companions, everything invested in being lethal at range. Harder for the first
few hours; devastating after level 15.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Starting** | ST 5, PE 7, EN 4, CH 1, IN 7, AG 9, LK 8 |
| **Developed** | ST 10, PE 8, EN 4, CH 1, IN 8, AG 10, LK 10 |
| **Traits** | Small Frame, Finesse |
| **Tags** | Small Guns, Lockpick, Speech |

**Perks:** 3 Awareness · 6 Quick Pockets · 9 Better Criticals · 12 Living Anatomy ·
15 Bonus Rate of Fire · 18 Gain Agility · 21 Action Boy · 24 Sniper · 27 Mutate! → Fast Shot

Build Small Guns to 150% and work through the Scoped Hunting Rifle → Sniper Rifle → Gauss
Rifle progression. The Mutate! swap at 27 turns Finesse into a second Bonus Rate of Fire once
Sniper has made aiming redundant.

:::tip
Delay the enhanced combat implants until you have temporarily recruited the companions who
give you XP or bonuses — Myron's jet antidote quest in particular.
:::

## Brawler

Unarmed, fast, and surprisingly durable. The most fun early game of any build, because your
fists are better than the guns you can afford.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Starting** | ST 6, PE 6, EN 5, CH 4, IN 7, AG 9, LK 5 |
| **Developed** | ST 10, PE 7, EN 5, CH 4, IN 8, AG 10, LK 7 |
| **Traits** | Gifted, One Hander |
| **Tags** | Unarmed, Lockpick, Speech |

**Perks:** 3 Awareness · 6 Bonus Move · 9 Better Criticals · 12 HtH Evade ·
15 Bonus HtH Attacks · 18 Action Boy · 21 Living Anatomy · 24 Slayer

One Hander gives a straight +20% because every Unarmed weapon is one-handed. **Do not take
Fast Shot** — it removes targeted attacks without reducing melee AP cost.

Slayer makes every hit a critical, with no Luck roll, which is why this build can ignore Luck.
Work through Brass Knuckles → Power Fist → Mega Power Fist.

## Diplomat

Talks its way through most of the game and lets three companions handle the rest.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Starting** | ST 4, PE 6, EN 4, CH 8, IN 9, AG 7, LK 5 |
| **Developed** | ST 9, PE 7, EN 4, CH 9, IN 10, AG 8, LK 7 |
| **Traits** | Gifted, Good Natured |
| **Tags** | Speech, Lockpick, Small Guns |

**Perks:** 3 Awareness · 6 Quick Pockets · 9 Better Criticals · 12 Living Anatomy ·
15 Bonus Rate of Fire · 18 Action Boy · 21 Gain Luck · 24 Sniper

Charisma 8 means four companions, and with Mirrored Shades you can push to five. Intelligence
9 opens essentially every "supersmart" dialogue option in the game.

Note the honesty here: this is still a gunfighter, because there is no way to finish the
Enclave with words. The difference is that it does more of the game peacefully and gets paid
better for it.

:::note Cult of Personality is worth considering
RPU fully implements it. Taken by a good character, it opens slaver-aligned content — Vortis's
Ranger safe house job in NCR, for instance — without wrecking your karma.
<Vanilla>The perk does nothing at all in the unmodded game.</Vanilla>
:::

## Dialogue requirements to keep in mind

As a rule of thumb across the whole game:

- "Smart" dialogue options need **IN 6–7**
- "Supersmart" options need **IN 8–9**
- IN 10 lines exist (Karl, Laddie, Gregory) but are all inconsequential

If a conversation seems thinner than the guide describes, chew a Mentat and try again.
