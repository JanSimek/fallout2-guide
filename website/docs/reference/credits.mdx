# Credits

## The information

This guide is a rewrite. Almost everything factual in it was originally documented by other people
over the past twenty-five years, and it would not exist without them.

### Per Jorner — *The Nearly Ultimate Fallout 2 Guide*

The definitive Fallout 2 reference, written between 2001 and 2008 and researched to an extraordinary
depth — not just played through but read out of the game's own scripts. Almost every mechanical detail
here, from the exact skill check thresholds to the reason Sharpshooter is bugged, traces back to that
document.

Per Jorner's copyright notice reads, in part:

> *"Of course that doesn't mean I have any claims on the information contained within, i.e. I don't
> mind if you grab every single piece of info and put it in your own walkthrough as long as you use
> your own words to do it."*

That is exactly what this guide does. The prose here is original; the facts are largely his.

The original is preserved at
[lemmings19.github.io/fallout-2-walkthrough](https://lemmings19.github.io/fallout-2-walkthrough/),
mirrored by **Lemmings19** after the original host went offline.

### The Restoration Project walkthrough

Everything about content the Restoration Project **adds** — the EPA, the Abbey, the Umbra Tribe, Vault
Village, the Slaver's Camp, the Shi submarine, Dr. Sheng, the Hubologist stash, Kaga, and the dozens
of smaller changes — comes from the community-maintained RPU walkthrough at
[f2rp.bgforge.net](https://f2rp.bgforge.net/), hosted by **BGforge** and maintained on
[GitHub](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/f2rp-walkthrough).

### The Fallout Wiki

Quest names, page links and reward cross-checking come from
[fallout.fandom.com](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Fallout_2_quests).

## The mod

**Restoration Project** was created by **killap**, who spent years restoring content that was cut from
Fallout 2 shortly before release.

**Restoration Project Updated** is maintained by **BGforge** and continues that work, bundling the
Unofficial Patch and a set of quality-of-life mods.

RPU includes work by many people:

- [Unofficial Patch](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2_Unofficial_Patch)
- [High quality music](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2-HQ-music)
- [Hero Appearance](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2_Hero_Appearance)
- [NPC Armor](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2_NPC_Armor)
- [Party Orders](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2_Party_Orders)
- [Cassidy talking head](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2_Cassidy_Head) — model by **Continuum**,
  voiced by **Dravean**
- Enhanced worldmap, extended flamer animations, additional rifle and wakizashi animations, improved
  Mysterious Stranger, and the high resolution patch

The full credits are in the
[RPU repository](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2_Restoration_Project/blob/master/docs/credits.md).
