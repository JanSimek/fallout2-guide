# Endings

:::caution[Spoilers, obviously]
:::

To get an ending for a location at all, it must be **named on your world map** — you must either have
visited it or had someone mark it for you.

Several endings are buggy or incomplete. Where that matters, it is noted.

## Arroyo

**Arroyo re-settled using the G.E.C.K.** — you always get this, even if you no longer have a G.E.C.K.
(having let both of them blow up with the Enclave, for instance).

## Modoc

| Ending | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| **Modoc flourishes** | Broker peace between Modoc and the Slags |
| Razed after the Slags are killed by townspeople | Deliver the message, then offer to help and fail — or join the attack |
| Disperses after the Slags are killed | Kill the Slags yourself, or deliver the message and immediately refuse further involvement |
| Disperses due to drought | Never deliver the message and never kill the Slags |

## The Den

| Ending | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| **The Den flourishes** | Kill Metzger, **not** Rebecca |
| The Den disperses | Kill both Metzger and Rebecca |
| Becomes a haven for raiders | Leave Metzger alive, kill Big Jesus Mordino |
| Becomes slaver central | Leave both alive |

## Vault City

| Ending | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| **Independent — Westin and Lynette marry** | Fix the power plant, kill the raiders, expose Bishop's plan, deliver both holodisks, **do not collect the final reward from Randal** (a bug is what makes this work), and leave Westin and Lynette alive |
| Becomes part of NCR | Fix the plant, kill the raiders, bring the Account Book or Bishop's Holodisk to Lynette. You get this if you miss the marriage ending for any reason |
| Annexed by NCR | Fix the plant, bring neither the Account Book nor Bishop's Holodisk |
| Population driven to NCR by radiation | Do not fix the power plant — blowing it up counts |

## Gecko

| Ending | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Uneasy truce with Vault City | Fix the plant, but **do not** get the Reactor Holodisk from Festus |
| Invaded by Vault City | Fix **and optimize** the plant |
| Destroyed | Blow up the power plant |

:::caution
You can fall between the first two and get **no Gecko ending at all**, even having fixed the plant.

The genuinely good Gecko ending — optimize the plant *and* get the Gecko Holodisk from Gordon after
talking to the Brain — **cannot be obtained** because of a bug in the endings script.

Rumours that giving McClure the Gecko Holodisk or doing Stark's scouting mission triggers the invasion
ending are wrong. Optimizing the plant always does.
:::

## New Reno

The two **impregnation endings take precedence over all others**, and are available to male characters
only.

| Ending | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Tribals arise | Impregnate either Bishop woman, leave her alive, and tell Mrs. Bishop she should move on |
| Bishop child | Impregnate either Bishop woman and leave her alive, but do **not** tell Mrs. Bishop to move on |
| Everyone dead | Kill all four bosses |
| Bishops / Salvatores / Mordinos rule | Leave that family in control |
| Mordinos wane | Leave the Mordinos in control, deliver jet to Dr. Troy but **never return for the antidote** (bug) |
| **Wrights rule fairly** | Leave the Wrights in control, **do not enter the SAD**, and either finish the murder quest (any way), turn the Wrights hostile, or never take the quest at all |
| Wrights seize power violently | Leave the Wrights in control **and enter the SAD** |
| Wrights attack the Mordinos and are slaughtered | Leave the Wrights in control, do not enter the SAD, take but never finish the murder quest, and do not attack them |

:::note[RPU changes the Wright takeover]
**Contaminating the SAD with a virus prevents the Wrights taking New Reno by force.** They can still
take it peacefully.
<Vanilla>Contamination has no effect on the endings.</Vanilla>
:::

### The power struggle

Each family starts with a **power counter of 100**:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Kill a boss | That family drops to **0** |
| Kill a bartender, casino bouncer, generic guard or unnamed adult Wright | −1 |
| **Mordino:** kill or recruit Myron | **+20** (a bug) |
| **Mordino:** kill Little Jesus | −1 |
| **Bishop:** break the raiders' strength | −20 |
| **Salvatore:** disturb the desert transaction | −20 |
| **Salvatore:** kill Mason | −5 |
| **Wright:** break the stills | −20 |
| **Wright:** kill Keith Wright | −2 |
| **Wright:** kill Chris Wright | −1 |

Highest counter wins. On a tie the priority is **Mordino → Bishop → Salvatore → Wright**.

In practice this rarely matters, since you will have killed the bosses of whichever families you
disliked.

## Myron

**Myron dead and forgotten** — hear his name from Jules, Little Jesus, Renesco, Reed or Myron himself,
and do not have him killed during the game.

## Vault 13

| Ending | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| **Genocide** | Kill Gruthar, **or** return to Vault 13 after the massacre |

:::caution
You can avoid this — and get **no Vault 13 ending at all** — by never triggering the massacre, or by
never returning afterwards.

If you do get it, the game blames **you** for killing the deathclaws regardless of who actually did.
:::

:::note[RPU restores the good ending]
In RPU it **is** possible to get a good ending for Vault 13. The key is not collecting Gruthar's
reward for fixing the computer, which is what starts the two-week clock on the massacre.
<Vanilla>There is no good Vault 13 ending at all.</Vanilla>
:::

## San Francisco

| Ending | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| **The Shi flourish** | Do not turn the Shi hostile, do not kill Dr. Wong |
| Sentient plant problems | Kill Dr. Wong without turning the Shi hostile |
| Population disperses after your rampage | Turn the Shi hostile |

:::note[RPU explains the plant ending]
The **[Dr. Sheng questline](/walkthrough/san-francisco)** is what the "sentient plant problems" slide
was always describing. In the unmodded game it appears with no context whatsoever.
:::

:::caution[The Shi are extremely sensitive]
Do everything correctly, then stand outside the tanker, throw a plant spike at a dockworker for **1
point of damage**, and sail for the Enclave — and you get the "demoralized and leaderless after your
rampage" ending.
:::

## Tanker vagrants

**The vagrants move on** — you always get this, even if you killed every one of them.

## Character epilogues

:::note[Added by RPU]
The Restoration Project restores **seven** endgame slides that the original game never shows —
epilogues for individual characters rather than for towns. They are marked "Seraph's Code" in the
scripts.
<Vanilla>`endgame.txt` in the unmodded game has 52 slides. RPU's has 59.</Vanilla>
:::

| Slide | How you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **The Elder** | Awarded automatically at the endgame — **unless the Arroyo Elder dies**, which disables it permanently. |
| **Harold** | **Talk to Harold** in Gecko, and leave him alive. Killing him cancels it. |
| **Marcus** | **Recruit Marcus**, and have him alive at the end. It is also awarded if Broken Hills reaches the "uranium runs out" ending with **karma ≥ 0** and Marcus alive. |
| **President Richardson** | **Talk to the President** on the Enclave presidential level. Hearing him out is worth it anyway — he has a talking head and a lot of dialogue. |
| **The Hubologists** (3 variants) | Which one depends on how far you took their space programme — see below. |

**The Hubologist slide** has three versions, chosen in this order:

1. You never **fuelled their spaceship** → the first variant.
2. You fuelled it **and** they got the **vertibird plans** (either by fetching them from Navarro or by
   stealing the Shi's) → the second.
3. You fuelled it but they never got the plans → the third.

:::caution
The whole Hubologist slide is skipped if you turned on them — the check requires you not to have
wiped them out.
:::

:::tip
Three of these cost you nothing: **talk to Harold**, **talk to President Richardson**, and **keep the
Elder alive**. The guide already sends you to all three for other reasons.
:::

## Getting the best endings

A rough checklist for the most positive outcome across the board:

1. **Modoc** — broker peace with the Slags
2. **The Den** — kill Metzger, spare Rebecca
3. **Gecko** — fix the plant, do **not** optimize it
4. **Vault City** — fix the plant, kill the raiders, deliver both holodisks, **skip Randal's final
   reward**
5. **Redding** — deliver the Jet Antidote, keep Dr. Johnson alive, leave the miners alone
6. **Broken Hills** — have Marcus jail Jacob's group
7. **New Reno** — leave the **Wrights** in control, never enter the SAD, resolve the murder correctly
8. **NCR and Vault 15** — complete the deal
9. **Vault 13** — fix the computer but **do not collect Gruthar's reward**
10. **San Francisco** — do not kill Dr. Wong, do not annoy the Shi
