# Known Issues

RPU includes the Unofficial Patch, which fixes hundreds of the bugs that older Fallout 2 guides warn
about. This page covers what is **still** a hazard.

Bugs specific to a single quest are noted on that location's page.

## Save your game properly

The single best defence against everything below.

1. **Keep plenty of save slots.** FOR:CE has no ten-slot limit; on the original engine set
   `ExtraSaveSlots=1` in `ddraw.ini`. See [Configuration](/getting-started/configuration).
2. **Rotate slots.** Keep a save from the start of each town.
3. **Never save in combat mode**, especially with a dead companion on the map. Such saves reliably
   freeze on end of turn when loaded.
4. **Never save while explosives are waiting to detonate.** This can corrupt the save slot entirely.

## The "too many items" bug

The most serious remaining problem, and an engine-level one.

**What happens:** late in the game, if a single map holds too many *different* items, the map becomes
corrupted when you leave it. Next time you try to enter, the game locks up completely — the orbiting
rocket cursor appears and never animates.

The count includes what you and your companions carry, items on critters, items in containers
(**including shops**), and everything in your car — whether the car is present or not.

:::caution[Warning signs]
The **skilldex button** on the main screen and the **sleep button** in the Pip-Boy turning into black
squares means the bug is coming.

There is a strong association with **Pip-Boy status entries**: eleven is reportedly fine, **twelve
causes problems**, in both Fallout and Fallout 2. Some players avoid the status screen entirely and
report never seeing the crash.
:::

**Prevention:**

- Do not carry quest items you have finished with. If you want to keep them, store them in a container
  on a map with **no shops** rather than in your car trunk.
- Do not sell a great many different item types in one place. **San Francisco Chinatown**, with two
  large shops, is where most players hit this first.

**Recovery:** there is no way to repair a corrupted map, but you can substitute the file from an
earlier save — `sfchina.sav` for Chinatown, for example. Critters and items revert to that save's
state and local variables may change, but global variables (quest status) and your car's contents are
stored elsewhere and survive.

## The car

The car is much better behaved than it was, and it can still be lost.

:::danger
- **Running out of fuel** is when the bug strikes: the car circle can teleport across the world map or
  vanish. **Refuel regularly.**
- The car circle can land **inside a town circle**, where clicking enters the town instead. The car is
  then lost. Reload.
- **The Arroyo bridge** map is replaced after the fourth dream. A car parked there is gone forever,
  along with any companion left on any Arroyo map.
- **Fake Vault 13** (Merk's holodisk) also replaces its map.
- Being **teleported** by a quest — Big Jesus to the Stables, for instance — has been known to detach
  the car. Driving around avoiding encounters until you run dry usually recovers it via the "Car Outta
  Fuel" map.
:::

See [The Car](/systems/car) for the full list of places it misbehaves.

## Companions

:::danger
- **Looting an unconscious companion** counts as stealing from a non-party member. Vic and Myron stop
  communicating permanently; Goris turns hostile when dismissed.
- **A dead companion's body and inventory vanish** if you save/load or change maps.
- **Sleeping with Miria or Davin without going through with the wedding** freezes all your companions
  in place. Fixed by getting married, the Val/Vic conversation in Vault City, or watching the Vault 13
  or San Francisco video sequences.
- Saving and reloading **upstairs at the Bishop residence** makes your whole party invisible. Sleep
  with one of the Bishops again, or dismiss everyone before you go up.
:::

## Ring fights

Every ring fight in the game — New Reno boxing, NCR's Lenny, San Francisco's martial artists — can trap
you permanently.

:::danger
- **Do not save during a ring fight.** Reloading leaves you stuck inside the ring after you win.
- **Do not end combat between killing your opponent and the "You win" sign.** Use **end turn**, always.
- The extra round after "You win" exists so you can save. Using it to attack is what causes most of the
  bugs.
- **Drop the Bag** before the NCR Lenny fight — it comes back empty.
:::

## Conversations that lock the game

:::danger
- **Liz in Broken Hills** — asking about the weapons stash and choosing "it's all over town", saying
  "You" when she asks what you plan to kill, or failing the Speech check after the "reliable source"
  line all try to start combat during dialogue. You can press 1 and Esc to escape, but you must
  **restart the game entirely** before it plays normally.
- **Mr. Bishop's safe** — if you already disarmed the trap, being asked to replace it and trying
  **crashes the game**. Go to the basement and back to re-arm it instead.
- **Modoc** — killing Grisham before sleeping with Miria or Davin freezes the game when he is supposed
  to walk in; killing Jo freezes the wedding ceremony.
- **NCR's Lenny** — using the Mutagenic Serum on him in the ring before damaging him leaves him alive
  but immobile and traps you in unending combat.
- **The EPA computer** — reading the "penpal greetings" mail permanently disables that terminal.
- **The Enclave foyer terminal** — activating the emergency shutdown without the Presidential Access
  Key at Science 75%+ permanently disables it, which is the only terminal that can run counter
  insurgence.
:::

## Places you can get permanently stuck

:::danger
- **Sierra Army Depot** — taking an elevator without an eye, with Repair below 41% and no explosives.
  Keep Repair above 41% and carry explosives before going down.
- **The Vault City vault armoury** — the door checks whether *it* sees you, not whether Martin does.
  Sneak lets you in but not out, and companions pass through it uselessly. Being an actual citizen
  avoids it entirely.
- **The Hubologist base** — you can get stuck between forcefields with guards preventing you from
  leaving combat to disable them. You can fire through the forcefields, at least.
:::

## RPU's own known issues

These are deliberate decisions or engine limitations, not bugs. Do not report them.

**EPA**

- Holograms show up on the motion sensor.
- You cannot reach the EPA entry area to return to the world map **without the resolution patch
  installed**. You can still play at 640×480 once it is installed.
- No animation when using plant spray — an sfall engine limitation.

**New Reno**

- Dancing at the Shark Club uses the default female critter regardless of your character's appearance.
  In-universe: you are wearing a wig. Out of universe: new critters would have to be added to the game.
- Some gangs around the city are not hostile despite floating text suggesting they should be.
  Arguably they are patrolling to defend New Reno.
- You can buy Three-Card Monte's cards through barter and he still complains about losing them.

## Reporting bugs

Genuine RPU bugs go to the
[RPU issue tracker](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2_Restoration_Project/issues) or the
[BGforge forums](https://forums.bgforge.net/viewforum.php?f=39). **Attach a savegame and a
screenshot**, and report each bug separately.

Crashes have their own
[reporting guide](https://github.com/BGforgeNet/Fallout2_Unofficial_Patch/blob/master/docs/crash.md).

Inconsistencies in this guide go to
[its own repository](https://github.com/JanSimek/fallout2-guide).
