# Items and Equipment

## Skill books

Books raise a skill by an amount that shrinks as the skill grows: **6–7 points** at 20–30%, down to
**1–2** at 70–90%. **Tagged skills gain double.**

**They stop working at 91%** — 92% if you raise a tagged skill from 90.

Reading takes `11 − Intelligence` hours.

| Book | Raises | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Guns and Bullets** | Small Guns | Klamath |
| **Scout Handbook** | Outdoorsman | Klamath (Trapper Town), Vault City vault |
| **Big Book of Science** | Science | Vault City |
| **First Aid Book** | First Aid | Modoc (Grisham's), Vault City (Randal, vault) |
| **Dean's Electronics** | Repair | Ghost Farm, Vault City |

:::tip[Read books before spending points]
Repair, Science, First Aid and Outdoorsman can all reach 91% for free. Do not spend a single skill
point on them until the books are exhausted.
:::

### The book exploit

:::note
Skills are calculated from stats, and the 91% cap is applied to your **current, artificially lowered**
skill. Lower the governing stat, read the books, and let the stat recover — you keep the points.

Ceilings reachable this way: **Small Guns, First Aid and Science 127%; Repair 118%; Outdoorsman 115%**,
assuming PE, IN and AG at 10, EN above 3, and all skills already at 91%.

- **−9 AG:** 2 Buffout, rest 5 hours, 2 Monument Chunks, rest 1 hour
- **−9 IN:** 2 Mentats, rest 24 hours, 2 Psycho
- **−9 PE:** a great deal of alcohol
- **−3 EN:** 2 Buffout, rest 6 hours, 1 Buffout, rest 6 hours

Read from the **inventory screen**, not a weapon slot — time does not pass until you close it, so your
stats cannot recover mid-session.

The **Game Difficulty** setting does the same thing more simply: easy → normal drops non-combat skills
by 20%, normal → hard by another 10%. Read the books back up to 91% and set it back. (Guns and Bullets
is exempt.) Combine both techniques **simultaneously** to maximise the gain.
:::

## Chems

| Chem | Duration | Effect | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Psycho** | 4+4 hrs | AG +3, IN −3, **DR +50** | The Den |
| **Buffout** | 6 hrs | ST +2, AG +2, EN +3 | Den (Tubby, grave), Modoc (Balthas') |
| **Mentats** | 24 hrs | **IN +2**, PE +2, CH +1 | Vault City (Lynette), Broken Hills (Eric, Professor) |
| **Jet** | 5 mins | AP +2, ST +1, PE +1 | The Den |
| **Rad-X** | 24+24 hrs | Radiation Resistance +50 | Toxic Caves |
| **RadAway** | — | Radiation Level −150 | Klamath (Sajag), Toxic Caves |
| **Antidote** | — | Poison Level −75 | Temple of Trials |

**Psycho is the best combat drug** — real damage protection plus AP. **Mentats** are the utility drug:
+2 Intelligence opens dialogue you would otherwise miss entirely.

:::caution
- You **cannot benefit from more than two doses** of a chem at once — but additional doses can still
  addict you.
- Stats raised by chems **drop below their original values** afterwards, by roughly the size of the
  bonus.
- **Psycho and Rad-X** wear off in two halves.
- **RadAway** takes full effect over 4 hours; **Antidote** over 2 minutes.
:::

:::note
Saving and reloading does **not** get you around the two-dose limit. The limit counts the drug
effects still queued against you, and that queue is written into the save and restored with it.
:::

:::

## Addiction

| Chem | Risk | Withdrawal | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Jet** | **50%** | AP −2, ST −1, PE −1 | **Indefinite** |
| Buffout | 25% | ST −2, EN −2, AG −3 | 7 days |
| Psycho | 20% | IN −2 | 7 days |
| Mentats | 15% | IN −3, AG −2 | 7 days |
| Nuka-Cola | 10% | None detectable | 7 days |
| RadAway | 10% | Radiation Resistance −20 | 7 days |

:::danger
**Jet addiction is permanent without the antidote**, and the antidote **does not work on companions**
— the engine gates the cure on whether *you* are addicted, not on whoever you use it on, so on a
clean-blooded character it does nothing at all.
One careless dose can permanently cripple a companion's Action Points.
:::

:::note[RPU adds two more]
**Alcohol** and the card game **Tragic** can now addict you — both were defined in the original files
and never implemented. Neither is dangerous.
<Vanilla>Neither addiction can be acquired, despite persistent rumours.</Vanilla>
:::

## Alcohol

Beer, Booze and Gamma Gulp Beer cost **PE −1** for 30 minutes; Rot Gut and Roentgen Rum cost **PE −2**.

:::tip
You can **use alcohol on other people** to lower their Perception before a Sneak or Steal attempt, or
to interfere with scripts — Keith Wright guarding the Wright door, for instance. Beer and booze also
make cows fall over.
:::

## The Bag

The Bag from the Modoc mole rat cave is the single most exploitable item in the game.

:::note
Items positioned **below the Bag** in your inventory are "protected" from scripts that remove them —
the engine mishandles the container item type.

This lets you: give Cornelius's watch to **both** him and Farrel for both quest rewards; keep the
original weapon **and** the upgrade from Algernon or Skeeter; have Myron make unlimited stimpaks from
the same ingredients.

Money and items stored **inside** the Bag are unaffected. If you want to carry the Bag without
exploiting it, keep it at the **bottom** of your inventory.
:::

The Bag also has a legitimate use: put **armed explosives** in it and drop it next to someone. Nobody
notices, and it is the cleanest assassination method in the game.

:::danger
The Bag is **confiscated and returned empty** before the Lenny fight in NCR. Drop it first.
:::

| Item | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Monument Chunk** | ST +3, AG +3, DR +50 for 1 hour. From the Talking Head encounter. |
| **Cookie** | AP +1 for 15 minutes. Modoc and the SAD. |
| **Mutated Toe** | Max HP −3, Poison +2 for 7 days. Its only real use is on Frank Horrigan, where it does the same thing. |
| **Fallout 2 Hintbook** | **10,000 XP per use** and raises every skill to 300%. Father Tully, after you finish the game. |

:::note
**Gold nuggets, uranium ore and refined uranium ore** can be switched to punch mode but cannot attack —
their range is set to zero. **Rocks** work, and add your Melee Damage to maximum damage.

**Pulse Grenades** do no damage to non-robot critters — but can do a lot to *you* unless you are
armoured, because the player character uniquely carries EMP resistance in the armour definition rather
than the critter definition.
:::

## Shops worth remembering

| Shop | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Randal's Amenities**, Vault City | Books, cheap stimpaks, combat shotgun at half the NCR price |
| **Buster**, NCR bazaar | **2mm EC**, Pancor Jackhammer, Ripper, Power Fist, Avenger Minigun. Scoped Hunting Rifle conversion for $500. |
| **Flying Dragon 8**, San Francisco | Power Armor, Tesla Armor, handbooks, stimpak ingredients |
| **Red 888 Guns**, San Francisco | **Gauss Rifle**, Power Armor, ammunition |
| **Mr. Chemmie**, EPA | Makes almost every consumable in the game from raw ingredients |

**NCR and San Francisco have the best ammunition shops by far.**

:::tip[Selling is always at full value]
Your items sell for a fixed price. Barter skill, discounts and Charisma affect only **their** goods.
So pay cash for expensive purchases and trade in your junk afterwards at leisure.

The catch: shopkeepers restock money along with wares, and any cash above their normal amount
**disappears** at restock.
:::
