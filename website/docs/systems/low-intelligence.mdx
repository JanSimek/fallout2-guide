# Low Intelligence

> *"Ooooh! Butunz!"* — The Chosen One

Set Intelligence below 4 and your character speaks only in grunts. Most people will not talk to you,
almost nobody gives you quests, and you can essentially never use a computer.

It is a genuinely different game, with a large amount of unique dialogue written specially for it, and
it is very funny. It is also considerably harder.

## The honest assessment

A single character point takes half the game away. You lose:

- Most quests, and therefore most quest experience
- Free training in Arroyo, Klamath and San Francisco
- The combat implants
- Almost every dialogue-based bonus in the game

You will level up **much** more slowly, which means most major battles arrive long before you are
ready for them. You cannot free Vic without killing Metzger's slavers, and you will not manage that at
level 3 without playing dirty.

The trade-off is unique content you will never see otherwise, and a real challenge.

## Building one

**Keep Intelligence at exactly 3.** You get few enough skill points as it is.

:::caution
**Skilled** looks like the obvious remedy and is not worth it. Fewer skill points is balanced by not
needing Speech, and there are not many skills you actually need.
:::

**Skill priorities:**

1. **One combat skill** — Small Guns, which carries the whole game
2. **Lockpick** for looting
3. **Doctor** for convenience
4. **Big Guns** later, as a second combat skill
5. Read every book you find

## Route

Location discovery is the hard part, since almost nobody will tell you anything.

1. **Klamath** — **Torr** gives you the Den if you ask about Vic before accepting the brahmin quest
2. **The Den** — Vic can give you Vault City, but you would have to kill Metzger first
3. **Wander east** and stumble across **Modoc**, then **Vault City** by chance
4. **The Vault City central computer** gives you four towns and **Vault 15**
5. **Vault 15** or **NCR** gives you **Vault 13** — or follow the deathclaws from Westin's pastures
6. Find the **Military Base** yourself, and use the **Survey Map** there for **San Francisco**
7. **Matthew** puts **Navarro** on your map

Gecko never appears on your map through dialogue.

## Getting the car anyway

A stupid character normally cannot buy the car. Here is how to get it regardless:

1. Go to **Gecko** and kill **Skeeter** for the Fuel Cell Controller — the other ghouls do not care
2. **Steal Lynette's Mentats** on your way through Vault City
3. Return to the **Den** with **$2750** and both car parts
4. **Eat the Mentats**, talk to Smitty, install the parts

You will need Mentats again for the NCR and New Reno upgrades, though you can manage without them.

## Companions

You can recruit **Sulik, Vic, Goris, K-9 and Dogmeat** — and anyone else if you use Mentats.

:::danger
You cannot **talk** to any of them except Goris and Dogmeat. That means you cannot tell them to put
weapons away entering NCR, and you cannot drop them off anywhere.
:::

Charisma is largely wasted on a stupid character, so you may not want a large party in any case.

## Things that work anyway

You can do anything that does not require dialogue:

- Fix **Bess's** leg in Modoc (but not sell her to Grisham)
- Help **Charlie** in Vault City with RadAway
- Get the XP for **finding the Raiders**, even without the quest
- Get the XP for **clearing the Redding mine**, without buying the deed

:::tip[Simulating quests]
If you know what triggers a reward, you can often produce it. Kill **the Dragon or Lo Pan** with Super
Stimpaks and collect the reward from the other.
:::

## Stupid-only content worth seeing

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Rose**, Modoc B&B | A free wasteland omelet **every single time** you talk to her |
| **Farrel**, Modoc | Gives you a **cookie** as a reward |
| **Modoc**, Slag note | **2000 XP** and Modoc's happy ending outright |
| **Vault City Auto-Doc** | Never malfunctions — and "ride agin" gives the **Autodoc Raised HP** perk |
| **Barkus**, Vault City | Talking to him gets you inside the vault |
| **Skeev**, Vault City | Talking to him gets you inside the vault as a "servant" |
| **Slim Picket**, Klamath | Hands over the Trapper Town key immediately |
| **Jagged Jimmy J**, New Reno | The "mortal coil" line paralyses his brain so you can take everything |
| **Professor**, Broken Hills | Gives you Mentats the first time you pass the Agility test |
| **Jack the healer**, Abbey | Drool at him long enough and he **literally melts**. 200 XP. |
| **The AHS-9**, San Francisco | Prepares the tanker for you, and the reward is repeatable |
| **Matthew**, San Francisco | Also prepares the tanker, skipping the fuel, NavCom and FOB quests |

:::caution[The Vault City Auto-Doc perks are bugged]
Clicking **Autodoc Raised Hit Points** or **Autodoc Lowered Hit Points** on your character sheet
**crashes the game immediately**.
:::

:::danger[Vault City will banish you]
A great many people downtown will call the guards and get you **permanently banished** if you talk to
them and wait — Lydia, Dr. Troy, Lynette and McClure among them. The **"Shamble off"** option gets you
out of a conversation safely, when it is offered.
:::

:::tip[Mentats work both ways]
You do not have to commit. Take a couple of Mentats and wait an hour for the comedown to drop your
Intelligence below 4, and you can see low-Intelligence content on a normal character. **Psycho** is
even more effective.
:::
