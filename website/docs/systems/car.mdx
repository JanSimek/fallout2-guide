# The Car

> *"I told 'em. 'We need traffic signs', I said. But did they listen? Noooo… let's not listen to ol'
> Lumpy!"* — Lumpy

The single biggest quality-of-life improvement in the game. It moves you between locations far faster
and makes random encounters much easier to avoid.

## Getting it

You need two things from two places:

1. The **Fuel Cell Controller** from **Skeeter** in [Gecko](/walkthrough/gecko), traded for a Super
   Tool Kit
2. **$2000** for **Smitty** in [The Den](/walkthrough/the-den)

Bring the **Fuel Cell Regulator** from Klamath's rat caves as well and Smitty installs it for **$750**
— the car then uses **50% less fuel**. Do it.

:::danger[Never use an item on the car before you own it]
Smitty comes running out to shout at you. Do it twice and he turns **hostile**, which is close to
unrecoverable.
:::

:::note[Low Intelligence]
You need to chew Mentats at least once to get the dialogue that earns you the car. See
[Low Intelligence](/systems/low-intelligence) for the full route.
:::

## Fuel

The car runs on **Small Energy Cells** or **Micro Fusion Cells**. Its charge shows as a percentage
when you look at it. Use the fuel on the **middle or forward section** of the car.

| Fuel | One full pack | Refuels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small Energy Cell | 40 | **20%** |
| Micro Fusion Cell | 50 | **50%** |

**Which to use** depends on your weapons. MFC powers the Pulse and Plasma Rifles; SEC powers Power
Fists, the Pulse Pistol and the Alien Blaster. If you shoot conventional guns, burn SEC. For barter
purposes the two are exactly equivalent, and you will end up with far too much of both.

:::note
Using SEC or MFC **on a companion** refuels the car — even if the car is on a different map. The car is
technically a companion itself.
:::

:::caution[The refuelling bug]
After choosing the backpack icon and selecting your fuel, if the mouse pointer happens to be over a
critter or object, your character interacts with **that** instead. Scroll the screen a little and try
again.

Standing next to the trunk while refuelling may open the trunk instead.
:::

## Running out

The car appears on the world map as a green circle marked **"Car Outta Fuel"**. Go there any time to
refuel. If you run dry inside a town circle, it appears on the town map instead.

:::danger[The moment you run out is when the car bug strikes]
The car circle can teleport across the map or vanish entirely. Rare in a normal playthrough, but real.
**Refuel regularly, and save.**

Worse: the car circle can land **inside a town circle**, where clicking takes you into the town rather
than to the car. The car is then **irretrievably lost** and you should reload immediately. As long as
some part of the car circle sits outside the town square you can still reach it by clicking there.
:::

## Upgrades

| Upgrade | Where | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fuel Cell Regulator** | Smitty, the Den — $750 | 50% less fuel used |
| **Blower** | The hooded man in the NCR bazaar — $1000, six hours | Better performance |
| **Engine and trunk** | T-Ray, New Reno — $500 ($300 with Barter) | Bigger trunk, better engine |
| **Grav-plates** | T-Ray, **after finishing the game** | Very fast, almost no fuel use |

:::danger[Get T-Ray's upgrade immediately]
A scripting glitch removes all of T-Ray's dialogue options **once you drive the car off the New Reno
map and return**. Buy the upgrade the moment you recover the car.
:::

:::note[The grav-plates trap]
To get the grav-plates you must **never leave the car unattended on Virgin Street** before finishing
the game, or T-Ray's dialogue bug locks you out.

One route: on first reaching New Reno, talk to Jules to get **Golgotha** on your map, leave the car
there, walk back to New Reno on foot and go to 2nd Street. Then collect the car — from then on you can
park on any New Reno map except Virgin Street.

A better car for the whole game is worth more than a better car after it.
:::

## The trunk

Capacity is **250**, rising to **275** with T-Ray's upgrade — and it is measured in **item size**, not
weight. Size is not displayed anywhere in the game:

| Item type | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Armour | 7–15 |
| Weapons | 1–6 |
| Most other items and drugs | 0–1 |

:::tip[Ammunition is free]
**Ammunition does not take up trunk space at all** — including rockets and fuel tanks. Store every
round you are not currently using. The exceptions are **9mm ball and BBs**, and you never find enough
of those to matter.

**Thrown weapons are not ammunition** and do take up space.
:::

Weightless items like **jet** still have size 1, so keep those in your own inventory.

:::tip[What to sell]
By the midgame you will have more spare weapons than any shopkeeper can pay for. There is essentially
**never** a reason to trade away ammunition — sell the excess combat shotguns first. Keep trading bulky
weapons for any ammunition that might conceivably become useful.
:::

## Places the car behaves oddly

- **The Raiders base:** go down the manhole and back up and the car appears gone. Leave the area, open
  the town map, and travel to the cliff — it is there.
- **New Reno:** the first time you leave it on Virgin Street it is **stolen**. This is a quest, not a
  bug.
- **Toxic Caves:** the car sits at the edge of the screen and you cannot reach the trunk.
- **The Arroyo bridge:** the map is replaced after the fourth dream. **A car parked there is lost
  forever.**
- **Fake Vault 13** (from Merk's holodisk): the map changes when you leave. Drive there and leave on
  foot and the car is gone.
- **The Bishop residence:** save and reload upstairs and the trunk may become inaccessible until you
  drive to another map.
