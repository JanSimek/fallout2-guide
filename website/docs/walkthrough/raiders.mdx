# Raiders

**Wallace** and **Sgt. Stark** in Vault City both ask you to find the raider base that has been
plaguing them.

:::caution RPU hides the base properly
In RPU the base is **not** simply sitting on the world map. There are only two ways to find it:

1. **Get the map from Bishop's safe** in New Reno — and you must *use* it from your inventory.
2. **Leave Vault City on foot between 18:00 and 06:00** and spot the raiders. This is an
   Outdoorsman check, but you can walk in and out repeatedly until it succeeds: **31%** is the
   minimum, and at 40% you should manage it within ten tries. You must have talked to Sergeant
   Stark about the raiders first.

<Vanilla>The base is a conspicuous green circle south-southwest of Vault City from the very
start of the game — despite Stark insisting it is nearly impossible to find. Chris Avellone has
said it was always meant to be hidden behind the map or the tribal guide.</Vanilla>
:::

:::caution
Take the second option and you must **leave your car behind** and walk back to wherever you
parked it.
:::

## Which entrance you get

There are two ways in: a **cave** ("Cliff") and a **manhole** ("Well"). Following the raiders
runs a second Outdoorsman check to decide which you arrive at. Higher skill favours the well.

**Take the well.** It is the better fight, and it is the only entrance from which the peaceful
ending is possible.

:::note
Go down the manhole and back up and your car will appear to be gone. It is not — leave the area,
open the town map, and travel to the cliff. It will be there.
:::

## The cave route

Three of the passages are heavily mined. Use **Traps** on the floor plates for **25 XP each**,
or step on one and start a chain reaction if you are impatient. They do little damage
individually.

:::danger
The **middle lower passage** has a mine that **cannot be disarmed**, and companions are
magnetically attracted to it.
:::

There are also **four invisible pitfalls**. Good Perception spots them, but unlike floor traps
they do not automatically stop your movement — so unless you move a hex at a time you will fall
anyway. Below is a cave with **giant scorpions** (tougher than radscorpions) and a scatter of
items among the bones, including a **hunting rifle** and **leather armor**. A ladder takes you
back up.

## The base

You get **500 XP** the first time you set foot on this map. Coming from the caves means two more
rows of mines and a chainlink fence with two locked doors.

You can now return to Vault City and report. **Wallace** gives you a Day Pass if you are not a
citizen; Stark sends you to Lynette; Lynette sends you back to kill them.

:::tip The fight
Approach from the **well**. Show yourself in the narrow opening to the main room and they come
running. Back behind the nearest corner and pass your turn repeatedly — Cassidy with a combat
shotgun can shoot them to pieces at point-blank range while they queue up in the passage trying
to reach *you*. This works for a melee party too.

Coming in from behind the fence instead means eating FN FAL fire across open ground.
:::

## Shadow-Who-Walks

The tribal guide Stark mentioned. He will not necessarily join the fight, and you can keep him
alive and neutral throughout.

:::note RPU's peaceful ending
**Only if you entered through the well.** Climb down, sneak to Shadow-Who-Walks and talk to him:

| Approach | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Convince him to stop working for the raiders | **3000 XP, +15 karma** |
| Threaten him into running | **2500 XP, +15 karma** |

Leave after talking to him and **all the raiders leave too**, with nothing but containers left
to loot.
<Vanilla>Shadow-Who-Walks does nothing at all in the original game. Clicking him 30–50 times
produces a secret message, which is the entirety of his content.</Vanilla>
:::

## The safe

Three **merc leaders** in combat armour stand in the northwest, each in front of a locked
container holding **dog tags**. Collect all three.

At the safe:

1. **Examine it for traps.** With Traps ~50%, save and retry to disarm it for a stick of
   **dynamite**.
2. **Use the dog tag numbers** — just keep all three in your inventory — to open it: **500 XP**.
   With **LK 9** you can crack it without the combination.

Inside, among other things, is the **Account Book**. Keep it.

:::note IN under 4
You can only open the safe by repeatedly "twisting its nose" (LK 9). You cannot disarm the trap,
but you can set it off by dropping your own explosives beside it.
:::

## Breaking their strength

Leave the barracks map with **four or fewer raiders alive** and you get a message that you have
broken them, plus **2000 XP**. No more raider encounters in the region.

:::caution
Any raiders still alive when that triggers simply **die** — you get no combat XP for them, though
you can loot the bodies. Finish them all off before leaving, Shadow-Who-Walks included.
:::

## Reporting back

Wallace and Stark just point you at **Lynette**. She pays **1000 XP and $1000**. Show her the
**Account Book** for another **1000 XP and $500**.

:::danger Order matters
If you carry both the **Account Book** and **Bishop's Holodisk** when you report, you miss the
Account Book reward entirely and skip straight to
<Quest>Deliver Lynette's holodisk to Westin in NCR</Quest>. **Drop the holodisk first.**
:::

:::caution Do not haggle
As a citizen you can demand $100–$500 for the information. Do not. If your Barter skill does not
support the figure you name, Lynette **expels you from the city and revokes your citizenship**.
You can only ask once, and asking forfeits the $1000 and 1000 XP for destroying the raiders.
:::
