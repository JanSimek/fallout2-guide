# Arroyo

You now have the Pip-Boy and a village that likes you. Arroyo is small, safe, and on a timer
most players never notice until it has already expired.

## First things first

Talk to the **Elder** for your main quests, **Vic's water flask**, and **$152**.
<Vanilla>Nothing here differs mechanically, but RPU adds dialogue about Kaga — a rival tribal
who wanted to be the Chosen One and who will hunt you throughout the game.</Vanilla>
Klamath is already on your world map.

Characters with Intelligence below 4 do not get the money.

:::danger[Never attack anyone in Arroyo]
Attacking a villager — or killing one with Super Stimpaks — makes the whole village hostile,
and the game ends. You can run for the exit, but the game ends anyway. This includes anyone
you angered back in the Temple of Trials.
:::

:::caution[Stealing has consequences here]
Using Steal on the unarmed wandering villagers costs you karma **even if you take nothing** —
they have nothing to take. RPU extends this: stealing from the **Elder's chest, Hakunin's
chest, or Aunt Morlis's vase** also drops your karma.
<Vanilla>The three containers can be looted freely with no karma penalty.</Vanilla>
Negative karma stops Hakunin from healing you.
:::

## Training

Free permanent skill points. Get all of them.

| Trainer | Where | Gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lucas** | By the stone head | Unarmed to 40%, or to 55% if you are already above 40%. Needs **AG 6**. |
| **Jordan** | Tent to the northeast | +10% Melee Weapons, if your Melee is already **36%+** |
| **Cameron** | Top of the map | +10% Unarmed **and** +10% Melee — but only if **AG ≤ 6** and you tagged **none** of the six combat skills |

:::caution[Order matters]
Because of a bug, you must get **Cameron's training before talking to Hakunin at all**.
Get Lucas's bonus before Jordan's if your starting Unarmed is low.
:::

Lucas also carries a bag of healing powder. Take it and leave him some scorpion tails you
cannot carry into the desert anyway. Barter *before* training, not after.

## Hakunin

The village shaman heals you to full and cures poison for free, any time, **provided your
karma is not negative**.

Once you clear his garden he will make healing powder from a broc flower plus a xander root.
Since you may never come back, scout the hunting grounds until you have equal numbers of
each and convert the lot.

:::note
If you have offended Hakunin, keep insulting him. His reaction level eventually drops so far
that he forgets he was angry — a bug, but a useful one. The talking head stays unimpressed.
:::

## Quests

### 1. Kill the evil plants that infest Hakunin's garden
Agree to it, or just kill the plants. **100 XP**, plus two healing powders when you report
back, plus the healing powder recipe.

:::tip
The plants' ranged spike attack is much weaker than their bite, and they run out of spikes
quickly. Hit once, back off two hexes, end turn, repeat.
:::

If your karma is negative, Hakunin offers this quest as a way to make amends — but you still
need to reach 0 karma before he pays out.

### 2. Rescue Nagor's dog, Smoke, from the wilds
Nagor is east of Hakunin's garden. Smoke is in the **hunting grounds** to the west.

Kill or sneak past the hostile geckos, grab every **broc flower** and **xander root** you see,
and stay off the green slime. Smoke is at the top left; he follows you home and helps kill
geckos on the way. **100 XP** for returning him alive.

Flowers and geckos respawn daily if you leave and return.

:::caution
Tell Nagor the dog is dead, or refuse the quest, and he runs off alone. Follow him and you
will find his body next to a dead gecko — the dog gone, the quest permanently stuck on your
list. You can take his spear.
:::

:::note[Geckos are opportunists]
In combat, normal and golden geckos will run for any iguana-on-a-stick or meat jerky dropped
on the ground and eat it. They will also **pick up punching weapons** — spiked knuckles, power
fists — and use them on you.
:::

### 3. Obtain flint to have Mynoc sharpen your spear
Talk to the guard at the bridge. With **PE 6** you can ask about his spear, and he sends you
to your aunt Morlis for flint. Five ways to get it:

- Trade her **3 healing powders** in dialogue
- Barter **1 scorpion tail**
- Ask nicely — **Speech 51%** and a successful check, **50 XP**
- Steal it
- Get caught stealing **twice** and she hands it over

You can take the Speech XP even after obtaining flint another way. A second piece of flint is
useless.

Mynoc turns one of your spears into a **sharpened spear**. Drop all your spears first and he
gives you a brand new one instead.

:::caution
Once you have visited Klamath or the Den, Mynoc's dialogue changes and this quest can no
longer be started or finished.
:::

There is also **$100** in Aunt Morlis's hut. Take it.

### 4. Fix the well for Feargus
Talk to Feargus by the well, then use Repair on it. **100 XP**, and it works at any Repair
skill whatsoever.

### 5. Find Vic the Trader
Not here. See [The Den](/walkthrough/the-den).

### 6. Retrieve the GECK for Arroyo
Not for a long while. **4000 XP** the moment you lay hands on a G.E.C.K. in
[Vault 13](/walkthrough/vault-13).

## The Arroyo clock

Four dream sequences occur on fixed dates: **23 Oct 2241, 21 Jan 2242, 21 Apr 2242** and
**20 Jul 2242**. Leaving Vault 13 after finding it, or finishing the tanker in San Francisco,
jumps you straight to the fourth.

| Elapsed | What you lose |
| --- | --- |
| 90 days (first dream) | Quests 1, 3 and 4; Hakunin's healing and powder-making |
| 91 days | The water flask and cash from the Elder |
| Fourth dream | **Arroyo entirely.** The bridge is destroyed. |

After the fourth dream, returning gives you a dying Hakunin, a story about vertibirds, and
**Navarro on your world map**. Nothing else. You do not need to come back at all.

:::danger
The bridge location is replaced with a different map. A car parked there is **lost forever**,
along with any companion left on any Arroyo map.
:::

## RPU additions

Once you have been to the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa), you can bring back **experimental seeds**
and plant them in Arroyo — assuming the village is still standing.
<Vanilla>There are no plantable seeds in the unmodded game.</Vanilla>

You can also learn where **Kaga** came from, which explains the recurring ambushes.
