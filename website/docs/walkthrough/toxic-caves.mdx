# Toxic Caves

You get this location from **Ardin Buckner** in Klamath, as part of
<Quest>Rescue Smiley the Trapper</Quest>.

Visit twice: once now to rescue Smiley, and once later when your Repair skill is good enough
to open what is downstairs.

## The goo

The tunnels are laced with green ooze that does **2 plasma damage per step**. To be safe, you
**and every companion** must be carrying a pair of **rubber boots** in inventory. There is a
locker near the entrance with a pair in it; buy or find more for your party.

:::caution Boots melt
Each step carries a 1% chance the boots dissolve, leaving that character unprotected. It
happens to companions too. Worth reloading for.
:::

:::note Armour and goo
**Power Armor** protects as if you were wearing boots. **Hardened** and **Advanced** Power
Armor do not protect, but nullify the damage anyway. The boots only work against the goo *in
this location*.
:::

:::tip The two extra toes
Step in the goo deliberately, without protection, and after a while your feet start to itch.
After exactly **15 steps** you get "Your feet burn and itch for a while, but then they feel
better." A month later you grow **two extra toes**, which can be amputated in Vault City — and
which have an obscure use much later.

This is the only way in the game to get them.
:::

:::caution
If you drive here, the car sits at the edge of the screen and you cannot reach the trunk.
:::

## First visit — Smiley

Kill your way past as many geckos as necessary and raid the entrance locker. Go down the
ladder and head **northeast** — the middle tunnel has the heaviest gecko concentration.

You will find an elevator, a broken generator, and **Smiley**. The first two are beyond you for
now. Loot the **wall locker** (easy to miss — it is a container type people walk past) and talk
to Smiley.

Walk him to the cave mouth and he makes his own way home. **1000 XP.**

:::note
If you are standing near him when a fight starts, Smiley joins in without being spoken to. He
is not much help.
:::

Come back after you have the Gecko Skinning perk to clear out the remaining geckos for pelts.

## Second visit — the elevator

Return with **Repair 50–60%** (or with Vic in your party) and an **Electronic Lockpick**.

1. Repair the generator. A Tool gives no bonus here.
2. Use an **Electronic Lockpick** on the elevator door. Nothing else opens it.
3. Go down and kill the **rocketbot**.
4. Loot everything.

:::note
This is the only place in the game where you genuinely need a lockpick *item*, unless your
Lockpick skill is dire.
:::

**What is down there:**

- **Bozar**
- **Combat Armor Mark II**
- **Plasma Pistol**
- A great deal of good ammunition

That is a serious upgrade for this point in the game, which is why the second visit is worth
planning for.

:::tip Getting the first turn
Hold the **A** key while a map loads and you enter combat mode with the first turn before
anything can react. Useful against the rocketbot, and against anyone you have just insulted in
dialogue — the other party normally initiates, unless you hold **A** as the conversation ends.

You can also use it here simply to sprint past the robot to the doorway, where you are safe.
:::

:::note The Robo Rocket Launcher
An exotic collector's item. Kill the robot with a **critical head hit that does no damage** —
flares or bare fists, with Better Criticals. The bug that denies you combat XP also stops the
bot purging its restricted inventory.

Then knock a companion unconscious **on the robot's hex**, open their inventory, and click the
arrow to switch to the robot. Two purple boxes appear: one **Robo Rocket Launcher** and up to
18 **Robo Rocket** rounds.

It has a very low Strength requirement and costs half the AP of a normal rocket launcher, but
does considerably less damage. Do not use Vic, Myron or Goris as the unconscious body — they
react badly to being looted.
:::
