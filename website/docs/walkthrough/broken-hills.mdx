# Broken Hills

> *"Sometimes I feel like a magic 8-ball. I just spout random sayings and walk away."*
> — Broken Hills resident

A uranium mining town where humans, ghouls and super mutants live together — the only place in
the wasteland where that is true, and there are people working hard to end it.

**Marcus** the mutant sheriff joins your party after quests 1 and 2. **1000 XP.** He is one of
the best companions in the game.

:::caution Marcus cannot enter Vault City
Not unless you are Captain of the Guard, and bringing him in anyway turns the city hostile.
:::

:::note The containers here are empty
Almost all of them. On the western map only two hold anything: a bookcase in Marcus's house
(**rubber doll**) and the small pot outside the top-right house (**Cat's Paw**). The eastern map
has nothing but the **Fuzzy Painting** in Eric's house.
:::

## Shops

**Liz** runs the general store (restocks 2–4 days). **Bill**, **Jacob** and **Doc Holliday**
restock every 3 days.

:::tip Ask to barter, do not press the button
For Bill and Holliday this halves the price. It matters less for Liz.
:::

**Liz's basement** holds four containers of weapons including a **combat shotgun**. She will not
let you down there. To get permission: go to **Phil's**, buy something and tip him to a total of
**$20** (a bug means tips are not actually deducted), and he reveals the weapons cache. Return to
Liz, ask about the weapons, say you heard from a **reliable source**, and tell her you will use
them on the mutants.

Good Sneak gets you down there without asking — harder at night, whether or not Liz is actually
in the room.

:::danger This dialogue can hard-lock the game
Choosing **"it's all over town"** when asking about the stash, telling her **"You"** when she
asks what you plan to kill, or **failing the Speech check** after the "reliable source" line, all
try to start a fight while you are still in dialogue. You can press 1 and Esc to escape, but you
will have to **restart the game entirely** before it plays normally again. Save first.
:::

**Doc Holliday** gives free healing if you listen to his life story — repeatable, unlike the
"ask what he does" route which works only once. He also mentions **Dr. Fung** in San Francisco,
which is useful later.

:::tip The footlocker exploit
By the arrival exit grid, stand between the exit and the fence to the north, on the half-offscreen
hex, then click the far side of the fence. In the upper left corner of the map are four
footlockers holding, left to right: **nothing, Jacob's stock, Holliday's stock, Bill's stock**.
All reset every three days — even Jacob's after you jail him.

The empty box is where the game parks a shopkeeper's inventory while you trade. Put **armed
explosives** in it and go talk to that shopkeeper, and they will be carrying them when you leave
dialogue.
:::

## Things to do

### Caravans and shovelling

**Bill** hires caravan guards to Vault City (21st), NCR (11th) and Gecko (1st). These jobs are
poor — **$200** each and no return caravan, so you end up stranded. The Redding runs are better.

**Shovelling brahmin dung** pays **$100** a time, and after five sessions gives you the
**Expert Excrement Expeditor** special perk (+5% Speech) and **500 XP**.

### Typhon's treasure

Talk to **Typhon** in the east of town:

1. Give him a **Cat's Paw**, wait one hour
2. Give him a **rubber doll**, wait three hours
3. Give him a bottle of **Rot Gut**

He tells you where his treasure is: **2000 XP**.

The rest is optional and comedic. Manipulate the town well, watch the treasure fall down it, then
find **Micky** the small treasure hunter nearby. He retrieves **10,000 bottle caps**, which are
worthless. You cannot get Micky back out.

### The chess-playing radscorpion

Talk to the **Professor** in the lab coat and take his tests against an artificially intelligent
radscorpion.

- **Agility** and **Perception** tests: **500 XP each**. Low stats can match it after a few
  attempts; you can never actually beat it.
- **Intelligence** test: unwinnable, because the scorpion cheats at chess.

To win the chess match, go to the field to the north where a **talking spore plant** is trying to
get your attention. Bring a **shovel** and move it to the Old Ghouls' Home: **1000 XP**, and it
tells you how to beat the scorpion. Return and win: **500 XP**.

The scorpion then attacks. Run away once and end combat and you need not kill it — but park your
companions elsewhere or they will finish the job.

:::tip
If you spare it, steal its **spectacles**. You can steal its tail too. Get caught stealing and it
talks to you.
:::

### The hidden mine area

Enter the mine to the north. Just inside the entrance on the right is a heap of rocks — put the
movement cursor just inside the wall and you can walk through. **1000 XP** for finding it.

**Chuck Stodgers** is on the floor and gets up as you approach. **Ask who he is** for another
**500 XP** — pick the other line, or be stupid, and he runs off instead. Take the **uranium ore**
from the footlocker.

There is a second hidden cave near the top right. It is empty.

:::note Infinite XP
If Stodgers cannot leave the map, you can save, reload and talk to him again for the same reward
indefinitely. Saving stops him running, as does entering combat mode; a companion parked in the
narrow passage blocks him permanently.
:::

### The refinery

Bring the uranium ore to the white ghoul in the refinery south of the caravan office. It costs
**$1000** and pays **500 XP**. A day later he offers to buy the refined ore back for **$1500**.

| Choice | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Keep the ore | Nothing — it has no use anywhere |
| Sell for $1500 | 300 XP |
| Sell for $1000 | **1250 XP** and karma |

:::note
Random encounter merchants around Broken Hills carry uranium ore, so you can do this without ever
finding Stodgers.
:::

### Dan's wife

**Dan** is northeast of the Old Ghouls' Home. Agree to look for his wife — you can charge him —
then, after finding the bodies in quest 2, break the news: **500 XP**.

## Quests

### 1. <Quest>Fix the mine's air purifier</Quest>

**Zaius**, in the building next to the mine entrance. Agree to fetch the parts.

:::caution
Ask Zaius for money — $500 to $1000 depending on Barter, half up front — and he will **not** tell
you the password to get the parts for free.
:::

The **Mine Parts** come from **Renesco** in New Reno, which appears on your map.

Then into the mine. **Leave behind any companion who cannot absorb 30–50 damage.** You and all
organic non-canine companions (plus the Robodog) take 10–15 damage periodically from radon gas
until the purifier is fixed.

**Route:** as the tunnels branch, go **right, left, left**. Use the Mine Parts on the purifier at
the top: **1500 XP**, and the gas stops.

Return to Zaius: the second half of your money, or a **combat shotgun** if you did not ask for
payment. The shotgun is worth more.

:::tip Gas protection
**Power Armor** works. So does the **gas mask from the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa)**.
<Vanilla>The EPA does not exist, so Power Armor is the only protection.</Vanilla>

Things that do **not** work, despite what other guides claim: Dermal Impact Armor, the oxygen
tank, the Toughness perk, and running in combat mode — which actually makes you take *more*
damage.
:::

### 2. <Quest>Find the missing people for Marcus</Quest>

Ask **Marcus** what is new. Tell him you will do it for free.

In the east of town there are three ways underground: the **outhouse behind Eric's**, a **carpet
two houses east of Eric's**, and a **manhole in the field**.

Go down, deal with the ants — pick them off at range, or your companions will empty valuable
ammunition into them — and enter the room with the mutilated bodies: **500 XP**. The bodies do
not appear until the quest is active.

Take the **note** from the severed female body. Your character concludes it is better not to tell
Marcus, and indeed you cannot. **Zaius** sends you to **Francis**, who admits to the killings and
claims self-defence. **Let him go: 1000 XP** — attacking him brings the whole town down on you.

Then tell Marcus about the bodies: **$500**, or a **scoped hunting rifle** if you refused money.

:::caution Do this last
Telling Marcus jails **Jacob**, so finish quests 4 and 5 first.
:::

:::note RPU additions
Marcus has a new line for when you have found the tunnels but not yet the bodies. You can also
tell him about the **NCR scientist's mutant experiments** — even after recruiting him, as long as
you are in Broken Hills. Minor XP, and the caravan run to NCR is cancelled over fears of poisoned
goods.
<Vanilla>Neither conversation exists.</Vanilla>
:::

### 3. <Quest rpu>Determine whether Chad is skimming money from the caravan runs</Quest>

RPU's addition.
<Vanilla>Chad's embezzlement is not a quest in the original game.</Vanilla>

Get it from **Marcus**. Go to the caravan guards and find **Max** — the only man in **green**
leather armour, standing outside near the back door, and the only person in that area **not** in
black leather.

:::note
Despite what some guides say, there is **nobody in combat armour** in Broken Hills. Look for
green.
:::

Max knows Chad is guilty and says he keeps records at home. Confronting Chad now gets a flat
denial. Talk to Max further and he explains that he designed Chad's complicated lock and tells
you how to open it.

Chad's house is at the far end of the east side. Unlock the door and check the **desk** for a
**briefcase**.

:::caution
With **PE below 7 or IN 4 or lower**, Chad appears behind you while you search. After a short
conversation he turns hostile — though he is a coward, and anything better than leather armour
usually makes him run. The briefcase will still be in the drawer afterwards.
:::

Two endings:

| Choice | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Blackmail Chad | **$1000** and 750 XP |
| Tell Marcus | **1500 XP**, no money |

Blackmail gives you two attempts: a Speech check at **skill −15%**, then a second at
**skill −35%**.

### 4. <Quest>Beat Francis at armwrestling</Quest>

**Francis** the super mutant is in the bar. You need **ST 8** to challenge him and **level 6**.

To actually win, you want ST and EN around **9/9** — 8/10 works with a lot of reloading. **Two
Buffout** will get you there. There is a random element, so save first.

Win: **Power Fist and 350 XP.** Lose: a **ball gag**.

:::caution
Do this **before** confronting him with the note in quest 2.
:::

:::tip
You can use **2 Buffout on Francis** and wait 6 hours for his stats to crash, which makes 8/5
enough. The effect is smaller than dosing yourself, and reloading resets him.
:::

### 5. <Quest>Break Manson and Franc out of prison</Quest>

Talk to **Jacob** — and do not tell him you like mutants, or the quest closes.

:::tip
Talk to **Manson** through the bars *before* talking to Jacob, ask about Broken Hills and pick
the long answer: **300 XP** for "preaching open-mindedness".
:::

Go to the jail in **daytime** with **no weapon readied**, or the guards may shoot on sight. Three
approaches:

- **IN 7 and CH 7** talks the guard into handing over the key
- **Kill the guards** without anyone else noticing — mind the minigun
- **Lockpick** the door, or get the key by stealing (hard) or barter (it is worth $0)

For the lockpick route, wait until the guard walks to the far end of the room. He stops you if he
sees you, and attacks if it happens twice more than a few seconds apart. Sneak helps.

:::tip
Once the door is unlocked, walk into an adjacent room and one of your companions will usually
open it for you.
:::

**1500 XP**, and you are teleported back to Jacob.

:::caution
The **"Err… no, not really"** line makes the guard turn hostile about a minute later, which is
easy to walk into.
:::

### 6. <Quest>Blow up the mine's air purifier</Quest>

Jacob gives you this after quest 5. The job is to bring the **Explosive Switch** to the purifier.

**The good ending:** tell **Marcus** about the plot. **2500 XP**, and Jacob's band go to jail.
The same happens if you tell Marcus where the escaped convicts are.

**Going through with it:** run into the mine and use the switch on the purifier. **1500 XP**, and
you are teleported out. Jacob then lets you loot **Liz's basement**.

:::caution
Afterwards there are **no mutants left in town** except Elmo the drunk, Bill closes his store and
stops offering jobs, and Broken Hills gets its worst ending.
:::

### 7. <Quest>Divert more electrical power to Eric's home</Quest>

**Eric** is in the east. Agree to get him more power for his air conditioning.

At the power station by the town entrance:

- Talk **Brian** into it with Speech — **one attempt only**
- Or use **Science** on the computer on the left

**1000 XP**, or **1500 XP** for a critical success (optimizing the whole grid). A critical failure
shocks you for 10–15 damage.

Eric's reward: **$150**, a dose of **Buffout** (not offered if you already beat Francis), or extra
karma plus the location of **NCR**.

:::tip
Take the karma. You can steal far more Buffout and Mentats — and the $150 — from Eric directly.
:::

## Dogmeat's armour

If you installed the **NPC Armor** option, talk to **Liz** in the hardware store and ask whether
she has anything special off the shelves. For **$2000** — and only with Dogmeat in your party —
she fits him with **kevlar armour**. Talk to Dogmeat to put it on or take it off.

Fully levelled and armoured, Dogmeat has roughly the protection of combat armor with somewhat
better laser resistance.
<Vanilla>There is no dog armour in the original game.</Vanilla>
