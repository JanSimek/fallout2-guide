# San Francisco

> *"What sort of stupid questions?"* / *"Oh, like: 'Why can't I use a sword or a chainsaw on my
> monitor?'"* — The Chosen One and Dave Handy

The last major stop, and the most tangled after New Reno. Three factions — the **Shi**, the
**Hubologists** and the **Brotherhood of Steel** — plus a tanker you need to make seaworthy.

**The Survey Map from the Military Base** is the only thing that puts this on your world map, short
of walking here.

:::danger[Point of no return]
Once you sail, you cannot return to the mainland before the game ends. Decide what companions,
weapons and ammunition you are bringing.
:::

## Shops

Two of the best in the game, each with two tables of different stock:

- **Flying Dragon 8** — general goods, some weapons and armour. **Lao Chou** gives surprisingly
  precise directions to the Tanker FOB and NavCom Parts once you have those quests.
- **Red 888 Guns** — guns, armour, ammunition. **Take the Gauss Rifle immediately.**

Both eventually stock **Power Armor**, and this is the only place to find **Tesla Armor**.

:::tip[How many Power Armors to buy]
Count your companions who can wear armour (Sulik, Vic, Cassidy, Lenny, Myron, Miria/Davin), add
yourself, subtract **three** (the free suits on the mainland), and add one if you intend to finish
Dr. Jing's quest.
:::

:::note
Every container in every San Francisco map is **empty**, with three exceptions: the two lockers in
the Brotherhood building, and the lockers, footlockers and one bookcase in the Hubologist base.
:::

## Dr. Fung

Heals HP and crippled limbs for $100–$600, sells stimpaks, and performs implant surgery.

:::note[Implants]
Dermal armour costs **$5,500**, its enhancement **$33,000**; phoenix armour **$8,500** and **$49,000**.
The enhancement correctly replaces the perk it supersedes. Each operation consumes a suit of combat
armour and costs you days of game time.
<Vanilla>The enhancement never removed the basic perk, so it did nothing at all, and Fung would
offer the basic implant again to someone who already had the enhancement.</Vanilla>
:::

## Martial arts training

**The Dragon** trains characters with **karma 250 or higher**: **+5% Unarmed per day**, up to four
times or until you reach 100%.

**Lo Pan** across the street gives identical training to characters with **karma −250 or lower**.

:::caution[The order matters]
1. Get **all** the training first — you cannot get it after taking the test of skills.
2. Take **both** tests of skills for the XP.
3. Only then challenge either man to mortal combat.

Ask Lo Pan for training when you are not evil enough and he stops talking to you. Say evil things to
the Dragon and the same happens.
:::

:::tip
Power Armor raises Strength and therefore Unarmed — take it off if the training is being wasted. You
can also use the Buffout comedown to temporarily lower ST and AG if your Unarmed is hovering just
over 99%.
:::

:::danger[Ring fight bugs]
Inherited from Per Jorner, and not re-checked against the ring scripts.<Unverified>Every entry in this
block wants confirming at the keyboard.</Unverified>

- **End combat after killing your opponent but before the "You win" sign and you are trapped in the
  ring permanently.** Reload immediately. Always use **end turn**, never end combat.
- Attacking "with armour and weapons" means fighting the whole town.
- Lose a test and you must fight all six opponents again from the start, including the ones you
  already killed.
- The extra round after "You win" exists so you can **save**. Using it to kill the opponent is what
  causes most of the bugs.
:::

## The Brotherhood bunker

Accessible once you complete <Quest>Get the vertibird plans for the Brotherhood of Steel</Quest>.

**Loot:** two rare **pulse weapons** and the one-of-a-kind **Brotherhood Armor**.

**ACE**, the medical computer, raises **ST, PE, IN and CH by one each** if you have the corresponding
memory modules — **red, green, yellow and blue**. Each takes a few weeks, so check the shops
between operations. ACE also heals HP, poison, radiation and crippled limbs for free.

:::tip[Retroactive skill points]
Skill points are awarded based on your Intelligence **the first time you open the character screen
after levelling**. Do not open it for a level or two (never more than three, or you lose a perk),
raise your Intelligence, and then open it — you get points at the new rate.

This works with ACE and with the Hubologist scan, but not with the Gain Intelligence perk.
:::

## The Steel Palace

Three types of usable terminal: **chemistry workstations** (the room on the left, and three along
the wall on the right), **physics workstations** (inside the railing on the right), and **biology
workstations** (past the throne room). Only the chemistry terminals work at first; the others must
be activated from the mainframe.

**The Shi mainframe** is past the throne room — use **Repair** on the forcefield emitter first.

| Science | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Good | Hack it for **3000 XP**, but the guards notice and **every Shi in the city turns hostile** |
| **115%+** | Hack it unnoticed for **7000 XP** |

:::caution
Failing a hacking attempt turns everyone hostile too. Get the password from **Ken Lee** if your
Science is not up to it — though using the password gives no XP.
:::

:::note[RPU changes the terminals]
Hacking a Steel Palace terminal now behaves as originally planned:

- **Science over 110%** — a chance to hack without anyone noticing.
- **Around 100%** — you still have a chance, but the Shi notice someone has been tampering (their
  floating text changes; nothing else happens).
- **Fail both checks** — that terminal is locked down. It can still be hacked again but **can no
  longer be accessed with a password**, so you must find another one.

For a less scientific approach, **three torn papers** combine into the three console passwords. They
are in the **small western kitchen area**, the **small bathroom behind it** (just right of the
toilet), and the **upper corner of the sleeping area**.
<Vanilla>There is one flat Science check and a single set of passwords.</Vanilla>
:::

:::note[There is only one tank of fuel]
Whichever way you divert it — mainframe, chemistry workstation, the Elron computer or Badger — the
fuel goes to the tanker *or* the shuttle, never both. Diverting it to the tanker takes it back off
the Hubologists.
<Vanilla>Only the mainframe checked. The workstations would happily fuel both.</Vanilla>
:::

## The Hubologists

**Getting inside** — any of:

- Tell the AHS-7 you have a letter for the AHS-9 (with the NCR quest)
- Tell them you need to see **Crockett**, having heard of him from Lao Chou or Dr. Jing
- Say you are thinking of joining
- Use **Repair** on the forcefield emitters — nobody minds

**Joining:** find **Vikki and Juan** to the northeast and sit through the sales pitch (or set it off
and go loot the footlockers while they talk). Agree to join, then see the **AHS-7** by the stairs.

:::danger[The zeta scan is permanent]
If you did not take the scan in NCR you must take one here to join. The result is either **+1 IN and
+1 LK**, or **−1 LK**. Save first.
:::

:::caution
Becoming a Hubologist means **Dr. Fung, Lo Pan and the Dragon will not talk to you**. Do everything
involving them first. The Shi, oddly, do not care.
:::

**Crockett** in the mainframe room upgrades **two suits of Power Armor into Hardened Power Armor** at
**$10,000 each** — you supply the Power Armor.

:::tip[Unlimited hardened suits]
With <Quest>Get some hardened power armor from Crockett</Quest> active, tell Crockett that Dr. Jing
will give him rocket fuel for the armour and he upgrades for **free** — and never complains about
running out of chemicals.

After each upgrade the dialogue option disappears; return to Jing to "reactivate" the quest and go
back for another. Repeat as needed.
:::

**Dave Handy** outside is worth a conversation. Ask where he is from and why he came here, and you
learn he has a crush on **Vikki Goldmann** downstairs. Mention it to Vikki, then return to Dave.

:::caution
Tell Dave **"she made fun of him"** and you get a one-shot chance to have him do quest-related work
for you. Tell him **"she thinks he's hot"** and you can come back later, but may lose XP.
:::

:::note
Asking Dave to format the Hubologist hard drive (Speech check) gives **+25 karma** and no XP. Tell
him Vikki likes him and you can have him do it **repeatedly**, collecting the karma each time — by
far the fastest way to raise karma in the game.

It does mean the AHS-9 will not give you <Quest>Kill Badger so the tanker vagrants will embrace the
Hub</Quest>. The AHS-7 still will.
:::

:::caution
The forcefields in both bases can be taken down with **Repair or dynamite** without alerting anyone.
You will need to do this to get out once you start killing people. **Bring a Tool** so you can
disable them in combat mode.
:::

## The tanker

Northwest from downtown to the docks, then north.

The **captain** upstairs will not talk to you until you finish either steal-the-plans quest or the
Brotherhood quest. Then he gives you the **NavCom, FOB and fuel** quests — all three must be done
before the tanker sails. The nearby computer tracks progress and is also what you use to depart.

:::note
One of the black-haired female vagrants carries a rare **XL70E3**; another has a **Gauss Rifle**, as
does Marc. Several punks carry **H&K G11s** you can steal.
:::

## Quests

### 1. The navigation computer needs the NavComp part to work
With the **NavCom Parts** from Vault 13 and the **Tanker FOB** from Navarro, take the ladder from the
locked room in the hold and use the parts on the navigation computer: **2000 XP**.

### 2. You need to use a FOB to access the navigation computer
Use the **Tanker FOB** from Navarro on the mechanism beside the hold door: **2000 XP**.

### 3. The tanker needs fuel
Several sources, **5000 XP** unless noted:

| Source | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Dr. Jing**, after quest 12 | Ask for tanker fuel as your payment |
| **Badger**, after quest 8 | He hacks it for you |
| A **Shi chemistry workstation** | The only way to get Shi fuel after it has already been moved |
| The **Shi mainframe** | **+3000 XP** if you had not yet got Ken Lee's permission |
| **Dave Handy** | 5000 XP if you told him Vikki mocked him and he takes it from the Shi; 3000 XP if from the Hubologists; **no XP** if you told him she likes him |

:::tip[Maximum fuel experience]
Give the plans to the **Hubologists**. Route fuel from the **Shi mainframe** to the Hubologists
(9000 XP). Have **Badger** fetch it from the Hubologists for the tanker. Have **Dave Handy** move
fuel to the tanker (3000 XP) and get a copy of the plans. Use a Shi **workstation** to move fuel to
the Hubologists again (6000 XP), wait, and return to Badger (6000 XP).

That is **24,000 XP** from a system that should give you 12,000, and it relies on three separate
bugs. Playing it straight gets you 9000 from the mainframe plus 3000 from Handy.<Unverified>The
repeatable half is inherited and was not traced; the fuel double-dip it used to rely on is now
closed, so the totals may no longer hold.</Unverified>
:::

:::note[RPU: use the Hubologist mainframe yourself]
If you already fuelled the Hubologist spaceship, you can **redirect it to the tanker yourself** from
the Hubologist mainframe: **5000 XP**. Very useful if Dave Handy or Badger are dead or
uncooperative.

You can also **reformat the entire Hubologist system** for **100 XP** — you need **Science 100%** to
hack it first, and they all turn hostile, so do it last.
<Vanilla>Neither option exists at the mainframe.</Vanilla>
:::

### 4. Find Badger's girlfriend in the hold below the ship
Ask **Badger** to hack a computer for you and he asks for this first.

Enter the hold by the manhole in his room or the stairs at the back, kill the monsters and aliens,
and talk to **Suze** in the northeast corner. Lead her to either staircase and she vanishes; follow
her up and talk to Badger: **5000 XP**.

:::caution
If Suze dies you get no XP, but Badger still hacks for you after three hours of mourning. Either
way he performs **only one** hacking job — three days if Suze is dead, one if she lives — and then
stops talking to you.
:::

:::note
Let Badger hack the **Shi** computer and, 1–3 days later, you will witness a cutscene of the Shi
executing him. Convenient if you have the quest to kill him.
:::

### 5. The Shi need plans for a vertibird from Navarro
Ask **Ken Lee** for fuel or to see the Emperor and he sends you for the plans. Navarro appears on
your map. **5000 XP** for delivering them to Dr. Wong.

### 6. The Hubologists need plans for a vertibird from Navarro
**Harry** the rocket supervisor gives you this once you join. **5000 XP.**

### 7. Steal the vertibird plans from the Shi / Steal the vertibird plans from the Hubologists
Mutually exclusive — you must have given the plans to one faction first.

**From the Hubologists:** ask **Dave Handy** to copy them, or have **Badger** hack their computer.
**5000 XP** to Dr. Wong.

**From the Shi:** **Badger** can do it in a day or three — and gets executed by the Shi for it.
**5000 XP**.

:::note
Once a faction has a set of plans they cannot lose it. You are only ever copying them for the other
side.
:::

### 8. The Hubologists need fuel for their spaceship
Harry's second job. Dave Handy and Badger **cannot** get this fuel — you must deal with **Dr. Jing**
(quest 12) or hack the Shi system yourself.

**6000 XP**, plus **3000 more** from the Shi mainframe.

### 9. Kill Badger so the tanker vagrants will embrace the Hub
The AHS-7's first task on joining. Kill Badger — Super Stimpaks, or a Bozar to the eyes — and return:
**3000 XP**.

### 10. Kill the Shi Emperor
From the AHS-7 or AHS-9 after killing Badger, or by telling the AHS-9 you have come to kill him for
the Shi.

The Emperor is a **computer** behind the throne. Two ways:

- **Format the hard drive** — every Shi in the city, including the shopkeepers, turns hostile
- **Kill Ken Lee** with Super Stimpaks — nobody notices

**5000 XP.** The AHS-9 then tells you to leave.

### 11. Kill the AHS-9
From **Ken Lee** after giving the Shi the plans, or by telling him the Hubologists sent you to kill
the Emperor. **5000 XP**, plus the password to the Shi computer.

:::danger[Mutually exclusive]
You cannot be rewarded for **both** assassination quests. Take quest 10 first and you cannot talk to
Ken Lee afterwards; collect the reward for killing AHS-9 and you cannot close quest 10 with AHS-7.

If you accept one after already having the other, the target **knows** and attacks on sight.
:::

### 12. Get some hardened power armor from Crockett
Requires quest 3 or 8 active and **no fuel moved yet**.

**Dr. Jing** is the scowling man in the southern room of the Steel Palace. Tell him you need fuel and
he asks for **Hardened Power Armor** from Crockett.

Your reward is your choice of **tanker fuel, Hubologist fuel, or $20,000**. **5000 XP** either way.

:::tip
If you trade the armour for money or Hubologist fuel, **Jing keeps it in his inventory** and you can
steal it back. Trade it for tanker fuel and it disappears.
:::

### 13. The Dragon wants you to take out Lo Pan — hand to hand
Ask the Dragon who he is and whether he would take you on (**Unarmed 125%**), or simply challenge him
to a **test of skills** (no requirement at all).

Fight six of his men one at a time, then he gives you the quest: **8000 XP**.

Kill Lo Pan (**2000 XP** if you did not take his test) and return: **3000 XP**.

:::note
You are fully healed before each fight and after passing the test — but not after failing.

The Dragon says it must be honourable combat, but **Super Stimpaks pay the same**.

**Lo Pan pulls a .223 pistol** when down to a third of his HP. You cannot loot his body.
:::

### 14. Lo Pan wants you to take out the Dragon — hand to hand, if possible
The mirror image. **8000 XP** for the test, **3000 XP** for the kill (no XP for the Dragon himself).

:::tip[Get both]
Challenge one fighter *before* taking that faction's quest, then report to the other. You collect
both rewards minus one test.
:::

### 15. Get Chip's spleen
**Chip** is the bald man outside Badger's room on the tanker.

1. Offer to get his spleen
2. Ask the **Flying Dragon 8** shopkeeper about it — **Dr. Wong** has it
3. Go to Wong and, **on your one attempt**, either make the *Silence of the Lambs* reference or tell
   him it is a **human** spleen. Simply saying Chip wants it back does not work. **2000 XP.**
4. **Dr. Fung** performs the operation — free if you have met Doc Holliday in Broken Hills, otherwise
   **$1000**
5. Tell Chip, wait a day: **3000 XP**

:::danger[Five day limit]
Chip dies of his spleenless condition **five days** after you agree to help.
:::

:::caution
Refuse to help put it back, refuse again when he pleads, then finally accept, and Chip stays angry —
the Dr. Fung dialogue option never appears and the quest is dead.
:::

### 16. Get the vertibird plans for the Brotherhood of Steel
**Matthew** outside the Brotherhood building, unlike his colleagues in the Den and NCR, will hold a
conversation. He puts **Navarro** on your map.

**20,000 XP** — the largest single reward in the game — plus access to the Brotherhood bunker. And he
lets you **keep the original plans** for the other quests.

:::note
Two days later Matthew disappears, even if he is standing in front of you. The bunker computer shows
you exactly why.

Delivering the plans also opens the **Brotherhood bunkers in the Den and NCR**, both of which contain
Brotherhood armor and good weapons.
<Vanilla>Those two bunkers stay sealed forever.</Vanilla>
:::

## The Shi submarine

:::note[An RPU addition]
A whole new step before the tanker can sail. This does not exist in the unmodded game.
:::

The wrecked submarine the Shi arrived in has **reactivated its automatic defences**, and will fire on
any ship in the area. You must disable it before you sail.

If you have not heard about it, the Poseidon Network computer warns you when you choose "go" and
refuses to continue.

:::note
None of this applies to **dumb characters** — Matthew presumably handles it along with the fuel.
:::

### Getting to the sub

You need the **small boat** moored opposite the tanker. It is guarded. Three ways past, besides
killing him:

**The Emperor.** Log on and check the submarine's history. Find out about the maintenance crew, add
yourself to the roster, and **download the passwords to your Pip-Boy**: **1000 XP**. The path is
*Database → History → Submarine status → Stationary Defense structures*.

**Lao Chou** in the Flying Dragon 8. He deals in information but you must know what to ask. Start
with your villagers' fate — from Hakunin, the Emperor, the Captain, or the Poseidon Network computer.
To get him onto the submarine you need one of:

- **Speech 91%**
- Sex, as a female with **CH 7** or the Sex Appeal trait
- **$2000**, or $1500 with a Barter roll (65% Barter ≈ 50% chance)
- Offer him time with **Miria**, if you are married

The **password** then costs **$10,000**, or **$5000** with a successful Barter roll (75% Barter ≈ 50%
chance). There is no way around paying.

**The boat guard.** With **Speech 96%** you can convince him to pretend you gave him the password:
**1000 XP**.

:::tip[Get it from one source only]
Every source checks whether you already have the password before granting it, so visiting the others
afterwards gains you nothing — and Lao Chou charges **$5,000–$10,000** for it either way. The boat
guard is free if your Speech is above **95%**.
:::

:::note
Beside the boat is **Meng**, a depressed dockworker. Talk him into moving on with his life
(**Speech 81%**) for **500 XP**. He knows a little about the submarine but cannot get you there.
:::

### Aboard

- **With a password** you go as a technician, alongside the maintenance crew.
- **Talked past the guard** and you go alone; the sub is empty.
- **Kill the guard and steal the boat** and your companions come with you — **the only time in the
  game they do**. Myron or Vic are useful here.

**500 XP** for reaching the sub.

Alone or with companions, the hatch is locked. Pick it (**Lockpick ~50%**, retry until it opens; an
electronic lockpick helps) or blow it off.

### Disabling it

Several methods, **one per game** — there is no XP for the individual method, so it makes no
difference which. Bring **tools, a crowbar or explosives**.

**First room.** The old power console in the upper left is in poor shape. Smash it with a **crowbar**,
or use **Repair** (100% ≈ 50% chance). If you came with the crew, a technician blocks the way — kill
him or find another route.

**Second room.** The old torpedo target control console in the lower right can be disabled with
**Science 101%**. With the crew present, a technician here can be tricked into doing it for you with
**Speech 81% or Science 81%** — one attempt with each.

**Torpedo room.**

- Disable the **torpedo propulsion system**: Repair roll (100% ≈ 50%). Or use an explosive on it —
  **do not arm it first**, and it only works from your backpack (easy Science roll).
- Disable the **torpedo arming and loading console**: Repair roll (100% ≈ 50%).

If a technician is in here he talks to you the moment you enter. Pass a **Speech or Repair** check
depending on your response — 70% in either gives roughly a 50% chance. Fail and he blocks you, and
turns hostile if you keep trying. If he accepts you and you want to plant explosives on a torpedo,
you need a **Traps** roll — **sneaking substantially improves your odds**.

Leave by the ladder on the side of the sub. Back on the docks: **1000 XP**.

:::tip
A locker in the torpedo room holds a **Command Chart** you can use on the Emperor. Go to the Chinese
Intelligence Network for some personal logs — flavour only, no XP.
:::

## Dr. Sheng

:::note[An RPU questline]
Four new quests, and the explanation for the sentient plant ending.
:::

### 17. Investigate the whereabouts of Dr. Sheng
**Dr. Sheng** studied sentient plants, went strange, and left on sabbatical.

Log onto a **biology station terminal** in the northern part of the Steel Palace and read the recent
personnel notes. Then ask **Dr. Wong** about Sheng and he marks Sheng's last known location on your
Pip-Boy.

At the abandoned building, strange humanoid creatures take you to their master. He asks you to clear
his laboratory of aggressive plant-creatures: **200 XP and $1000**, plus the location of the
**[EPA](/walkthrough/epa)** if you do not have it, plus quest 18.

:::caution
His creatures are tough. Refuse to do his quests — or to meet him at all — and they attack.

And once you have done all his quests you have to fight him anyway. His last two quests cost you
**karma**.
:::

### 18. Bring Dr. Sheng some plant seeds
He wants the **experimental seeds** from the [EPA Green Level](/walkthrough/epa) — use the terminal
to take them from the seed bank. **1000 XP.**

### 19. Deliver the plant seed sample to Dr. Wong
Sheng's "gift" for the Shi.

:::tip
Ask **"why the change of heart"** — that is what lets you warn Dr. Wong about the seed later.
:::

**200 XP** plus a **super stimpak** if you ask Wong for a reward. Warn him about the seed and you get
the same XP but no stimpak.

### 20. Eliminate Dr. Wong without alerting the Shi
Sheng wants Wong dead quietly. Ask for something up front and he gives you **poison**.

**1000 XP** for the assassination. He promises a triple reward and **does not honour it** — instead he
sends his creatures after you.

### 21. Eliminate Dr. Sheng
Tell **Dr. Wong** about Sheng and he offers a reward for stopping him: a **CAWS, sniper rifle and
minigun**, or **ammunition and medical supplies**, or **$5000**. **1000 XP** on your return.

## Other RPU content

### The missing biology corpse

Read about the **Xeno program** at a Shi biology workstation and you learn a body is missing, with the
Hubologists suspected.

The corpse is in an **icebox in the far southeast of the Hubologist map**. Return it to Dr. Wong:
**800 XP**, or **$500** in cash.
<Vanilla>The Xeno program is not mentioned and there is no corpse.</Vanilla>

### The Hubologist stash

Search the files of the **Hubologist mainframe** and this location is added to your map. You need
**Science over 80%** to have any chance — retryable — but realistically **100%**, which is what the
finance records need.
<Vanilla>The stash does not exist.</Vanilla>

A guard quizzes you on arrival. Three possible questions:

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who was the founder? | **Dick Hubbel** |
| The church believes everything is symbolised by? | **A turning wheel** |
| What is meant by Enlightenment? | A state achieved when the seeker has gained so much knowledge he becomes an integral part of the universe and leaves this earthly existence behind |

Then he asks why you are here: a hard **Speech** roll (100% ≈ 50% chance), then an easier one (80% ≈
50%). Pass and he lets you in; fail and he attacks.

Kill him instead and you can blow the door with dynamite — everyone inside will be hostile.

**On the third floor** a guard can be sent away by claiming you are taking his shift (Speech roll,
70–80%). Talk to the robed man on floor one first so you have a name to use.

The three **safes** on the third floor need **Lockpick over 100%**; an electronic lockpick lowers that
somewhat. They are explosion-proof.
