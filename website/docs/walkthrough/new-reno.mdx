# New Reno

> *"Watch out! Pink deathclaw, three o'clock!"* — New Reno junkie

The largest and most tangled location in Fallout 2. Four families run the city — **Mordino**,
**Salvatore**, **Bishop** and **Wright** — and only the Wrights are not scum.

:::caution[Toe the line]
Turn down a job, speak disrespectfully, ask too many questions, or come back to a boss before
finishing his job, and he will either stop giving you work or simply attack. Save often, and
pick the polite dialogue option.
:::

## Being a Made Man

Each family has a quest chain ending in **Made Man** status, which gets you:

- A popularity boost with the citizenry
- Free services at the Cat's Paw
- Access to **Eldridge's special stock** at the New Reno Arms — a **Bozar** and a **Super
  Sledge**, among other things
- A bullet in the head if you visit any other family

**You can do every quest for every family.** The trick is to complete each family's final quest
but **not return to the boss for the reward**, which is the moment you become a Made Man.

Two rules if you want everything:

1. **Do not become a Made Man of the Salvatores** until you have Mordino's quest to kill
   Salvatore.
2. If you are playing good, become a Made Man of the **Wrights last** so you can leave them
   alive.

:::note[Assassinating bosses cleanly]
Each boss has a way to be killed without being blamed — detailed under each family below.
Killing a boss silently (Super Stimpaks) or by his special method does **not** cost you Made Man
status. Attacking anyone in a family openly does.
:::

## Around town

### Virgin Street

**Jules** sells drugs and information ($25–$100 per family, depending on Barter — Father Tully
is cheaper). Get him to name the families at minimum. Follow the **jet** thread and the
**Stables and Golgotha** appear on your map; ask about **Vault 13** for Vault City, about
**Redding** for Redding.

**The Cat's Paw.** Sex here sometimes grants a temporary +1 stat for an hour:

| Service | Stat |
| --- | --- |
| Oral attention | Strength |
| Basic, dirty sex (CH under 6) | Agility |
| Full body workout (CH 6+) | Agility |
| Just talk | Intelligence |
| "Special… dramatic… request" (IN 6+) | Strength |

:::tip
The **"talk"** option counts as sex and, the first time, gives a **permanent +3% Speech** (+2% if
tagged). Take it.

**Miss Kitty's date** needs **CH 9**. In RPU the date gives **+1 Agility** permanently — though
it does not happen until you return from the Oil Rig.
<Vanilla>The date has no reward at all.</Vanilla>
:::

**Big Jesus Mordino** holds court on the first floor of the Desperado.

:::note[Killing Big Jesus]
His health is not what it should be. Use **Jet, Psycho, Buffout or — cheapest — a Nuka-Cola** on
him and he has a heart attack: **1000 XP**, and you walk out. You do **not** get this XP for
killing him in combat, so if you are turning on the Mordinos, do this first.

Bring Myron to Big Jesus and Big Jesus attacks.
:::

**Little Jesus** at the bar introduces you to Big Jesus if you have **ST 6** or wear combat or
power armour. Talk to him holding a **knife, combat knife, ripper or throwing knife** and he
teaches **+5% Melee Weapons for $200**. Female characters with **CH 6** or Porn Star status get
**+10%** instead, by other means.

### Second Street

**Jagged Jimmy J** is the main jet dealer. Listen to his scar story; with **CH 6** he gives you
**2 free jet**. Ask where he gets his jet and the **Stables** appear on your map.

**Three-Card Monte** is an RPU addition, near the big sign between Virgin and Second Street.
<Vanilla>Monte does not exist.</Vanilla>

He gambles — and cheats. He also sells information on Richard Wright's overdose, Pretty Boy
Lloyd, your missing car, Eldridge, and the location of Golgotha, at **$100–$200** for Eldridge
and **$250–$1000** for quest information (the lower you offer, the harder the Speech check).

**Beat him at his own game:** steal his deck and replace it with a **marked deck** — or simply
buy his deck and sell him your marked one (**100 XP**). Marked cards are on a shelf upstairs at
the Desperado, or dug up from one of the northern graves at Golgotha.

After **11 wins** you are $200 richer and he cannot pay his debt, so he gives you information
free — **once**, and only for quests that are currently active.

:::tip
Money you spend buying information goes into his gambling pot. Buy everything, then win it back.
:::

:::caution
Once you have cleaned him out he will not gamble with you again, even if you buy more
information.
:::

**The Barking Man** is another RPU addition, at the very back of the building northwest of Jules.
With **Science 75%** you can determine he has epilepsy. See the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa) for how
to help him.
<Vanilla>He does not exist.</Vanilla>

### The Jungle Gym — boxing

Talk to **Stuart Little**. Four opponents of increasing difficulty:

| Opponent | Purse | XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Joqq | $50 | 500 |
| Pete McKneely | $50 | 750 |
| Evan Holyfeld | $400 | 1000 |
| The Masticator | $1000 | 2500 |

Those figures assume a 50% cut; **Speech 76% and Barter 76%** gets you 75%.

Win four matches for the **Prizefighter** reputation: +5% Unarmed, popularity in New Reno, and
the right to empty the gym lockers.

:::tip[Boxing is easier than it looks]
On normal difficulty, 65% Unarmed with ordinary gloves is enough to kill the first two in round
one and knock out the other two. If you are doing no damage, use **aimed blows to the head**.
Ask Little for time to rest so you can save before each match. You regain HP equal to your
Endurance at the end of every full round.
:::

:::note[The permanent resistance bonus]
At the **start of every match**, if you have gloves, you gain a **permanent** +3 Damage Threshold
against normal damage and a bonus to Damage Resistance — +1% across the board with normal gloves,
or **+2% normal and +5% laser** with the **Plated Boxing Gloves** from the Shark Club basement.
There is a −1 DT penalty for plasma and explosive.

You keep this even if you lose the match. So: lose the fourth match deliberately, ask Little for
one more shot, and win the fifth (the Masticator's replacement is much weaker) — a fifth
toughness bonus for $500. Better value than any combat implant.
:::

:::danger[The Masticator can cost you an ear]
At around **115 HP or fewer**, he may tear your ear off — you get a **Player's Ear** item and
**permanently lose 1 Charisma**. Reload.

Fight on with **Unarmed ~160%+** and you may take *his* ear, worth $750 and nothing else.
:::

:::caution
- **No kicking, no bare fists.** Switching to kicks or removing your gloves disqualifies you.
- **Do not kill the Masticator in the Shark Club basement** if you plan to box — Stuart Little
  disappears.
- Lose a fight and you must ask for a rematch **immediately** or you get no further chances.
- If you lose by knockout you may lose the next fight before it starts — a queued lost turn
  carries over. Enter combat mode once between fights to clear it.
:::

### The Golden Globes

Work as a "fluffer" for $5 and get poisoned. Or, with **CH 9** or New Reno popularity (Made Man
or Prizefighter), audition for porn stardom — you need **EN 8, AG 8** and "experience".

Pass and you can shoot a film monthly for **$500** (with IN 8, CH 8 or Speech 75%) or **$200**,
plus **1500 XP** the first time and the **Porn Star** reputation.

:::caution
After the first film you cannot shoot again while addicted to jet, and insisting means the
Corsican Brother never speaks to you again.
:::

### The Shark Club

Bishop's casino. **Pick up the Plated Boxing Gloves** in the basement.

:::tip[The stand-up comedian]
One of the best things in the game. There are **eight** routines, a special one if you bring
**Myron**, and another after you beat the Enclave.
:::

RPU adds paid entertainment work:
<Vanilla>Neither job exists.</Vanilla>

| Job | Pay | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Male comedian** | $300 each time, 150 XP first | **CH 8**, Speech 76%, or Smooth Talker. A bad joke pays nothing. |
| **Female dancer** | $250–350 weekly, 150 XP first | **CH 7 and AG 7**, or Porn Star |

Black leather and the ball gag both have a small effect on dancing.

**Old Man McGee** stands at a slot machine in the Shark Club — also RPU. Hit the jackpot on his
machine and he demands the winnings or attacks.
<Vanilla>McGee does not exist.</Vanilla>

Without **CH 9**, the Good Natured trait, or **Speech 35%+**, you have no option but to fight.
Offer the money first, then choose: give it all (**+10 karma**) or talk him down to half
(**+5 karma**). He always accepts **$500**, or **$250** with a successful Barter check. Fail any
check and he attacks.

You can also **repair and clean his slot machine** for **50 XP**, or **100 XP** on a critical
success. One attempt only, but it still works after you have won.

### The Bishop residence

Tell the guards by the stairs you have **Moore's briefcase** — and have it on you — or they will
not let you up. Once granted, the stairs stay open.

**The Magic 8-Ball** is in a pool table nearby. With **LK 8+** it gives you a set of clues:

- Listen to what Curtis says in Vault City to get the wrench
- Set Bess free in Modoc after selling her
- Get the Hintbook from Father Tully after the game
- High ST and EN beat Francis at armwrestling
- The Easter Egg exists
- The "Trash" cross in Golgotha
- The secret password to a computer in the Vault City vault
- The Desperado toilet with the grenades

**Mrs. Bishop** will sleep with characters of either sex who have **ST 6 or CH 6**. Afterwards,
keep "talking" to her until she mumbles her **safe combination**, then lockpick it.

:::tip[The Pip-Boy Lingual Enhancer]
With **IN 9**: first ask her *downstairs* about the G.E.C.K. or Vault 13 — you must not have
found them yet — and get her to mention Vault City. Then, after sleeping with her, ask if she is
from Vault City. Eventually she gives you the **Pip-Boy Lingual Enhancer** (250 XP), which grants
a **permanent +10% Speech and 500 XP** when used.
:::

Other pillow talk:

- The **combination to Mr. Bishop's safe**, if you have tried it and failed (you still have to
  disarm the trap)
- After Mr. Bishop is dead, tell her to move on (**IN 7**): **1000 XP**
- **NCR and Vault City** on your world map

:::danger[Angela Bishop]
Male characters can sleep with her — but follow her upstairs and then turn her down and she
**cries rape**, bringing the guards. Remove power armour before talking to her.
:::

:::caution[The disappearing party bug]
Save and reload on the top floor after going upstairs with either Bishop woman and **your whole
party vanishes** when you return to the first floor. Fix it by sleeping with one of them again,
or simply dismiss everyone before you go up. The car trunk may also become inaccessible until you
drive to another map.
:::

**Mr. Bishop's safe** is trapped. Use Traps on it before picking, or set the trap off with
dynamite. Either way a guard runs in to investigate, so retreat to the far corner of the screen.
Inside: the **Raiders Map** and **Bishop's Holodisk** for Lynette.

:::note[Assassinating Mr. Bishop]
Sleep with Mrs. Bishop. In bed, ask about her husband, whether they love each other, then his
"other pursuits" — which opens the option to ask if she wants him dead. With **Traps 76%** you
can suggest an accident, and she tells you about the safe trap.

Use **Repair 76%** on the safe to change the combination, go downstairs and wait 10 minutes. No
XP, and it sometimes only does 1 damage to him.

If you already disarmed the trap, the game asks you to replace it — and **attempting to do so
crashes the game**. Go to the basement and back to re-arm it instead.
:::

### Salvatore's Bar

:::note[Assassinating Boss Salvatore]
Steal his **Oxygen Tank**. For the full **2000 XP** assassination, three conditions must hold:
Salvatore must not have the tank, **you must be carrying the Poison Tank** from the Desperado
basement (this is a scripting error, but it is required), and Salvatore must not be hostile.

A less subtle kill, or one without the Poison Tank, is worth **500 XP**. Mason turns hostile but
you can leave while Salvatore expires.
:::

### The commercial district

**Renesco the Rocketman** sells goods (restocks 2–5 days) and holds the **Mine Parts** for Broken
Hills. Give the password from Zaius, or pay **$100** (**$200** if you try to haggle).

:::tip[The Pip-Boy Medical Enhancer]
One of the most obscure items in the game. Give Renesco the **spectacles** from the Broken Hills
radscorpion and ask for a **favour** — **not a discount**, which cancels it. Then talk to him and
ask how the glasses are working out. **Do this 20 times.** Exasperated, he hands over the
Enhancer: **500 XP and a permanent +10% Doctor**.
:::

**Eldridge at the New Reno Arms** sells goods (restocks 1–3 days), upgrades weapons, and sells a
**Computer Voice Module** for $3000 (talk him to $2500 with Barter, or **$1500** with CH 9 or a
critical Barter check).

**Algernon** in the basement does **every weapon upgrade for free** if you hand him the item.
Also down there: your first **Electronic Lockpick** — which means you can now go back to the
[Toxic Caves](/walkthrough/toxic-caves) — and, in a partly obscured pot near the broken mattress
in the rightmost corner, the **Easter Egg**.

Enter through the **back door** to loot the place, and not at night when Eldridge is in the back
or the dogs attack. Look for the staircase behind the metal shelves.

:::note
Eldridge's upgrade prices were meant to scale with Barter. The only effect is that at **Barter
91%+ all upgrades are free**.

RPU makes him leave to check on his dogs if they bark too much, as originally intended — so watch
for him moving. His front door is also locked at night now.
<Vanilla>Eldridge never leaves his post and his door is never locked.</Vanilla>
:::

### The church

**Father Tully** answers questions about the families for any kind of booze. Rest two hours if he
is passed out. Get him to tell you about the **Salvatores and their laser weapons** — that
matters for quest 6.

If you are **Married**, he will divorce you for a bottle. Your spouse runs off permanently and
you become **Separated**.

:::tip
The poor box is worth **−3 karma** to loot, and donating a coin gives **+3 karma**. Both fire
only once, so you can withdraw your donation and come back weekly for more.

The small garden behind the church has **2 broc flowers and 3 xander roots**. Easily missed.
:::

:::note
Return to Father Tully after finishing the game and he gives you the **Fallout 2 Hintbook** —
10,000 XP every time you use it, and it raises all skills to 300%.
:::

### The East Side

Children run around outside the Wright mansion during daytime; if they are absent, wait until
noon and re-enter the map. They lead you into the desert to witness the secret transaction if you
have **CH 7**, the Empathy perk, a passed Speech check, or give them a handgun.

**1500 XP** if you stay hidden until the transaction completes. Walk straight down to leave
unseen.

:::caution
Give the kids a gun and all four have shot themselves by the time you return. You can retrieve
the gun. Stealing it back before you leave the map prevents this.

Slavers and Made Men of other families cannot talk to them at all, and you must remove power
armour first.
:::

:::note[Assassinating Boss Wright]
Give a kid a gun and tell him to wave it in his father's face and pull the trigger. No XP. You
can still get the desert transaction by talking to the kids again.
:::

**Orville's safe:** use Traps on it repeatedly — even after it says you succeeded — until you get
the **100 XP** message and free **dynamite**. Then pick it. Leaving and returning re-traps it,
for more free dynamite.

## Quests

### 1. Recover your stolen car
The first time you leave the car on Virgin Street it is stolen. This is not the infamous car bug.

Walk to where it was and you get the option to follow the tracks. Better: talk to **Cody**, the
boy outside the Cat's Paw, and ask him to lead you there — **500 XP**.

:::caution[Protect Cody]
Ask Cody who stole it and he implicates Jules. If you do, you **must** tell Jules you will kill
him if anything happens to Cody, **before leaving the map**.

Cody runs off if you approach in power armour, offer him drugs or food, are a Slaver or
Childkiller, have Lenny or Marcus with you, or have karma −101 or lower. Wait a minute without
leaving the map and he calms down; otherwise he never speaks to you again.
:::

At the chop shop, find **T-Ray** in the garage:

| Method | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Made Man of the Bishops — say Bishop sent you | Offer $1500, $900 or nothing; he will not argue |
| **Bluff that you are a Made Man** | Bugged: you succeed by **failing** the Speech check with **CH 7 or less**. Retry until you fail. **+750 XP** |
| Pay **$1000** ($750/$500 with Barter) | Straightforward |
| Sleep with him | Female, **CH 7+** or Porn Star |
| Just drive away | They attack after the second attempt |
| Kill everyone | Also an option |

**750 XP** for resolving it any of the first five ways.

:::danger[Get the upgrade immediately]
If T-Ray is alive, pay **$500** ($300 with Barter) for the car upgrade: **much bigger trunk** and
better engine performance.

**Do this before driving the car off the map.** Once you leave and return, a scripting glitch
removes all his dialogue options and the upgrade is gone forever.
:::

:::note
T-Ray is the most heavily bugged character in the game. If you let him mention payment as a Made
Man, **$2700 is taken from your inventory immediately**, on top of anything you then offer. It
all ends up in his inventory, so you can take it back.

He also claims to sell batteries. He never has any.
:::

### 2. Find out who was responsible for Richard Wright's overdose
Talk to **Keith Wright** and ask for work — he refuses Berserkers, Childkillers and anyone at
−101 karma or worse, and there is a Speech check (harder for Slavers). A recommendation from
**Chris Wright** at the railway station also works.

Then talk to **Orville Wright**.

:::danger
Never suggest Richard took jet voluntarily. Orville attacks.
:::

Keith names four suspects: **Jules, Jimmy J, Little Jesus and Renesco**. With **IN 7** you can
ask which room was Richard's.

1. Search the **bookcase inside the door** of Richard's room for an **empty jet canister**:
   **500 XP**
2. Show it to **Jagged Jimmy J**, who identifies it as poisoned: **500 XP**
3. Mention that to **Renesco**, who reveals the **Salvatores** ordered it: **1000 XP**

(Alternatively, dig up Richard's grave at Golgotha and examine it — same total XP, but you become
a **Grave Digger**.)

Report to Orville:

| What you say | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Finger any one dealer | 500 XP, $250, 2 booze, 20 shotgun shells |
| Name the **Salvatores** | $350, 2 booze, 40 shotgun shells |
| Deduce their motive (**IN 7**, and you must know all four families) | **+2000 XP** |

:::tip[Get both]
Say Richard was poisoned, mention **Renesco's** name, then tell Orville that Renesco was not
ultimately responsible, then work out the Salvatores' motive. You collect the 500 and the 2000,
and Renesco comes to no harm. It does not work the other way round.
:::

:::caution
Fingering **Little Jesus** turns the Mordino family hostile. Naming anyone already dead makes
Orville attack. Do **not** tell Orville you opened the grave.
:::

### 3. Find a way into the Sierra Army Base
See [Sierra Army Depot](/walkthrough/sierra-army-depot). Return to Orville after getting inside
and you become a **Made Man of the Wrights**: **1000 XP**, plus free booze and beer from the
station shelves.

:::caution
Contaminate the base with the virus before reporting back and you get **no reward at all**.
:::

### 4. Bust up Wright's still
Save before talking to **Mrs. Wright** — many dialogue branches end with her refusing to speak to
you again. You must have heard of the Wright family first.

Ask about her husband and say his alcohol business is making New Reno worse. She tells you to
meet her at the church next morning. Wait at Father Tully's until **6 a.m.**, offer to wreck the
stills, and go to the basement.

Stand in the niche beside **any of the first three stills from the right** and use **Science,
Repair, or a crowbar/tool/wrench**. All of them are destroyed: **500 XP**.

Return to Mrs. Wright and ask for a cash reward: **$237 and the Louisville Slugger**, a unique
weapon.

:::tip
Do not miss the **ice fridge** among the barrels — it holds a Cat's Paw magazine among other
things.
:::

:::caution
Dynamite works but alerts the Wrights. Mrs. Wright will not talk to Porn Stars or Slavers unless
you already have the quest.
:::

:::note
Use a **scorpion tail** on the stills before destroying them and junkies and addicts across New
Reno drop dead over time — Father Tully included.
:::

### 5. Track down Pretty Boy Lloyd, recover the stolen money, and make an... example of him
Tell **Mason** you have information or want a job to get an audience with **Boss Salvatore**.
Pick up the **laser pistol** from the footlocker on your way out — Eldridge wants one.

Lloyd is behind a locked door in the **Desperado basement**.

:::danger[Lloyd must not escape]
Salvatore will know, and you will be attacked when you return.
:::

Get him to reveal the money's location in Golgotha, then either kill him or — better — **make him
come with you** (**IN 6**). You can intimidate him with **ST 6**, combat or power armour,
Berserker status, or simply having a companion.

**If you go alone:** the third grave from the right in the top section. Use **Traps** on it for a
**plastic explosive**, then a **shovel** (this one does not make you a Grave Digger), then step
onto the open grave to enter the fallout shelter and take the **$1000**.

**If Lloyd comes with you:** tell him to dig (you get the explosive), dig yourself (the bomb goes
off in your face and he attacks), or check for traps. Then send him down first and **throw the
landmine in after him**, which is both effective and funny.

:::caution
Waiting for Lloyd to come back up lets him escape. Killing him costs **10 karma** — unless a
companion lands the final blow, which costs nothing. The landmine costs only 5.
:::

Return to Salvatore with the correct amount: **500 XP, $500**, and the next quest.

:::tip[Salvatore's respect]
There is always a respectful line available with **CH 6 or IN 8**. Take it. To boost his respect
early: on first meeting, say you have information and use his name, then immediately leave
dialogue by saying you are in the wrong room. Repeatable, and it does not offend him.
:::

### 6. Go visit Renesco the Rocketman and collect Mr. Salvatore's tribute of $1000
**Empty your inventory first** — you are about to need the space.

Ask Renesco for the tribute and keep insisting Salvatore sent you. When he says he is broke,
suggest a **discount in exchange for you paying the tribute** (**IN 7**), then say you want to see
his stock **right away** — you get one chance.

You can now buy his entire inventory for almost nothing.

:::tip
The discount modifier is applied **each time you enter that dialogue node**. Leave the barter
screen and re-enter by asking to make purchases again, and prices drop by another factor. Trade
back and forth and you can take everything, cash included, for **$1**.
:::

Give Salvatore the $1000 and he returns **$250** ($100 if he dislikes you), plus **750 XP**.

:::caution
Do not go back to Salvatore for confirmation, and do not threaten Renesco with violence. Before
returning for the next quest, make sure you have heard about the **"lightbringers"** from Father
Tully or Jules.
:::

### 7. Help guard a secret transaction taking place in the desert
Only offered if Salvatore likes you and you have not destroyed the Enclave.

If you know about the laser weapons, ask for one. Then tell **Mason** that Salvatore said to give
you a lightbringer and ask for training: you get a **laser pistol** and **+5% Energy Weapons**.
He gives you 24 hours; be late and he attacks.

In the desert, **do not move until you are told you can**, or everyone opens fire.

Return to Salvatore: **1000 XP**, and another **1000 XP** for accepting Made Man status. Refuse
and he attacks. Collect your reward from Mason:

| Standing | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Both like you | $300, 80 small energy cells, **Metal Armor Mark II** |
| Salvatore only | $150, Metal Armor |
| Neither | $50 |

:::tip[Fly to Navarro]
RPU lets you **sneak aboard the vertibird** during the transaction. It takes you straight to
[Navarro](/walkthrough/navarro).
<Vanilla>The vertibird cannot be boarded.</Vanilla>

This is a very fast way to Navarro and a very fast way to die. See the Navarro page first.
:::

:::caution
You can do this quest **and** get the 1500 XP from following the Wright kids — but you must go
with the **Wrights first**. Take this quest, tell Mason you need more time, then follow the kids,
and you end up among the Salvatores with no XP.
:::

### 8. Find some endorphin blockers to make a cure for Jet
Ask **Myron** about a cure for jet addiction. With **IN 7 and Science 81%** (or IN 9 and Science
76%) you convince him one might exist: **2000 XP**, and Vault City appears on your map.

Talk to **Dr. Troy**: another **2000 XP** and **2 Jet Antidotes**.

:::caution[The correct order]
1. Get Vault City's <Quest>Deliver a sample of jet to Dr. Troy</Quest>
2. Recruit Myron and talk to him about jet production
3. Deliver the sample and introduce Myron to Troy
4. **Then** talk to Myron about the cure
5. **Then** talk to Troy about endorphin blockers

Pick up the antidote from Troy first, or blackmail him, and neither Myron nor Troy will discuss a
cure at all.
:::

### 9. Assassinate Westin in NCR without making it look like a murder
Offered after you deliver Moore's briefcase. Bishop offers **$500**; push to **$750** (Barter) or
**$1000** (Barter or IN 8).

:::note
A bug means asking for $750 actually pays $1000 — and another bug makes the $1000 Barter check
*easier* than the $750 one.
:::

Finish every NCR and Vault City quest involving Westin first. Then use **the poison from Doc
Jubilee's house**, or a dose of **jet, Buffout or Psycho**, on Westin and he has a heart attack.
Planted explosives also count as natural causes. A Sneak-kill does not.

**2000 XP** and the next quest.

:::tip[RPU's subtler method]
Get Doc Jubilee to mention **"pecker pills"** — look at his shelf (open it) *before* talking to
him, or get caught trying to steal his heart pills. He will not sell them under any circumstances.

Then get the fake pills from **Renesco** for **$200** — question the price and he raises it to
$300, but you can then talk him into making them free by reminding him about the **Wright
affair** or the **Salvatore** business, depending on how you handled those. **Do not mention
Bishop**, or he refuses outright.

Put the fake pills in Jubilee's shelf. Westin dies within 14 days, at his next medical pickup —
ask Jubilee when that is by asking if he has reconsidered selling the pills.
<Vanilla>The pecker pills route does not exist.</Vanilla>

**Finish everything on the ranch map before Westin dies**, or you will not be let in. And note a
bug: you must be **in NCR on the day he is due to die**, or the countdown restarts.
:::

:::note
A **Super Stimpak** on Westin does 10,000 damage and still satisfies the "not a murder" condition.
:::

### 10. Murder Carlson in NCR
See [NCR](/walkthrough/ncr). On return: **2500 XP** for Carlson, and **1000 XP** for becoming a
**Made Man of the Bishops**.

:::danger
If you were ever pushed into the conversation where Bishop leveraged the raiders or his wife
against you, he does not trust you enough to make you a Made Man and **attacks instead**.
Refusing his offer also means a fight.
:::

### 11. Deliver Big Jesus's package to Ramirez at the Stables
Ask **Big Jesus** for work. Without Little Jesus's introduction you need a Speech check first —
retryable, as long as you do not start pleading. Then he needs you to be male with **ST 6**, or
wearing combat or power armour, or to pass another Speech check (easier with CH 6).

You are teleported to the Stables. Give Ramirez the box: **500 XP**. Return to Mordino for
**$100** and the next quest.

:::danger
You can open the box and find 5 jet. Give him the **opened** box and he attacks, and the whole
Mordino family turns on you.
:::

### 12. Collect tribute from the Corsican Brothers
The **Corsican Brother** at the Golden Globes hands over **$250** immediately, and you get a
chance to audition. Return to Mordino: keep **$125**, plus **500 XP** and the next quest.

### 13. Assassinate Boss Salvatore for Big Jesus Mordino
**750 XP and $500** on return — **1000 XP** for a proper assassination. With **CH 6+** you also
get a **Leather Armor Mark II** and a Grease Gun. Get the armour and thank him properly (or have
CH 9) and he offers you Made Man status: **1000 XP** to accept, an assault to decline.

:::caution
If you already killed Salvatore, this quest is never offered.
:::

## Nikki and Pretty Boy Lloyd

RPU adds **Nikki** in the back room of Salvatore's casino. She knows where Lloyd is hiding.
<Vanilla>Nikki does not exist, and Lloyd's location comes only from the standard dialogue.</Vanilla>

**Bribe her $500** — what Lloyd owes her for her part in the scam — and she tells you: **250 XP**.
Threaten her instead and she only says he is at the Desperado, without mentioning the boiler
room, and you get nothing. There is a Speech check, but not a hard one.

## An extended way to the Stables, for women

RPU restores a route the original game only half-implemented.
<Vanilla>Volunteering through Miss Kitty simply drops you at the Stables gate with nothing
else.</Vanilla>

1. Pay **Jules $75** to tell you about the Mordinos, then ask everything about jet — the
   "factory?" and "maker?" questions — to learn about **Myron** and that he receives prostitutes.
2. Talk to **Jagged Jimmy J**: ask where he gets his jet, then more about Myron. You should get
   the option *"How do I meet this Living-God…?"*
3. Ask **Miss Kitty** to volunteer as the next girl sent to the Stables. **500 XP.**

Female characters can collect this even after finding the Stables another way.
