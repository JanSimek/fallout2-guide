# Navarro

> *"Now I suppose you're going to tell me you're just out looking for a water chip. Do I look dumb?"*
> — Enclave patrolman

The Enclave's mainland refuelling base. It holds the **Tanker FOB** you need to finish the game, the
**vertibird plans** three factions want, a suit of **Advanced Power Armor**, and one of the hardest
fights in the game if you handle it badly.

## Getting here

- **Matthew** at the San Francisco Brotherhood puts it on your map, as does **Harry** or **Ken Lee**
  when they send you for the plans.
- **Returning to Arroyo** after the fourth Hakunin dream reveals it.
- RPU adds a third route: **stow away on the vertibird** during the Salvatore desert transaction in
  New Reno.
  <Vanilla>The vertibird cannot be boarded.</Vanilla>

:::danger[The vertibird route is a suicide run]
The crew find you when you reach the landing pad. You can **jump out and fight** or **surrender**.

Surrender and you are taken to the base commander for questioning. You can answer some of his
questions and ask a few of your own — and then he tries to kill you regardless. There is no way out
of the fight.

**Surrendering is still the better option**, because you start alone with him and can escape from
there much more easily than from the landing pad. But do not take this route early or unprepared.
:::

## Chris

A robed man greets you and tries to send you away.

With Matthew's quest you can ask about the Enclave and say you want to join. Pull it off and you get
**1500 XP and the password** — **one attempt only**. Bringing companions means a second Speech check.

When you are done, ask him about vertibirds and mention the Brotherhood. You then have about **20
seconds to kill him** before he raises the alarm: **500 XP**.

:::caution
Several dialogue options make him attack immediately, or sound the alarm and then attack.

**Arrive in the car and he opens dialogue at once.** On a second visit that can leave you with no
option but to attack — which risks putting the base on alert. **Deal with Chris on your first visit.**
:::

## Getting inside

Three ways:

1. **The shack with the trapdoor** — the easiest. Chris stops you if he is alive. Puts you in the
   underground section.
2. **The stony path through the woods** to the green exit grid, then talk to the guards. You need the
   **password** and must be **alone**.
3. **The air vent** in the southwest corner of the gas station map, with the **Blue Pass Key** from
   the Gecko power plant. This lets you unlock the outer cell door and releases Xarn early.

:::danger[The woods are mined]
The mines there **cannot be spotted**, even at PE 10 with an absurd Traps skill.
:::

:::note[The companion counting bug]
Most Navarro scripts assume the car counts as a companion, which it does not. The practical effect:
**you can bring one companion and the guards still consider you alone.**

Two exceptions: **Chris** needs you to bring one companion to think you are alone (if you have the
car), and the **guard outside the commander's office** raises the alarm for anyone except K-9 — his
script was fixed in the patch. Sneak past him.

If you never bought the car, none of this applies.
:::

:::caution
With a few exceptions, **people inside will not talk to you unless you are wearing Power Armor**.
Show up with more than one companion and they attack.
:::

## The underground section

**Loot the northwest room:** a suit of **Advanced Power Armor**, a **Blue Memory Module**, a **Plasma
Rifle** and more.

The **quartermaster** talks to you in any armour, even the Vault 13 jumpsuit, and just tells you to
get suited up.

**The computer room:** hack it with good Science — failure sets off the alarm — or ask a **scientist**
and pass a Speech check for the password, **"Snookie"**. You can also learn the nickname from Cookie.
Ask the computer about the main base to get quest 3.

:::tip
Once you have the password you can go and mock the female guard outside with it.
:::

## Ground level

**Plasma turrets** make this map dangerous.

:::danger
A sign says tampering with the turrets means death. Using **any** skill on one makes it attack.
:::

**Quincy** in the northeast garage has the **Vertibird Plans** in the locker behind him. Two
scientists — one next door, one in the comm central to the south — will tell you about the tanker and
the plans. Once you have asked either of them, tell Quincy a tech sent you and he hands the plans
over. **3500 XP.** Very good Sneak lets you simply take them.

**Raul** is in the vertibird hangar. If you told Quincy that Raul sent you, tell Raul that Quincy
wants to see him and he walks off to sort him out.

:::caution
**Save before talking to the sergeant** south of the comm central lift. He is worth hearing once — and
then **reload**, because otherwise he patrols the grounds indefinitely and attacks if he finds you
anywhere but beside the vertibird.
:::

:::caution
Do not approach the southern gate from the inside unless you gave the guards the password from the
outside.
:::

## Quests

### 1. Deal with the deathclaw
**Dr. Schreber** is in the soundproofed room. Ask him about the deathclaw and you can get past the
cell door either way.

| Choice | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Kill **Xarn** | 500 quest XP + 1200 kill XP |
| **Free him** — needs the **Blue Pass Key** for the southern door | **1500 XP** |
| Team up with him, if the alarm is already sounded | 1500 XP |

There is a Blue Pass Key in **Dr. Schreber's desk** if you did not bring the one from Gecko.

:::tip[Kill Schreber anyway]
**1000 XP and +25 karma**, and nobody notices — the room is soundproofed. Threatening him makes him
run; end combat and he is harmless, but killing him is better value.
:::

:::note
Xarn stays with you until you approach the red exit grid on the gas station map, or until you open the
cell door with the pass key. If you free him he turns up in Vault 13 later.

The greedy route: **team up (1500 XP), open the door (1500 XP), then kill him on his way out (500
XP)**. The base has to be on alert for this.
:::

### 2. Fix K-9
From **Dr. Schreber** when you ask about the cyberdog, or from K-9 itself after you kill Schreber.

Get the **K-9 Motivator** from the vertibird hangar above ground — tell **Raul** that Dr. Schreber
sent you for permission to open the locker behind him — and use it on the dog. **3500 XP**, Science
check required.

With **karma −100 or higher** and no Childkiller status, K-9 joins immediately, **even over your
Charisma limit**. You can walk around Navarro with him and nobody reacts.

### 3. Retrieve the FOB from the base Commander
Appears after you ask the computer about reaching the Enclave, or ask **Cookie** about it.

Ask the guard outside the commander's office **who gets to come in** and he mentions the cleaning
crew. Walk out of his sight, **wait one hour**, then tell him you have come to clean the office.

Inside, the commander will not let you open the locker holding the **Tanker FOB**. Options:

- **Sneak-Lockpick** it — needs **Sneak 60%** and a high Lockpick
- Use a great many **Super Stimpaks** on the commander
- **Easiest:** with this quest already active and good Speech, tell the guard outside you have come to
  fetch the Tanker FOB. The commander lets you walk in and take it.

**3500 XP.**

## Fighting the whole base

If you would rather shoot your way through:

- **Kill the turrets first, from a distance.** They are not tough but they hit hard. **As long as any
  turret survives on the ground level you cannot leave combat mode**, which is maddening.
- **Keep your party together.** Use the "stay where you are" combat option.
- **Work upward from the underground.** Take a lift to the surface, clear the immediate area, go back
  down to heal, then take the next lift. Far easier than a frontal assault.
- **Leave reckless companions behind.** Goris is impossible here.
- You do not have to kill **Cookie**.
- Apart from Chris, attacking from the start costs you **no quest XP**.

:::tip[A melee exploit]
Once the underground battle starts, stand directly in front of one of the **south-facing vents** — the
hex edge should touch the wall. The one in the L-shaped corridor to an elevator works, as does the one
in Xarn's room.

Guards then cannot shoot you; they run up and stare. You cannot shoot them either — or use a Super
Sledge — but **unarmed attacks and range-1 melee weapons work fine**.
:::
