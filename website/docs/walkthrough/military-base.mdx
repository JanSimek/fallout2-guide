# Military Base

> *"I hope dad comes back soon. I miss his magic tricks."* — Melchior Jr.

The ruins of the Mariposa Military Base from Fallout 1. Entirely optional, short, and one of the
best sources of equipment before Navarro.

**Goris** is the only one who will mark it on your map, though Chris in Navarro and Jimmy in Vault 13
both mention it. It has a large world-map circle, so it is always findable once you know it exists.

## The surface

Loot the tents. The wolves circle and howl for a while before attacking.

**The entrance is blocked by rocks**, and dynamite alone does nothing. The solution:

1. Pick up the **Metal Pole** from the sidewalk
2. **Use it on the cart** to attach it
3. Get **dynamite** from the shack to the north and **use it on the pole** — no need to arm it
4. **Push the cart** (ST check; retry until it works)

**5000 XP.**

## Level 1

Rats — the irradiating kind. Loot the bodies; the holodisks are flavour only.

**Grundel** the mutant is to the north, hunting rats with his back turned. If you have the
**Mutagenic Serum** from Dr. Henry in NCR, this is a much better test subject than an innocent
mutant in Broken Hills. Walk up behind him and use it.

Use **Repair** (50%+, a tool helps) on the generator to restore lights and the elevator: **1500 XP**.

:::note
The blue boxes are searchable.
:::

## Level 2

A real fight, which this game does not offer often.

The two mutants in the far right room can sometimes be taken out without alerting anyone. The two
large groups will notice if you attack either.

:::tip
Take the narrow passage to the bunk bed room, shoot one mutant from there, and pick them off as they
come to you. Set your companions' combat settings so they do not charge forward. **The Bozar is
outstanding** in these close quarters.
:::

**Best loot:** a full suit of **Power Armor** in the locker room.

## Level 3

Mutants and rats. Harder than level 2, because there are enemies with **laser and plasma rifles** who
land vicious criticals. Do not let them swarm you — retreat behind corners with AP to spare.

**Best loot:** the **Green Memory Module** in the locker.

:::note
Walk into the locker room and scroll right to overhear two super mutants discussing how to prepare a
"rat surprise".
:::

## Level 4 — Melchior

One lone mutant standing in the radioactive goo that used to fill the vats. Stepping in it hurts.

This is **Melchior the Magnificent**, the stage magician whose son you met in Redding — and his
small furry creatures have mutated. There is no way to avoid the fight.

**Every turn he summons reinforcements**, in this order:

1. **Four deathclaws**, two at a time
2. **Four fire geckos**, one at a time — each can breathe fire **twice per turn**
3. **Four floaters**, one at a time
4. **Six mutated mole rats**, one at a time

Kill **all** the pets as well as Melchior — set companions to attack "whomever is closest" — and the
battle is worth **14,315 XP**.

:::tip A reliable strategy
Bring companions past the bottleneck at the entrance to his room. Once he turns hostile, run back
just behind the wooden support; with luck a companion will block the passage. His pets cannot reach
you and gather around him instead, where you can shoot them freely.

Save first — this fight can crash.
:::

Afterwards, take the **Gauss Pistol** from the ammo crate.

:::note The Flame Breath
Fire geckos normally leave a **golden gecko pelt**. Kill one with a **0-damage critical to the eyes**
and it leaves its **Flame Breath** instead — a flamethrower that looks like a rifle, uses the Small
Guns skill, and cannot be reloaded.

Retrieving it needs the same trick as the Robo Rocket Launcher: another lootable body on the same
hex. Melchior's corpse, a downed companion, or a dead Enclave soldier near the elevator all work.
:::
