# EPA

:::note[An RPU location]
The Environmental Protection Agency is the Restoration Project's largest addition: a six-level
underground facility, three recruitable companions, a drug synthesiser and a great deal of loot.
None of it exists in the unmodded game.
:::

## Getting here

Three ways onto your map:

1. **Recruit Myron and then try to dismiss him** — he tells you where it is.
2. A **[random traveller](/systems/random-encounters)** somewhere near New Reno.
3. Doing a quest for **[Dr. Sheng](/walkthrough/san-francisco)** in San Francisco.

:::danger[Bring a rope]
The elevators are broken. Without a rope you cannot get below the surface at all, and there is
none to be found here.
:::

## The surface

A horde of **spore plants** covers most of the map. Alone you can weave between them; with a
party they will charge off and fight all of them, which takes a while.

**The white shed** to the left of the entrance holds:

- **A can of plant spray** — equip it and use it on a plant to kill it instantly
- **A big metal pole** in one of the lockers. **Take this.** You need it.

There is a second can of plant spray in the **locked storage shed** north of the white shed, along
with **cans of dog food** — use one on **Dogmeat** to fully heal him.

Grab a piece of **junk** from the scattered debris. You need one of those too.

:::tip[Save one spore plant]
Quest 5 requires you to test a weapon on something **outside**. Leave one plant alive on the
surface or you will have to go wandering the wastes to find a target.
:::

The main building has a locker with **stimpaks** and a computer describing the facility's levels.
Go down the **right elevator shaft** with your rope.

:::caution
**Skynet and all dogs cannot climb down.** They wait for you at the top.
:::

## The Doctor

The **warehouse** on the surface holds the last scientist left in the EPA — 202 years old, kept
alive by a cryogenic chamber he modified so his mind stays conscious through hibernation. He built
**Mr. Chemmie**, and he created the **centaurs, floaters and "Aliens"** now roaming the wasteland,
using the FEV stored here. He keeps captured villagers and his own "warriors" in the cages.

He is worth talking to at length. He is also a monster, and the dialogue does not hide it.

:::caution[He judges you on skills]
**Science above 70%** gets him to explain what he does. Below that he dismisses you, and you need
**Speech above 100% or Science above 100%** to talk your way back in.
:::

:::tip[Bring Myron for a free Solar Scorcher]
With **Myron in your party**, ask the Doctor about **jet**. He is fascinated, they compare notes,
and he hands you a **Solar Scorcher** as thanks — no quest required.
<Vanilla>Neither the Doctor nor this conversation exists.</Vanilla>
:::

:::caution[The serum is a real +1 Agility]
Once you have done his other jobs he offers to test an agility serum on you. You black out for a
couple of hours and wake with **+1 Agility, permanently**. It is safe.
:::

:::danger
Attack him and he sets off an explosion that **releases his caged warriors**. He is not a
pushover, and you get one warning float.
:::

### Collect Gecko Pelts for the Doctor
He wants research material on golden geckos: **10 golden gecko pelts**.

Klamath's **Gecko Skinning** perk from Smiley is what makes this cheap — every golden gecko you
kill from then on carries a pelt, and the woods around the still are full of them.

He then offers the lighting job, and after that the serum.

### Find the tool the Doctor left behind
He has mislaid a tool somewhere in the sublevels and asks you to fetch it. Accepting **opens the
maze**, which is how you get at the rest of the facility from his side.

Bring him the **drill**: **750 XP**.

:::note
He is transparently using you as a test subject — the asides he mutters to himself say so. You can
call him on it when you hand the tool over, which leads to a fight.
:::

:::note[He also gives the lighting quest]
<Quest rpu>Fix EPA lighting problem</Quest> can come from the Doctor as well as from the
hologram — he wants the lights fixed so he can get into the main complex himself.
:::

## Air, Water and Power Facility

Rats, mantises and small scorpions. Lockers here hold a **Super Tool Kit**, an **Electronic
Lockpick** and a couple of books.

One of the four **power generators** is malfunctioning — leave it for now; you need quest 1 first.

On the north wall are three large vents. The **middle one** is just big enough to squeeze through,
but there is a spinning fan in the way. **Use the metal pole to jam the fan.**

## Maintenance level

Two working elevators, northwest and northeast. A locker with a **laser pistol**, and a depressed
Mr. Handy walking around.

## Level layout

| Elevator | Sub-level | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| West | W1 | **Orange** — conference rooms and cafeteria |
| West | W2 | **Yellow** — robot control |
| West | W3 | **Indigo** |
| East | E1 | **Red** — security wing |
| East | E2 | **Violet** — Mr. Chemmie, and the way to Blue |
| East | E3 | **Green** — botany and the petting zoo |

There is also a **Blue Level**, which officially does not exist.

**Start with Orange.**

## Orange Level

Four **holograms** argue in a loop to the west and will not talk to you — a nearby magnetic field
is interfering. In the room to their left are two computers: use the **left** one, run a
diagnostics check, then **Repair** it (**over 40%**): **100 XP**. Now you can talk to them.

:::tip[Pop Rocks and Nuka-Cola]
In one of the cafeteria fridges is a bag of **Pop Rocks**, and a cooler holds **Nuka-Cola**. Equip
the Pop Rocks and use them on yourself, then do the same with the Nuka-Cola.

Surround yourself with companions first, and save. It is worth it.
:::

:::caution
The room to the north holds mutated fruit flies in a highly toxic chemical atmosphere. There is
**nothing** in there. Do not go in without the gas mask.
:::

### 1. Fix EPA lighting problem
:::tip[Do this first]
Several doors stay locked until you finish it.
:::

From the **Director of Public Relations** (the female hologram) — but only after you have tried to
open one of the password-protected doors and asked her for the password. The nearest is the room
just south of her.

You need **Speech 81%** — or 61% and asking her twice.

She wants the **left-hand power generator** on the Air, Water and Power level repaired. With
**Repair above 55%** you learn it needs a piece of **junk**: **500 XP**.

Return for the password.

### 2. Clear out the plant problem
**Hologram 10031** runs ground maintenance and cannot control the robots. He hires you as
assistant groundskeeper to clear the surface spore plants. If you already killed them, just say so.

**500 XP** and the **keys to the storage shed** on the surface.

:::caution
Consider waiting until you have quest 5, so you still have a plant to shoot at.
:::

### 3. Fix the Voice Computer
The **Director of Security** — the obnoxious one in power armour — has no robot control because the
voice computer is broken.

The **EPA Robot control computer** is on the **Yellow Level**; it is the one that says "BEEP!"
periodically. Run a diagnostic, then repair it: **76%**, or **56%** with any tool. **500 XP.**

Return for an **access card** that opens most doors on the **Red Level**.

:::note
The fourth hologram, the Director of Operations, does nothing at all.
:::

## Yellow Level

Connected to the Violet level through a very toxic chemical room. **Enter that room from the
Violet side**, where the gas mask is, unless you already have protection.

### 4. Fix hologram 00000
The **Director of Science** speaks only in binary. You need **Science 100% and IN 6** to
understand him.

He needs the **tape reel** near him repaired (**81%**, or **51%** with any tool), and then a
reboot — which you only learn about with the same Science and Intelligence. **500 XP.**

### 5. Test the Solar Scorcher for the Director of Science
Once fixed, he asks you to field-test an experimental weapon. The **Solar Scorcher** is in the
locker beside the tape reel machine — locked until you have this quest, and unopenable before then.

Go **outside** — genuinely outside, not the sub-levels — shoot something, and report back:
**500 XP**. You keep the gun.

:::note
The Solar Scorcher is the same weapon that used to appear only in the
[Guardian of Forever](/systems/random-encounters) special encounter.
:::

## Indigo Level

No quests, but three things worth your time.

**The beeping computer** in the room directly south of the elevator holds research notes.

- Read the notes on **a cure for epilepsy**, then go and cure the **Barking Man** in New Reno:
  **100 XP and 3 Stimpaks**.
- Push **"keep searching"** to find the **Mass Fluoridation Project**: **200 XP**. Each attempt
  rolls against your Luck, so it can surface early; failing that it is guaranteed on the **22nd**
  press. Every search advances the clock **half an hour**, so brute-forcing it costs most of a day.

**The three modified auto-docs** in the room below let you change your appearance — hair length,
baldness, hair colour, and a sex change.

:::danger
These machines can kill you. Save first.
:::

**The Brave Little Toaster** sits on a desk in a room to the far left. He is desperate to make you
toast; it has been a century. Repair him (easy roll): **450 XP**.

:::tip
Talk to him afterwards and you gain the ability to **jinx slot machines** — using it makes a slot
machine surrender all its money. Redding and New Reno are suddenly much more profitable.
:::

## Red Level

Most doors need the **security access card** from quest 3.

### 6. Fix the hologram that speaks with static
The hologram below the elevator is drowned in static. You need **IN 5** to realise what is wrong
and **Repair 51%** — or 21% with any tool — to fix the red blinking projection machine.

Talk to him again for the **password to the Blue Level elevator**. This is the only way to get it.
He can also explain most of the odd items you have found in the facility.

:::note
There is a museum here, complete with a short film. And a great many lockers — check all of them.
:::

:::danger[The electric chair]
Still operational, switchable from the computer in the room above the elevator. Standing too close
while it is on is fatal. It is also possible to give a companion a small push.
:::

## Violet Level

Mostly empty, but important.

**The gas mask** is in the small wall locker in the hallway to the contaminated area. Equip it in
an active item slot and you are immune to the toxic rooms — here, on the Yellow level, and in the
Broken Hills mine.

:::tip
To the right of the big computer are **two fans clogged with goo**. Clean them for **50 XP each**
and the air becomes breathable permanently, so you no longer need the mask on this level.
:::

### Mr. Chemmie

The big computer is a drug synthesiser. Give it ingredients and it makes almost anything:

| Item | Ingredients | Science |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stimpak | Broc flower, xander root, empty hypo | — |
| Super Stimpak | Stimpak, fruit, Nuka-Cola | — |
| Poison | Empty hypo, scorpion tail, Nuka-Cola | 41% |
| Poison antidote | Scorpion tail, booze | 46% |
| First Aid Kit | Stimpak, empty hypo, poison antidote, RadAway | 56% |
| Doctor's Bag | Stimpak, empty hypo, poison antidote, First Aid Kit | 61% |
| Buffout | First Aid Kit, beer, Nuka-Cola | 71% |
| Mentats | First Aid Kit, fruit, booze | 81% |
| Psycho | Mentats, Buffout, fruit, Stimpak | 86% |
| Rad-X | First Aid Kit, Nuka-Cola | 86% |
| RadAway | Nuka-Cola, empty hypo, Stimpak | 91% |

**Special recipes:**

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Heart Pills** | With **Cassidy in your party and Science 101%**. Using them on Cassidy **cures his heart condition** — after which Buffout no longer kills him. |
| **Marijuana** | Pre-war. CH +2, LK +1, PE −1 for 11 days, then a further −1 PE after 7 days. **Science over 100%**. |
| **ERSI** | Vital to anyone just out of a cryo-chamber. You must learn about it before the option appears. |

:::tip
The Heart Pills alone justify the trip. All those broc flowers and xander roots from Arroyo turn
into an unlimited stimpak supply here.
:::

## Green Level

The botanical garden and the petting zoo — which holds humans, deformed and maddened by genetic
experimentation.

**The EPA botany terminal** on the east side holds a **botany file**. If you have
<Quest rpu>Collect information about botany for Brother Paul</Quest> from the
[Abbey](/walkthrough/abbey), download it to a holodisk.

**The seed storage tanks** hold **modified corn** and **experimental seeds**.

- Plant the **experimental seeds** in the fertile ground in the southwest corner of the garden:
  **100 XP**. Come back in three days for a random new bush (sometimes a spore plant).
- Plant the **modified corn** in [Arroyo](/walkthrough/arroyo): **100 XP**. It changes nothing for
  the village, and must be done before the fourth dream.

:::caution
Read the computers by the cells before lowering any force fields. Everything in there is hostile.
:::

:::note
Pay attention to the **hole in the wall of the wanamingo holding cell**. That is where the Blue
Level's occupants came from.
:::

## Blue Level

The secret level. Take the elevator on the **Violet** level using the password from the Red level
hologram.

You arrive on a platform surrounded by **wanamingos**. Linger and they come through the hole in
the fence.

**The large force field** ahead lowers via **Repair** on the computer to its right — a hard check,
around **20% overall chance at Repair 100%**, but retryable indefinitely. A well-placed explosive
in front of the computer does the same job.

### The hibernation chambers

Up the stairs are **three hibernation chambers**, each holding a recruitable companion. The
computer inside the door describes all three and lets you **extract one**.

:::danger[You get one]
Extracting one companion permanently locks the other two chambers — and their personal lockers,
which cannot be opened by any means afterwards.
:::

The computer warns that you need a can of **ERSI**. Go up to Mr. Chemmie and make one — the recipe
becomes available only now — or ignore the warning and see what happens.

:::tip
Do not miss the **footlocker** in this room. It holds, among other things, a special syringe worth
a permanent **+1 Agility**.
:::

## The three companions

Full details on [RPU Companions](/companions/rpu-companions). In brief:

### Kitsune

A Chinese spy who worked for the Americans. Needs **CH 5**, or **CH 2+ with Speech above 80%**.

:::danger[One shot]
Fail the recruitment and she is gone permanently. The same applies if you try to pick her up again
while at **CH 1**.
:::

Ask about her past and she eventually teaches you **+10% Sneak, Lockpick and Steal**. Her
lockpicking exceeds yours and she will use it automatically, as Vic does with Repair.

**Best at:** Unarmed, Melee, Small Guns, Throwing. 80 → 130 HP, 9 AP throughout.

### Cat Jules

Ex-army, reassigned to EPA guard duty, volunteered for human/feline DNA experimentation. A tribute
to *Red Dwarf*.

Ask and he trains you **+10% Small Guns, Big Guns and Energy Weapons**.

**Best at:** Small Guns, Energy Weapons, Unarmed, Melee. 80 → 130 HP, 8 → 10 AP.

:::danger
Same as Kitsune: **CH 1** at recruitment and he vanishes for good.
:::

### Dex

A sociopathic killer according to his file; innocent according to himself. With **negative karma**
he drops the pretence and tells you everything. Good characters never find out.

Ask for tips and he teaches **+15% Speech and Barter**.

**Best at:** Speech (which he cannot help you with), Melee, Unarmed. Limited to unarmed weapons,
knives, pistols and SMGs. 80 → 130 HP, 8 → 9 AP.

:::danger
He angers easily before recruitment — pick your lines carefully.

Female characters can sleep with him beforehand. At **0 or positive karma he kills you
afterwards**.
:::
