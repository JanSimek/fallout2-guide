# Golgotha

> *"Nothing more boring than bein' in a coffin for months."* — Coffin Willie

New Reno's cemetery. **Jules** puts it on your map if you follow the jet conversation.

Digging any grave other than the two legitimate ones costs karma and eventually earns you the
**Grave Digger** reputation, which has no mechanical effect.

:::tip[Selective digging]
Save, dig everything to see what is where, then reload and dig only what you want. Most graves
are not worth the karma.
:::

## The full haul

Digging every grave yields:

$237 · 2 Flower · 3 Knife · **1 Mirrored Shades** · 90 Morningstar Mine Scrip · 1 Leather Jacket ·
2 10mm SMG · 1 Switchblade · 1 Leather Armor · **1 Doctor's Bag** · 1 Empty Hypodermic ·
1 Shovel · 1 Crowbar · 1 Rope · 2 Rot Gut · 1 Shotgun · 20 12ga Shotgun Shells · 1 Gold Tooth ·
**1 Pack of Marked Cards** · 1 Loaded Dice · 2 Gamma Gulp Beer · 1 Roentgen Rum · 1 Spear ·
1 Healing Powder · 1 Metal Armor · 1 Spiked Knuckles

The **Mirrored Shades** are the prize — a permanent **+1 Charisma** while worn, which can mean
another companion. The **marked cards** are what you need to beat Three-Card Monte.

## The two free graves

Neither of these makes you a Grave Digger:

- **Anna Winslow's grave** — the one you refill during
  <Quest>Return Anna's locket</Quest> in the Den.
- **Pretty Boy Lloyd's fallout shelter** — the third grave from the right in the top section, if
  you have talked to Lloyd about it. See [New Reno](/walkthrough/new-reno).

:::caution
Lloyd's directions make no sense and the grave is **not** the one marked "Arch Stanton". Count
from the right.
:::

## The Mordino stash

If you talked to **Myron** about it, the third grave from the top in the **southern** section —
previously empty — now holds:

1 Buffout · 3 Jet · 1 Antidote · 40 .44 Magnum JHP · 1 .44 Magnum Revolver · $171 ·
**1 Gold Nugget**

:::caution
This one **does** make you a Grave Digger. Myron is supposed to point out the grave, but only on
your very first visit to the map, and even then not clearly.
:::

## Two oddities

**Coffin Willie.** Dig the grave at the top left of the bottom section and a ghoul climbs out
muttering. With Lenny in your party it turns out to be his father — and nothing else happens.
This does not make you a Grave Digger.

**The "Trash" cross.** In the right-hand section there is a solitary cross marked *Trash* that
shows the hand icon. If you got the clue from the **Magic 8-Ball**, use it for **$374**.

:::note
A bug means you must also have received the 8-Ball's toilet-grenade clue for this to work, which
in practice is not a problem.
:::
