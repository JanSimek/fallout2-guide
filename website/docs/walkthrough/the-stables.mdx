# The Stables

> *"You should be glad I'm with you to point out all your stupid mistakes."* — Myron

The Mordino jet operation. Despite what Jules and Jagged Jimmy J say about security, you can walk
around opening doors and chatting to guards freely — just do not loot containers where they can
see you.

**Jules**, **Jagged Jimmy J** and **Little Jesus** can all put this on your map.

## Getting in to Myron

Three ways past the door to Myron's room:

- Show a **Stables ID Badge**
- Tell the guards you have an appointment — female characters with **CH 6** or Porn Star status
- **Let a companion walk through the door**, which opens it, and step through without talking to
  anyone

**Getting the ID badge** from **Marjorie Reed**:

| Method | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Talk about jet and Redding | **Science 76%** — 500 XP |
| Charm her | Male, **CH 6** plus Smooth Talker, CH 7 or Speech 66% — 500 XP |
| Steal it | Any Steal skill |

:::caution
You cannot talk to Marjorie with Myron in your party. Drop him off first.
:::

## Myron

Talk to him about jet and get him to join: **750 XP**. You may well want to dismiss him
immediately afterwards — but get [New Reno quest 8](/walkthrough/new-reno) from him first.

:::note
Myron spawns **3 Super Stimpaks, 3 Stimpaks, 2 jet and 1 red condom** the first time you recruit
him, attack him, or make him hand over his inventory. This matters only if you were planning to
sneak in and steal from him without talking.

He is also the only way to learn about the **EPA** without a random encounter — try to dismiss
him and he tells you where it is.
<Vanilla>Myron's EPA dialogue is unvoiced and unreachable; RPU unlocks it.</Vanilla>
:::

:::tip
You can kill Myron in front of the guards **after** recruiting him and they will not react at all.
:::

## The grunting brahmin

In the cattle pen, look for a brahmin with a **different description** that goes about grunting.
Use **Doctor** on it: **100 XP and 50 micro fusion cells**.

:::caution
Fail the skill check **three times** and the cow attacks — and you forfeit the cells permanently,
even if you leave and come back or kill it.
:::

:::note
You can make the brahmin dung disappear by using a shovel on it. This is not useful.
:::

## Afterwards

Once you have killed the Mordinos in New Reno you can come back and clear this place out. You
cannot free the slaves — some of them will fight alongside the guards.

:::caution
Turning the guards here hostile turns the Mordinos in town hostile, and vice versa. The two
guards outside Myron's room are the exception; you can fight them without anyone noticing.
:::
