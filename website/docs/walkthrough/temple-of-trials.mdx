# Temple of Trials

> *"It's a peaceful village. Except for the plants possessed by evil spirits… and of course,
> the temple of trials filled with those deadly spear traps and the man-eating giant ants."*
> — The Chosen One

You start outside the temple with a spear and a tribesmate named Klint.

:::danger Do not attack Klint
There is nothing to gain and a great deal to lose. Attacking Klint — or Cameron — makes the
entire village of Arroyo hostile when you arrive, and that ends the game.
:::

## Level 1 — the entrance hall

Kill the ants, explore the side rooms, and open the second door with whatever miserable
Lockpick skill you have. Loot everything: chests, pots and bones. Take as many scorpion tails
as you can carry — they are your first trade goods.

The experience here is worth having at level 1. Do not rush past it unless you have done the
temple many times before.

:::tip The temple hit-and-run
Attack once, then spend the rest of your AP running. Ants and scorpions can only close two
hexes and still attack, so **three hexes puts you out of reach**. A character with AG 8+ gets
two attacks per cycle instead of one, which makes the whole dungeon noticeably faster.
:::

## Level 2 — the traps

Your character will start spotting floor traps automatically. If nothing happens, walk back
and forth a few times; a very low Perception may need several passes.

- **Disarm them** for 25 XP each, if you have the patience.
- **Avoid them entirely** by hugging the right-hand wall.
- **Set one off** and you can pick up the sharpened pole that nearly went through your chest.

:::note
"You see a raised plate" is the game's universal message for a spotted floor trap. Learn it —
they appear throughout the game, and they rarely appear alone. A critical failure while
disarming reports that you set the trap off; usually it does not actually detonate, but you
cannot try again.
:::

Partway through you need to blow open a door. The explosive charge is in a tall pot nearby.
Watch the trap on the doorstep.

:::tip Setting explosives
Set the timer in your inventory and drop the charge, or put it in an active item slot, close
the inventory and use it from there — which drops it automatically. Even with a terrible Traps
skill the 10-second minimum is enough to get clear, "detonates prematurely" message or not.
:::

## Cameron

The final test. Three ways past him:

| Method | XP | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fight him unarmed | 300 | None |
| Steal his key and unlock the door | 600 | Any Steal skill, and nerve |
| Talk him out of it | 600 | IN 4, Speech **tagged** |

:::caution It is a fight to the death — for you
Cameron says it is not, and he is lying by omission. He will happily land a killing blow on
you at 1 HP. You only need to take 20 HP off him before he stops.
:::

If you somehow kill him, take the key and unlock the door yourself (still 300 XP). The
villagers will not hold it against you.

:::tip
Drop your spear before talking to him and he fights bare-handed too — but drop it well away
from him, or he will pick it up on his first turn and beat you with it. A sharpened pole is an
even better decoy: he does slightly more damage with it but hits less often, and gets two
attacks per turn instead of three.
:::

After the fight, your belongings are in a chest in the previous room. Collect them, then take
the exit grid at the top.

## Should you just run?

Yes, if you have done this before. Sprinting for each exit means fighting six or seven ants
and nothing else, and if you talk your way past Cameron the whole temple takes a couple of
minutes.

On a first playthrough, do it properly. The XP matters at level 1 and the trap-spotting
lesson pays off for the rest of the game.
