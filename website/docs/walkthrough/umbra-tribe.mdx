# Umbra Tribe

:::note[An RPU location]
Restored content, previously known as the Primitive Tribe. None of this exists in the unmodded
game.
:::

Seven quests, a shaman with a ghost problem, and the resolution of the storyline Sulik has been
carrying since Klamath.

:::caution[Sulik will leave your party]
If Sulik is with you when you arrive, he effectively drops out to catch up with old friends.
Wait a day or two before you can ask him to travel with you again.

**Do not leave anything important in his inventory** — you will have to steal or buy it back.
:::

## Quests

### 1. Find out what's haunting the tribe's shaman
Just talk to the shaman to start it.

Go to the **wilderness area to the south at night** and listen to the spirit of the dead girl.
She only appears once the quest is active.

Return to the shaman and help him hold the ceremony: **500 XP**.

This quest gates quest 5, so do it early.

### 2. Collect some weapons for the Tribe's warriors

The **Head Warrior** in the northeast tent — Krom — wants his people armed.

He takes **10 knives and 5 spears**. Sharpened spears are preferred; plain spears make up any
shortfall, so you do not have to sharpen them all.

**300 XP, +15 karma, and +10 tribe reputation.**

:::tip
Knives are cheap and everywhere by this point. If you have been hoarding the combat knives from
Klamath's rat caves and the Den, you may already be carrying the lot.
:::

### 3. Accompany Krom to meet with the trader
**Krom**, in the northeast tent, leads the tribe's warriors. He is off to deal with a shady
trader and wants your worldly judgement.

**Investigate the crate** before deciding. With **PE 7** you notice the stimpaks are fake.

| Outcome | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Spot the fakes and call off the deal | **600 XP, +20 karma** |
| Make the deal | 500 XP, +15 karma |
| Spot the fakes and kill the trader | 300 XP, +15 karma |
| Kill the trader for no reason | 300 XP |

:::note
Female characters with **CH 7** can have a brief romance with Krom before setting out.
:::

### 4. Fix the well for Marli and the tribe
**Marli** stands by the well.

:::caution[Bring a rope]
There is no rope anywhere in this location. Bring one with you or you cannot start.
:::

Pick up the nearby **bucket**. Use the **rope** on the well first, then the **bucket**. Talk to
Marli: **400 XP and $150**.

:::note
If you are carrying a **10mm SMG** when you report in, Marli notices and asks for it. Handing it
over is worth only **50 XP and a Nuka-Cola** — not worth it unless you have a spare. If he gets
it he runs off and does not return until after you have beaten the Enclave.
:::

### 5. Sulik's sister is missing, find her
From Sulik. This is what he has been trying to do since you met him.

Two ways to locate the [Slaver's Camp](/walkthrough/slavers-camp):

- Ask **Metzger** in the Den about his business — **Speech 51%**
- Ask any of the **slaves you freed** from Metzger

Freeing **Kurisu** takes you straight back here. See
[Slaver's Camp](/walkthrough/slavers-camp) for how.

### 6. Find out what happened to the hunting party
Requires quest 1 to be finished, and you may need to wait a day or two before Krom will offer it.

Talk to the guard at the cave and he lets you in. Inside is a **golden gecko** and an injured
hunter, **Serin**.

:::danger[Attack the gecko immediately]
Wait to see what happens and the injured hunter **dies**. You get no XP, and quest 7 becomes
unavailable.
:::

After the fight, listen to Serin's story and get him out:

- **Heal his leg on the spot** with a good Doctor skill
- **Carry him out** if you are strong enough — Sulik can do it if he is with you
- Or leave him and go for help

**600 XP** when you get back to the village map.

### 7. Collect firewood for the tribe's fishermen
The woman in front of the tent in the fishermen's area. There are at least **four piles of
firewood** in the wilderness south of the village. Bring her **three**: **200 XP**, or **$75 and
125 XP** if you take payment.

### 8. Get medicinal plants for the shaman
After you rescue Serin you are placed in front of the shaman's tent. He thanks you; ask if there
is anything else.

He wants **5 xander roots and 3 broc flowers**. If you stockpiled these in Arroyo you already
have them. **100 XP and some healing powder.**
