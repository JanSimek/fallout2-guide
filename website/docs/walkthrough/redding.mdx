# Redding

> *"Why, I hear that tribals eat their damn dead."* / *"How else could we grok their essence?"*
> — Josh and the Chosen One

The second mining town. Redding is a prize being fought over by Vault City, New Reno and NCR, and
your choices here decide who wins it.

## People and places

**Mayor Ascorti** owns the Ace casino and city hall. He sells you the deed to the **Great
Wanamingo Mine** for **$1000** and buys it back for **$2500** once you clear it — plus **1000
XP**.

:::tip
You can kill every wanamingo *before* buying the deed. Ascorti knows immediately, so you can buy
and sell it back in one conversation.
:::

**Painless Doc Johnson** performs **combat implant surgery more cheaply than anyone else in the
game** — assuming you learned about implants in Vault City, and can spare a suit of normal
Combat Armor for each:

| Implant | Redding price | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dermal Impact Armor | $5,000 | 2 days |
| Dermal Impact Assault Enhancement | $30,000 | 5 days |
| Phoenix Armor Implants | $8,000 | 3 days |
| Phoenix Assault Enhancement | **$30,000** (he says $48,000) | 6 days |

:::caution
The implant option does not appear if anyone in your party is hurt or you are irradiated. Get
treated first.
:::

Give him a dose of **Jet Antidote** from Dr. Troy for **2500 XP**. You can charge him **$2000**
for it without losing karma — or give it free for +25 karma.

**Madam Modjeska** south of the saloon explains the politics. A man with a rifle will introduce
you to **wanamingos**.

**The casino cashier** carries a few thousand dollars for barter, replenishing every two weeks.
Gambling needs at least 25% skill to attempt.

:::note[Gambling numbers]
- Slot machines break even at **Gambling 80%**; win too much and the machine shuts down for a day.
- Craps breaks even at **110%**. At **120%** you win slowly but steadily — hold down the 5 key.
  Above that you can make tens of thousands quickly.
- The check is easier at **lower** bets, so 100% works if you press 1 instead of 5.
- You must stand **to one side** of a slot machine to use it.
:::

**The mole rat fights** run from 12:00 to 17:00. You can bet and win about half the time.

:::caution
**Lou** (Malamute saloon) and **Josh** (the restaurant) will not talk to you with non-human
companions in the party — Lenny, Marcus, the Robodog, Goris, Laddie or the Brain Bot. Drop them
off first.
:::

:::note
The graves are all empty. Do not waste a shovel.
:::

## Caravans

The best-paid caravan work in the game, from the mine headquarters in the north:

| Leader | Destination | Pay | Departs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ben Wade | Vault City | $1,000 | 1st of odd months |
| Savinelli | New Reno | $1,500 | 21st of even months |
| Stanwell | NCR | $2,000 | 11th of even months |

Return trips pay the same, with the parity of the months reversed.

**How caravans work:**

- Zero to four encounters per trip. The leader tells you when it is safe to move on
  (*"Let's move out"*). Hostile encounters must be cleared even if nobody attacks you.
- **Leaving the map before the all-clear counts as abandoning the caravan.** The leader is
  effectively dead: no pay, no further jobs from him.
- You cannot bring the car.
- If the leader dies he stays around invisibly to give the all-clear.
- Stealing from or attacking caravan drivers and guards is ignored by the others — but killing
  Stanwell, Wade or Savinelli turns Redding hostile.
- Sometimes you must run off an encounter map twice to make progress.

:::caution[Caravans are buggy]
They can dissolve on the world map for no visible reason, and leaders sometimes fail to appear
or refuse to talk at the destination.

If the leader dies but you complete the trip, a clerk *may* appear and pay a reduced wage. You
can talk to him repeatedly for $50, $100 or $150 each time — he only names the correct figure
when it is $150. There will be no more caravans on that route.
:::

:::note
**New Reno mobsters** must be killed to proceed even though they are flagged as good and are not
hostile. In a **Den caravan** encounter, killing all the slaves drives the hostile counter below
zero and the caravan master never realises the fight is over.
:::

## The underground

Three entrances from the downtown map: the **well** south of Sheriff Marion's, a **manhole in the
Malamute saloon**, and a **manhole at the back of the casino**.

The route runs: a few containers and two wanamingos (do not miss the skeleton) → north to a cave
with ladders up to the well and the opened grave in the mining camp, plus rats and two more
wanamingos → west into wanamingo-infested caves → down a ladder to the main mine level, where
most of them are.

Killing them all clears the mine: **3500 XP**. The elevator west takes you up into the derelict
Wanamingo Mine building, which is also a viable way in.

:::tip[Fighting wanamingos]
Level 12 or so with companions. **Combat shotguns** are excellent; a 10mm SMG barely scratches
their armour. Snipers should take **eye shots** at range and **cripple the legs** up close —
crippling any limb makes them flee, but legs make them bad at it.

You only have to kill all **but one** to clear the mine. The eggs are optional, but leaving them
means more wanamingos later.
:::

:::note[Hidden loot]
A **Metal Armor Mark II** lies in the room northeast of the elevator, to the right of the broken
crate. Easy to miss.

Entering the casino from the manhole and quietly killing the guard behind the metal door (Super
Stimpaks or planted explosives) gets you a fridge and footlocker with several thousand **mine
scrips** plus the store's contents. If the guard sees you and you leave, he shoots on sight when
you return.
:::

## Sheriff Marion's jobs

Marion has hurt his leg and needs a deputy. **You must be level 10** before he considers you.

:::note
You can heal his leg with **Doctor**. He will not notice and will keep complaining about it.
:::

:::tip[A repeatable bug]
As long as you do **not** agree to take the next job, Marion pays your monetary reward **every
time you talk to him**. This works for the eviction and the next two jobs.
:::

Take everything in his house while you are there. The badge he gives you is entirely cosmetic.

### 1. <Quest>Evict the widow</Quest>

**Widow Rooney** is in the building south, next to the well.

| Choice | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Tell her to leave | **1000 XP** and $100 — plus a free beer from Ascorti every time you visit |
| Give her the **$120** she needs | **1500 XP** and $100 |

:::caution
Paying Rooney's debt directly to the mayor gets you **no XP at all**. Give the money to her.
:::

### 2. <Quest>Break up the bar brawl</Quest>

Two miners are squaring off in the bar. Talk to them.

:::caution[You must jail someone]
Talking both parties down does nothing. The patch notes claim that getting Bette to back down
completes the quest; it does not — it sets the same variable as running out the door. There has
never been a peaceful completion.

Do **not** provoke them into an actual fight; the whole town turns on you.
:::

Arrest one or both. **1500 XP and $300.** Which one makes no difference beyond a few lines of
commentary. (Kokoweef is the "good" mine, if you must choose.)

### 3. <Quest>Find out who cut the whore</Quest>

Talk to **Lou** in the Malamute saloon for the clue: a **Morningstar Mine scrip**. Go to the
Morningstar Mine and ask around.

**Obidiah Hakeswill** confesses as soon as you address him:

| Choice | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| **Take him into custody** | **1250 XP and $500** |
| Kill him | $500, no XP |
| Let him go | Nothing |

:::caution
Kill Hakeswill *before* getting the quest and returning to Marion pays $500 and jumps you
straight to the eviction job — meaning you can never get the first two jobs.
:::

### 4. <Quest>Kill Frog Morton</Quest>

Morton's gang occupies the old Wanamingo Mine building to the west, and only appears once you
have this quest — along with **15 mines** scattered through the ruins.

:::tip
Visit the map *before* taking the quest to clear the rats at your leisure. Note that rats will
set the mines off; bandits automatically disarm any they spot.
:::

The gang is heavily armed but comes out in a stream. At level 10–12 with companions you should
manage. Three more men are in the buildings to the south.

**3000 XP** when the gang is dead, plus **$1000** from Marion.

Marion then tells you Frog has three brothers — **Snake, Toad and Newt** — who will come for you
on the world map, one at a time. This is a supply of free weapons and ammunition.

:::tip[Frog's H&K G11]
Excellent at this stage, but ammunition is scarce. Buster in NCR sells some, and there is a
stack in the Toxic Caves. Kill Frog **before he attacks** and you inherit 100 rounds — enough to
clear the wanamingo mine with.
:::

### 5. <Quest>Clear Wanamingo Mine</Quest>

See [The underground](#the-underground). **3500 XP**, plus the deed transaction with Ascorti.

### 6. <Quest>Find the excavator chip</Quest>

There are **two** chips, both on the lowest level of the mine among the toughest wanamingos: one
in the excavator itself, and one on the ground to the northeast beside a piece of machinery.

You can only sell one. Take it to **Dan McGrew** or **Marge LeBarge**: **$1000 and 2500 XP**.

:::note[Which buyer?]
It only affects Redding's ending. **Dan supports New Reno, Marge supports NCR** — though the
game is inconsistent about this. Jimmy Hoffa has them the wrong way round, and if you sell to
McGrew, LeBarge complains about "those prissy NCR bastards" McGrew is in league with.
:::

## Fannie Mae

RPU adds a resolution to a character the original game left hanging.
<Vanilla>Fannie's jet addiction cannot be cured or worsened — the whole storyline is a dead
end.</Vanilla>

:::caution[Male characters only]
:::

**Fannie Mae** works at the Malamute Saloon. Pay for her company (**$60**) and she tells you a
sad story and asks for more money to clear a debt. Give her the **$200**, and **Lou** tells you
that you are not helping — Fannie is a jet addict.

**To cure her:** get the **Jet Antidote** from Dr. Troy in Vault City (or San Francisco) and ask
Lou to slip it into Fannie's drink. **1000 XP.**

**To kill her:** follow the same steps, then talk to her again. She asks for another **$150**.
Give it to her and she eventually overdoses. Leave the map and come back and she is gone. Lou
calls you an "idjit" and nothing else happens.

## RPU changes worth knowing

- **The mine support beams are destructible.** Explosives will bring sections down.
  <Vanilla>The beams are ordinary scenery.</Vanilla>
- **Killing all the miners prevents Redding's good ending**, regardless of what else you do.
