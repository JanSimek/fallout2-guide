# Abbey

:::note[An RPU location]
The Abbey of the Book was cut from the original game and restored by the Restoration Project.
None of this exists in the unmodded game.
:::

A monastery of monks preserving pre-war knowledge, sitting on a large library and a rather less
scholarly basement.

## Getting here

**Father Tully** in New Reno tells you about it and puts it on your map — and gives you quest 5.
You can also stumble on it by exploring, but quest 5 requires Tully.

## Quests

### 1. Kill the beast beneath the church
Ask the **head monk** on the third floor about a **G.E.C.K.**. He sends you to find **Brother
Thomas** and a missing book in the basement.

The basement holds mutated rats, several radscorpions, and a **large deathclaw**. Fight through to
Thomas's body and the book: **500 XP** plus kills.

Take the book to the head monk: **200 XP** — or **$250 and no XP** if you sell it to him. **Brother
Paul** then gives you the **computer access card**, which you need for quest 7.

:::danger[Have the book with you]
The head monk only asks for it **once**. Killing the deathclaw alone gets you the access card and
50 XP, but the book becomes useless.
:::

:::tip
Talk to the Abbot about the purpose of the order and follow that thread. With **Speech above 90%**
you can convince him to use their knowledge to help people: **200 XP**.
:::

### 2. Deliver the machine part to Brother Max
**Elaine**, the stout nun on the second floor, gives you this. **Max** is in the copying room in
the **east section of the basement** — the entrance is the door in the floor behind the man
preaching in the large chapel.

**100 XP and a Scout Handbook.**

### 3. Repair the water pump and report the status to the head monk
Talk to the head monk or **Dave** the merchant. Examine the pump to discover it needs a motor:
**100 XP**.

Dave says **Happy Harry in Vault City** has one, for **$500**. Bring it back and he asks whether he
should fix the pump or you should:

| Who fixes it | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Dave | 300 |
| **You** | **800** |

To do it yourself, use the motor on the pump first. You then get a message about needing tools —
**pliers or a Super Tool Kit** work; a **wrench does not**. There are pliers on the bookcase
nearest the door in **Trader Bill's shop**.

Report to the head monk: another **50 XP**.

### 4. Find out who is digging up the graves
**Samuel** the gravekeeper gives you this along with a clue. Talk to the farmer, **Mr. Peterson**,
to the south.

Wait until **midnight** and talk to the old man wandering the graveyard with a shovel. **Tom** is
poor enough that he steals food and robs graves to live. Tell him to ask Mr. Peterson for help:
**1000 XP**.

Talk to Tom again and he gives you a **lighter** — which you need for quest 6.

### 5. Inform Brother Thomas and Father Tully about the whereabouts of each other
Back in New Reno, tell **Father Tully** that Samuel is fine: **750 XP**.

Bring him a bottle of **Abbey wine** — from one of the monks in the west basement, or from Hank in
quest 6 — for another **500 XP**.

### 6. Bring a lighter to Brother Hank
**Hank** is the moonshiner in the west basement.

Lighters: **Officer Darrow** in Vault City Amenities "A" sells one, one of **Becky's male guards**
in the Den has one to steal, and **Tom** gives you one after quest 4.

No XP, but Hank gives you a free bottle of **Abbey wine** for Father Tully.

### 7. Collect information about botany for Brother Paul
**Brother Paul** — the man in the grey robe in the second floor library — asks for botany research.
You need **Science 70%** to get the quest.

The data is on the **botany terminal on the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa) Green Level**. Copy it to a
**holodisk**.

Bring it back and he tells you to upload it to the main Abbey computer. Log on with the **access
card** from quest 1 and choose **"add information"**: **750 XP**.

:::caution
You need the access card, which means **quest 1 must be done first**.
:::

### 8. Escort Brother Jason to Gecko
**Jason** is outside, between the church and the cemetery — a *Tragic: The Gathering* addict who is
unhappy here. If you have been to Gecko, offer to take him there: **800 XP and a Stealth Boy**.

### 9. Format the Abbey's book database
Also from Jason, and **mutually exclusive with quest 8**. He believes wiping the database will
force the order to change.

Requires the access card from quest 1. **500 XP and −5 karma.**

:::caution
Do not go back to Jason afterwards — he gives no reward and will "pretend" to be hostile so nobody
connects him to it.
:::

Quest 8 is worth more XP and a Stealth Boy. Take that one.

## Other things to do

**Brother John** on the second floor has a bad heart. Give him **[heart pills](/walkthrough/epa)**
from Mr. Chemmie and he gives you a **Scout Handbook** — or charge him **$400**. Neither affects
karma or reputation, and the other monks do not care.

**Jack the healer** is in the lower left corner. He heals you (not companions). Ask him for
medical supplies with **CH 8 and Speech 101%** and you get a **paramedics bag and a field medic
kit**.

:::note
A low-Intelligence character can only drool at Jack. Keep it up and he literally melts. **200 XP.**
:::

## Armour for Marcus

With the **NPC Armor** mod installed, talk to **Trader Bill** and ask whether he has found anything
special. He sells you **metal armor for Marcus** for **$3000** — but only with Marcus in your party.

Tell Marcus to wear it **through dialogue**, not with the "wear best armor" button.

Fully levelled and armoured, Marcus is better protected than combat armor, though short of power
armor.
