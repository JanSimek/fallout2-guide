# Vault Village

:::note An RPU location
The village outside Vault City is restored content. None of this exists in the unmodded game —
Sergeant Stark simply has nothing to say about a village.
:::

**Sgt. Stark** puts this on your map once you have scouted around Gecko and reached NCR, asking
you to exterminate the mole rats plaguing it.

Do that and you get **800 XP and $750** from Stark. It is also the worst possible outcome. The
mole rats are the **only** reason Vault City has not already invaded and enslaved the villagers.

:::caution One month
Vault City moves in a month after Stark gives you the quest. The clock is real.
:::

## Quests

### 1. <Quest rpu>Get weapons for Connar to be used at the nearby village</Quest>

**Connar** asks you to arm the village so they can defend themselves, and hands you **$5000**
up front. He sends you to **Joe**, near Cassidy's bar in Vault City, who sends you to
**Happy Harry**.

The price depends entirely on Speech:

| Speech | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Low | $10,000 — you are out of pocket |
| ~50% | Around $5000 — you break even |
| 80%+ | **$2000** |

You get more than one attempt, so keep trying until you are happy with the number. Bring the
guns back to Connar: **800 XP**.

Stark's raid then fails. You can **rat Happy Harry out to Stark** for selling weapons — he is
sent to the detention center in the Courtyard, and you get to help yourself to everything in his
shop.

:::tip Already carrying guns?
If you happen to have suitable weapons the first time you talk to Connar, hand them over
immediately. You keep the **$5000** and get the same XP, and a small Speech roll squeezes another
**$1000** out of him.
:::

:::note
Pretend you know **Joe** when you first speak to him and he gives you a **10mm pistol**.
:::

### The better solution — talk to McClure

Arming the village is not the best ending. Talk to **Joe** and he refers you to **Councilman
McClure**, and mentions that ghouls may be behind the mole rat problem. Ask **Connar** about the
rats and he says the same, and offers you one of the **amulets**.

1. Tell **McClure** you think you can stop the mole rats by finding who controls them. He
   postpones the raid by **an extra month**.
2. Go to the [Gecko](/walkthrough/gecko) junkyard, down the manhole, and find **The Brain**.
3. Do him a favour — repairing or optimizing the power plant, depending on what you have already
   done. If you have done both already, no favour is needed.
4. He calls off the mole rat attacks: **500 XP**.
5. Report back to **McClure**. He agrees to leave the village alone, which simultaneously
   completes Vault City's <Quest rpu>Resolve the problem with the nearby village</Quest>:
   **1000 XP and +15 karma**.
6. Return to **Connar**: **1200 XP and +25 karma**.

:::tip Take the money first
If you accept Connar's $5000 and then solve it McClure's way instead, you keep **half** the
money — or all of it, if you are that sort of person. Doing it McClure's way *without* taking
the money first gives you no extra XP, so there is no reason to be principled about it.
:::

:::caution
If you have already met McClure before first speaking to Connar, you get the option to suggest
a deal immediately. Same solution, same XP, but **no money**.
:::

### 2. <Quest rpu>Put the village on a trade route</Quest>

:::caution
Only available if you solved quest 1 by **negotiating with McClure**, and only after at least
**one month** has passed since you finished it.
:::

Connar's wife **Ann** says the village needs to be on a trade route to survive. She wants you to
persuade trader **Robert Baron**. Any of:

- Talk him into it — easy Speech roll
- Pay **$500** with an easy Barter roll, or **$1000** if you fail it
- Intimidate him — **ST 7**; below that he turns hostile
- Offer Miria, if you are married; or yourself, as a female character with **CH 7**

Report to Ann: **800 XP**, or **$100 and 400 XP** if you ask to be paid.
