# Sierra Army Depot

> *"Is that a REAL robot?"* / *"Yeah, but don't bother it. I think it wants to destroy the human
> race."* — Wright kid and The Chosen One

Four levels of abandoned military base, a great deal of high-end equipment, and the only place to
build **Skynet**.

:::caution[The only way here]
There is no way to get the SAD on your world map except by taking the quest from **Orville
Wright** in New Reno.
:::

## Getting in

The surface is a mess of bodies and **turrets**. Leave your companions at the exit grid, take a
scoped hunting rifle, and pick the turrets off from outside their range.

**Surface loot:** 1 Plasma Grenade · 3 Frag Grenade · 2 Desert Eagle .44 · 2 10mm SMG ·
2 10mm Pistol · 1 Assault Rifle · 24 10mm AP · **1 Sniper Rifle**

**The howitzer:** to the northwest is a bunker with **three traps outside, one on the door, and
one on the locked ammo crate inside**. Take the **Howitzer Shell** and use it on the howitzer in
the building to the right — which has **three mines in front of the door, one in the gap by the
truck, and one near the barrels and tyres**.

Fire the howitzer at the depot entrance to blow it open.

:::caution
Any companion standing by the doors takes damage from the blast.
:::

:::note[Do not use the backup power switch]
The fenced area in the northeast does electrical damage, and the manhole below has a switch that
puts the base on backup power. This turns the impassable **yellow** forcefields into **red** ones
that damage you as you pass. You can disable yellow fields painlessly with **Repair**, so this is
purely a downgrade — and it makes the base darker.
:::

## Forcefields and alarms

**Every forcefield in the depot can be disabled with Repair.** You need **41% minimum** to have
any chance; a Tool helps, a Super Tool Kit does not.

:::danger[You can get permanently stuck]
Take an elevator without an eye, with poor Repair and no explosives, and you may be trapped
forever. Keep **Repair above 41%** and carry explosives before you go down.
:::

Each level has its **own** alarm state. The alarm triggers if you:

- Enter two incorrect passwords at the level 1 password computer
- Fail to enable or disable a specific forcefield more than twice
- Use **your own eye** on a retinal scanner

:::tip[Robot combat]
**Aimed shots to the sensors** take all the fight out of robots and turrets.
:::

:::note[Free XP from dormant robots]
Use **Repair or a Tool** on each dormant bot: **200 XP** for "You seem to have disabled the robot",
**75 XP** for "You seem to have repaired the robot completely". Works on every bot in the depot
except the passive ones in the repair bay.
:::

## Level 1

Once inside you can report back to Orville Wright.

- Take the **Password Paper** from the desk (and the Evacuation Holodisk, which is flavour only —
  all holodisks here are).
- Access the computer: enter the correct password to **deactivate every forcefield on the level**.
  Or, with some Science, enter a **wrong** password deliberately and then hack it: **200 XP**.
- Search the rooms to the right for a **Yellow Memory Module**, **Dixon's Eye** and a
  **Bio Med Gel**. Do not miss the wall lockers.
- The barracks lockers hold **Combat Armor**.
- **The gym punching bags give +5% Unarmed each** (4% if tagged).

:::tip
Save the punching bags until after you have had **San Francisco's** unarmed training, so you do
not waste the bonus against a lower cap.
:::

- The second computer gives **250 XP** for "hacking": choose the diagnostic option, then decline
  to run the test. With the alarm active you need **IN 8 and decent Science**, or **PE 7** and
  the help option.

:::caution
Actually running the diagnostic test locks the terminal. Failing a hack attempt locks you out of
**both** this terminal and the level 2 alarm terminal permanently.
:::

**Releasing the robots** (two wrong passwords) brings a squad out from behind sliding panels. Each
**riflebot** carries a **Robot Motivator** — you need one of these for Skynet, so this is the
easiest place to get one.

**The elevators:** the right one opens with **Dixon's Eye** and serves levels 2 and 3. The left
one can simply be **lockpicked** and serves all four levels.

## Level 2

- The computer beside the left elevator turns off the automated repair system: **200 XP**
  (Science check, or **PE 7 and LK 8**). A critical failure disables the terminal.

:::note
Leaving the repair system on does nothing — it relates to an unimplemented feature.

RPU changes this: the **repair bay now works**, repairing damaged robots and replacing destroyed
ones with new ones, on any level of the depot.
<Vanilla>The repair bay is inert.</Vanilla>
:::

- **The room to the south** (Lockpick 100% recommended) holds the serious loot: a **Plasma Rifle**,
  **2 Combat Armors**, and every ammo crate worth opening. Do not miss the row of small crates
  behind the wall.

:::tip[8250 free XP]
The bots in that room — with one exception — **do not fight back** when attacked, and killing them
does not alert the level. The exception is the **rocketbot closest to the yellow forcefield**.

Save first: your companions will not fire on the active bot deliberately, but they may clip it
while burst-firing at something behind it.
:::

- The **alarm computer** in the middle: if you set the alarm off, you can turn it off twice — via
  "hack" (**500 XP**) and "attempt to turn off" (**550 XP**). Hacking needs **IN 8** and
  **Science 80–90%**; at **106%** it is automatic. **PE 7** lets you run the self-diagnostic to
  disable it, but with no XP.
- The **bottom-right computer** runs the shock plates and the level's forcefields, each with its
  own hack, and both of them work. **Science 90%** succeeds automatically; below that it is a roll
  at **−40**, and a critical failure kills the terminal for good and costs you **20 damage**. Each
  hack pays **200 XP** and is offered only once, so the terminal is worth **400 XP** altogether.
  Seeing the shock-plate option needs **IN 8**; the forcefield one does not. Guessing the password
  is a Luck check that turns either system off and pays nothing.
  <Vanilla>Hacking the forcefields left them standing.</Vanilla>

:::danger
Reading the **"penpal greetings"** mail infects the computer with a virus and renders it
permanently inoperable.
:::

- The **laser tripwire** does nothing, but the passages either side of it are lined with traps.
- Near the top of the level is a **hidden searchable crate** with rockets and grenades.
- The middle-right room holds a **cookie**.

:::note
RPU makes the **trip wire work as originally intended**.
<Vanilla>The trip wire is decorative.</Vanilla>
:::

## Level 3

You need **Clifton's Eye** for the new elevator — it is in the **wall locker in the far northeast
room**, and it also grants clearance for the other elevator.

:::danger[The alarm computer here is different]
**Do not tell it you are Dixon.** The robots on levels 1–3 turn hostile, and all four alarm
computers plus the level 1 password computer will shock you if you touch them again.

Tell it your **name**, or that you are a **friend**, to turn off this level's alarm.
:::

Two traps in the left branch of the T-junction.

### Skynet

Access the terminal by the large mainframe. **Skynet** asks for help escaping. You can ask it to
**reset the security systems**, which returns hostile robots to dormant.

:::caution
Choosing **"It's nice to want"** angers it, with the same consequences as claiming to be Dixon —
unless you have **Speech 76%** and apologise.

Anger Skynet, then talk to it again and agree to help, and it turns off the level 3 alarm while
turning **on** the alarms for levels 1–2, to keep you here.
:::

### Building the robot

The unfinished project is at the top left. Three components:

1. **Robot Motivator** — from any riflebot; easiest on level 1
2. **Bio Med Gel** — there is one on the floor right there, and another on level 1
3. **A Brain** — from level 4

**500 XP** for installing the brain, whichever one you use.

:::danger
Activating the robot before it is complete makes it **explode**.
:::

| Brain | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Abnormal Brain** | A pack mule. You have to kill it to get rid of it. |
| **Chimpanzee Brain** | Also a pack mule, but you can tell it to wait somewhere. |
| **Human Brain** | A mediocre sniper. Cannot level up. |
| **Cybernetic Brain** | The real Skynet: auto-repair, levels up, genuinely good. |

:::danger[Angry Skynet]
Assemble the bot with the **Cybernetic Brain** after angering Skynet and it is *meant* to turn on
you the moment you step out onto the depot surface, killing whichever companion is nearest with
1000 points of explosion damage. It never does.

The routine is broken twice over. It asks for the closest party member from a list that includes
the robot itself, so it always picks itself; and it then measures the distance with a tile *number*
where the engine wants an object. The interpreter refuses the second one before the call even
happens, and the error kills the whole procedure:

```
Error during execution: pointer expected, got c001
Current script: scripts\WCBrnBot.int, procedure Kill_The_Party
```

`c001` is the engine's tag for an integer. Because the script aborts there, nothing further in it
runs — not the 1000 damage, not the branch that would have turned the bot hostile, not even the
retry timer. It fails once as you arrive and is not heard from again. Played through: Skynet
angered, the bot built with the Cybernetic Brain and recruited, then out onto the surface — the
party took no damage at all, and thirty seconds of walking about produced no further attempt.

Assemble the bot **before talking to Skynet at all** and it is friendly, even if the level 3 alarm
is active.
:::

:::caution
Once you extract Skynet, the alarm computers and the level 1 password computer stop working.
Collect any XP from them first.
:::

## Level 4

The alarm computer here is offline, and the robots do not turn hostile unless attacked.

### The organ extractor

Whatever you pick on the organ dial, it only extracts **brains**.

Set the **species** dial, get somebody into the extraction chamber and tell the computer to extract.
Everything alive in the chamber dies — **in your party or not** — and every corpse matching the
species leaves one brain. **Human** gives a Human Brain; **Canine** and the scratched-in **Mutant**
and **Deathclaw** give an Abnormal Brain; Feline, Road Runner and Rodent give none. Two matching
victims give two brains.

**500 XP**, once, for the first extraction that actually has a victim in the chamber.

:::danger
**−100 karma per victim**, whether or not it produced a brain. An empty chamber costs nothing and
gives nothing.

Dogs work: Laddie, K-9, the Robodog, Dogmeat and the pariah dog all match **Canine** and leave an
Abnormal Brain. The Brain Bot dies as readily as anyone else, but robots match no species, so it
costs you the karma and gives you nothing.

Stand in the chamber yourself and you die too.
:::

### Storage

Use the computer, choose **"cadavers"** and **Private Dobbs**. Talk to him and watch. You can pick
up a **LE Red Ryder BB Gun** from his remains — but not if you kill him before he melts. If he
does not run, shut down the forcefield first.

**Getting a brain from storage** is much better than the extractor. Your Science skill determines
how many brains you are offered (zero to four):

| Target | Science |
| --- | --- |
| Human Brain ~50% of the time | 90% |
| **Cybernetic Brain** | **121%** — and still a small failure chance, so save |

:::caution
Retrieving a brain destroys the storage retrieval bot, so **get Dobbs first**.
:::

:::danger[The virus]
Retrieve **any** virus and the vial breaks. Contamination then spreads one level per game minute and
kills you the moment it reaches the level you are standing on, so you have between **two and four
minutes** depending on how deep you are. The on-screen countdown always runs to the four-minute mark,
so it overstates your time everywhere but the entrance level. Even if you escape, you die instantly if
you ever re-enter.

RPU adds a consequence: **contaminating the SAD prevents the Wright family from taking over New
Reno by force.** They can still do it peacefully.
<Vanilla>Contaminating the base has no effect on the endings.</Vanilla>

If you want **Skynet** as well, extract the virus **before** the brain — the robot explodes after
fetching the brain. After the vial breaks you have just enough time to assemble Skynet and run.
:::
