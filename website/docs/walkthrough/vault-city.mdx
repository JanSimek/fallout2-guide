# Vault City

> *"PING! I Say!"* — Vault City medical terminal

A segregated city: a shabby **Courtyard** outside and a tidy inner city that will not let you
in. It is not a nice place. That is not a reason to start shooting.

Vault City is the hub of the midgame. Almost every thread here — Gecko, the raiders, NCR, New
Reno, Redding — runs through somebody in this town.

## The Courtyard

**Cassidy** owns the bar. Talk to him about life in Vault City and he joins you: **300 XP** and
a genuinely excellent rifle shot. He has a heart condition — never use Buffout on him, though
saving first and trying it once is educational.
<Vanilla>Cassidy has no talking head in the original game. RPU restores one, well voiced.</Vanilla>

**Ed** lives by the brahmin pen. If you have talked to Vic about him and show him the Vault 13
flask, he puts **the Den, Modoc, Broken Hills, Redding and New Reno** on your world map. **500
XP.**

**Happy Harry** runs a shop, restocking every 3 days.

:::tip
Barter with Harry once — you need not buy anything — and a batch of his stock is dumped into
his *stealable* inventory. He only does this once, but it stays there until you take it.
:::

**Curtis**, south of the bar, has lost his doll. It is behind a wall just to the north. Return
it or rip it up — **100 XP** either way — but if you give it back, listen to his "dialogue" and
then dig up the **wrench** from under a pile of rocks a little further along. You need it
shortly.

:::caution
Curtis is one of the three children who increment the child-kill counter **twice**. Be careful
with area weapons near him.
:::

**Ben Wade** in the Greeting Office runs a medical caravan to Redding on the 1st of every even
month and pays **$1000** for an escort. There is also a locker there with what is probably your
first **geiger counter**; the Greeting Office lockers can be looted in broad daylight.

## Getting inside

You need a Day Pass or citizenship papers. **Wallace** at the Customs Office is the
gatekeeper. Options, in rough order of quality:

| Method | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy False Citizenship Papers from Skeev** | $200, or $150 at Barter 50% — then threaten to expose him and he gives you **$300 back**. You end up ahead. Best option. |
| Talk Wallace round | **Speech 75% or IN 7**. Pick the long, eloquent lines. |
| Wear only your vault suit | Or the Bridgekeeper's Robes. Automatic with IN under 4. |
| Offer to find the raider base | Do it, come back, get a pass. |
| Claim to be a trader | Show gecko pelts, uranium ore or a gold nugget — or claim you are about to sell your companions. Then pay **$500**. |
| Mention Redding's jet problem | If you know why the shipments slowed. |
| Steal the Day Pass | From the Customs Office locker at night, or standing where Wallace cannot see you. |
| Pickpocket Wallace | Straightforward. |

:::note[What each gets you]
- **Day Pass** — daytime only, no vault, no Randal's shop, and you are **searched for drugs and
  alcohol** (Buffout, Psycho, Mentats, Jet, and all five alcohols). Some citizens throw you out
  after 6 p.m.
- **False Citizenship Papers** — no search, Randal's shop works, but still no vault. Once you
  have shown them to the gate guards and Randal you never need them again.
- **Citizenship** — everything.
:::

:::caution
Do not show **Sergeant Stark** or **Lynette** the false papers. Either will expel you from Vault
City permanently. Lynette will *promise* you a Day Pass for naming your supplier. She is lying.

Using the papers, never refer to the First Citizen as "he", and do not mention "all the vaults".
:::

:::caution[Who you can bring inside]
**Marcus and Lenny** are refused entry unless you are Captain of the Guard, and bringing them
in anyway turns the city hostile. **Goris** walks in without comment — apparently the cloak is
convincing.
:::

## The doctor and the Auto-Doc

**"Doctor" Andrew** in the Courtyard charges $225–$500 for healing. His **Auto-Doc malfunctions
50% of the time**, taking your money and giving nothing.

**Repair it for 100 XP.** Do this.

:::danger
Buy an implant without repairing the Auto-Doc first and **you die**.
:::

He also removes the **two extra toes** from the Toxic Caves for $500 ($300 discounted), giving
you a **Mutated Toe** — which has a use at the very end of the game.

:::note
Until the Auto-Doc is repaired, the toes grow back after 30 days, and you can farm mutated toes
indefinitely. Do not *use* one on yourself or a companion; it lowers maximum HP for a while.
:::

## Inside the city

**The Information Center** — lament the death of books (**IN 7** or the Comprehension perk) for
a free **Dean's Electronics** and **Big Book of Science**.

**Randal's Amenities** is one of the best shops in the game: books, stimpaks at ~$200,
Leather Armor Mark II at $1000, and a **combat shotgun for ~$3000** against roughly $6500 in
NCR. Restocks every 3 days.

:::caution
**Ask Randal to trade** — do not press the barter button. Pressing barter skips the barter
modifier and you pay more. Several shopkeepers work this way.
:::

**The Servant Allocation Center** buys companions: Miria $250, Davin $200, Sulik $100, Vic $100
— doubled on a successful Barter check. Barkus bids on only one companion at a time, in that
order. Their equipment ends up in the footlocker next door.

**The Corrections Center** holds **Citizen Sergeant Stark**, who gives you quests 9 and 10 and
tells you about the raiders.

:::danger
Once you mention the raiders to Stark you **must** offer to go find them, or he accuses you of
being one and attacks.
:::

There is a free suit of **metal armor** in the second locker from the top — stand behind it
relative to the nearby guard.

**The Central Council** holds three people:

- **Gregory** administers the citizenship test. Passing outright needs **PE 9, IN 9 and LK 9**,
  and the physical examination disqualifies you if you have grown the extra toes. **1000 XP**.
- **McClure** is the only sane official in the building. Go to him when you want something done.
- **Lynette**, First Citizen, is the reason everyone remembers Vault City. Get quest 4 from her
  and then avoid her.

Both McClure and Lynette can put **Gecko** on your map.

:::tip[The Parlor Room]
Order alcohol-Z repeatedly — hold down the 2 key. After **100 drinks** (~$2000, and each heals
3 HP) you may gain a permanent perk based on Luck:

| Luck | Perk |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Alcohol Lowered HP II (−4 HP) |
| 2 | Alcohol Lowered HP (−2 HP) |
| 9 | Alcohol Raised HP (+2 HP) |
| 10 | Alcohol Raised HP II (+4 HP) |
:::

## The vault

:::tip[Sneaking in]
Move close to the vault opening, enter combat mode, and run onto the exit grid. The guards do
not object.
:::

### Level 1 — medical

**Dr. Troy** offers free healing. With **Doctor 75%** you can talk medicine with him for the
**Vault City Training** perk (+5% Doctor, +5% First Aid) — but see quest 3 first. Once you have
Training, come here for poison or radiation treatment and you can ask for **Vault City
Inoculations** (+10% poison and radiation resistance).

**Nurse Phyllis** is worth several conversations:

| Path | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Convince her to see the outside world ("similarity of citizens") | 300 XP |
| Get her to raise sterility with the doctor ("no children") — needs **Doctor 75% and PE 6** | 300 XP |
| Date her — male only, **CH 6** plus one of Sex Appeal, Kama Sutra Master, CH 9 or Speech 75% | 100 XP |
| Donate a sperm sample — male only, in the "no children" thread | 50 XP at IN 4, 250 at IN 5–6, **500 at IN 7+**. Ask for a magazine and you get a **Cat's Paw**. |

**The medical database** — use Science on it (needs **Science 51%**, or Doctor 76%) with
**Doctor 75%** for **500 XP** and the information on combat implants. Then keep searching:
22 more times for a new message, 30 more after that for another, then 2 more for a **secret
message**.

:::note[Using computers]
You generally need **Science 51%** and **IN 4** to use a terminal at all. Usable computers are
almost always named something specific when you hover over them — "Vault City medical
terminal" — rather than just "computer".
:::

### Level 2

Pick the doors (**Lockpick 80%**) and loot the footlockers. Take the **Computer Voice Module**.
The crates hold only water chips, which are genuinely not worth taking.

Two doors need **ST 8** — or ST 7 with a crowbar. Behind one is the **Red Memory Module**,
which raises Strength much later. Buffout will get you through if you are short; just do not
get addicted.

**Repair the rattling vent** for **100 XP and 50 micro fusion cells**. You need **PE 6** to
notice it, or go to level 3 and wait for the central computer to announce an obstruction, then
come back.

:::note
RPU adds material here about the **vault experiments** — background that pays off in
[Vault 13](/walkthrough/vault-13).
<Vanilla>The original game never explains what Vault City's vault was for.</Vanilla>
:::

### Level 3

Loot the **locker room** and the **armoury**. Use Science on the lowermost computer in the
leftmost room: **350 XP**.

**Martin** is usually here, singing "Maybe". It is a good song; do not tell him to stop.
Complimenting his singing gives **+1 karma every time**, indefinitely.

:::danger[The armoury trap]
If you entered the vault illegally, the armoury door can trap you inside permanently. The door
checks whether *it* sees you, not whether Martin does, so killing or scaring him does not help.
Sneak mode lets you pick and open it — but not get out. Companions pass through it like ghosts
and cannot help.

Being an actual citizen avoids this entirely. So does coming when Martin is on level 2 (between
2 a.m. and 7 a.m.). The armoury holds a free 10mm SMG, grenades and ammunition — nothing worth
the risk.
:::

**The central computer** is the talking one on the right. Using it puts **Vault 15** on your
map. With **PE 7** you will notice a slot for your Pip-Boy when you log off; plugging in gives
you a **Vault City Travel Log** and adds **New Reno, Redding, Broken Hills, NCR and Vault 15**
to your world map.

## Quests

### 1. Resolve the problem with the nearby village
RPU's addition, and the entry point to [Vault Village](/walkthrough/vault-village).
<Vanilla>The village does not exist in the original game.</Vanilla>

After you finish scouting around Gecko and the route to NCR, **Sgt. Stark** puts the village on
your map and asks you to exterminate the mole rats.

- **Kill all the mole rats** and report to Stark: **800 XP** plus kills, and **$750**.
- **Talk to a resident** first and you learn the mole rats are the only thing keeping Vault City
  from invading and enslaving them. The peaceful solution is worth **1000 XP** and is covered
  under [Vault Village](/walkthrough/vault-village).

:::caution[One month deadline]
Deal with it within a month or Vault City enslaves the villagers and the quest is struck off.
:::

### 2. Get a plow for Mr. Smith
Smith is in a tent in the north Courtyard. Buy the plow from Harry — **$800**, or $600 at Barter
51% — and give it to him: **250 XP, a Desert Eagle .44 and 20 .44 Magnum JHP**.

:::caution
You can ask Smith for $150 up front, but then you forfeit the gun. Do not.
:::

### 3. Deliver a sample of jet to Dr. Troy
Fiddly to start. You need **Doctor 75%** to qualify for the medical tour. Then either:

- Tell him you are something of a doctor and try to **leave the conversation** when he offers
  the tour, or
- Take the tour and leave conversation **immediately afterwards**.

Either way he stops you and asks you to smuggle jet into the vault. Give it to him for **$1000**
— or ask **$1200** at Barter 75%, **$1500** at Barter 95%, if you already have jet on you.

Return in **three months** for two doses of **Jet Antidote**, which go to Doc Johnson in
[Redding](/walkthrough/redding).

:::tip[Cut the wait, and get 750 XP]
Talk to **Myron** about jet production first, then when handing over the jet start the thread
beginning *"you don't want to inhale that crap"*. The antidote takes **one month** instead of
three, and you get **750 XP**.

You cannot collect that XP if the New Reno endorphin blocker quest is already active — so do
this **before** taking that quest from Myron.
:::

:::caution[Order of operations]
You will not get this quest at all if you have already told Troy about the endorphin blockers
after talking to Myron. If you tell Troy you are Captain of the Guard, he sensibly does not ask
an officer to smuggle drugs.
:::

**Alternative:** blackmail Troy for **$500/month** ($1000 at Barter 75%; press for more and he
calls the guards). If you do, never accept medical treatment from him again — it is fatal, and
the dialogue is very funny.

### 4. Solve the Gecko powerplant problem
Talk to **Lynette** about citizenship until she mentions doing Vault City a special favour.
Continued in [Gecko](/walkthrough/gecko).

:::caution[Report to McClure, not Lynette]
- Tell Lynette you **destroyed** the plant and she is unhappy.
- Tell her you **fixed** it and she is also unhappy; talk back and she bans you from the city.
- Tell her you "took care of the problem" without elaborating and she accepts it — but you get
  no reward.

**Tell McClure you fixed it** and he makes you a **citizen of Vault City**. Then go and tease
Lynette.
:::

### 5. Deliver Moore's briefcase to Mr. Bishop in New Reno
**Thomas Moore** preaches in the inner city. Play along and he gives you a briefcase for
New Reno, which appears on your map. **500 XP and $250** on delivery.

:::danger
Do not ask him for payment and do not threaten to turn him in. He slips a note into the case
telling Bishop to kill you on arrival.
:::

:::note
You **must** take this quest to do work for Bishop later. Slavers can get it with **CH 9 or
Speech 96%**.

Afterwards you learn Moore is an NCR agent, and you can turn him in to Lynette for **500 XP** at
no karma cost. Just do not mention brahmin maulings.
:::

### 6. Deliver Lynette's holodisk to Westin in NCR
Kill the raiders, bring **Bishop's Holodisk** from New Reno to Lynette: **2500 XP** and this
quest. Deliver to Westin in [NCR](/walkthrough/ncr) for **2000 XP**. Come back without the
Westin Holodisk and you get **$1000**.

### 7. Rescue Amanda's husband, Joshua
**Amanda** is in the eastern corner of the Courtyard; **Joshua** is in the Servant Allocation
Center. Talk to **Barkus**:

- Bribe him — $250 to $1000 depending on Barter
- Invent a mysterious disease — **Speech 80% or Doctor 75%**
- As a citizen: claim he is a bad influence or possibly infected — **Speech 75%**
- As Captain: simply order it

**500 XP**, plus **$32** from Amanda.

:::caution
Not available to Slavers, Childkillers, or anyone with karma below 0. And **get the wrench from
Curtis first** — Joshua's family leave the map once you collect your reward.
:::

### 8. Deliver beer and booze (10 each) to Lydia
**Lydia** the bartender wants 10 beer and 10 booze smuggled in. Ways past the search:

- Be a citizen
- Use False Citizenship Papers
- Drop the alcohol outside, walk through, pick it up after the gate opens
- **Put it on your companions**, who are apparently above suspicion

**$300 and 250 XP.** Lydia can also put Gecko, Broken Hills, Redding and New Reno on your map.

### 9. Deliver pliers (tools) and a wrench to Valerie
**Valerie** in Maintenance is waiting on a Super Tool Kit. Give her a **tool** and the **wrench**
from Curtis: **$50 and 250 XP**.

Wait **one day** — a good window for quest 10 and a first look at Gecko — then return for a
free **Super Tool Kit**. You can steal the other four from her.

She then upgrades weapons more cheaply than anyone else, with a discount at **CH 6 or Barter
75%**.

:::caution
Do **not** ask for the discount when upgrading a **Cattle Prod** — a bug returns a normal one
instead of the upgrade. Pay the full $750.
:::

:::note[Vic's daughter]
If Vic is with you there is a long conversation between them first. Afterwards, talk to Val
about Vic — with **CH 8 or Speech 75%** you can persuade her to give him a chance: **250 XP**.
:::

:::tip[No wrench?]
Playing without children means no Curtis. There is a wrench in one of the Redding mine
headquarters, one in the vault, and ghoul caravans on the Gecko–Broken Hills route carry them.
:::

### 10. Scout the eight sectors around Gecko and return to Stark
Run a circle around Gecko. **$300 and 350 XP.**

### 11. Enter NCR and return to Stark
Go to NCR — and visit the Hubologist for your **Luck boost** while you are there. Return for
**$500, 750 XP and a free Motion Sensor**.

### 12. Help Charlie
**Charlie** lies in a tent west of the bar. Use **Doctor** on him (100 XP) to diagnose radiation
poisoning, use **RadAway** on him (100 XP), and get another **100 XP** when he stands up.

:::note
While he is still on the ground you can keep using RadAway on him for **100 XP each time**.
:::

### 13. Stop Bishop's raider band from attacking Vault City
See [Raiders](/walkthrough/raiders).

## After the game

Vault City is one of two places — New Reno being the other — where most people have new dialogue
after you finish the game. You get access to the inner city and the vault whether or not you had
it before.

:::caution
This also **cuts off** unexplored quest options, since key figures switch to post-game dialogue.
Randal's shop effectively closes, though you can click him until he stops talking and then loot
him clean.
:::
