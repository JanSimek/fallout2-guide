# The Den

> *"You'll not find a greater hive of scum and villainy. Now where did I hear that?"*
> — Whiskey Bob

Smaller than Klamath in map terms, but this is where wasteland politics starts. The Den is
split into east and west, plus a **residential area** that RPU adds.
<Vanilla>The residential district does not exist in the original game — it was cut before
release.</Vanilla>

Both main areas are full of chem addicts who occasionally attack unprovoked. Hurt them a
little and they run; you can also just run and end combat. Killing them costs no karma, but do
not make a habit of it.

## The pickpocket children

The kids standing beside the doorways at **Becky's, Tubby's, Flick's and Frankie's** all steal
from you as you pass, at roughly **65% skill**. The two outside Ananias's house are the only
harmless ones.

Running does not help — the steal is instantaneous even though the animation plays after you
have gone by. Your options:

- **Sneak** past them.
- **Enter combat mode**, walk past, end combat.
- **Let them try, then steal it back.** Use Steal on each kid to check. They will not fence
  your item until you walk away, and getting caught has no consequence. Once you have robbed
  them back, you are safe from that specific child for 1–5 minutes — usually enough to get in
  and out.

If they do fence it, the item goes to the nearest shopkeeper and you will have to buy it back.

:::caution
Kill a child and you become a **Childkiller**, which has consequences across the whole game.
See [Karma and Reputation](/character/reputation). If a *companion* does the killing, you are
not blamed.
:::

:::note Playing without children
If your version hides children, they still steal from you — you just cannot steal back. Your
missing item will eventually reappear in a shop.
:::

## Shops and people

| Who | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tubby** | East side | Hunting rifles, .44 Magnums, 10mm SMGs. Restocks in 1–2 days. |
| **Flick** | East side | Similar stock. Restocks 2–4 days. |
| **Rebecca** | Becky's Casino | Drinks, gambling, and three quests. |
| **Frankie** | The Hole | Drinks, and the still quest. |
| **Mom** | Mom's diner | Cheap healing, stimpaks, and the orphanage quest. |
| **Metzger** | Slaver guild | Vic's owner. |
| **Smitty** | Junkyard | **The car.** |

**Loadout suggestion:** a hunting rifle for Vic, a 10mm SMG for Sulik, a .44 Magnum for
yourself. It is expensive but it carries you to Vault City comfortably.

:::tip Cheap experience
- **Leanne** in Becky's — buy her a Nuka-Cola ($5) and listen to her talk about the Master.
  **350 XP.**
- **Stacy** in Mom's — buy her a drink ($10) and hear about her cat. **200 XP.**
:::

:::note
Ask **Tubby** about chems — pay $100, offer $50 with a Speech check, or intimidate him with
**ST 8** or the Presence perk. This puts **Redding** on your world map even though he never
mentions it.

Ask **Mom** about vaults and she puts **Vault City** on your map. **Joey** puts New Reno on it
once you have the Anna quest.
:::

:::caution Do not sleep with Joey
A patch bug means that after a female character sleeps with him, he relocates east of Becky's
and then vanishes from the game permanently when you leave the map.
:::

## Petey

RPU adds a druggie who accosts you in a forced encounter asking for money. Give him **$5** for
**+1 karma**. It only works the first time — refusing costs you nothing.
<Vanilla>Petey does not exist in the original game.</Vanilla>

## The graveyard

Use a shovel on the graves if you want to be a **Grave Digger** (−5 karma each; the title
itself does nothing). The full haul across all graves:

$72 · 12 Beer · 1 Buffout · 1 Jet · 27 Rock · 1 Rubber Boots · 2 Throwing Knife · 1 Knife ·
1 RadAway · 24 10mm AP · 1 Combat Knife · 1 Flower

## The slaver guild

You can **sell companions** to Metzger — any human except Vic. The rest of your party remain
oblivious. Metzger's prices are bugged so that a *lower* Barter skill pays better: at Barter
90% you get the base rate (Miria $1100, Davin $900, Myron $800, Sulik $1200, Cassidy $1300);
at Barter 40% you get roughly 30% more.

You can also **join** as a Slaver. This makes you hated by Sulik, Cassidy, Keith Wright and
many others. The benefit is slave runs: you are teleported to a map, kill the unruly captives,
and get **$100 per surviving slave** (typically ~$1000), plus 500 XP for the first run.
Metzger stops offering once your captured total hits 30.

:::caution
Leave a slave run without killing all the armed slaves and Metzger tries to have you shot on
your return.
:::

### Killing Metzger

Come back once you have Sulik, Vic and Cassidy with combat shotguns — realistically, when you
return for the car. Rewards:

- **1500 XP** for killing Metzger
- **1250 XP** for telling the slaves he is dead
- **$1000** from Rebecca — but only if you make her understand *you* did it. Pick the "put up
  a good fight" line. One chance only.

:::tip Tactics
- **Lock them in.** The doors work.
- **Snipe from Mom's diner.** The back room has windows overlooking the guild. Slavers cluster
  around your first victim and will not come around the building.
- **Psycho.** Tubby usually has a dose or two, and the guard outside Vic's door has one you can
  steal. Maximised damage resistance means you take a couple of points per shot — though
  criticals still hurt.
- **Bring your allies from the gang war.** Whoever you sided with in
  <Quest>Help Lara attack Tyler's gang</Quest> keeps their green outline and will fight the
  slavers with you. Tyler uses player animations, so he can wield *any* weapon in the game —
  hand him a Bozar and enjoy yourself.
:::

:::note Shared drug effects
Chems used by one critter affect every critter sharing its template. One slaver taking Psycho
gives *all* slavers damage resistance. Leaving and re-entering the map, or reloading, clears
it. This also works in your favour with friendly caravan guards.
:::

:::caution
If Tyler has been killed for any reason, Metzger attacks the moment you speak to him.
:::

## The Brotherhood bunker

Locked at first. **After you deliver the vertibird plans to Matthew in San Francisco**, the
bunker here becomes fully explorable. Inside is a locker with **Brotherhood armor, a combat
shotgun, ammunition and medical supplies**.
<Vanilla>The bunker is permanently sealed in the original game — the man outside simply turns
you away forever.</Vanilla>

The guard outside makes more sense if you are level 4 or lower, which usually means a stupid
character.

## Quests

### 1. <Quest>Free Vic from his debt by getting his radio from his house in Klamath and paying Metzger</Quest>

The most important thing you will do in the Den.

**Getting past the guard at Vic's door** — any of:

- Get Metzger's permission
- Ask nicely (Speech check)
- Bribe him: Barter check, then **$300**
- Pass the Barter check, say "Screw you", then nag him
- Fail two Speech/Barter checks so he tells you to see Metzger, then nag him
- Have **Science tagged or 59%+**
- Have **Repair tagged or 59%+**
- Become a Slaver
- Sneak-Lockpick the door
- **IN under 4:** choose the "Meko veek" and "Seve" options

Talking to Vic completes the Arroyo quest <Quest>Find Vic the Trader</Quest> (no XP in itself).
Give him **his** radio — a generic one will not do.

Metzger wants **$1000** for his freedom. A female character with **CH 6** can sleep with him
for a $500 discount. Killing Metzger frees Vic for nothing.

Once free, Vic puts **Vault City** on your map, and you get **200 XP** for asking him about
being a trader. Then recruit him.

### 2. <Quest>Get car part for Smitty</Quest>

Smitty in the junkyard has a car that does not run. Bring him the **Fuel Cell Controller**
(found later, in Gecko) and he installs it and sells you the car for **$2000**.

If you also bring the **Fuel Cell Regulator** from Klamath, he installs that for **$750** and
the car uses **50% less fuel**. Do it.

:::danger Do not touch the car before you own it
Using any item on the car makes Smitty come running out to shout at you. Do it twice and he
turns **hostile**, which is close to unrecoverable — you can sneak up and talk to him to buy
the car anyway, but you may not get the chance.
:::

:::note
You can use the trunk for storage before buying the car. You can also kill Smitty and install
the Controller yourself with **Repair ~110%**, retrying until it takes — but you cannot install
the Regulator without him, so it is a poor trade.
:::

### 3. <Quest>Return Anna's locket</Quest>

**The Great Ananias**, in the southern building on the west side, tells you about a ghost next
door. Go there between **midnight and 4 a.m.** and you will see a shimmering figure. Talk to
her and learn about the stolen locket.

Mom points you at **Joey**. Get the locket by:

- Frightening him with Anna's revenge (**IN 6**)
- Paying **$50**, or $20 with good Barter
- Threatening him (**ST 5** plus a Speech check — failure means a fight)
- Killing him

Give Anna the locket: **250 XP**. Her bones materialise on the carpet.

Take the bones and a **shovel** — there are two in the room — to the cemetery, find **Anna's
grave**, dig it up, put the bones in, and cover it again. **1200 XP** (the message window
under-reports it as 600). Digging this particular grave does not make you a Grave Digger.

:::note RPU adds Anna's diary
The diary is in the **bookshelf behind Anna** — the one with a shovel lying in front of it.
Reading it in your inventory gives you the clues to the whole quest. **Use** it for **100 XP**.
<Vanilla>The diary does not exist in the original game.</Vanilla>
:::

:::caution
There is a second gravestone marked Anna ("done to death by a banana"). It is not hers.
:::

### 4. <Quest>Collect money from Fred</Quest>

Ask **Rebecca** for work. Fred is usually in Flick's house, indistinguishable from the other
junkies except that he is "a citizen of the Den".

Demand **$200** — patience gets you there even with mediocre Intelligence and Speech. Then let
him keep $100 or $150 and return to Becky for **$100 and 200 XP**.

:::tip Come back later
If you let Fred keep *any* money, return after 3–6 months (randomly decided). He has struck it
rich at Becky's tables and rewards you with **$2000, 200 small energy cells, 250 micro fusion
cells and a plasma grenade** — regardless of how much you actually let him keep. A scripting
glitch always awards the best outcome.
:::

:::caution
Rebecca gives no jobs while your karma is below 0. If Fred dies before you talk to him, you
cannot get the next quest either.
:::

### 5. <Quest>Get book from Derek</Quest>

Rebecca's second job. The book lies on the ground in the east part of town, in one of four
places:

- The graveyard, just east of the church
- The outhouse north of the Hole
- Behind a wall just southwest of the car behind Metzger's
- Beside a barrel south of the graveyard

Return it for **$80 and 300 XP**. Derek — outside Mom's, also "a citizen of the Den" — is
irrelevant; you never need to meet him.

### 6. <Quest>Sabotage Becky's still</Quest>

Ask Rebecca for a drink to learn her prices, then ask **Frankie** at the Hole why his cost
more. He asks you to find out.

Back at Becky's, wait for the guard by the stairs to wander over to the roulette table — 2–3
minutes, and you must keep your distance. Pick the door, go down, find the still.

Report to Frankie: **$100 and 400 XP**, and he gives you the sabotage quest. From here:

| Action | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Tell him to buy from Rebecca instead | **900 XP** |
| Smash the still with the nearby **crowbar**, then report | **$500 and 700 XP** |
| Both — but you must tell him in the *same* conversation | 700 + 900 XP |

A tool or wrench works as well as the crowbar; dynamite works too and is entirely unnecessary.

:::caution Order matters
Breaking the still makes Rebecca refuse to talk to you or let you gamble. **Do not do it until
after she has paid you for killing Metzger.**
:::

:::note
3–7 days after you break it, Becky repairs it — you need to leave and re-enter the map to start
the timer and again for the repair to take. You then have to break it again to report to
Frankie a second time.
:::

### 7. <Quest>Deliver a meal to Smitty for Mom</Quest>

Straightforward. **150 XP**, plus a stimpak from Smitty if you deliver within 24 hours, plus a
free meal from Mom worth 5–20 HP.

### 8. <Quest rpu>Find a way to get the orphans off the street</Quest>

RPU's replacement for the pickpocket problem.
<Vanilla>There is no orphanage and no way to stop the children stealing.</Vanilla>

1. Talk to **Bobby**, the boy in the southwest of **Den Residential** who lives with an abusive
   father. Listen to his story.
2. He points you at **Mom**. She knows of a building that would serve as an orphanage, but it
   is full of squatters.
3. Deal with the squatters:

| Solution | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Kill them | 500 |
| Talk them into leaving | 750 |
| Talk them into *working* at the orphanage for room and board | **1000** |

The two peaceful options need **Speech above 80%**; save and retry.

4. Tell Mom the squatters are gone. Return in a few days and the orphanage exists — meaning no
   more children stealing from you in every doorway.

:::caution
You must actually **visit the orphanage** for the thieving to stop.
:::

5. Go back and see how Bobby is doing: **500 XP** for helping him to a better life.

### 9. <Quest>Lara wants to know what is being guarded in the church</Quest>

**Lara** leads the punks on the west side.

Talk your way past the guards — **500 XP**, or **700 XP** if you do it *before* talking to Lara,
or 300 XP as a slaver. Failing the Speech check, **ST 8** bulls you through. Failing everything,
rest an hour and talk to Tyler again to get in — but then no XP for entry.

Sneaking does not work; Marc always spots you.

Examine the crates (**500 XP**) and report back for **$200**.

:::danger
Killing Tyler or Metzger before this chain is finished terminates all four gang war quests with
no reward.
:::

### 10. <Quest>Get permission from Metzger for gang war</Quest>

An errand. **200 XP**, plus **$50** from Lara.

### 11. <Quest>Find weakness in Tyler's gang guarding the church</Quest>

Talk to **Tyler** and learn his gang is throwing a party. **200 XP.** Low Charisma just means
trying again. Report to Lara — no money yet.

### 12. <Quest>Help Lara attack Tyler's gang</Quest>

RPU extends this into a two-stage fight.
<Vanilla>The original is a single battle against the whole gang at the church.</Vanilla>

Agree and you are placed outside the church. Fight through the guards there — then **Lara asks
you to follow her to the party in Den Residential** and finish the rest of the gang. You can
rest and heal between the two, but you **cannot back out**. Afterwards, return to the church
and talk to Lara for payment.

Lara pays **$400** whether you fight or not; if she dies, take it from her body.

**Alternative endings:**

| Approach | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Side with Lara normally | $400 |
| Tell **Tyler** the plan first (you have until 6 a.m.), then turn on Lara mid-battle | $400 from her corpse, plus **$100 and 1000 XP** from Tyler |
| Side with Lara, then kill her entire crew before they can kill Marc | **1000 XP** and $400 from Lara's corpse |
| Warn Tyler, then kill Marc and Tyler anyway | **1100 XP** for "ambushing Lara's gang" |

:::note
Whichever side you pick, you can run off the map the moment you are teleported, come back, and
still collect the reward — you just will not have any bodies to loot.
:::

:::caution Looting the bodies
Marc and Tyler stand on a scenery object called "Floor" which blocks clicking. Aim for a part
of the body sticking out past it, or come back once the corpse has decayed. The same problem
appears at a New Reno casino entrance and the Vault 15 vault door.
:::
