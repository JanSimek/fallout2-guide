# Klamath

> *"Most people have evil spirits. You? You have stupid spirits. Go see shaman, get hole in
> head…"* — Sulik

A quiet trapping town that will feel like a metropolis after Arroyo. Read the notice board
for a sense of what is going on. The town greeter, **Aldo**, wants money for the same
information and is not worth it — though he does inexplicably carry a **molotov cocktail**
you can steal, which has a use later.

## Shops

Two, both restocking every two days:

- **The Golden Gecko** — rough trapper bar. **Sajag** gives a discount if you are charming
  enough, or if you simply keep pestering him about it. You need a low or middling town
  reputation to be welcome here.
- **Buckner House** — friendlier, and where you will find Sulik.

Worth buying: **all the 10mm AP**, healing powder if you have fewer than ten, and the
**leather armor** if you can afford it — especially as a melee character, since there is
nothing else to spend money on for hours yet. Skip stimpaks; they are overpriced at this stage.

:::note[Renting a room]
$25 buys eight hours of rest and HP equal to four times your healing rate. Companions are not
healed at all. Practising your Doctor skill is a better use of the time.
:::

:::tip[Selling is always free money]
Your items always sell for the same value — Barter skill and discounts only affect the price
of *their* goods, never yours. A dollar of their money always buys a dollar of your equipment.
So pay cash for expensive things and trade in your junk afterwards at leisure.

The catch: shopkeepers restock money along with wares, and any cash they hold above their
normal amount **disappears** at restock.
:::

## Sulik

Standing about in Buckner House. He came looking for his sister, who was taken by slavers, got
drunk, wrecked the place, and is now working off a **$350** tab.

Three ways to free him:

| Method | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Offer to pay his debt **in conversation with Sulik** | 500 |
| Pay Maida Buckner directly | 0 |
| Ask for his freedom as the reward for <Quest>Rescue Smiley the Trapper</Quest> or <Quest>Rescue Torr</Quest> | 500 |

Always talk to **Sulik**, not Maida. Get him as early as you can — experience is not divided
among party members in this game, so a companion is pure profit.

:::tip[1000 XP for Sulik]
Do one of Ardin's quests and ask for Sulik's freedom (500 XP). Then, *before* speaking to
Maida, talk to Sulik — he is not technically free yet — and pay the $350 for another 500 XP.
:::

:::caution[Low Intelligence]
A stupid character cannot do the Smiley or Torr quests, so paying the debt is the **only**
way to get Sulik. You must offer the very first time you talk to him: always pick the second
line. Charisma 1 characters cannot recruit him at all.
:::

First thing to do: take Sulik's **leather jacket** and wear it yourself.

## Training

| Trainer | Where | Gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **John Sullivan** | Golden Gecko | +10% Unarmed, +10% Melee, **150 XP**. Use the "magic" dialogue path for a free beer first. |
| **Slim Picket** | Trapper Town | Outdoorsman, if your skill is **28% or lower**. Adds double your Intelligence (minimum 7). |
| **Smiley** | After the rescue | Gecko Skinning perk, plus +1–6% Outdoorsman |

:::note[Slim pays you]
Slim asks for $50 or $100 depending on town reputation, but a glitch means you **receive** the
money instead of paying it. If you turn him down after he names a price, you get no second
chance. If he says something about your tribe being "mostly farmers", just ask again.
:::

:::caution
Steal the Scout Handbook from the kid near Slim, but **do not read it** until after his
training — the book will push you past the 28% threshold.
:::

## The dog and Vic's shack

There is a dog wandering the town. Feed it **meat jerky** and it hands over the **Trapper
Town Key** and follows you around the map as an ally for as long as you stay.

:::caution
Do not talk to the dog until you have jerky in hand, or you may have to fight it. Running off
the map calms it down. Killing it means no key.
:::

**Vic's shack** is in the northeast. Inside is his **radio**, which you need later in the Den.

:::caution[The interior door is locked and trapped]
RPU locks and traps the inner door. Finding and disarming the trap is a straight **Traps** roll
against your skill; opening the lock is **Lockpick − 30%**, so you need over 30% Lockpick to
have any chance at all. Retry as often as you like until you critically fail and jam it.

Aldo's stolen **molotov cocktail**, thrown at the door, is the reliable fallback.
<Vanilla>The door is unlocked and untrapped in the original game.</Vanilla>
:::

## Trapper Town and the rat caves

Trapper Town is the western part of Klamath. Slim has locked the door north because of rats.
Ways through:

- Pick it (25 XP)
- Use the key the dog gave you
- Steal the key from Slim
- Barter for it — it costs **$1**
- Talk him into it
- Sleep with him, as a female character
- Throw Aldo's molotov at it
- Kill him and take it

Beyond is a complex leading to a hole in the floor and three cave levels full of rats.

**What to collect:**

| Item | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **10mm Pistol** | On a trapper corpse — your first decent weapon |
| **Combat Knife** | In the rat nest |
| **Sledgehammer** | On the ground |
| **Tool** | By a scattered robot corpse in a corner |
| **10mm JHP** | Southwest of the lowest level — sweep the mouse over the big pile of bones |
| **Dynamite** | On a shelf, for the locked door if Lockpick is under 70% |

Behind that locked door is a ladder up to an otherwise unreachable part of Trapper Town.
Take the Nuka-Cola, loot the fridge to the northeast, and — most importantly — take the
**Fuel Cell Regulator** from the car. That is worth **200 XP** silently.

:::note[Nuka-Cola machines]
The working machine near the car takes a coin and dispenses a Nuka-Cola, occasionally hitting
you for 1–2 damage on the way out. Each machine holds 15–30 bottles and restocks every 1–7
weeks. Nuka-Cola is an ingredient for Super Stimpaks later, so it is not entirely worthless.
:::

:::tip[Can't find the 10mm Pistol?]
If you left the caves to rest, the corpse decayed and the gun is now hidden under the blood
pool. Bodies decay in hours; blood can persist for years.
:::

## The Duntons

Two brothers living north of the square. At night they walk to the Golden Gecko, leaving the
house lootable — the door needs **Lockpick 70%**.

They are the town's problem, and in RPU you can finally do something about it.

:::caution
If they catch you searching the bookcase or see you in the side room, they attack immediately.
Most possessive NPCs give one warning first, and they must be able to *see* you — sneaking or
standing behind a companion breaks line of sight.
:::

:::note
Each Dunton spawns a set of **spiked knuckles** on his first combat round. Enter combat mode,
click end turn, steal them, and repeat for as many as you want.
:::

## Quests

### 1. <Quest rpu>Figure out who is rustling the brahmin</Quest>

RPU's substantially extended version of the rustling storyline.
<Vanilla>The original game has no way to expose the Duntons at all. The radscorpion limbs are
worthless and the confrontation dialogue exists in the scripts but is never used.</Vanilla>

1. Talk to **Maida Buckner** and ask what is going on in town. She mentions the rustling.
2. Take **either** Torr's guarding job or the Duntons' rustling job — it does not matter
   which. What matters is that you visit the pastures and collect the **radscorpion limbs**
   from the shack to the southwest, which are the Duntons' bugman costume.
3. Report to **Ardin Buckner**. She wants more evidence.
4. Examine (binocular icon) the **brahmin behind the Duntons' place**. This is a Perception
   check; retry until it passes. The brands have been altered.
5. Report back. **300 XP or $150.**

Order is flexible — you can even kill the Duntons first and start the quest afterwards. You
must find the limbs before the rebranding evidence counts.

:::caution[The Duntons leave permanently]
Once you have proven their guilt and leave the Klamath map, they are gone for good.
:::

**Confronting them directly** works too, as long as you have not yet reported the rebranding
to Ardin. **Speech 51%** resolves it peacefully for **250 XP**; failure means a fight.

:::tip[Pick your ground]
Confront them on the pastures map or in their own house. Do it in the Golden Gecko and
everyone in the bar joins in against you.
<Vanilla>In the original game the whole town *except* the Golden Gecko turns hostile. RPU
swapped this around.</Vanilla>
:::

Once confronted, they will not speak to you again.

### 2. <Quest>Refuel the still</Quest>

**Whiskey Bob** in Buckner House. Buy him a drink ($5) first — with **IN 7** you can talk him
into handing over the two booze he is carrying, recouping the cost.

He offers $50; ask for more and a Barter check gets you $65 promised, though a scripting glitch
means you only receive **$55**.

Take the southeastern exit into the woods, work down to the shack in the southwest, grab a
**firewood** heap and use it on the still. **100 XP.**

:::caution[This is one of the few timed quests]
Refuel within **one day** or you get no experience for it.
:::

You can instead sell the still's location to **Sajag** for $50, a beer and 100 XP (unlisted).
You must do this **before** collecting Bob's reward — you cannot have both.

:::tip
Do this after you have Sulik *and* the Gecko Skinning perk. The woods are full of golden
geckos, which are dangerous now and profitable later.
:::

:::note
Whiskey Bob refuses to talk if your town reputation is Neutral and karma is −3 or worse — a bug
in his reputation check. Nudge your town reputation in either direction (buying a round works)
and he becomes talkative again.
:::

### 3. <Quest>Rescue Smiley the Trapper</Quest>

**Ardin Buckner's** fiancé is missing. She puts the [Toxic Caves](/walkthrough/toxic-caves) on
your world map.

Rescue him, and choose your reward: **Sulik's freedom** (worth $350, plus 500 unlisted XP) or
**$100**. Take the freedom.

Then talk to Smiley for the **Gecko Skinning** perk, and ask him about gecko hunting for a
random **+1% to +6% Outdoorsman**. If he claims you already know more than he does, persist —
you get it anyway.

Gecko Skinning puts a sellable pelt in every gecko you kill except fire geckos. It is your main
income for the next several hours.

:::tip[Double reward]
Get the cave location from Ardin but **decline** the quest. Rescue Smiley anyway. Return to
Klamath — Ardin is still talking about him, so accept the quest properly, go back and rescue
him again. You now have two Smileys, each of whom will train you.

This only works if you do not enter the Toxic Caves between the first rescue and re-accepting
the quest — not even by saving and loading. Avoid talking to the first Smiley immediately
after accepting, or he starts following you around town.
:::

:::caution
If you agreed to search for Torr before getting this quest, you must finish
<Quest>Rescue Torr</Quest> first.
:::

### 4. <Quest>Guard the brahmin</Quest>

**Torr** wants help guarding his cattle from "bugmen". He only asks once, so do not talk to him
until you are ready.

Agree and you are moved to the pastures. Kill the radscorpion that appears, then the rest of
them — **250 XP**.

There is a second solution. The two shady figures west of Torr's camp are the Duntons. Approach
them, agree to help steal the cattle, then **talk them out of it** (Speech check). Same 250 XP
and +50 karma, silently.

Collect the **radscorpion limbs** from the shack to the southwest — you need them for quest 1.

:::danger[You cannot return to the pastures]
Once you leave this map it is gone. Take everything, including every scorpion tail you can
carry.
:::

:::caution
Killing a cow or turning Torr hostile fails the quest.
:::

:::note[Repeatable]
After scaring off the Duntons you can tell Torr you will guard the brahmin again, kill the
scorpions and leave for another 250 XP — but then you do not get <Quest>Rescue Torr</Quest>.
:::

### 5. <Quest>Rustle the brahmin</Quest>

The Duntons' side of the same coin. Instead of scaring them off, tell **Torr** to take off
(knocking him out does not work), then report back for **150 XP, $50 and 5 meat jerky**.
Declining the reward softens the karma hit.

You can also get this quest from the Duntons in town, provided your town reputation is not too
good.

:::caution
- With **Charisma 10** on your first arrival, your town reputation is too high and they will not
  offer. Arm-wrestle one of them first to fix it.
- Do not address them by name — it can abort the work dialogue. Which twin you are speaking to
  is randomised each time.
- Turn one down and you effectively lose your chance with the other.
- Killing Torr or any brahmin fails the quest and sends the Duntons running.
- **Saving and reloading after scaring off Torr** makes both Duntons stop speaking to you
  permanently, killing both quests.
:::

:::danger
Completing this quest spawns new brahmin in the pen behind the Duntons' house that run
**Arroyo brahmin scripts**. Attacking them ends your game.
:::

### 6. <Quest>Kill the rat god</Quest>

Slim tells you about **Keeng Ra'at**, who lives on the second level of the rat maze. You will
know him when you see him.

Two solutions:

| Method | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Kill him in a fight | 300 |
| **Collapse the cave on him** | **600** |

For the collapse: four of the wooden support beams near where Keeng Ra'at is normally found
show the "use" icon when examined. Place an explosive next to one and part of the cave comes
down.
<Vanilla>The support beams are not destructible in the original game — fighting him is the
only option.</Vanilla>

:::danger[Loot first]
The collapse blocks off part of the cave permanently. Get the **Fuel Cell Regulator** and the
hidden pistol *before* you set the charge.
:::

Bring Sulik. And note that once the rat is dead you can no longer get Slim's training, so do
that first.

### 7. <Quest>Rescue Torr</Quest>

Only available if you told Torr to abandon his brahmin.

Go to **Ardin Buckner**. Torr went looking for the herd and has not come back. Finger the
Duntons and offer to help — one chance only.

He is in the canyon to the northwest, standing near a **mad robot**. Kill the robot with
hit-and-run, look around the area (it is worth seeing), talk to Torr, and return to town —
**200 XP**. Then talk to Ardin and ask for **Sulik's freedom** (500 XP). Ask for money instead,
or if Sulik is already free, and you get $100 and much less karma.

:::tip
Run off the map after the robot attacks and it will be peaceful when you return — at which
point you can **Repair** its broken motivator.
:::

## Miscellaneous

- **The bathhouse.** You can pay for company. **Jenny** is a friend of Vic's and will put the
  Den on your map without payment. Maida, Sajag and Whiskey Bob can all do the same. Sally
  refuses to talk if you double-crossed the Duntons at the pastures.
- **The Yellow Reactor Keycard** found in the canyon has **no use in this game**, despite
  decades of rumour. It is not present in the unpatched game at all.
- **Locking doors** gives 25 XP each, if you are that sort of player.

:::caution[Lockpicking]
Save before picking anything important at low skill. A critical failure jams the lock until the
clock next passes midnight — though lockpick *items* still work on jammed doors. Lockpicks
themselves never break.
:::
