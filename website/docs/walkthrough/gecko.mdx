# Gecko

> *"Apples rule. If it weren't for a conspiracy on the part of fruit manufacturers we'd all
> have apples."* — Wooz

A small town of ghouls built around an atomic reactor that is somehow still running. Vault City
will tell you the radiation is lethal. It is not — you do not need Rad-X here, and the ghouls
are not the vicious killers the propaganda claims.

The reactor quests are among the most lucrative in the game.

## People

**Lenny** the doctor stands with Harold in the Manager's Office. He heals HP and crippled limbs
(not eye damage) for a nominal fee — free if you are broke, or if you talk to him about your
ancestor the Vault Dweller. That same conversation lets him **join your party**, provided your
karma is at least 1. Recruiting him is **+5 karma** and improves your standing in Gecko, so do
it even if you drop him immediately.

:::caution
Do not get into Lenny's *"Vault 13 owes us one"* G.E.C.K. thread — it shares a variable with
Harold's stimpak reward below, and you cannot have both.
:::

:::note RPU's Lenny
Lenny now carries a **zip gun** when you first meet him, and if you give him a radioactive drink
he **glows in the dark** — a RadAway turns it off again.

The optional new Lenny model looks better but uses rifles instead of pistols and has **no
running animation**, so he permanently trails the party.
<Vanilla>Lenny has no zip gun, does not glow, and there is no alternate model.</Vanilla>
:::

**Wooz** at the Harp gives you a free pack of **Tragic** cards for getting him to explain the
game. You can play him — each game costs or wins you one pack. Winnable at low Gambling skill,
and not worth the time.

:::note
RPU implements the cut **Tragic addiction**. It is harmless, but it exists now.
:::

**Percy** in the junkyard runs a shop with very fair prices (restocks every 3 days) and gives
you quest 5. He has a **Yellow Pass Key** in his house, free for the taking.

**Skeeter** upgrades weapons and gives you quests 3 and 4. In the leftmost room of his house is
quite possibly your first set of **lockpicks**.

**Lumpy**, in the shack north of Skeeter's, puts **Broken Hills** on your map if you ask about
his name and follow the conversation.

:::caution
Ask **Percy** to barter in dialogue. Pressing the barter button skips the modifier and you pay
normal prices.
:::

:::note
Skeeter has the same bug as Eldridge in New Reno: at **Barter 91%+** he does upgrades for free.
:::

## The power plant

You need a **yellow** and a **red** keycard for the first door of each colour. The guard
directly ahead as you enter has both — steal them, or find them in containers. There is a blue
one too; it has no use here.

:::tip
Try sneaking past the ghouls in the plant. The commentary is worth it.
:::

## Quests

### 1. <Quest>Solve the Gecko powerplant problem</Quest>

Get it from **Harold** here, or from **Lynette** in Vault City. It appears under both towns in
your Pip-Boy either way.

#### The good ending — fix the reactor

Harold tells you the reactor needs a hydroelectric magnetosphere regulator. Go back to Vault
City and talk to **McClure** — **not Lynette**, who will ban you for it. He sends you to
**Randal** at the Amenities shop for the part.

Three ways to install it, in ascending order of reward:

| Method | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Run through the reactor room yourself and use the part on the red-wheeled machinery (Repair check) — 10 damage every few steps | 4000 |
| Talk **Festus** into installing it (Speech check) | 4250 |
| Program the **robot** to do it | **4500** |

Then talk to **McClure** and he makes you a **citizen of Vault City**.

:::tip Harold's reward
Afterwards, go to Harold, ask what he does, and tell him about your ancestor. **3 Super Stimpaks
and 5 Stimpaks.** You get one shot at this thread — follow it to the end.
:::

#### The robot

Access the computer in the room next to the reactor. Initialise with the codes containing
**A, B and C** in that order. Choose the coolant maintenance sequence, read the operating
instructions, and enter a program.

The correct sequence is **the order of the planets, from outermost inwards**. If that means
nothing to you, examine the machinery around the rim of the reactor — each piece is labelled —
and direct the robot to each in turn.

:::caution
If you fiddled with the robot controls **before** getting the part, choose "Back" then
"Initiate Repair Sequence" to transfer the part to the robot.

Leaving the plant while the robot is walking can get it stuck. You can then steal the part from
it, or kill it, and do the job yourself.
:::

:::tip The Enclave talking head
Easy to miss. At the computer, choose **"Initiate general maintenance sequence"**, download the
code (**Science 50%**), enter the codes with **X, Y and Z** in that order, then **"List other
stations"** and **"More"**. Pick either of the first two options and you get a talking head.

In RPU this is not merely a curiosity: the Enclave officer **sends a squad after you**. The
encounter usually triggers shortly after you leave Gecko. You can talk your way out with
**Speech above 85%**, or fight.
<Vanilla>The conversation is a dead end in the original game — nothing whatsoever happens.</Vanilla>
:::

#### The evil ending — meltdown

Three ways:

| Method | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Program the robot to shut the coolant valve | 550 |
| Give the **clipboard** with the coolant report to **Hank**, who does it for you | 550 |
| Shut the valve by hand — three uses with a ST check each, at 10 damage per few steps, then 15 | 0 |

Then **2500 XP** when you leave the reactor, which is sealed permanently afterwards.

:::caution
Meltdown turns every ghoul in the plant hostile. In town, only ghouls you *talk* to attack;
named ghouls like Skeeter, Harold and Wooz stay friendly unless a fight starts near them.

**24 hours later everyone in Gecko is gone**, Lenny included if he was not in your party — along
with everything he was carrying. Only Lumpy and the junkyard dogs remain.
:::

:::note You can have both
Fix the reactor for 4000–4500 XP, then sabotage it anyway for another 550 + 2500. Or sabotage
first and install the part before it blows. The game does not stop you.
:::

### 2. <Quest>Optimize the powerplant</Quest>

Only possible if you **fixed** the reactor rather than destroying it.

Talk to **Festus** again for a **Reactor Holodisk**, take it to the Vault City central computer,
and bring it back to Festus to use on the reactor computer: **2500 XP**.

:::tip Skip the trip
Ask McClure about optimizing the reactor *before* telling him you repaired it and he has the
data optimized for you.
:::

**The extra experience:** in the junkyard, go down the manhole in the shack left of the abandoned
reactor building. Past some ghoul-guarded access tunnels — and **Zomak**, who has things worth
stealing — you will find **The Brain**, a mutated mole rat with plans for world domination.

Agree with everything he says. He gives you a **talisman** (which does nothing) and points you at
**Gordon** for optimization data. Gordon gives you the **Gecko Holodisk**; take it to
**McClure** for **750 XP**.

:::caution
Gordon will hand over the disk without you meeting the rat at all, as long as you talk to him
**before** repairing the reactor.

Talk to McClure about the Gecko Holodisk after fixing the plant but before getting the Reactor
Holodisk from Festus, and the handover silently fails.
:::

:::caution Cheezy Poofs
The Brain will not talk to you while you are carrying Cheezy Poofs — hand them all over or he
attacks. If you killed Keeng Ra'at in Klamath, he opens by mourning his "sole remaining kin".
:::

:::note Which ending you want
**Optimizing the plant condemns the ghouls** — Vault City annexes Gecko to control the reactor,
which is Gecko's bad ending. **Fix but do not optimize** for the neutral ending. See
[Endings](/reference/endings).
:::

### 3. <Quest>Get super repair kit for Skeeter</Quest>

Ask Skeeter about car parts. He trades a **Fuel Cell Controller** — the part Smitty needs for
the car — for a **Super Tool Kit**. **250 XP.**

You will normally have the kit from Valerie in Vault City. Smitty in the Den also stocks one
with about a 40% chance per restock.

### 4. <Quest>Get 3-step plasma transformer for Skeeter</Quest>

Skeeter asks for this when you bring the tool kit, provided you are carrying at least one weapon
he can upgrade.

**Jeremy** in the reactor has the part. Steal it, or give him a **Part Requisition Form** —
found in Harold's house and carried by Festus. Ask Festus for it and any of four excuses works
with a Speech check or good standing; you can also steal or barter for it.

**350 XP and a free weapon upgrade.** Take the **.44 Magnum (Speed Load)**.

:::tip Repeatable
Steal the part, then get another for each additional Requisition Form — and Skeeter performs one
free upgrade and pays 350 XP each time. You need to give him something to upgrade between
rewards.
:::

### 5. <Quest>Find Woody the ghoul for Percy. Try the Den</Quest>

Percy puts the Den on your map. Pay **the Great Ananias $25** to see the mummy — it is Woody.
Wake him and watch him run: **1000 XP**. Return to Percy for **4 RadAway and 3 Rad-X**, which
you will never need.
