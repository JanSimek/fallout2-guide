# New California Republic

> *"Tandi job. Me want."* / *"You can't take the president's job!"* / *"No?"* / *"NO! You are dumb."*
> — The Chosen One and Gunther

The largest and most organised civilisation in the wasteland. Four maps: the **bazaar** outside the
walls, **downtown**, the **Council Hall**, and **Westin's ranch**.

:::danger[No weapons downtown]
The guards are serious about this. Walk around downtown with a weapon readied and they attack
almost immediately, and moving out of sight does not help once you have been warned.

**Never travel straight to the downtown map** unless you are certain your companions have their
weapons holstered — you have very little time to tell them once you arrive.
:::

:::note
Goris's claw is not checked, despite what other guides say. Neither are the three inferior Brain
Bots.
:::

## The bazaar

**Buster's store** is one of only two shops in the game carrying **2mm EC** ammunition. He also has
micro fusion cells, Needler ammo, 4.7mm caseless, and — worth trading surplus weapons for — a
**Pancor Jackhammer** (the best shotgun in the game), a **Ripper**, a **Power Fist** and an
**Avenger Minigun**.

He converts a **hunting rifle into a scoped hunting rifle for $500**, which is exactly the
difference in value between them.

:::caution
Buster opens at **08:01** and attacks after one warning if you come at night. His three shelves
restock separately: 2–4 days, except the right-hand one at 1–4 days.
:::

**The hooded man** on the left will fit a **blower to your car for $1000** if you give him six
hours. Do it — you can barter goods back to recover the money.

:::note
You do not have to spend six hours in NCR. But after six hours have passed you must be on this map
for about a minute — resting ten minutes works — before it is finished.
:::

**Stanwell** offers a **$2000** caravan guard job to Redding on the 11th of every odd month.

**Mira** in the Rawhide Saloon sells booze and information. Her patrons often carry **200 shotgun
shells** each, which restock every time you enter the map.

## Merk

He holds the back room of the Rawhide Saloon between **16:01 and 08:00**. Information costs **$50**
before he will answer anything.

To get work you must first prove yourself. Any of:

- **Fight Lenny the super mutant** in the secret basement, restricted to melee and unarmed weapons.
  You must have talked to Lenny at least once first.
- **Have Myron in your party** and tell Merk you know someone who can make jet. He pays **$1000** —
  and, due to a bug, Myron stays with you.
- Tell him you are a **"badass mofo"**, if you are a Childkiller or Berserker.
- Take the Hubologist quest.

:::danger[Before fighting Lenny]
- **Drop your Bag first.** It is confiscated along with your weapons, and comes back **empty**.
- Guns are returned **loaded**, and separate stacks of the same weapon **multiply** — a scripting
  error in the return routine. Eight Desert Eagles come back as fifteen.
- **Do not save during the fight.** Reloading leaves you trapped in the ring after you kill him.
- Laddie, Dogmeat, Goris, the pariah dog, K-9 and inferior Brain Bots are **not** hidden and will
  fight alongside you. Anything on their corpses is lost when you leave.
:::

Steal Lenny's stimpaks and power fist beforehand to make the fight much easier. Mira gives you a
booze afterwards.

:::caution
Do not tell Merk you got his name from **Doofus** — he refuses to speak to you until you kill
Doofus, and killing him changes nothing.

The **Vault 13 Holodisk** he sells for $1000 leads to an empty cave that relabels itself "Fake
Vault 13". You can steal the money back. Do not bother.
:::

## Officer Jack

**Dorothy**, outside the building northeast of the police station, asks for help with a madman
holding a bomb during daylight hours. That is **Jack**, a policeman whose ex-wife is **Mira** in the
bazaar.

| Outcome | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| **Talk him down** — **Speech 80%** or the Speaker perk | **6000 XP**, plus 3000 XP and four books from Dorothy |
| Kill him once he starts talking, then tell Dorothy there was no alternative | 3000 XP and the books |
| Let him blow up, then **repair the bank of monitors** on the wall (Science or Repair) | 3000 XP and the books, plus the loot in his remains |
| Let him blow up and let Dorothy try to fix it | She may die. Nothing for you. |

The talk-down dialogue path is **1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 2**.

The books are a **Big Book of Science, a Scout Handbook, a First Aid Book and a Dean's
Electronics**.

:::caution
Shoot Jack **before** he starts his speech and Dorothy and/or the guards attack you.

Repair the **monitors on the wall**, not the nearby computer.
:::

:::tip
Repair the computer *before* anything happens and collect Dorothy's reward. If Jack then damages it
and you fix it again, she pays **another 3000 XP and two more books**.
:::

If Jack lives, he ends up raving in a police station cell — still armed. If he dies, quest 10
appears in your Pip-Boy.

## The Hubologist

Go to the church downtown on your first visit. You want two things from her: **quest 5** and the
**zeta scan**.

:::danger[Save before the scan]
The zeta scan **permanently** either raises your Luck by **2** or lowers it by **1**. Save, take it,
and reload if you get the wrong result.

It also makes you an AHS-1 Hubologist, which does nothing except prevent you from taking the
slightly different scan in San Francisco.
:::

## Doc Jubilee

His hospital bookcase is lootable. He charges a flat **$50** for healing, HP only, no companions.

:::tip
Fail a Steal check on the **Heart Pills** or the **Poison** hypo and he makes you put them back. But
put them in your inventory and then **drop them from within the loot interface** and he never
notices — pick them up afterwards.

Both matter: the poison for Bishop's Westin contract, and the heart pills for RPU's
[subtler route](/walkthrough/new-reno).
:::

Ask him about Vault 13 and he mentions **Saltbeef**.

## Dusty's Cantina

**Leave your companions outside.** **Hoss** in the corner provokes a fight when you approach. Beat
him to **23 HP**, or simply put a gun in an active item slot, and he backs down. Dusty then gives
you a free booze every time you talk to him.

:::caution
Companions nearby will draw weapons and bring the guards down on you.
:::

## Westin's ranch

Get in with a recommendation from **Sheriff Dumont** (ask about work) or with Vault City's
<Quest>Deliver Lynette's holodisk to Westin in NCR</Quest>.

**Saltbeef**, the old drunk in the nearest building, will tell you — for a beer, but not Rot Gut —
how he found Vault 13 and scribbled a map on the back of a painting. **Doc Jubilee** sells that
painting for **$10,000**, which you can steal back. Examine it and **Vault 13** appears on your map.

## Carlson's estate

Two ways in:

1. Steal the **Presidential Pass** from Gunther in the Congress House and use it (backpack icon) on
   the gate guard **before** talking to him.
2. Ask **Merk** about Carlson (**$50**). If he likes you — quest 1 done and no offence given — he
   mentions a bodyguard job, and the gate guard lets you through.

:::danger
Entering without permission means fighting an entire map of guards.
:::

**To kill Carlson:** five Super Stimpaks and a ten-minute wait, or open the gate to his garden, walk
around the house and shoot him through the window. A Sneak-kill also works. Push the kid to the far
side of the yard first. Take his **Pancor Jackhammer** afterwards.

## Quests

### 1. <Quest>Kill Hubologist in NCR for Merk</Quest>

:::caution[Get the zeta scan and quest 5 first.]
:::

**2000 XP** when she is dead, even without telling Merk first.

:::note[Killing quietly]
Three reliable methods, useful throughout the game:

1. **Super Stimpaks.** Each does 9 damage. Use the Awareness perk to read the target's HP, divide by
   9, round up, feed them that many in quick succession from an active item slot, then wait 10
   minutes.
2. **Explosives.** Set a timer and plant them with Steal, or drop them a hex or two away while
   sneaking. Better: put them in **the Bag** from Modoc — or any nearby container — and leave it
   next to the target. Nobody notices.
3. **Sneak-kill.** Enter Sneak mode and kill them in one combat round.
:::

:::danger[Do not leave the map while waiting]
Explosives and Super Stimpak overdoses will kill the target, but the **death script never runs** —
so variables are not set, quests stall, and shop inventories do not transfer. With the Hubologist
specifically you would get neither the 2000 XP nor credit from Merk.

Leaving the map while waiting for a Super Stimpak overdose is also likely to crash the game.
:::

### 2. <Quest>Retrieve papers from Dr Henry</Quest>

Offered by Merk after quest 1. Take the papers from the desk at the doctor's place. **1000 XP and
$1000.**

:::caution
You get **one** chance to accept — unless you pick the "information" line, which gives you a second.
:::

### 3. <Quest>Test mutagenic serum on a super-mutant. Try Broken Hills</Quest>

Talk to **Dr. Henry** with **Science 80%+** and ask about cyber-genetics. Use the serum on any super
mutant and they dissolve: **1000 XP**.

Report back and you can have **Cyberdog** as a companion. You probably do not want him.

:::tip
**Elmo** in Broken Hills is the traditional test subject. Evil characters can use **Gond** in the
Ranger hideout — the other Rangers attack, but the guards do not care.

Use it in **combat mode** and the mutant takes a turn or two to die. Kill it first and loot the
corpse and **the serum may still be there**, so you can complete the quest and still get combat XP
for a mutant you wanted to kill anyway.
:::

:::danger
Using the serum on **Lenny** in the Rawhide Saloon turns Merk hostile. Worse: using it on him in the
ring *before* damaging him leaves him alive but immobile and traps you in **unending combat**.
Damage him at least once first — or use it on his corpse before combat ends.
:::

### 4. <Quest>Get the map from the NCR Rangers, for Vortis the slaver</Quest>

:::caution
Save before talking to **Vortis** in the bazaar — annoy him as a good character and he will not talk
to you again. The overseer in metal armour attacks on sight between **04:00 and 12:00**, so rest
until noon.
:::

Take the map from a table in the garage of the **Ranger headquarters** downtown — the place with a
super mutant and people in combat armour. Return to Vortis for **$500**.

:::note[RPU: the Ranger safe houses]
There is a **map on a table in the back room** of the Ranger building. **Use** it to put the three
**safe houses** on your world map.
<Vanilla>The safe houses do not exist.</Vanilla>
:::

### 5. <Quest rpu>Wipe out the Rangers' safe houses to the north, for Vortis</Quest>

RPU's slaver-aligned continuation.
<Vanilla>Vortis has no second job.</Vanilla>

Available if you are a **Slaver**, have **karma below −250**, or have the **Cult of Personality**
perk. Vortis offers **$1000** to destroy all three safe houses.

With **Speech above 80%** you can ask for help — there is a Speech roll, so sometimes you get none.
Take the help: you are then **transported automatically** between safe houses, even though Vortis's
men only stick around for the first fight.

:::tip
With his men along you also get the option to **double-cross Vortis** and side with the Rangers. He
never finds out, and the Rangers stay alive until you decide otherwise.
:::

:::caution
Give the map to Vortis and the Rangers at the safe houses will already be dead when you arrive —
unless you start his second quest without finishing it.

You may still be attacked by angry Rangers even if you joined them, killed Vortis, and otherwise
played well.
:::

### 6. <Quest>Free the slaves in the slave pen, for the Rangers</Quest>

Get quest 4 first. Talk to **Elise**, say you hate slavers, and agree to prove yourself.

Go to the slavers, hand over the map, kill them, and free the slaves by accessing the terminals
(any skill, or the hand icon — the check tests both Science and Lockpick).

Return and join the Rangers: **3000 XP and a Ranger Pin**.

:::tip
You only need to open **one** set of cages. You can even kill all the slaves first and still finish.

Faster route: tell Elise you hate slavers, then that you need to think about it, and leave dialogue.
Talk to her again and agree to become a Ranger — you get the 3000 XP immediately and the quest
appears already crossed out.
:::

### 7. <Quest rpu>Wipe out the Slaver's Camp to the north west, for the Rangers</Quest>

RPU's counterpart to quest 5.
<Vanilla>The Slaver's Camp does not exist.</Vanilla>

Learn its location from **Metzger** (**Speech 61%**) or from one of his freed slaves. Both require
that you have Sulik's <Quest rpu>Find her</Quest> quest from the
[Umbra Tribe](/walkthrough/umbra-tribe) first.

Go directly and wipe it out, or tell the Rangers first and bring them along. Either way, report back
to the Rangers: **800 XP**.

### 8. <Quest>Deliver Hubologist's field report to AHS-9 in San Francisco</Quest>

Two ways to get it:

- Answer **"Yes"** on first meeting the Hubologist, then either pick the **"Transcendency"** line
  (Speech check) to imply you outrank her, or the **"aura"** line followed by asking how things are
  going (**IN 8 and Speech 65%**).
- Take Merk's kill order and tell her about it, then say you will not do it. The dialogue ends but
  the letter appears in your inventory.

**500 XP** on delivery, and nothing else. You can still kill her for Merk afterwards.

### 9. <Quest>Deliver Westin's holodisk to Lynette in Vault City</Quest>

Deliver Bishop's Holodisk to Westin; if you are a Vault City citizen he gives you this in return.
**1500 XP**, an **H&K G11** and 2 frag grenades from Randal.

:::tip[Become Captain of the Guard]
With **CH 8** and a high enough respect counter, Lynette makes you **Captain of the Guard** — which
unlocks companion healing from Dr. Troy, lets Marcus and Lenny into the city, and opens several
locked containers.

To raise her respect (needs **Speech 75%** and citizenship): say you have a question, ask why they
do not live in a vault, then pick the last option to leave dialogue. Repeat **10–15 times**, then
hand over the disk.

As Captain you get **2500 XP** for the delivery instead of 1500.
:::

### 10. <Quest>"Take care of Officer Jack" for Mira</Quest>

Merk points you at **Mira**; you need **ST 7** and a stupid character to get the quest properly.

Kill Jack — or simply let him blow himself up — for **2000 XP** and **an H&K P90c** from Mira.

:::note
The quest appears in your Pip-Boy whenever Jack dies, even if you never took it, so **any** character
can collect Mira's reward. But she only gives you the P90c if **you** killed him; letting him
self-destruct gets you nothing usable.
:::

### 11. <Quest>Stop officer Jack from blowing up the power plant</Quest>

See [Officer Jack](#officer-jack) above.

### 12. <Quest>Stop brahmin raids</Quest>

Talk to **Felix** at Westin's ranch. With a recommendation from Dumont or Lynette he unlocks the
door. **Westin** gives you the quest if you say Dumont sent you, ask where to find Vault 13, or ask
for more work after killing Bishop.

Talk to Felix again and you are teleported to the pastures. Wait — **two talking deathclaws** appear
and leave.

Report back: **1000 XP and $200**, plus the location of Vault 15.

:::tip[Follow the deathclaws to Vault 13]
With **Outdoorsman 100%** an exit grid appears in the northeast corner once they have gone. Follow
them and you find **Vault 13**.

Even without the skill you can click one of the half-offscreen hexes at the top and your character
will run off the screen onto the exit grid. You get to Vault 13, but it does not appear on your
world map.
:::

:::danger
**Do not attack the brahmin** — being in combat mode when the deathclaws appear can lock up the game.

Leaving the pastures by either exit grid before they appear, or refusing to follow Felix at all,
makes the quest uncompletable.
:::

### 13. <Quest>Eliminate Mr. Bishop</Quest>

With Bishop's Westin contract active, tell **Westin** that Bishop sent you. He offers **50% more**
than Bishop did — and you can lie about the figure to inflate it further.

Kill Bishop and return for payment, but **no XP**.

:::tip
Killing **Westin** and taking Bishop's next quest is worth considerably more.
:::

### 14. <Quest>Murder Carlson in NCR</Quest>

See [Carlson's estate](#carlsons-estate). **2500 XP** from Bishop.

### 15. <Quest>Retrieve Parts/Gain Access to Vault 15</Quest>

Dumont, Karl, Dusty, Doc Jubilee and the slave overseer all mention that **President Tandi** is
looking for a specialist.

Go to the Congress House, tell **Feargus** and then **Gunther** you are there for the job, and talk
to **Tandi**.

:::danger[Do not ask for a reward]
Ask up front and you get **$500** instead of $6000. The XP is the same either way.
:::

Either win condition works: kill **Darion** and talk to **Zeke** about letting NCR annex the Squat,
**or** simply walk into the vault and take the parts.

**Gunther pays 5000 XP and $6000.**

:::tip[The Vault 13 bonus]
If you found Vault 13 and Gruthar let you in, you can tell Tandi about it **when you report back
about Vault 15, and only then**.

If the deathclaws are still alive you can only tell her they are there, and she thinks you are mad.
But if you **killed Gruthar**, or visited after the Enclave wiped them out, you can tell her it is
free — and get a bonus: **$20,000** if you ask for that much, **$10,000** otherwise, plus **2000 XP
and 35 karma** either way.
:::

## The Brotherhood bunker

Empty at first. **After you deliver the vertibird plans to Matthew in San Francisco** it becomes
fully explorable, with lockers holding **Brotherhood armor, a sniper rifle, ammunition and
medical supplies**.
<Vanilla>The bunker is permanently sealed.</Vanilla>

## A note on empty containers

NCR is mostly bare. Downtown, the only worthwhile containers are the **map in Ranger HQ**,
**Dr. Henry's papers**, some items in **Dusty's Cantina**, and **Doc Jubilee's bookcase** — plus
Duppo's stock and the police HQ lockers, which require killing everyone. The Council and ranch maps
are completely empty.
