# Walkthrough Overview

Fallout 2 opens up almost immediately. After the Temple of Trials you can go anywhere the
world map will let you walk, and it will happily let you walk to Navarro at level 3 and get
killed there.

This walkthrough is arranged in the order the game is balanced for. Follow it and you will
arrive everywhere at roughly the right level with roughly the right equipment.

## How each page is organised

1. **Getting there** — how the location appears on your map, and when to come.
2. **Before you start** — anything that will bite you if you do it in the wrong order.
3. **Quests** — numbered as the Pip-Boy numbers them, with every solution worth knowing.
4. **Other things to do** — non-quest content: trainers, shops, loot, easter eggs.
5. **Leaving** — what closes off when you go.

Quest headings link to the [Fallout Wiki](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Fallout_2_quests).
See the [Quest Index](/reference/quest-index) for the complete cross-referenced list.

## Timers to be aware of

Fallout 2 has one long clock and several short ones.

- **The game ends after 13 years** of in-game time. A completionist run uses three or four.
  Not a real constraint.
- **Arroyo has a 90-day clock.** After the first Hakunin dream sequence you can no longer
  start or finish the Arroyo side quests, and Hakunin stops healing you and making healing
  powder. After 91 days the Elder no longer hands over the water flask and cash.
- **Arroyo closes entirely** after the fourth dream sequence, which is forced when you leave
  Vault 13 or when you finish preparing the tanker in San Francisco. The bridge is destroyed
  and the map is replaced.
- **The Vault City village** must be dealt with within a month of getting the quest, or the
  villagers are enslaved.

:::danger
The Arroyo bridge becomes a different map entirely. **Any car parked there, and any companion
left on any Arroyo map, is lost permanently.**
:::

## General advice

**Search everything.** Bookcases, shelves, dressers, desks, lockers, chests, footlockers,
fridges, ice chests, bones, pots, tables, workbenches, tool boards, wall safes, floor safes,
ammo crates, poor boxes, pool tables and mining machines are all containers. You will learn to
recognise them on sight.

**Items do not decay.** Anything you drop or leave on a corpse stays there — as long as the map
remains accessible, and as long as you can still *click* on it. An item hidden entirely behind
opaque scenery is gone.

**Critical hits are disabled for the first day** of game time, and critical failures for the
first five days. Aimed shots in the Temple of Trials are wasted effort.

**Learn the hit-and-run.** In melee, attack once and spend the rest of your AP running away.
Most early enemies cannot close the distance and attack in the same turn. Three hexes is
enough against ants and scorpions.

**Save often, and rotate slots.** Turn on `ExtraSaveSlots` — see
[Configuration](/getting-started/configuration).
