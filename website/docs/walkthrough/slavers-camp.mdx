# Slaver's Camp

:::note[An RPU location]
Metzger's slave holding camp is restored content and does not exist in the unmodded game.
:::

Where newly captured slaves are held before being moved on. **Kurisu**, Sulik's sister, is one
of them.

## Getting here

You need <Quest rpu>Find Sulik's missing sister</Quest> active first — it is logged as soon as
Sulik agrees to join you in Klamath. Then any of:

- Ask **Metzger** in the Den about his other holding facilities. The question needs **CH 6** or
  **Speech 51%** to appear, then a **Speech − 10%** roll, and you only get one attempt.
- Ask any of the **slaves you have freed** from Metzger, if your Speech is not up to it
- Buy the location from **Vortis** in [NCR](/walkthrough/ncr) for **$1000** — **$2000** if your
  karma is above 500

## Rescue Kurisu, Sulik's sister
Kurisu is in a cell in the small shack to the northeast.

### The peaceful route

1. Talk to **Don**, the camp leader, and convince him Metzger sent you for his keys. **Two
   Speech rolls**, at **− 15%** and **− 25%** — at 75% Speech that is only about a 30% chance
   overall, and failing either one starts a fight. Opening with "I'm here to join" → "Actually
   yes, I do need something" → "Metzger sent me" skips the first roll, leaving a straight 50%.

   A different slaver stops you on the way in. He wants **Speech 51%** for "Metzger sent me" and
   then a **Speech − 20%** roll — or, with a human companion along and **Speech 60%+**, "I got some
   slaves you guys might be interested in" passes with no roll at all.
2. The shack is watched by a single guard, but only if he sees you — and once Don has handed over
   the keys he waves you through without a word. Otherwise you need **Speech 61%** to open with
   "Metzger sent me to check on his goods"; below that he attacks. When he asks where the usual
   guy is, say he was **killed on a slave run** (a further **Speech − 5%** roll). Claiming the flu
   is an instant fight. Or simply wait until he walks around the back.
3. Open the cell with Don's keys.

### Straight in

Sneak into the shack without talking to anyone and pick the cell lock — but every lock in the camp
is at **− 100%**: − 80% with plain <Item>Lock Picks</Item>, − 60% with an <Item>Expanded Lock Pick
Set</Item>. Even 90% Lockpick leaves you a 30% roll at best with the expanded set, and bare-handed
you need to clear 100% before you have any chance at all. Don's keys are the real route.

### The loud route

Kill everyone. Don's keys are on his body if you did not get them earlier.

## Freeing the rest

Once you have Kurisu you choose whether to slip away with her or free **all** the slaves first —
which means killing every slaver in the camp.

The large slave pen opens with **Don's keys**, or with a difficult lockpick.

When you are ready to leave you are taken automatically to the Umbra Tribe.

| Reward | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Rescuing Kurisu | 1000 |
| Reuniting her with Sulik | 500 |

:::caution
If you leave the slavers alive, they will be **hostile** the next time you come here.
:::

:::tip[Bring backup]
The **NCR Rangers** will help you wipe the camp out — see
[New California Republic](/walkthrough/ncr).
:::
