# Slaver's Camp

:::note An RPU location
Metzger's slave holding camp is restored content and does not exist in the unmodded game.
:::

Where newly captured slaves are held before being moved on. **Kurisu**, Sulik's sister, is one
of them.

## Getting here

You need <Quest rpu>Sulik's sister is missing, find her</Quest> active from the
[Umbra Tribe](/walkthrough/umbra-tribe) first. Then either:

- Ask **Metzger** in the Den about his business — **Speech 51%**
- Ask any of the **slaves you have freed** from Metzger, if your Speech is not up to it

## Rescuing Kurisu

Kurisu is in a cell in the small shack to the northeast.

### The peaceful route

1. Talk to **Don**, the camp leader, and convince him Metzger sent you for his keys. **Two
   Speech rolls** — 75% Speech gives you roughly a 50% chance overall.
2. The shack is watched by a single guard who questions you if he sees you (**Speech 51%** —
   and do **not** claim the usual guy has the flu). Or simply wait until he walks around the
   back.
3. Open the cell with Don's keys.

### Straight in

Sneak into the shack without talking to anyone and pick the cell lock — but that needs
**Lockpick 90%** for a 50% chance.

### The loud route

Kill everyone. Don's keys are on his body if you did not get them earlier.

## Freeing the rest

Once you have Kurisu you choose whether to slip away with her or free **all** the slaves first —
which means killing every slaver in the camp.

The large slave pen opens with **Don's keys**, or with a difficult lockpick.

When you are ready to leave you are taken automatically to the Umbra Tribe.

| Reward | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Rescuing Kurisu | 1000 |
| Reuniting her with Sulik | 500 |

:::caution
If you leave the slavers alive, they will be **hostile** the next time you come here.
:::

:::tip Bring backup
The **NCR Rangers** will help you wipe the camp out — see
[New California Republic](/walkthrough/ncr).
:::
