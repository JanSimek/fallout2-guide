# Vault 13

> *"When I was five, my uncle was decapitated by a watermelon."* — Dave

The vault your ancestor came from, now home to a pack of intelligent deathclaws. This is where the
plot resumes.

## Getting here

Three ways:

- The **leftmost computer on Vault 15 level 3** (Science check)
- **Saltbeef's painting**, bought from Doc Jubilee in NCR for $10,000
- **Following the deathclaws** from Westin's brahmin pastures with **Outdoorsman 100%**

## Arrival

Opening the vault door and entering the first floor is worth **2000 XP**.

Open the inner door and deathclaws appear — but they want to talk. The conversation with **Gruthar**
ends one of three ways: you agree to fix their computer, you are ejected, or there is a fight.

:::danger Evil characters are refused
Karma **−1 or lower**, Childkiller or Berserker status and Gruthar will not let you in. Your options
are sneaking, leaving, or fighting.
:::

:::tip
You can **sneak in or run past Gruthar in combat mode** and nobody treats you differently afterwards.
You can then fix the computer and collect the reward regardless of your morals.
:::

:::caution
Using **Steal, First Aid or Doctor** on Gruthar turns him hostile — he attacks the next time you talk
to him. Almost certainly a bug, but a fatal one.
:::

## Levels 1 and 2

Mostly conversation, and most of it is worth having. The stories are good, and Dave's is very funny.

**The doctor on level 1** heals for free — HP and poison, no companions.

:::danger
**Do not accept healing while irradiated.** Joseph will try to *double* your current dose. How well
he succeeds depends on your Radiation Resistance.
:::

**Matt** on level 2 asks for help escaping. You must ask why he is not allowed on level 1 when the
option first appears, or you never get another chance.

:::danger The Matt crash
If **Matt dies but Gruthar is still alive**, the game **crashes** when you leave and return, or when
you save and reload inside the vault. This happens however he dies.

Do not get him killed unless you are also killing the deathclaws, or you are never coming back.
:::

## Level 3

The lockers here can all be picked with nobody caring. They contain the **G.E.C.K.** — remember your
Arroyo quest — and the essential **NavCom Parts**.

## Quests

### 1. <Quest>Fix the Vault 13 computer</Quest>

The computer is in the **Overseer's room** on level 3. Look inside it to find the problem: it needs a
**Computer Voice Module**.

Sources: **level 2 of the Vault City vault** (free), or **Eldridge** in New Reno ($3000, or $1500
with CH 9 or a critical Barter check). Ask **Jimmy** and he points you at both.

Use the module on the terminal — or just access the terminal with it in your inventory.

Talk to **Gruthar**: **5000 XP**, and he hands you the **G.E.C.K.** if you have not already taken it.

:::danger Collecting the reward triggers the massacre
**Two weeks** after Gruthar pays you, **Frank Horrigan** and an Enclave squad arrive and butcher
every deathclaw except Goris. You can watch the recording on the computer afterwards.

**To keep the deathclaws alive to the end of the game, do not collect the reward.** See
[Endings](/reference/endings).

Killing Gruthar prevents the massacre — and gives you the bad ending anyway.
:::

:::note RPU restores the good ending
In RPU it **is** possible to get Vault 13's good ending. The vault also holds the real explanation of
the **vault experiments**, which ties into what you can learn in the Vault City vault.
<Vanilla>Vault 13 has no good ending, and the experiments are never explained.</Vanilla>
:::

### 2. <Quest>Talk to Goris</Quest>

Gruthar tells you Goris knows more about certain subjects. The quest completes simply by starting a
conversation with him.

Ask him **everything** — asking what he knows about the Enclave puts the **Military Base** on your
map — and then ask him to join your party.

:::danger Ask everything first
Leave the conversation early and you will only ever be able to ask him to join. The questions are
gone.
:::

Goris is one of the best companions in the game. See [Goris](/companions/goris).
