# Vault 15

> *"Uh… I have information that… could lead to establishing a network of… um, all the existing
> vaults throughout the country. That's it."* — The Chosen One

The ruined vault from Fallout 1, now a squatter camp sitting on top of a Khan raider gang.

Tandi puts it on your map with <Quest>Retrieve Parts/Gain Access to Vault 15</Quest>, as does the
Vault City central computer.

## Getting people to talk

Nobody is friendly at first. **Zeke** ignores you, **Dalia** blocks the path northeast. Your only
option is **Rebecca**, standing south of the tent in the middle. Follow her into the tent and
choose carefully — this leads to quest 1.

:::tip[The blunt approach]
Kill Zeke for the keycard, then kill Dalia or start quest 1 to make her move. Killing one squatter
does not turn the others hostile.
:::

## Getting into the vault

Use **Zeke's keycard** on the metal door in the cliff face.

:::danger
Opening the door without the keycard costs you **10–20 damage**. You *can* pick it with a
preposterous **Lockpick 250%** — no lockpick item helps — but you still take damage every time you
open or close it.
:::

Inside, **simply do not talk to the guards** and you never have to fight them. If the woman in front
of the elevator addresses you, tell her your name is **Pat** (needs adequate Intelligence and
Speech) and they take you for one of their own.

:::caution
Failing that conversation turns the Khans on levels 2 and 3 hostile as well.
:::

## Level 1

**The doctor** heals you for free — HP, poison and radiation, no companions. He will not speak to
you at all if you have killed any of the squatters.

The wall terminal by the forcefield emitter toggles the entrance forcefield once power is restored.

## Level 2

:::tip[Do not miss]
**Combat Armor** in the footlocker. The **.223 Pistol** is also worth having.
:::

Use **Science** and then **Repair** on the generator to restore power: **3000 XP**. This also
activates the forcefield at the level 1 entrance.

:::tip[Start the fight here]
If you expect trouble with the Khans, begin the battle on this level. It avoids the exposed starting
position in front of the elevator.
:::

## Level 3

- The **leftmost working computer** gives you the location of **Vault 13** on a Science check.
- The **middle computer** toggles the entrance forcefield. Turn it **off** — it becomes very
  annoying on the way out if you have not already cleared level 1.
- One locker holds the **computer parts** Tandi wants.
- The **H&K P90c** here is loaded with **9mm** ammunition but can only be reloaded with 10mm. A
  known oddity.

## Quests

### 1. <Quest>Rescue Chrissy</Quest>

Someone overheard your conversation with Rebecca. Dalia now lets you past. Leave the map to the
northeast.

Three approaches:

**Quietly:** go along the top of the screen (Sneak helps but is not required), **use Traps twice** on
the north door of the building — once to find the trap, once to disarm it — pick the lock, repeat for
the northeast door, and there is Chrissy.

:::caution
If **Phil** sees you leaving, he attacks immediately.
:::

**Diplomatically:** approach Phil and choose the **"one of us will die"** line. He hands over the key
to the shack — which unlocks and disarms in one action. Do not talk to him again; he attacks.

**Loudly:** kill Phil and Karla. Karla has a **sniper rifle**, so do not stand in the open.

Return to Rebecca: **2500 XP** and an introduction to Zeke, which unlocks quests 2 and 3.

### 2. <Quest>Kill Darion</Quest>

Talk to **Zeke** after quest 1. He asks you to kill Darion and gives you the **keycard**, plus NCR on
your map.

**6000 XP.**

:::tip[Setting up the fight]
You can walk into Darion's room on level 3 without anyone attacking, as long as you do not talk to
them or get too close to him. His guards stay put even if you fight the other Khans on the level.

Position your companions around the room first, then start it by talking to Darion or shooting
someone.
:::

:::caution
After Darion dies everyone in the vault turns hostile — except the doctor — and they will start
drinking the chems you were planning to loot.
:::

### 3. <Quest>Give Spy Holodisk to authority in NCR</Quest>

The computer in Darion's room gives you a **Spy Holodisk** on a Science check.

Tell **Tandi**, then **Gunther**, and show him the disk as proof (**IN 7 required**): **4000 XP and
$4000**. Feargus, the spy, walks away and there is nothing you can do about it.

### 4. <Quest>Restore Vault 15 power</Quest>

See [Level 2](#level-2). **3000 XP.**

### 5. <Quest>Complete Deal with NCR</Quest>

Handled automatically in conversation with **Tandi** when you finish
<Quest>Retrieve Parts/Gain Access to Vault 15</Quest> — unless you completed it by delivering only
the parts.

No XP. It decides Vault 15's ending.

:::danger
The Squat map is **replaced**. Anything you left lying there is inaccessible afterwards. Your car
survives, as do idle companions (except Laddie) and items on critters.

Zeke ends up in an NCR-style house complaining that you doomed the squatters, which is a bug.
:::

:::note
RPU expands **Doctor Jones's** dialogue to give proper closure after you clear out the raiders.
<Vanilla>He has nothing new to say afterwards.</Vanilla>
:::
