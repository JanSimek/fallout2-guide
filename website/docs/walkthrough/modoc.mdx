# Modoc

> *"Was Jonny abducted by aliens?"* / *"Woof?"* / *"Okay, okay… Maybe someplace else, then?"*
> — The Chosen One and Laddie

An ordinary farming community sitting on top of a self-contained mystery worth a great deal of
experience. You will usually stumble across it on the way to Vault City; only **Ed** in Vault
City puts it on your map deliberately.

:::caution RPU can cripple you here
Modoc contains the two events RPU made capable of breaking limbs: the **outhouse explosion**
and the **Ghost Farm trapdoor**. Companions are affected too. Watch for the on-screen
**"Crippled"** and **"NPC Crippled"** messages, and for companions who suddenly refuse to
engage in combat or a character who cannot run.

Crippled limbs do **not** heal with rest or First Aid. You need the **Doctor** skill (retry as
needed), a **Doctor's Bag**, or a town doctor — Vault City is the nearest.
<Vanilla>Neither event can cripple anyone in the original game.</Vanilla>
:::

## Around town

**Jo** runs a fairly pointless shop on the left, restocking every 2–3 days. He gets better
stock if you trigger any of the Slag endings.

**The Bed & Breakfast** in the north serves some notable meals:

| Order | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Brahmin fries | Break the eating record, get slightly poisoned |
| Glass of water, **$1000** | Comes with a cookie. You can steal the money back. |
| Wasteland omelet, **$25** | **Full HP restore and cures poison** |

:::note
If Cornelius dies, Rose stops talking to you. With **IN under 4** you get a free omelet every
single time you talk to her.
:::

## The chicken

You will hear rumours of a mystery chicken cooped up beside the inn — behind a chainlink fence
and guard dogs. Obviously you must investigate.

Pick the door and deal with the dogs. Nearby traders sometimes join in, so be careful. If you
would rather not fight, open the door and leave the map; they will be gone when you return.
Setting off the toilet explosion also tends to jar the gate open and disperse them.

**ST 8** lets you move the rocks in front of the henhouse door for **100 XP**; otherwise use
explosives.

That is some chicken. Kill it for the combat XP if you can — but no more omelets afterwards.

:::danger
Open the henhouse and leave the map **without** killing it, and it will kill everyone on the
map, idle companions included. Come back later and it hunts you down. Your town reputation is
somehow unaffected.
:::

## Bess

South of the slaughterhouse is an injured brahmin. Use **Doctor** on her — keep trying if your
skill is at least 25%; a Doctor's Bag does not help — for **200 XP**.

She follows you around town as long as you stay above ground and inside Modoc. Bring her to
Grisham and you can send her for slaughter (−5 karma). She is moved to the pen north of the
slaughterhouse, where you can either:

- **Open the gate and let all the cattle out** — +10 karma, but your town reputation drops.
- **Come back later** for **100 meat jerky** in a crate in the slaughterhouse.

:::note
Other brahmin in the pen are 2-hex critters and will jam in the gate. Stand yourself or a
companion to one side to let them manoeuvre. After they escape, a **special encounter** waits
for you somewhere in the wilds around Modoc.
:::

:::caution
Leave the map after Bess gets out of the pen but before she gets off the map and she vanishes
with no karma gain and no reputation drop.
:::

## Miria and Davin

Grisham's children. Either can be slept with regardless of your gender, with **CH 8** (or CH 7
with a positive town reputation). Grisham then walks in on you.

With decent Speech, and only if you slept with someone of the opposite sex, you can spin a
story about a medical examination. Otherwise: shotgun wedding. You gain the **Married**
reputation and a companion you cannot dismiss.

:::tip Save first and see it once
The wedding is genuinely funny. But Miria and Davin are useless as companions and **cannot
level up**, so do not keep one unless you plan to sell them to Metzger.
:::

:::caution Put your gear down first
You have to pick every item back up off the floor afterwards. Stash your inventory with a
companion or in the nearby bookcase before knocking on any doors.
:::

:::danger Bugs to avoid
- **Sleeping with Miria or Davin without going through with the wedding** freezes your
  companions in place — they follow you between maps but will not move except in combat. Fixed
  by getting married, having the Val/Vic conversation in Vault City, or watching the Vault 13
  or San Francisco Brotherhood video sequences. It affects companions you recruit *later* too.
- **Killing Grisham first** freezes the game when he is supposed to walk in.
- **Killing Jo first** freezes the game during the wedding ceremony.
- With Miria, **never pick the "you know you want it" line** — despite the wording it is rape,
  and it turns the town hostile. Female characters can trigger it too.
- **Running from Grisham** instead of talking turns all of Modoc hostile.
:::

Marrying gives you a spouse even over your Charisma limit — but dismiss another companion and
you cannot get them back without dismissing someone else, and you can never dismiss the spouse.

## Quests

### 1. <Quest>Farrel has a rodent problem in his garden. Remove the infestation</Quest>

**Farrel** lives southeast of the B&B. **300 XP**, no money.

These rats poison and irradiate on hit, so send companions in and shoot from range. There is a
free **rope** in the garden — you need it for the well.

:::caution
There should be **six large and five small** rats. If the quest will not complete, check the
woods to the right and just off the map edge. Occasionally a rat simply fails to spawn; save
before accepting.

The quest also shares a flag with the butcher on Main Street. If the state gets confused: rest
on Main Street until evening and watch for the butcher's end-of-day float to force the rats to
"killed"; visit Main Street in daylight to force them back to "not killed".
:::

### 2. <Quest>Cornelius has lost his gold pocket watch. Find it and return it to him</Quest>
### 3. <Quest>Farrel wants you to find Cornelius's gold pocket watch. Find it and return it to Farrel</Quest>

Cornelius and Farrel have fallen out over the watch. Cornelius is senile; tell him he "needs
serious help" and he gives you the quest. Farrel gives you the same job, and one watch strikes
out both entries.

**Where the watch is:** go to the toilet, open it, and climb down into a dung-filled cave
blocked to the north by rubble. Plant **dynamite** at the rubble and use the ladder.

This is the famous **toilet explosion**. You have just redecorated half of Modoc. **500 XP.**

Go back down, north, kill a rat and search the nest for **Cornelius's gold watch**, a
**shovel**, a **fruit**, and a very useful **bag**.

Hand the watch to either man and say the rat had it: **1500 XP**.

:::caution Placement matters
The blast must **touch** the rock pile — within about two paces — to trigger properly. You and
your companions take damage if you are inside the blast radius when you climb the ladder; the
game always detonates explosives when you leave a map, which is why the timer does not matter.

Set the dynamite as close to the rocks as you can, and push companions clear before climbing.
This is one of the two places RPU can cripple a limb.
:::

:::danger
After the explosion the B&B map is **replaced with a new one**. Anything dropped here or stored
in a container — except the lost and found box — is lost. Companions survive the transition,
except Laddie.
:::

:::tip Finding the bag
Look at the lower tip of the crescent formed by the rat's nest. It uses the same graphic as a
bag of coins, with a small brown "flag" just visible above the trash.
:::

**Evil variants:**

- Tell Cornelius that Farrel keeps the watch in the "safe" (a locker) in his house. They fight.
  No XP, large reputation drop, and the locker is empty anyway. Pointless.
- Return the watch to Cornelius and say **Farrel stole it**: **500 XP**.

### 4. <Quest>Jonny is missing. Find him and bring him home to Balthas</Quest>

**Balthas** the leatherworker has lost his son. You need **PE 6** to notice, **IN 6** to offer
help, and a **Neutral or better** town standing for him to give you the quest.

Jonny is not in Modoc — see [The Ghost Farm](#the-ghost-farm) below. But there is a diversion
here: go to the **town well**, remove the boards, use the **rope**, and climb down. On the
ground are some coins (−1 karma each; take them anyway) and, at the north end, a **BB gun**.

:::caution
Show Balthas the BB gun and he concludes Jonny is dead — and keeps the gun, which is worth $300
loaded. Probably not what you want.
:::

**Laddie:** if you are **Accepted or better** in Modoc, Balthas sends Jonny's dog with you. He
joins even over your Charisma limit. Follow him to the well and talk to him — the dialogue is
worth the detour.

:::tip
If Laddie will not follow you, your reputation is too low. Do a simple quest first — guarding
Grisham's brahmin or killing Farrel's rats. Do **not** open with setting Bess free; that
reputation hit is hard to recover from.
:::

:::caution
Bring Jonny's BB gun onto the Main Street map while Laddie is there and he enters permanent
woofing mode, after which you cannot talk to him at all. Park him at the B&B while you fetch
the rifle.
:::

:::note
This is the only time in the entire game you use a **rope**.
:::

### 5. <Quest>Guard brahmin herd for Grisham</Quest>

Grisham offers as you end a conversation. Agree and you are teleported to the pastures. **Ten
dogs** come from the north and gang up on a cow.

Payment is **$1000 minus $100 per brahmin killed**, plus **250 XP**.

:::caution
Save *every* brahmin and a bug denies you the XP and the reputation gain — you only get the
money. Let at least one die.
:::

:::tip
The dogs likely out-Sequence you. Run forward a little and enter combat mode immediately so
they waste their opening turn closing the distance. A frag grenade into the largest pack pulls
most of them onto you and off the cattle.
:::

:::caution
Leave the map without killing the dogs and things get buggy — Grisham demands $1500 from you
every time you speak to him thereafter, and paying has no effect.
:::

### 6. <Quest>Help Bess the Brahmin</Quest>

See [Bess](#bess) above. **200 XP.**

### 7. <Quest>Kill the "chicken"</Quest>

See [The chicken](#the-chicken) above.

### 8. <Quest>Something strange is happening at the farm northeast of Modoc. Investigate and report back to Jo</Quest>

Ask **Jo** about a G.E.C.K. He gives you this quest and puts the **Ghost Farm** on your map.

Two outcomes:

- **Kill the Slags** and report back: **500 XP**. Jo is unhappy if you admit they were ordinary
  people rather than ghosts.
- **Talk to them** and take quest 9 instead. Much better.

:::caution
Report that you killed the Slags, then leave and return, and **Balthas and Jo will no longer
trade with you**. The same happens if the townspeople attack the farm during quests 9–10.
:::

### 9. <Quest>Deliver Slag message to Jo in Modoc</Quest>

Bring Vegeir's message to Jo. You move on to quest 10 unless you immediately tell Jo you want
out, which pays **700 XP** and leaves everyone in a strange holding pattern: townsfolk talk
about a deal that never happens, the Slags wait for it, and Jo never speaks to you again. After
**31 days** Modoc attacks the farm.

:::note From this point on
Rose lets you search the **lost and found box** and gives free omelets forever, but you can no
longer order anything else. The box is often empty but sometimes holds knives, throwing knives,
stimpaks, 10mm JHP, a leather jacket or a 10mm pistol — and, at odds of about 1 in 200 per map
entry, a **10mm SMG**.

Stand on the hex immediately to the right of it and you can loot it freely.
:::

:::note IN under 4
A stupid character gets **2000 XP** for delivering the note and triggers Modoc's happy ending
outright, but never receives quest 10.
:::

### 10. <Quest>Jo is suspicious of the Slags. Find out about the dead bodies at the Ghost Farm and find out what happened to Karl</Quest>

Jo gives you **31 days** — enough time to walk to the Den and back.

Tell him about the **fake bodies** and about **Karl being in the Den** and you broker an
alliance between Modoc and the Slags: **3500 XP**. Jo tells you about Gecko without putting it
on your map, and gives you a one-time discount large enough to clear out his entire stock if
you trade back and forth.

You can then return to the Den and tell **Karl** to go home: **500 XP**.

:::caution
If you do not give Jo both pieces at once, **tell him about Karl before the bodies**, or you
will not be able to send Karl home.
:::

**The bad endings:** tell Jo you want out (700 XP), or tell him to attack. The attack teleports
you to the farm, where the townsfolk turn out to be a serious fighting force. You kill a few
guards and ghosts and the game assumes the rest died too. No quest XP, and **Jonny cannot be
saved**. Missing the 31-day deadline produces the same result without you.

:::tip
Run off the map during the attack and, when you return, the Slag protectors will be dead and
the Modoc townspeople will have gone home alive.
:::

### 11. <Quest>Jonny's in the Slag caves. Find a way to get Jonny back home to Balthas</Quest>

Appears once you talk to Jonny, if you have quest 4. **Vegeir** will not release him until
quests 9 and 10 are done. After that he even provides an escort. Talk to Balthas for a
**combat leather jacket and 2500 XP**.

### 12. <Quest>Go to the Den and tell Karl it's alright to come back home</Quest>

See quest 10. **500 XP.**

## The Ghost Farm

> *"Oh no! Don't you know Rose uses dog in her dishes!? Laddie is probably a burger by now."*
> — Jonny

Blood patches on the ground and bodies impaled on stakes. Examine the bodies with good
Perception, or several times over, and you will notice they are **fakes**. At night, white
shapes drift through the corn, sounding distinctly unghostly.

**Two ways to meet the Slag leader:**

1. Walk into the building and step on the **carpet** in the middle of the room. You fall
   fifteen feet into a cave and are met by armed guards.
   :::caution
   This is the second RPU crippling event. Check your limbs afterwards.
   :::
2. Wait until **midnight**, when a patrol appears outside the farm. Talk to them and surrender.

Refuse to go with them and you will have to fight everyone here.

**Vegeir** wants to open negotiations with Modoc and asks you to carry a message. Accept or
shoot your way out — but either way you are given free run of the caves, so loot everything.
There is an easily missed row of **shovels** in the room under the pitfall.

:::note
Because of how the Slag critters are distributed, you do not have to kill everyone above and
below ground for the game's "Slag counter" to reach zero and let you report back to Jo.
:::
