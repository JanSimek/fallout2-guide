# Lenny

**Found in:** [Gecko](/walkthrough/gecko), the Manager's Office

A ghoul doctor. Useful as a pack mule with a healing skill, and not much more. Take him only if you
favour large parties.

Talk to him about your ancestor the Vault Dweller and he joins, provided your karma is at least 1.
Recruiting him is **+5 karma** and improves your standing in Gecko, so do it even if you dismiss him
straight away.

:::caution
Do not get into his **"Vault 13 owes us one"** G.E.C.K. thread — it shares a variable with Harold's
stimpak reward, and you cannot have both.
:::

## Statistics

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Main skill** | **Doctor** |
| **Also good at** | Small Guns |
| **Animations** | Knife, pistol, SMG |
| **HP by level** | 129 · 154 · 181 · 206 |
| **AP by level** | 7 · 7 · 7 · 7 |

Enormous HP, and **7 AP forever** — he never gets faster.

He is the only companion who heals with **Doctor** rather than First Aid: 10–25 HP instead of 5–15.

## Weapons

Anything with a **Strength requirement of 4** — a Desert Eagle is ideal. A 10mm SMG then H&K P90c if
you do not mind him spraying the party. In Power Armor he can manage a **.223 Pistol**, **H&K G11E**
or **Gauss Pistol**.

## RPU changes

- He carries a **zip gun** when you first meet him.
- Give him a **radioactive drink** and he **glows in the dark**. A RadAway turns it off.
- The optional **new Lenny model** looks better but uses rifles instead of pistols and has **no
  running animation**, so he permanently trails behind.

<Vanilla>No zip gun, no glow, and only the one model.</Vanilla>

## Losing him

- **Leaves** if you become a Childkiller or Slaver, drop to karma −101, or have **CH 1**
- **Attacks** if you are in Gecko having wrecked the plant or turned the ghouls hostile
- **Will not rejoin** if your Gecko reputation is Hated or worse — killing one or two ghouls is fine

:::danger
If you cause a meltdown in Gecko, everyone there **vanishes 24 hours later**, Lenny included — along
with everything he was carrying.
:::
