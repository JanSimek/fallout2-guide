# Lenny

> *"I'm h'happier than a bull b'brahmin in a pasture-full o' cows! I'd b'be honored to j'join you. I m'may
> be old and I cain't m'move t'too darn fast, leastwasy not for too long, b'but I kin sure p'patch you up
> when y'you need it."* — Lenny

Lenny is Harold's assistant in Gecko, a ghoul who was a doctor before the war. He is no great fighter —
7 AP for good and Small Guns as his only real combat skill — but he has plenty of hit points, a Doctor
skill that climbs from 85% to 125%, and recruiting him is worth **+5 karma**. Before he joins, he also
heals you cheaply, or for nothing.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [Gecko](/walkthrough/gecko), on the Settlement map, standing by Harold |
| **Joins if** | you asked about the G.E.C.K. and your ancestor, karma above 0, his reaction above 30, CH 2+, IN 4+, a free slot |
| **Costs** | Nothing. +5 karma and +10 Gecko reputation the first time; no XP |
| **Best at** | Doctor 85%, Barter 80%, First Aid 75%, Small Guns 70% |
| **Weapons** | Knife, spear and rifle (default look); knife, pistol and SMG with `lenny_new_look=0`. Fists and throwing with either |
| **Levels** | 3 level-ups, one for every five of your level-ups from level 10 while he is with you ([how it works](/companions/overview)) |

## Recruiting

He has nothing to say to a character with **IN 3 or less** except to go home.

The way in is his memory of your ancestor, and it starts with the G.E.C.K.:

1. *"Lenny, have you ever heard of something called a Garden of Eden Creation Kit?"* →
   *"Well, my ancestor, the Vault Dweller, said that Vault 13 owes us one. I need to find it."*
   Now he knows who you are.
2. *"You met my ancestor?"* → *"You saw the Vault Dweller? Where?"* → *"Running?"* → *"You wanted to join
   my grandsire?"* → *"What do you mean?"* → *"My ancestor was the founder of our tribe…"*
3. *"I am following in my ancestor's footsteps. Would you like another chance? Will you join me?"* —
   shown only when you have a free slot.

He says yes if your **karma is above 0** and his **reaction to you is above 30**; otherwise *"I have work
to do here. I c'cannot join you."* He also turns you down at CH 1.

:::danger[Ask him before you take the G.E.C.K.]
The G.E.C.K. question disappears for good once Gruthar hands you the G.E.C.K. in
[Vault 13](/walkthrough/vault-13). If you had not asked it by then, *"You met my ancestor?"* never
appears — Lenny can never be recruited, and his healing is never free.
:::

### Getting his reaction above 30

Your karma itself adds nothing to Lenny's reaction. It starts at **5 × (your CH − 2)**; half your Gecko
reputation is added, a good karma title adds up to 20 and the Presence perk 10, and Sex Appeal gives a
female character 20 but costs a male one 20. The conversation above adds 25, so with **CH 4 or more** and nothing counting
against you, you are over the line.

If he still refuses, ask *"What do you do here?"* → *"You can heal people?"* and hear out his pre-war
story, taking the sympathetic answers down to *"I'm sorry to hear that, but I think you're right. Let me
ask you about something else."* That adds 50, and **it can be repeated**.

### Healing in Gecko

Until he joins, Lenny is a doctor for hire. Ask what he does and he offers:

| Option | Heals | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *"I'm wounded. Can you help me?"* | You, to full HP, crippled limbs included. Takes 8 hours. | $30, or $60 with a crippled limb |
| *"One of my friends is hurt. Can you help them?"* | You and every hurt or crippled companion, to full. Takes 8 hours. | $30, or $60 for more than one patient (you count) |

It is **free once he knows you are the Vault Dweller's descendant** (the G.E.C.K. line above), and if you
are short he takes what you have and heals you anyway. He does not treat poison or radiation.

:::caution[The healing ends when he joins]
While he is in your party, or waiting for you, talking to him opens the party menu, which has no healing
service. Get patched up first.
:::

## Westin's heart pills

This belongs to the RPU content in [New Reno](/walkthrough/new-reno). Once you know about Westin's drugs,
or have Cassidy along, his party menu offers *"I need a professional opinion, Lenny. What do you know
about managing a heart condition?"*; while you carry <Item>Heart Pills</Item>, *"Can you tell me anything
about these pills I found, Doc?"* His verdict on heart pills taken with New Reno's pep pills:
*"Your heart'll explode like a b'blood s'sausage."*

Hearing him out unlocks:

- Renesco's *"I need you to fix me up with a special order."* without going through Doc Jubilee, and his
  *"complex PDE5-inhibitor compound"* option without Science 80%;
- a line at Doc Jubilee that needs Science 80% **and** Lenny's advice.

## Gear

He carries a <Item>Pipe Rifle</Item> in his pack, not in his hands, with one round of
<Item>10mm JHP</Item> loaded and a pack of 24 more. Its ST 5 requirement costs him 20% to hit.
<Vanilla>He carries nothing.</Vanilla>

### Weapons and the new look

RPU's `lenny_new_look` option (in `mods/rpu.ini`) picks his art, and the art decides what he can hold.
**The default is `1`**, the new look.

| Setting | Look | Weapon classes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lenny_new_look=1` *(default)* | New ghoul art | Knife, spear, rifle (rifles and shotguns) |
| `lenny_new_look=0` | Original ghoul art | Knife, pistol, SMG |

The option can be changed mid-game in either direction. It is read when a game is loaded and applied
then and on every map change, and a weapon the new art cannot hold is taken out of his hand first. His
*"what weapons can you use"* answer follows the setting. The new art has no running frames, so he may
trail behind when you run.
<Unverified>The missing frames are confirmed; whether he actually falls behind in play is
not.</Unverified>

He has **ST 4** (each point short of a weapon's requirement is −20% to hit), **7 AP** (one shot a turn
with any of the guns below) and Small Guns as his only real weapon skill. Compare the options on
[Object Database](/database).

**Default look — rifles and shotguns:**

- <Item>Shotgun</Item> — 12–22, ST 4, and its Accurate perk adds 20% to hit. The best fit early on.
- <Item>Sawed-Off Shotgun</Item> — 12–24, ST 4 and Accurate, but only 7 hexes of range.
- <Item>Combat Shotgun</Item> (15–25) and <Item>Pancor Jackhammer</Item> (18–29) — ST 5, so the −20%
  cancels against Accurate's +20%. Both can burst, see below.
- <Item>M72 Gauss Rifle</Item> — 32–43 at 50 hexes; ST 6 costs 40%, less 20% for Accurate.
- <Item>Hunting Rifle</Item> (8–20) and <Item>Sniper Rifle</Item> (14–34) — long range, but ST 5 with
  nothing to offset the penalty.

**With `lenny_new_look=0` — pistols and SMGs:**

- <Item>Desert Eagle .44</Item> (10–16), <Item>14mm Pistol</Item> (12–22, Accurate), <Item>H&K P90c</Item>
  (12–16; single shots for 4 AP, 12-round bursts for 5) and <Item>10mm SMG</Item> (5–12) all need only
  ST 4.
- <Item>PPK12 Gauss Pistol</Item> — 22–32, ST 4, Accurate, 50 hexes. The pick of them late in the game.

Knives work in either look, spears only in the new one, all on his Melee Weapons of 50%. A
<Item>Combat Knife</Item> (3 AP) gets two stabs a turn.

:::caution[He bursts without asking]
His Burst setting offers only *Always!* or *Sometimes* — none of the careful options. With a Combat
Shotgun, Jackhammer or SMG he bursts half the time on his default *Sometimes*, checked only by the engine's weak last-moment
safety test. Party Orders' **D** key ("Don't use bursts!") makes him — and the rest of the party — fire
single shots with those weapons.
:::

### Armour

Lenny is one of the companions the combat control screen's *Use Best Armor* works on: give him armour
and press it. His look never changes, since the bundled NPC Armor mod does not cover him.

<Item>Power Armor</Item> and Hardened Power Armor give party members **+3 Strength** as well, which takes
Lenny to ST 7 — enough for every rifle and shotgun above. They weigh 85 and 100 lb against his 125 lb
carrying capacity. The two Advanced Power Armors give +4 (ST 8) and weigh 90 and 100 lb.

His gear menu has *"I need you to remove the armor that you're wearing."* while he wears some.

### Radioactive drinks

Give him <Item>Gamma Gulp Beer</Item>, <Item>Roentgen Rum</Item> or <Item>Rot Gut</Item> and he says
*"I'm f'feeling a b'bit warm now."* — and **glows** until you give him <Item>RadAway</Item>
(*"That's b'better."*).
<Vanilla>He does not glow.</Vanilla>

## Healing

- **Skilldex Doctor.** If his Doctor skill is higher than yours and everyone else's in the party, using
  Doctor from your Skilldex has Lenny do it. It is his best skill at every stage.
- **"You should heal yourself now."** uses a healing item he carries if he has one. Only with none does
  he roll his **Doctor** skill — 10–25 HP for an hour. Take his stimpaks away first if you would rather he
  spent the hour than the stimpak.
- **Automatically, out of combat**, he uses a carried <Item>Super Stimpak</Item>, <Item>Stimpak</Item>,
  <Item>Healing Powder</Item>, <Item>Doctor's Bag</Item> or <Item>First Aid Kit</Item> when he drops to
  60% HP (his default *Stimpaks when hurt a bit*) or 50% (*any drug*). *Stimpaks when hurt a lot* is
  not one of his options, so 30% is not available for Lenny. A First Aid
  Kit or Doctor's Bag uses up one of the same three daily uses you have.
- **In combat** he takes healing items below 60% HP on the default setting, 2 AP each.
- **He never heals you or the other companions** while he is in the party.

Party Orders' **F** key works for him too; its free medical roll uses his Doctor skill.

## Using him

**Combat settings.** Distance can only be *Stay close to me* or *Stay where you are* — he never charges.
Weapon preference is *None* or *Ranged then melee* (the default); only *None* lets him throw anything.
By default he goes for the weakest enemy.

**Places that object to him:**

- **Vault City** shuts and locks its gate while he is in your party, unless you are Captain of the Guard
  or the Enclave is destroyed. Tell him to **wait** outside — a waiting companion is no longer in the
  party list, so the guard stops objecting.
- **Dr. Troy** shouts *"A ghoul! Guards!"* and turns hostile if Lenny is with you, unless you are Captain
  of the Guard. (Talking to him also works once the Enclave is destroyed, but the check he repeats every
  20–40 seconds ignores that.)
- **Lynette** is set against you from the start if you talk to her with him along, and two of the replies
  make her hostile.
- **Lou** and **Josh** in [Redding](/walkthrough/redding) only brush you off with a remark.

:::danger[Do not loot him while he is unconscious]
Looting an unconscious Lenny runs the same script handler as stealing from him, and his has no exception
for party members: he turns hostile at once.
:::

## Losing him

He checks these constantly while he is in the party:

| Cause | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Karma below −100 (the Karma Beacon does not count) | Leaves: *"Too many bad things happen around you. I am leaving before something happens to me."* |
| Childkiller reputation | Leaves and **never speaks to you again** |
| Slaver reputation | Leaves — and again every time you take him back |
| **The Gecko plant is destroyed, a meltdown has started, or its destruction is set up** | Leaves, never speaks to you again, and **attacks on sight** |
| Your CH drops to 1 | Leaves |
| Gecko is hostile to you while you are on a Gecko map | **Attacks you** as soon as he can see you — his script checks this before any reason to leave. Only if he cannot see you does he leave, for good |

Settle <Quest>Solve the Gecko powerplant problem</Quest> peacefully if you want to keep him.

- **After "wait here"**, *"Let's go."* is refused — and he leaves the party — at CH 1, with a full party,
  or when your Gecko reputation is −15 or worse (*"…You're a d'disgrace to the Vault Dweller's
  m'memory…"*).
- **Hitting him while he is not in your party** (before he joins, or while he waits) makes him attack you;
  on any Gecko map the town turns hostile too. While he is with you, the game never runs that check, so
  a stray shot does not set him off.
- **Being caught stealing** from him while he is not in your party makes him hostile.
- **After a meltdown**, a timer starts the first time you enter the Settlement; come back **more than 24
  hours later** and the townsfolk are gone — Lenny with them, unless he is in your party.

If he dies, nothing special happens to his gear. He has no ending slide.
