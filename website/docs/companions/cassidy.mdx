# Cassidy

John Cassidy runs the bar in Vault City's Courtyard and is sick of the place. Take him: he costs
nothing, recruiting him is worth 300 XP, and by his last level-up he has **160% Small Guns** and a
36% critical chance. The one thing to remember is his heart — **never use Jet, Buffout or Psycho on
him** until you have cured it.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [Vault City](/walkthrough/vault-city), in his bar in the Courtyard, outside the vault |
| **Joins if** | Intelligence 4+, Charisma 2+, a free party slot, karma −100 or better, not a Slaver or Childkiller |
| **Costs** | Nothing |
| **Best at** | Small Guns 95%, Melee Weapons 90%, Energy Weapons 80%, Unarmed 80%, Barter 80% |
| **Weapons** | Pistols, rifles and shotguns, spears (not in combat armour), unarmed weapons |
| **Levels** | 4 level-ups, one for every four of your own from your level 10 on, while he is in the party — see [levelling](/companions/overview#levelling) |

:::note[Talking head]
RPU gives Cassidy a talking head and voiced lines (head from Mutants Rising, voice by Joey Bracken).
The manual zip install always includes them; the Windows installer lists them as an optional
component, **"Talking head and voice for Cassidy"**. Without the files he simply talks in a plain
dialogue window. <Vanilla>Cassidy has no talking head.</Vanilla>
:::

## Recruiting

Every useful line needs **Intelligence 4 or more** (Smooth Talker ranks count). A dim character only
gets to grunt at him, and he answers "Sorry, I don't speak imbecile."

He does not offer at once. Get him talking about Vault City — ask what he has on tap or whether
anything interesting is going on, then keep going: "It can't be that bad here", the raid, what it is
like most of the time, why he stays (his bad heart), and finally "Why don't you leave?" That opens
**"I'm heading out that way again. Why don't you come along?"** Accept with "Sure. Why the hell
not?" and "Let's hit it."

The first time he joins you get **300 XP** and **+1 Vault City reputation**. He hangs an "out of
business" sign on the bar, and the first time you enter the Courtyard with him, the three bar
regulars are removed for good.
Once he has made the offer, later conversations open with the shortcut "You looking to get out of
here yet?"

He also sells drinks — $20 a beer, $30 a whiskey — but the script only takes your money. You get
no item.

### When he says no

| He refuses if | What he says |
| --- | --- |
| Your Charisma is exactly 1 | "No offense or nothin', but you don't look too capable of leading anybody…" |
| You have the Slaver reputation | "Naw, I don't travel with slavers…" |
| You are a Childkiller, or your karma is below −100 | "Naw, you have the look of somebody with one foot in the grave and the other in hell…" |
| Your party is full | "Naw... you look like you got enough of a crowd already." |

A refusal costs nothing — fix the problem and ask again. Two perks change the karma test:
**Karma Beacon** doubles your karma for it, so he refuses below −50, and with **Cult of
Personality** he never refuses on karma at all.

## Clearing his name

Cassidy has no Pip-Boy quest, but he has a record. With him in the party, ask **Sergeant Stark**
"You the one who busted up Cassidy's bar?" — the line stays on offer until you have settled the matter
as Captain of the Guard.

- **As Captain of the Guard** you can order Stark to pardon Cassidy and pay restitution. Stark hands
  **you** **$500**, and you get **500 XP** and **−3 Vault City reputation**.
- Otherwise Stark insists Cassidy was guilty. You can drop it, or pick a fight with him ("You know, I
  think my fist and your face are about to have a conversation.").

## Gear

He arrives with a <Item>Sawed-Off Shotgun</Item>, some <Item>12 ga. Shotgun Shells</Item>,
a <Item>Rot Gut</Item>, $72 and the leather armour he is wearing. Asked, he says he is best with
"shotguns and rifles, mostly", not bad with pistols, and "can also use a spear pretty good".

### What he can hold

RPU ships the NPC Armor mod, which limits Cassidy to **pistols, rifles and shotguns, spears and
unarmed weapons**. Everything else is refused: knives, clubs, sledgehammers, SMGs — including the
<Item>14mm Pistol</Item> and <Item>.223 Pistol</Item>, which use the SMG animation — and every big
gun, which rules out the <Item>Laser Rifle</Item>, <Item>Plasma Rifle</Item> and the other
laser-rifle-class weapons. In combat armour he also loses his spear animation. The restriction comes from the mod's sfall
global script; that it works under FOR:CE has been checked in the source, not in a running game.

RPU's optional "New rifle animations" component gives the Hunting Rifle, Scoped Hunting Rifle,
Sniper Rifle and the three FN FALs a new animation; Cassidy is allowed that one too.

His **Strength of 6** meets the requirement of every pistol, rifle and shotgun, so none of them
costs him to-hit.

### What to give him

| When | Weapon | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At once | <Item>.44 Magnum Revolver</Item> | 12–18 damage for **4 AP**, so with 8 AP he fires twice a turn — 24–36 against the Sawed-Off's single 12–24 (5 AP) |
| Mid game | <Item>Combat Shotgun</Item> or <Item>Pancor Jackhammer</Item> | 15–25 and 18–29 per shell; both fire a 6 AP burst |
| Long range | <Item>Sniper Rifle</Item> | 14–34 at range 50, 6 AP |
| Late | <Item>M72 Gauss Rifle</Item> | 32–43 at range 50 for 5 AP — two shots a turn once he has 10 AP at stage 4 |
| Late, pistol | <Item>PPK12 Gauss Pistol</Item> or <Item>YK32 Pulse Pistol</Item> | 22–32 and 32–46 for 4 AP, two shots a turn from the start; the Pulse Pistol uses his Energy Weapons |

Give him **one** gun at a time. When he switches weapons, the AI ranks them by average damage
(doubled for a weapon with a perk) and ignores AP cost, so it never prefers a 4 AP pistol for
firing twice. Compare anything else on [Weapons and Armour](/equipment).

For a brawler Cassidy, <Item>Spiked Knuckles</Item> (4–10), a <Item>Power Fist</Item> (12–24) or a
Mega Power Fist (20–40) all work with his Unarmed 80–140%. **Set his Weapon Pref. to "Unarmed"
first**: punch weapons count as unarmed attacks, and his default "Ranged then melee" never uses them.
"None" allows them too, but ranks any gun he carries above them. A <Item>Sharpened Spear</Item> (4–12, 4 AP) is the melee alternative.

Only his metal- and power-armour looks (and the borrowed advanced-power-armour and robe looks) have a
throwing animation; in his starting leather look he never throws grenades.

## Using him

His default settings are "Ranged then melee", "Stimpaks when hurt a lot", "On your own", run away
at "You need a tourniquet", Burst "Be careful not to hit me" and "Whomever you want". His
Burst options stop at "Sometimes" — he cannot be set to "Always!" — and he has no Coward
disposition. What each setting does is on the [overview](/companions/overview).

Outside combat he patches himself up from his own supplies — super stimpak, stimpak, healing powder,
doctor's bag, first aid kit — once his HP drops to 60% ("Stimpaks when hurt a bit"), 30% (the
default) or 50% (either "Any drug" setting). Asked to heal himself with no supplies, he uses First
Aid.

Power Armor's +3 Strength adds 6% to his Unarmed and Melee. On the Combat Control panel his best
skill can then read Melee Weapons (as recruited) or Unarmed (stages 1–2) instead of Small Guns. It
is only a label — weapon choice does not look at it — and from stage 3 Small Guns is back on top.

His attack barks include "Gonna grind you into a thin red paste...", "I'm gonna reduce you to an
undertaker's nightmare." and "Gonna blast you into ground round...". With
[Myron](/companions/myron) along he will offer to put a bullet in Myron's head. He never does.

## His heart

:::danger[Jet, Buffout and Psycho kill him]
Use any of the three on Cassidy and he gasps "What th--?! No, my heart! I...\*hrrrkkk\*", collapses,
and a moment later "Cassidy dies of a heart attack." Every other item is safe: stimpaks, super
stimpaks, alcohol and healing powder just get a comment, and he refuses cigarettes.
:::

The heart attack only happens when **you** use the drug on him. If he takes a combat chem himself —
possible only with Chem Use set to "Any drug some of the time" or "Any drug any time" — nothing
happens.

### Curing it

<Item>Heart Pills</Item> cure him for good. Use one on him and he says "Ah, that actually feels
good." From then on Jet, Buffout and Psycho work on him like on anyone else ("Those heart pills must
be working."). One use is enough, and — going by the script and FOR:CE's source — the pill is not used up.

The certain source is **Mr. Chemmie**, the drug computer on the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa#violet-level)'s
Violet Level. With **Science 101%** and **Cassidy in the party**, its advanced menu offers nitroglycerin
once; choose "Create." and it makes one pill, with no ingredients.

Other places deal in heart pills too. Doc Jubilee in NCR has lines about them while Cassidy is with
you. <Unverified>Whether you can get a real pill from him was not traced through his
script.</Unverified> At the [Abbey](/walkthrough/abbey), once you tell Brother John you have heart
pills, he takes one whether you give it or sell it — use one on Cassidy first. [Lenny](/companions/lenny) has advice on the subject too.
<Vanilla>Heart pills do nothing for Cassidy, and the condition cannot be cured.</Vanilla>

## Losing him

- **Hurt him while he is not in your party** (before you recruit him, or while he waits) and he
  turns hostile, attacking on sight whenever he is not in your party. That is never reset — but his
  dialogue does not check it, so if you get to talk to him before he sees you, he can still be
  recruited. Hitting him while he is **in** your party — on purpose or with a stray burst — does not
  set him off.
- Get **caught stealing** from him while he is not in your party and he turns hostile too. Looting
  him while he is unconscious in the party is safe.
- **He never leaves over karma** or a reputation you pick up after he joins. But if you tell him to
  **wait**, asking him back runs the same checks as recruiting him (Charisma 1, Slaver, Childkiller
  or karma, full party), and if you fail he stays behind.
- **Selling him** is possible. **Metzger** in [The Den](/walkthrough/the-den) pays 2 × 650 × (160 + your
  Barter) ÷ (160 + Metzger's Barter), keeps Cassidy's gear, and costs 15 karma. **Big Jesus Mordino**
  in [New Reno](/walkthrough/new-reno) offers 100 chips, or 200 if a haggle succeeds, and you keep
  the gear; asking costs 3 karma, haggling another 3, the sale 10. He only asks about Cassidy when
  Miria, Davin, Sulik and Vic are not with you.
- If he dies, his gear stays on the body — until you leave the map or load a save, when a companion
  killed in combat vanishes with everything he carried (see [Death](/companions/overview#death)).
