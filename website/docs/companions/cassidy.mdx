# Cassidy

**Found in:** [Vault City](/walkthrough/vault-city), the Courtyard bar

Quite possibly the best companion in the game. Excellent with a rifle and a capable hand-to-hand
fighter. Talk to him about life in Vault City and he joins: **300 XP**.

:::note
RPU gives Cassidy a **talking head**, restored and well voiced. Turn it on in the installer.
<Vanilla>He has no talking head.</Vanilla>
:::

He handles energy pistols well. In a melee party: **Spiked Knuckles**, then **Mega Power Fist**.

## His heart

:::danger[Never give Cassidy Psycho, Buffout or Jet]
His heart condition kills him. Every other drug is fine, Super Stimpaks included.
:::

:::tip[RPU can cure him]
Take Cassidy to **Mr. Chemmie** on the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa) Violet Level with **Science 101%** and
you can synthesise **Heart Pills**. Using them on Cassidy cures the condition permanently.
<Vanilla>The heart pills found in NCR have nothing to do with Cassidy, despite decades of rumour, and
the condition cannot be cured.</Vanilla>
:::

## Quirks

- **His combat settings reset regularly.** A known bug, and genuinely annoying — check them
  periodically.
- **Power Armor switches his specialty to Melee Weapons.** Not a bug: at low levels his Small Guns
  skill is within 6 points of his Unarmed and Melee, and Power Armor's +3 Strength pushes those past
  it. It corrects itself as he levels.
- He recites combat messages from **Wasteland** — "ground round", "thin red paste", "undertaker's
  nightmare". Some of the best barks in the game.

## Losing him

Will not join a **Slaver, Childkiller**, or anyone at **karma −101 or lower** — but he does **not
leave** if you become one after recruiting him.

You can also **restore his good name**: as Captain of the Guard in Vault City, order **Sergeant
Stark** to pardon him and pay restitution. **$500 and 500 XP.**
