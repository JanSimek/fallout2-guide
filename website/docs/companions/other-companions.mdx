# Other Companions

Four more critters can join your party for longer than a quest. None of them ever levels up.
**Laddie** is free and harmless. The **pariah dog** is a curse you should
kill. **Davin** and **Miria** come with a shotgun wedding and are hard to get rid of. General rules
are on the [Companions](/companions/overview) page.

## The pariah dog

A ragged dog with a glass eye that *"could expire at any moment"*. It joins you whether you like it or
not, drags your Luck down to nothing and never fights. **Kill it.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | The Pariahs [special encounter](/systems/random-encounters#special-encounters), from player level 6 |
| **Joins if** | You fail a Luck roll while it can see you. You don't get a say. |
| **Costs** | Your Luck, the Jinxed perk, and a party slot |
| **Best at** | Surviving: 750 HP, AC 25, 40% normal DR. It never attacks. |
| **Weapons** | None |
| **Levels** | None |

### How it joins

The encounter can happen **from level 6**, anywhere in the desert, and normally only once. While the
dog can see you and isn't in your party, it rolls against your Luck. The roll is repeated every 10
seconds, and again as soon as a map loads. The first time a roll fails, *"The dog seems to take a
liking to you. This can't be good."*, and it has joined.

- A roll fails when a random 1–10 is higher than your Luck. **Luck 10 is immune.** At Luck 9 a
  roll fails 10% of the time, at Luck 5 half the time.
- There is **no party-size check**. It joins over your Charisma limit, then **counts against it** like
  any other companion.
- It has no dialogue, so there is no refusing it. **Leave the map before a roll fails**, or accept
  that you will have to kill it.

### What it does to you

- **Jinxed.** You get the Jinxed perk. While you have it, **any** missed attack has a 50% chance to
  become a critical failure. That goes for you, your companions and your enemies.
- **Luck.** Once, while the dog is with you and your Luck is above 1 (above 2 with Gifted), your base
  Luck is set to 1 — 2 for a Gifted character, whose trait then adds its own +1 — and any bonus Luck is
  cleared. A Gifted character ends at **Luck 3**, everyone else at **Luck 1**.
- **It never fights.** Its script makes it flee every combat turn, whatever its Combat Control settings
  say.

### Getting rid of it

There is **no dismissal**. Its script has no dialogue and no way to leave the party. Once it has
joined, **killing it is the only way out**.

When it dies, Jinxed is removed and your base and bonus Luck go back to what they were when it took
them. **Any base Luck you gained while it was with you is lost**, so don't raise Luck until it is
dead. With 750 HP and 30–60% DR it takes a while. Killing it yourself earns **5 karma**.

## Laddie

Jonny's border collie in [Modoc](/walkthrough/modoc#4-jonny-is-missing-find-him-and-bring-him-home-to-balthas),
*"really alert and intelligent"*. Balthas sends him with you to look for his son. He is **free**: he
never counts against your Charisma limit. He is also very fragile.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Modoc main street. Balthas, at the tannery, sends him with you. |
| **Joins if** | PE 6, IQ 6, and a Modoc reputation of at least 1 (or karma 500+) **when you first ask Balthas about Jonny** |
| **Costs** | Nothing. He doesn't even take a party slot. |
| **Best at** | Unarmed 72%. 20 HP, AC 5, no DT or DR. |
| **Weapons** | None |
| **Levels** | None |

### Recruiting

1. With **PE 6** (and IQ 4), ask Balthas *"You seem a bit preoccupied; is there something wrong?"*
2. With **IQ 6**, pick *"Pardon me, my name is… Now that we're properly introduced, you seem troubled."*
3. Now your standing decides:
   - **Modoc reputation 1 or more** (what the script calls Accepted), or **karma 500 or more**: he opens
     up. Go on to step 4.
   - Negative reputation, or karma −500 or lower: he won't say. You can come back later.
   - **Reputation exactly 0**: he gives you the quest **without Laddie**, and the question in step 1
     is gone for good.
4. Ask *"Why don't you tell me what your problem is…"*, then either *"Where does Jonny normally play?
   Does he usually play with someone else?"* (**IQ 8**) or *"I'm sorry about your kid, but what could I
   possibly do?"* followed by *"Well, yes! I am quite a skillful tracker…"*, which always works. Either way
   you get <Quest>Jonny is missing. Find him and bring him home to Balthas</Quest>, and Laddie joins
   you a moment later.

:::caution[Raise your reputation first]
A neutral standing starts the quest without the dog and closes the offer. Earn some Modoc reputation
**before** you first ask Balthas what is wrong.
:::

Laddie adds no XP of his own. The quest's 2500 XP, +10 Modoc reputation and
<Item>Combat Leather Jacket</Item> come from Balthas.

### Using him

- On Modoc's main street he **runs to the town well** and waits there until you have found Jonny's BB
  gun. Ask him *"What's the matter boy, are you trying to tell me something?"* (IQ 4) or *"Did Jonny
  fall down the well, Laddie?"* (IQ 10) for a game of twenty questions. It is pure flavour.
- Elsewhere his menu has *"Do you know where Jonny is, boy?"* (IQ 10), *"Stay here, Laddie."* and
  *"Let's go find Jonny."* To take back a Laddie you told to stay, talk to him and end the conversation.
- **While you carry <Item>Jonny's BB Gun</Item>, he only barks.** Carry it onto the main street while he
  is there and that becomes permanent. After that he can't be given orders or called back from waiting.
- Every Combat Control option is open to him, but with 20 HP and no armour it hardly matters. **Keep him
  out of fights.**

### Losing him

- **He leaves when he finds Jonny.** At the Slags' underground lake he runs to the boy and leaves your
  party. Once Jonny is home, he leaves you on the main street and stays by him. His dialogue has no
  join option, so **a Laddie who has left you this way never rejoins**.
- He also leaves if you **hit Jonny** while he is with you, and leaves and **attacks** if you become an
  enemy of Modoc.
- Hitting Laddie himself while he isn't in your party doesn't make him hostile.
- Killing him yourself costs **10 karma** and 5 Modoc reputation.

## Davin and Miria

Grisham's son and daughter, on Modoc's main street
([walkthrough](/walkthrough/modoc#miria-and-davin)). The only way to recruit either is to sleep with
them and be caught by their father. Neither ever levels up. Both have 35 HP and no damage resistance,
both refuse to wait, and neither can be dismissed. They are worth taking for the wedding scene, or to
sell.

| | Davin | Miria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where** | Modoc main street | Modoc main street |
| **Joins if** | You marry them (below). No party-size check. | Same |
| **Costs** | A party slot, until you sell, divorce or bury them | Same |
| **Best at** | Unarmed 46%, Melee 36% | Unarmed 46%, Melee 36% |
| **Weapons** | Knife, club, pistol | Knife, SMG |
| **Levels** | None | None |
| **Wedding gifts** | 2 <Item>Stimpak</Item>s, 1 <Item>Tool</Item> | 4 Stimpaks, 2 <Item>Antidote</Item>s |

### The seduction

Both use the same test: **Charisma + 1 if a Charisma roll succeeds + 1 if your Modoc reputation is
positive** must reach 9. The Charisma roll succeeds when a random 1–10 is at most your Charisma, and it
is rolled again each time the line could appear.

| Charisma | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 9 or 10 | Always |
| 8 | 80%, or always with a positive Modoc reputation |
| 7 | 70%, and only with a positive Modoc reputation |
| 6 or less | Never |

Neither checks your gender.

- **Davin:** *"Why don't we get more comfortable?"* isn't in his very first greeting. It appears when you
  come back, and after you have asked him one of his questions. Either of the replies that follow (IQ 8 or IQ 4)
  goes through.
- **Miria:** ask *"What do you do around here?"*, then *"I really would like to know more about you."*
  (this is the Charisma test), then *"Well, I've never known anyone as interesting as you are."*, then
  *"I think this going too far. Can't we just talk?"* Despite the wording, that last line is the one
  that leads to bed and the wedding. *"I thought you'd never ask…"* and, for a man, *"You know, I'm just
  a normal guy…"* end it there. *"You slut!…"* and *"You are a slut, aren't you!"* insult her
  for good.

:::danger[Never take the "you know you want it" line]
With Miria, *"You're right, enough talk. Let's get it on! You know you want it."* has **no Charisma
test**. It, and *"All right! You know what I want, baby!"* later in the conversation, lead to an
assault scene:
**−50 karma**, Miria cries for help, Grisham confronts you, and **all of Modoc turns hostile**. Male and
female characters alike can pick these lines, and there is no wedding.
:::

### The wedding

Afterwards the screen fades, the clock jumps to noon if it is not daytime, and **your armour is taken
off** and put in your inventory. Nothing else leaves your pack. Grisham walks in with a
<Item>Shotgun</Item>. If Grisham is already dead, "Uncle Biff" turns up in his place with a
<Item>Sawed-Off Shotgun</Item>. If Jo is dead, he is brought back for the ceremony.
<Unverified>The Modoc page reports that a dead Grisham or a dead Jo freezes the game. RPU's scripts
handle both cases, but a freeze can't be ruled out from source.</Unverified>

Talk to Grisham:

- **Same-sex pairing:** it's a wedding either way.
- **Opposite-sex pairing:** *"Let me explain it to you gently, pops…"* and *"This isn't what it appears
  to be."* both end in a wedding. *"I'm a doctor, and I was just giving her/him a complete physical."*,
  followed by *"Germs? I mean GERMS!…"*, makes a **Speech roll**. Pass it and there is no wedding and
  everything goes back to normal. Fail it and you're married.
- **Don't run.** Walk out of Grisham's area and he turns hostile. Leave the map during the scene and
  **Modoc becomes your enemy**. Hitting Grisham does the same.

The scene stops your companions from following you. The wedding and the doctor story both undo this.

After the ceremony your new spouse joins you, **even over your Charisma limit**, and counts against it
from then on. They bring the gifts listed above. If this is your first marriage, you get the
**Married** reputation: *"You got hitched. Hey, it's your problem, not ours."* There is no XP.

### Stats

| | Davin | Miria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HP / AP / AC | 35 / 7 / 4 | 35 / 7 / 5 |
| SPECIAL | ST 4, PE 5, EN 5, CH 4, IN 5, AG 4, LK 4 | ST 3, PE 5, EN 4, CH 5, IN 5, AG 5, LK 4 |
| Small Guns / Melee / Unarmed | 21% / 36% / 46% | 25% / 36% / 46% |
| First Aid / Doctor | 20% / 15% | 20% / 15% |
| Speech / Barter | 20% / 16% | 25% / 20% |
| Carry weight | 125 | 100 |
| DT / DR | None | None |

### Gear

Their art decides what they can hold. **Davin** can use knives, clubs and pistols, **Miria** knives and
SMGs. The 14mm Pistol and .223 Pistol count as SMGs here. Their own words agree: Davin is *"pretty handy
with the knife… I guess I could use a club… shoot a pistol once"*, and Miria prefers *"Sharp weapons
like knives… I can use weapons like an SMG in a pinch"*. Compare everything in
[Object Database](/database).

- **Melee 36% is their best weapon skill.** A <Item>Combat Knife</Item> suits both: 3–10 damage, ST 2,
  and 3 AP, so two stabs a turn on their 7 AP.
- **Davin** (ST 4) can also take a <Item>Super Cattle Prod</Item> (20–32, ST 4, 4 AP, one swing a
  turn, needs <Item>Small Energy Cell</Item>s) or a Louisville Slugger (12–30, ST 4, 3 AP, two swings
  a turn).
- **Miria** (ST 3) should stay with knives. Every SMG-class weapon needs **ST 4 or more**, so she always
  takes at least −20% to hit, on top of Small Guns 25%.
- Davin's art can throw grenades (with Weapon Pref. set to "None"). Miria's can't.
- **Armour** works on both, and *Use Best Armor* can pick it for them. Their look never changes,
  because the NPC Armor mod doesn't cover them. Their gear menu can take armour off again.
- **Healing:** told *"You should heal yourself now."*, they use the first Super Stimpak, Stimpak,
  Healing Powder, Doctor's Bag or First Aid Kit they carry. With none of those, they fall back on First
  Aid for 5–15 HP. Keep the wedding Stimpaks on them.

### Using them

- **35 HP and no DR.** Keep them behind you.
- **They won't wait.** *"No, baby; I wanna come with you."* They also refuse medium and far follow
  distance, so close is the only setting.
- Every Combat Control option is available to them.
- Neither has a "leave the party" option.

### Getting rid of them

| Way | Where | You get | Karma | Their gear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sell to **Metzger** | [The Den](/walkthrough/the-den#the-slaver-guild) | Davin $900, Miria $1100 when your Barter equals his; more with better Barter | −15 | Stays with Metzger. The spouse turns up as a slave in his pen. |
| Sell to **Barkus** (Servant Allocation Center) | [Vault City](/walkthrough/vault-city#inside-the-city) | Davin $200, Miria $250; $400 / $500 if a Barter roll succeeds | −10, plus −3 once for considering it and −3 once for haggling | Goes into Barkus's box |
| Sell to **Big Jesus Mordino** | [New Reno](/walkthrough/new-reno#virgin-street) | Davin $200, Miria $250; $400 / $500 if a Barter roll at +10 succeeds | −10, plus −3 once for offering and −3 once for haggling | **Handed to you** |
| **Divorce** from Father Tully | [New Reno church](/walkthrough/new-reno#the-church) | Costs one bottle | None | **Lost with them** |

- **Barkus** makes his offer while you have any companion. He picks Miria first, then Davin, then Sulik,
  then Vic.
- **Father Tully** offers *"Can you do divorces?"* while your spouse is in the party, if you have never
  divorced before. He
  wants a bottle of <Item>Roentgen Rum</Item>, <Item>Gamma Gulp Beer</Item>, <Item>Booze</Item>,
  <Item>Beer</Item> or <Item>Rot Gut</Item>. Your spouse runs off and vanishes **with everything they
  carry**, so strip them first.
- Metzger, Barkus and the divorce all end the marriage, and you become **Separated**: *"Your spouse is no
  longer with you. I hope you're happy."* **A sale to Big Jesus leaves you Married.**
- **Death** also ends the marriage. Killing them yourself costs **10 karma** and 5 Modoc reputation.
- Once the marriage is over, Grisham asks after your spouse. Tell him they are dead and he has a fatal
  heart attack: **−20 karma**.
- They also leave if you become an enemy of Modoc, and attack if you talk to them in that state.
  Hitting either of them in Modoc before the wedding makes the town your enemy.
- Once the marriage has ended, a living ex-spouse only has a line or two for you. There is no way back.

## Temporary followers

These join your party for part of a quest, or for mechanical reasons. None of them is under your
control for long.

| Who | Quest | What happens | Covered in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Smoke** | <Quest>Rescue Nagor's dog, Smoke, from the wilds</Quest> | Joins when you reach him in the hunting grounds. He leaves the party as soon as you take him off that map. | [Arroyo](/walkthrough/arroyo#2-rescue-nagors-dog-smoke-from-the-wilds) |
| **Bess** | None | Heal her crippled leg (First Aid won't do it) for **200 XP** and +5 karma. She then follows you on the Modoc maps, without taking a party slot. | [Modoc](/walkthrough/modoc#bess) |
| **The "chicken"** | None | Once out of its coop, it joins your party to follow you between Modoc maps. It kills everyone on a map it hasn't cleared yet, including companions you left there, and attacks you whenever it is visible and can see you. | [Modoc](/walkthrough/modoc#the-chicken) |
| **Pretty Boy Lloyd** | <Quest>Track down Pretty Boy Lloyd, recover the stolen money, and make an... example of him</Quest> | Joins when he agrees to take you to the money. He leaves the party as soon as you arrive in Golgotha. | [New Reno](/walkthrough/new-reno#5-track-down-pretty-boy-lloyd-recover-the-stolen-money-and-make-an-example-of-him) |
| **The car trunk** | None | Joins the party once you own the car, so that it travels with you. It is an item, so it never counts against your limit. | [The Car](/systems/car#the-trunk) |

Jonny, Karl and Doc Jones also have entries in the game's party list, but no script ever adds them to
your party.
