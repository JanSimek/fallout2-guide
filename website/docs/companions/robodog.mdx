# Robodog

Dr. Henry offers "a Cyber-Hound Mk. II, Personal Security Model" as payment for field-testing his
serum. The game calls it the **Cyberdog**. It has only 9–12 AP and 97–137 HP, and it fights only with
its teeth. In its favour, it is **immune to EMP** and you can repair it anywhere with a toolkit. Take it
if you have a slot and nothing better. [K-9](/companions/k9) has far more AP and AC.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [NCR](/walkthrough/ncr), Downtown: next to Dr. Henry in his lab |
| **Joins if** | Dr. Henry gives it to you after the serum test (Science 80% and Intelligence 4 to get the job), Henry is alive to pay, and you have a free party slot |
| **Costs** | One super mutant. The test itself gives 1000 XP. |
| **Best at** | Unarmed 110%, immune to EMP |
| **Weapons** | None: teeth only |
| **Levels** | 4 level-ups: the first at player level 9–12 if it is with you by then, then one in every 4 of your level-ups ([how levelling works](/companions/overview)) |

## Recruiting

This is Dr. Henry's quest, <Quest>Test the mutagenic serum on a super-mutant. Try Broken Hills</Quest>;
see [NCR](/walkthrough/ncr) for the walkthrough.

### Getting the serum

Henry offers the job only while he is standing at his lab. Every option needs Intelligence 4.

1. Introduce yourself: "I'm &lt;name&gt;, the Chosen One. I'm looking for Vault 13." Henry mentions
   cyber-genetics.
2. With **Science 80%** you can ask about it: "Cyber-genetics? I don't remember ever studying that
   one." or "Vault Behavioral project?" → "What's this about cyber-genetics?".
3. Continue through his explanation: "Field testing? What do you mean?" → "I'm not letting you stick
   any needles in me!" → "What's in it for me?".
4. He offers the Cyber-Hound. Answer "Cyber-hound? Sure, why not." and he hands you the Mutagenic
   Serum, "fitted up in a stim hypo".

The quest brings no karma change at any point.

### Testing the serum

Use the serum on any **super mutant**. The first use completes the quest for **1000 XP**. The target
melts and dies about two seconds later. On anything else you get "You are supposed to use this on a
super-mutant." and you keep the serum.

:::danger[Not on Marcus]
The serum only checks that its target is a super mutant, so it works on
[Marcus](/companions/marcus) too, and kills him.
:::

The script does not check that the target is alive, so a super mutant's corpse should also count.
<Unverified>Derived from the serum's script; not tested in game.</Unverified>

### Collecting the dog

Go back to Henry: "So, how did it go? Is the test done?" → "Yes." → "It killed him! You said it would
cure mutants!" or "He's dead, Jim." → "What about my pay?". He answers "Of course. The cyberdog is all
yours."

If you tell him "You're a monster! I want nothing to do with you." instead, the conversation just ends.
Nothing else happens, and you can talk to him again and still ask for your pay.

:::caution[Keep Henry alive]
Nothing else can hand the dog over. **If Henry dies before paying you, Robodog can never join.**
:::

Now talk to the dog. Before Henry pays, it only floats noises like "Arf! &lt;click&gt; Woof!".
Afterwards it opens its menu with "Woof! Wo-wo-wo-&lt;click&gt;woof?". Pick **"Let's go."**:

- with a free slot, it joins;
- with a full party, it says ".whirrrrr... I am not a Daggit." and stays where it is. Ask again once
  you have room.

It has no karma or reputation requirement.

A level-up also heals it fully. The player-level ranges assume Robodog has been in your party since
player level 9: the earliest level at which each stage can come, and the level by which it is certain.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | 13–16 | 117<Vanilla>Stage 2 has 107 HP, the same as stage 1.</Vanilla> | 11 | 18 | 120% | 15 | 22% |

Nothing else changes: ST 6, PE 9, EN 8, CH 1, IN 3, AG 8, LK 5, Sequence 18, carry weight 175, and DT 4 /
DR 30% against normal damage. Its DR against EMP is so high that **EMP does it no damage at all**. All
its other skills stay put, including a Melee Weapons 100% it has no use for.

## Gear

**There is nothing to equip.** Robodog uses the same art as K-9, which has no weapon animations, and
FOR:CE never has a dog pick a weapon from its inventory. **It cannot wear armour** either: FOR:CE does
not let four-legged companions wear it, and the NPC Armor mod does not cover dogs. Its menu has no gear
option.

**You cannot give it anything.** It has no Trade screen ("This critter can't carry anything.") and
cannot be stolen from, so it never carries healing items. "You should heal yourself now." therefore
always falls back on its own First Aid (24%): 5–15 HP on a success, and 15 minutes pass. Carry a
<Item>Super Tool Kit</Item> or <Item>Tool</Item> yourself for repairs (below).

**Combat settings:** Weapon Pref. is locked to "Unarmed", and there is no "Snipe" distance and no burst
setting. Robodog shares its default custom combat settings with K-9, so a change made for one applies to
both.

## Repairing it

Unlike K-9, Robodog can be repaired. Use your **Repair** skill on it, or use a toolkit on it:

| Used on Robodog | Bonus to your Repair roll |
| --- | --- |
| Repair skill | none |
| <Item>Tool</Item> | +10% |
| <Item>Super Tool Kit</Item> | +30% |

A successful roll heals **10–40 HP**. Either way, the message is "Cyberdog has been repaired some." as
long as it is still hurt. **That message does not mean the roll passed**, so watch its HP.

Repairs are limited by a counter. Each **successful** repair adds 50 to it. You can try again only while
the counter is **below your Repair skill**; otherwise you get "Cyberdog can't be repaired anymore at
this time." The counter drops by 50 about once an hour. In practice:

- Repair 50% allows one success in a row, Repair 51–100% two, Repair 101–150% three, and so on.
- You usually get one extra, because the counter's first drop tends to come straight after your
  first repair.

It also heals like a living creature: stimpaks, First Aid and Doctor all work on it.

## Using it

- **It bites less often than K-9.** An attack with no weapon costs 3 AP, so it bites three times a turn
  at 9 AP and four times at 12 AP. Its Melee Damage is added to the maximum damage of every bite.
- **Pulse grenades cannot hurt it.** As far as Robodog is concerned, you can throw them at robots
  freely.
- **Its levels start early,** from player level 9, but they are small: +10 HP each, and +1 AP at every
  stage except the third.

## Orders

Its menu offers:

- "You should heal yourself now." (only when it is hurt);
- "We need to change our distance from one another.": close, medium or far ("Arf-arf proximity set to
  close.");
- "I need you to wait here until I come back.": "Ee-bee-dee-bee-ee-bee-dee-bee!";
- "That's it for now.".

## Losing it

- **Waiting:** "Let's go." brings it back, subject to the party-size check. **There is no way to dismiss
  it for good.**
- **Hurting it:** its script ignores damage you deal it, and while it is in your party the game does
  not even run that part of it. <Unverified>Whether the engine turns it against you for the rest of
  that fight was not traced.</Unverified>
- **Parked dogs rejoin NCR's side.** Whenever Robodog is not in your party, including while it waits
  for you, it is put back on NCR's team the next time you enter its map. <Unverified>Whether a
  waiting Robodog then fights alongside NCR's guards against you was not traced.</Unverified>
- **Stealing from it** is impossible: the game does not let you steal from Robodog.
- **The EPA:** it cannot follow you down the broken elevator shaft. It leaves the party and waits at the
  top.
- **Death:** killing it yourself costs 10 karma and 5 points of NCR reputation, and counts as killing a
  good character towards the Champion and Berserker reputations.
