# Myron

The boy genius who invented Jet, kept by the Mordinos in New Reno's Stables. As a fighter he is weak
— 50% in every weapon skill but Throwing (16%) when you meet him — but he is worth recruiting: **750 XP** for talking
him out, 4000 XP more from the Jet cure, stimpaks and super stimpaks from ingredients you carry,
and three favours if you try to leave him behind, one of which puts the EPA on your map.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [The Stables](/walkthrough/the-stables) in New Reno, his room behind a guarded door |
| **Joins if** | You have a free party slot — nothing else is checked |
| **Costs** | Nothing, once you are past his guards |
| **Best at** | Science 87%; Small Guns, Big Guns, Energy Weapons, Unarmed, Melee Weapons and Barter 50% |
| **Weapons** | Pistols, knives, unarmed weapons |
| **Levels** | 4 level-ups, one for every four of your own from your level 6 on, while he is in the party — see [levelling](/companions/overview#levelling) |

## Recruiting

### Getting past the guards

Two Mordino guards watch his door. While one of them can see it and you lack permission, the door
will not open — for you or for a companion. Permission is cleared whenever you leave the Stables.
Ways in:

- **Show a <Item>Stables ID Badge</Item>**: "I'm here to see Myron. I have a badge."
- **A female character with Charisma 6+** or the porn-star reputation can flirt her way in.
- **After Myron's date** (below) the guards wave you in.
- **A Mordino made man** can simply say Myron is expecting him. A **Salvatore** made man is never
  stopped by the door at all — but Myron's guards attack a made man of any other family on sight.
- **Kill both guards.** Each lowers the Mordino family's power counter by 1 (see
  [Endings](/reference/endings)), and Myron has extra lines for you afterwards ("Your guards are
  too busy being dead…").

Once Myron is dead, the guards attack on sight — unless he had already left the Stables with you.

### The badge

**Marjorie Reed**, on the ground floor, carries the badge and hands it over at the end of a
conversation about Myron — **500 XP**. Getting there needs one of:

- **Science 76%+ and Intelligence 7+**, steering the talk to science.
- **A male character** with Charisma 7+, or Charisma 6 plus Smooth Talker or Speech 76%+.

She will not talk to you while Myron is in your party.

### Myron's date

Jagged Jimmy J can tell you about Myron's "friends": with Intelligence 7, once you already know about
Myron's girls from Marjorie or Jules, follow "Except that I heard Myron has a lot of prostitute...
friends." with "You mean prostitutes, don't you?"

Then, at the Cat's Paw, ask **Miss Kitty** "I heard some of your girls are chosen to go... visit
Myron." Only a **female character with Charisma 6+** can take it further. You get **500 XP**, two
hours pass, and you arrive at the Stables. The offer disappears once Myron has left the Stables.

### Talking him into leaving

All the lines here need Intelligence 4+. Ask who he is; he boasts that he made Jet. Then:

1. "You made Jet?" — or call it bullshit.
2. "Why did you make Jet?"
3. "'As happy as they're able?' Are you happy here?"
4. "Well what? Are they treating you right?"
5. "You're too valuable to be here, Myron. You should leave." — or, with INT 6+, "Maybe I could help
   you…" — and keep saying so the next two times he hesitates.

**Shortcut:** with Smooth Talker, Charisma 8+ or Speech 76%+, answer "Why did you make Jet?" with
"How about I suggest a new circle to you? Mordino doesn't know talent when they see it - I do." and
then "Maybe we can help each other…"

If your party is full he declines; otherwise "Just follow me." takes him along. Later conversations
open with "You again. So... ready to go?"

Joining gives **750 XP**, knocks 20 off the Mordino family's power counter, and sets his ending slide
(below). He joins with his gun put away.

A character with Intelligence 3 or less gets entirely different dialogue and can never recruit him.

:::note[His welcome gift]
The first time he joins, he adds **3 Super Stimpaks, 3 Stimpaks, 2 Jet** and a red condom to his own
inventory. The gift is created only once, and other things create it first: hurting him, making him
hostile, any combat turn in his presence, or robbing him.

You can rob him with the low-Intelligence dialogue, or after killing his guards ("Actually, I want
everything you have." or "Sure. Give me all the drugs you got."). That hands you his things (or just
his drugs), gift included, for **−3 karma** — and if you recruit him afterwards he brings nothing extra.
:::

## The Jet cure

Myron starts <Quest>Find some endorphin blockers to make a cure for Jet</Quest> — in the party or
not. Full details are in [New Reno](/walkthrough/new-reno).

1. Ask "Is there any cure for Jet?", then "Myron, you're a pretty smart fella. Consider it a
   challenge…" (INT 5).
2. "It's basically a highly addictive meta-amphetamine, right?…" — **Science 76% and INT 7**.
3. "What about using some kind of endorphin blocker?…" — **Science 81%**, or Science 76% with INT 9.
4. "Well?" — **2000 XP**. He suggests Vault City's medical warehouse; ask about it and Vault City
   goes on your map.

Finish it with **Dr. Troy** in [Vault City](/walkthrough/vault-city): "Do you have any endorphin
blockers?" gives another **2000 XP**, two Jet Antidotes, +3 Vault City reputation and +10 karma.

:::caution[Do the cure talk before the antidote]
The cure talk is offered only while Vault City's Jet business with Troy has not reached the
antidote. Collect the antidote from Troy or blackmail him first, and neither he nor Myron will
discuss a cure.
:::

## Gear

He comes with a <Item>Needler Pistol</Item> (12–24, range 24, 5 AP), one
<Item>HN Needler Cartridge</Item>, 3 <Item name="Stimpak">stimpaks</Item>, a <Item>Super Stimpak</Item>, an
<Item>Empty Hypodermic</Item>, a <Item>Condom (Red Package)</Item> and $54 — plus the welcome gift
above. Asked what weapons he is good with, he says "Beats me."

His room holds Jet (five in two containers), a spare needler cartridge, an empty hypodermic, a
stimpak, and a bookshelf with a <Item>Big Book of Science</Item> and a
<Item>Cat's Paw Magazine</Item>. Myron watches those containers only while he is not in your party.

### What he can hold

Under RPU's NPC Armor mod Myron may use only **pistols, knives and unarmed weapons**. The
<Item>14mm Pistol</Item> and <Item>.223 Pistol</Item> use the SMG animation and are refused. The
restriction comes from the mod's sfall global script; that it works under FOR:CE has been checked in
the source, not in a running game. RPU's optional "New wakizashi blade
animations" component gives the Wakizashi Blade, normally a knife, its own animation; Myron has it
only in his normal look, not in combat or power armour.

With **Strength 4**, only the <Item>.44 Magnum Revolver</Item> and the .44 Magnum (Speed Load)
(Strength 5) cost him to-hit: −20%.

### What to give him

His Energy Weapons overtakes Small Guns at his first level-up (65% against 55%) and reaches 95%, so
move him to energy pistols once you have cells to spare. With 7 AP he fires once a turn whatever
the pistol; at stage 4 (8 AP) a 4 AP pistol fires twice.

The rest are in the [Object Database](/database). Knives use his Melee (50–60%), which never gets

:::caution[Take the Jet off him]
His welcome gift puts **2 Jet in his own inventory**, and his default Chem Use, "Any drug some of the
time", lets him take drugs he carries in combat. A companion's Jet withdrawal never ends — see
[Chems](/companions/overview#chems). Take the Jet, or set Chem Use to a stimpak option. You cannot
use Jet on him yourself; he refuses it.
:::

### Armour

He starts with none. Under NPC Armor, <Item>Combat Armor</Item>,
<Item>Combat Armor Mark II</Item> and <Item>Brotherhood Armor</Item> give him a combat-armour look,
and <Item>Power Armor</Item> and Hardened Power Armor a power-armour look.
<Item>Advanced Power Armor</Item> and robes show the player's own art for them. Leather jackets,
leather armour and metal armour work but leave him looking as he always does.

## Using him

His defaults are "Ranged then melee", "Any drug some of the time", "Snipe the enemy", run away at
"Your finger hurts", Burst "Be careful not to hit me" and "Whoever is closest". He has **no
Berserk** disposition but does have Coward. His Burst options are "Always!", "Sometimes, don't worry about
hitting me" and "Be careful not to hit me", and Run Away offers only "Abject coward" and "Your finger hurts" — he cannot be told to
stand his ground. What each setting does is on the [overview](/companions/overview).

**Leave Run Away alone.** As he comes, he runs only below 10 HP, or when crippled or blinded.
Picking either Run Away option recalculates that from his maximum HP: "Abject coward" sends him
running at the first scratch, "Your finger hurts" below 75%.

Outside combat he heals himself from his own supplies at the same thresholds as any companion (60%,
30% or 50% HP depending on Chem Use). Asked to heal himself with no supplies, he uses First Aid. Try
to heal him at full HP and he tells you to stop mothering him.

:::danger[Keep him away from Big Jesus Mordino]
If Myron is in Big Jesus's room or in his sight, Big Jesus forces a conversation — "You have... left
your post." — and **every answer starts a fight** with the Mordino family.
:::

## Crafting

With Myron in the party (not waiting), choose "I had some questions..." and then "Can you make any
drugs?".

| Makes | Ingredients (per item) | Cost | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <Item>Antidote</Item> | 1 <Item>Scorpion Tail</Item> or 1 Small Scorpion Tail | 1 hour | None; big tails are used first |
| <Item>Stimpak</Item> | 1 <Item>Broc Flower</Item>, 1 <Item>Xander Root</Item>, 1 <Item>Empty Hypodermic</Item> | 1 hour | None |
| <Item>Super Stimpak</Item> | 1 <Item>Stimpak</Item>, 1 <Item>Nuka-Cola</Item>, 1 <Item>Fruit</Item> | 1 hour | **Science 76% and Intelligence 8** to unlock (below) |
| <Item>Jet</Item> | — | — | Never. "Sorry, ain't got the 'raw materials' I need... don't really wanna get 'em, either." |

- He counts ingredients in **your inventory and his**, and takes yours first. What he makes goes into
  your inventory.
- Ask for one, two or three, or — when you have more than three sets — as many as possible. Each item
  takes an hour of game time. There is no money cost, no skill roll and no XP.
- **Unlocking super stimpaks:** open the stimpak option, then ask "Can you make super stimpaks?" (or
  "If you can make stimpaks, couldn't you make some super stimpaks?" when you lack the stimpak
  ingredients). The question needs Science 76%+ and Intelligence 8+ (Smooth Talker counts towards
  the Intelligence). Asked once, it stays in the drugs menu for good.

## Asking him to wait

Tell Myron to wait and he begs to stay: "you keep me around, I'll do you a favor." Ask "Like what
kind of favor?" What he offers depends on how far you are:

1. **The EPA.** If it is not on your world map yet: "I've heard of a secret building with lots of
   strange vegetation around it."
2. **The Mordino stash.** Otherwise, if you have not found it: "Could be I know one of Mordino's
   little stashes in Golgotha outside of Reno." Just hearing this marks Golgotha and tells you about
   the stash.
3. **Carrying more.** Otherwise: "I'll... I'll carry more of your stuff!" — +10 carry weight, once.
   A level-up resets his stats, but his script adds the 10 back whenever his carry weight is at 125–134.

Only **"Okay. You can stay."** collects the favour and keeps him with you. So repeat the request
three times to get all three. "Forget it, Myron. Wait here until I get back." leaves him behind with
nothing, and there are lines to make him wait or stay with no favour at all.

He is not the only way to the EPA: Dr. Sheng in [San Francisco](/walkthrough/san-francisco) and two
kinds of random traveller can also mark it. When you first reach the EPA with him, he remarks that
this should be the place he marked.

### The Mordino stash

The stash is a grave in [Golgotha](/walkthrough/golgotha). Bring a <Item>Shovel</Item>; uncovering a
grave the first time costs **5 karma**. It holds a Gold Nugget, $171, a
<Item>.44 Magnum Revolver</Item>, <Item>.44 Magnum JHP</Item>, an <Item>Antidote</Item>, 3 Jet and a
<Item>Buffout</Item> — but only if you knew about the stash **before** you first open it.

Once you know about the stash, bring Myron within 4 hexes of the grave and he stops, turns to it and
says "That's the grave where Big Jesus' stashed those extra chips." Before that, asking him where it is only gets
"It's around on Golgotha somewhere... I'll let you know when I see it."

## The EPA Doctor

With Myron in the party, ask the Doctor on the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa) surface what drugs he has
made, then "Say, have you ever heard of Jet?", and introduce Myron as its creator. The two talk shop
for five hours, and the Doctor gives you a **Solar Scorcher** as thanks. You need to get the Doctor
talking first — he wants Science above 70%, or Speech or Science above 100% to change his mind.

## Losing him

:::danger[Never loot him while he is unconscious]
Opening his inventory while he is down counts as stealing from him. He turns hostile, and from then
on talking to him makes him attack you. It never wears off.
:::

- **Hurting him while he is not in your party** (before you recruit him, or while he waits) makes him
  hostile for good. If he never left the Stables, the Mordinos and his guards are alerted too. Hitting
  him while he is **in** your party — on purpose or with a stray burst — does not set him off. **Getting caught stealing** from him there has the same effect.
- **Picking a fight in dialogue** — "Kid, your ego just wrote a check your body can't cash. Get ready
  to die." or the low-Intelligence threats — makes him hostile.
- **He never leaves over karma.** If you tell him to wait, the only check on rejoining is a free
  party slot.
- **Selling him:** **Metzger** in [The Den](/walkthrough/the-den) pays 2 × 400 × (160 + your
  Barter) ÷ (160 + Metzger's Barter), keeps Myron's gear, and costs 15 karma. **Merk** in [NCR](/walkthrough/ncr)
  pays **$1000** with no karma change, you keep the gear, and Merk then offers you work. Big Jesus
  does not buy him.
- **Killing him** knocks 20 off the Mordino family's power counter, unless recruiting him already did.
  His gear stays on the body — until you leave the map or load a save, when a companion killed in
  combat vanishes with everything he carried (see [Death](/companions/overview#death)). Once he has
  left the Stables with you, his old guards do not care.

### His ending

Myron's [ending slide](/reference/endings) — "Myron died less than a year after the defeat of the
Enclave, stabbed by a Jet addict while drinking in the Den." — plays if he is alive at the end and
you know of him, recruited or not. Killing him removes it, and so does selling him to Metzger (his
script counts that as a death). A Myron sold to Merk still counts as alive and keeps the slide.
