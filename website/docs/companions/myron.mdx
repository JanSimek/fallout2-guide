# Myron

**Found in:** [The Stables](/walkthrough/the-stables)

The teenage chemist who invented jet. Insufferable, and worth recruiting anyway — briefly.

The plan: pick him up, start the jet cure quest, have him convert every ingredient you are carrying,
then leave him somewhere convenient like outside a shop you visit often. Come back when you have more
ingredients.

Recruiting him: **750 XP**.

## What he makes

He checks **both** his inventory and yours for ingredients.

| Product | Ingredients | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Antidote | Scorpion tail | — |
| Stimpak | Broc flower, xander root, empty hypodermic | — |
| **Super Stimpak** | Fruit, Nuka-Cola, Stimpak | **IN 8 and Science 76%**, and you must first ask him to make Stimpaks |

Stimpaks are limited by the number of **empty hypodermics** in the game. Super Stimpaks are
effectively unlimited, since fruit, Nuka-Cola and stimpaks all restock.

:::note
The [EPA's Mr. Chemmie](/walkthrough/epa) does everything Myron does and a great deal more, and does
not complain. Once you reach the EPA, Myron's chemistry is redundant.
:::

## The Mordino stash

Try to dismiss him and he offers to reveal a **Mordino stash in Golgotha** if you keep him. The stash
is real — see [Golgotha](/walkthrough/golgotha) — though he never actually points it out.

:::tip Two more things from dismissing him
- He tells you the location of the **[EPA](/walkthrough/epa)**. This is the most reliable way to find
  it. <Vanilla>The EPA dialogue is unvoiced and unreachable.</Vanilla>
- After you dig up the stash, he promises to carry more instead. This was meant to raise his carry
  weight by 10 and does not work.
:::

## Weapons

The **Needler Pistol** until you run out of ammunition, then a **Plasma Pistol** or **Pulse Pistol**.
Expect a lot of misses. The **Alien Blaster**, if you go to the trouble of getting one, is his best
weapon.

:::caution
You cannot use jet on him — but put it in his inventory and **he will use it on himself in combat**.
:::

## Where he cannot go

- **Big Jesus Mordino** attacks on sight if Myron is with you.
- **Marjorie Reed** at the Stables will not talk to you with Myron along.

## Losing him

:::danger
Access Myron's inventory while he is **unconscious** and he refuses to communicate from then on,
permanently. The same applies to Vic.
:::
