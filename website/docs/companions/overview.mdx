# Companions

> *"Wow! I feel as if I've passed some arbitrary experience value and gained more power!"* — Marcus

Companions fight for you, carry your loot, and occasionally die in ways you did not expect.
Experience is **not** divided among them, so a companion is pure profit.

**Your limit is Charisma ÷ 2, rounded down.** See [SPECIAL](/character/special).

## Equipping them

Give each companion the best **armour** and **weapon they can actually use** that you are not using
yourself, plus a handful of stimpaks.

:::caution[Animations, not skills]
A companion can equip any weapon they have **animation frames** for — which does not mean they are any
good with it. Lenny can hold an Alien Blaster because it uses the pistol animation, and will miss with
it constantly.

They will not equip a weapon they have no ammunition for, or one that conflicts with their current
combat settings.
:::

Two animations are non-obvious: the **14mm Pistol and .223 Pistol use the SMG animation**, and the
**Tommy Gun uses the rifle animation**.

:::note[Grenades]
A companion can only throw grenades if their weapon preference is set to **"None"**. That rules out
Sulik and Lenny despite them having the animations.
:::

:::tip[Set combat options immediately]
Open each companion's custom combat settings the moment you recruit them. The defaults will get them
killed, and will get *you* killed when they burst-fire through your back.
:::

:::note[RPU: Party Orders]
The Party Orders mod is bundled with RPU. It lets you give standing combat orders rather than
negotiating with the AI. Turn it on in the installer.
<Vanilla>Combat behaviour is controlled only by the per-companion settings dialogue.</Vanilla>
:::

## Healing

Told to heal, a companion works through: **Super Stimpak → Stimpak → Healing Powder → Doctor's Bag →
First Aid Kit → their best healing skill**. That skill is First Aid for everyone except **Lenny**, who
uses Doctor (10–25 HP against First Aid's 5–15).

:::caution
A companion using a healing **skill** counts against **your own** three-times-a-day limit.

If they are holding a weapon you may have to end the conversation before they can heal — they need to
play the put-away animation first.
:::

## Levelling

Every time **you** level up, each companion who has not levelled recently has a **chance** to gain a
level. Miss it and the odds improve next time. They do not need to be present when you earn the XP,
only at the moment you level.

Most companions level **four or five times** in a game, gaining HP, AP, carry weight and combat skill
non-randomly.

:::tip
Read their level-up comments. They start bland and become genuinely funny at higher levels — Marcus
and Myron especially.
:::

## Chems

**Companions never get irradiated or poisoned.** They do suffer normal chem after-effects, and they
can become addicted.

:::danger[Jet addiction is permanent for companions]
The Jet Antidote does not work on them. One careless dose can leave a companion permanently reduced to
a handful of Action Points.
:::

:::note
Levelling up interacts oddly with drugs. A companion who levels while boosted has the comedown applied
to their *new* stats and ends up below where they should be. One who levels during withdrawal gets a
lasting boost when it wears off. The next level-up corrects it either way.
:::

## What makes them leave or attack

| Companion | Refuses to join / leaves |
| --- | --- |
| **Sulik** | Leaves if you become a Childkiller, Berserker, or karma −101 or lower. Attacks if you become a Slaver or cause trouble **in Klamath**. Will not rejoin if Klamath reputation is Hated. |
| **Vic** | Attacks if you attack **Valerie** in Vault City while he is with you. |
| **Cassidy** | Will not join a Slaver, Childkiller, or karma −101 or lower — but does not leave if you become one later. |
| **Lenny** | Leaves for Childkiller, Slaver, karma −101, or **CH 1**. Attacks if you are in Gecko having wrecked the plant. |
| **Marcus** | Will not join below 0 karma. Will not rejoin at −101, Slaver or Childkiller. Attacks anywhere if you are an enemy of Broken Hills. |
| **Goris** | Will not join below 0 karma, negative Vault 13 reputation, Berserker or Childkiller. Leaves if your karma drops below 0. |
| **K-9** | Leaves for Childkiller or karma −101 — and **cannot be re-recruited afterwards**, ever. |

:::danger[Never loot an unconscious companion]
Accessing the inventory of a downed companion counts as **stealing from a non-party member**.
**Vic and Myron** will stop communicating permanently, and **Goris turns hostile** the moment you
dismiss him.
:::

:::caution[Dead companions vanish]
If a companion dies and you save/load or leave the map and return, the **body and everything on it is
gone**. This applies before you recruit them too.

Saving while in combat mode with a dead companion produces a save that **freezes on end of turn**.
:::

## Recovering from a fight

If you attack a companion who is not currently in your party, they fight back. Whether you can recruit
them afterwards depends on the individual:

| Behaviour | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Peaceful if you leave and return | Laddie, Robodog |
| Peaceful after letting them start one fight, then leaving and returning | Sulik, Lenny, K-9 |
| Holds a grudge, but recruitable if you talk before they attack | Cassidy |
| **Permanently hostile** | Vic, Marcus, Myron, Goris, Dogmeat |

The Brain Bot never turns hostile from being shot — but turns **permanently** hostile if you steal from
it.

## Useful tricks

:::tip[Infinite carry weight]
Tell a companion to **wait**, then trade with them. While waiting, they evaluate goods by **price**
rather than weight, so you can load them with anything. One player reports giving Vic 800 pounds of
gear.

The cost is a little under half their Action Points. If you overload them beyond what one barter
session can unload, use **Steal** to get it back.
:::

:::note[Weapon skill helpers]
**Vic, Lenny and Myron** will use their special non-combat skill automatically if you try to use that
skill on something you are not standing next to — and their skill is higher than yours. So Lenny will
heal people with Doctor, and Vic will disable forcefields.

You can only trigger this from the skilldex, so you cannot have Myron hack a computer for you.
:::

:::note[Outdoorsman]
A companion with a higher Outdoorsman than yours uses theirs automatically while travelling. Only
**Sulik** has a meaningful figure — 80% at his highest level.

A **Motion Sensor** applies its bonus to whoever has the highest skill, but only if **you** are
carrying it.
:::

:::note[The flare trick]
Give a lit **flare** to a companion with no other weapon and have them ready it. They become a
permanent light source — the effect persists after the flare disappears. Works for **Vic, Davin,
Marcus and Myron**.
:::
