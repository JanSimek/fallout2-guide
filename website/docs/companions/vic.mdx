# Vic

**Found in:** [The Den](/walkthrough/the-den), locked in Metzger's building

The trader your village sent you to find. Worth the **$1000** Metzger wants, unless you are going solo
or building an all-melee party.

See [The Den](/walkthrough/the-den) for the ten ways past his guard, and remember he needs **his own**
radio from Klamath, not a generic one.

## Statistics

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Main skill** | Repair |
| **Also good at** | Small Guns, Energy Weapons at high levels |
| **Animations** | Throw, knife, pistol, rifle |
| **HP by level** | 70 · 78 · **78** · 102 · 111 · 117 |
| **AP by level** | 8 · 8 · 9 · 9 · 10 · 10 |

The repeated 78 is not a typo — he really does stall for two levels.

## Weapons

| Stage | Weapon |
| --- | --- |
| The Den | 10mm Pistol or Hunting Rifle |
| Vault City | Combat Shotgun |
| Later | Sniper Rifle or Pancor Jackhammer |
| San Francisco | **Gauss Rifle** |

At his top two levels he handles a **Pulse Pistol** or **Alien Blaster** well.

:::caution
His Strength is 5 until his last two levels, so hold off on the H&K CAWS. The Gauss Rifle also wants
ST 6 but is accurate enough that the penalty barely matters.
:::

## His daughter

**Valerie** in Vault City is Vic's daughter. Bring him to her and there is a long conversation — after
which you can persuade her to give him a chance with **CH 8 or Speech 75%**: **250 XP**.

:::danger
Attack Valerie while Vic is in your party and he attacks you. Curiously, he does not care if he is out
of the party at the time, even if he watches you do it.
:::

## Repair

If Vic's Repair is higher than yours, he handles Repair actions on distant objects automatically —
disabling forcefields, for instance. Trigger it from the skilldex.

:::tip
Vic in your party means you can open the [Toxic Caves](/walkthrough/toxic-caves) elevator without
raising your own Repair skill.
:::
