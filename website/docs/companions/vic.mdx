# Vic

> *"Sure, I'll join up with you! This old fart has one big adventure left in him…"* — Vic

The trader the Arroyo Elder sent you to find. Take him: your karma and reputation never drive him
away, Repair is his best skill at every stage, so he does your Repair jobs, and his Small Guns reaches
135% and his Energy Weapons 145%. Freeing him costs $1000 and gives no XP in itself; the XP comes from
what he knows and from his daughter.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [The Den](/walkthrough/the-den), East Side — a locked room in the Slavers' Guild, watched by a guard |
| **Joins if** | He is free (Metzger paid or dead) and you have a free party slot |
| **Costs** | <Item>Vic's Radio</Item> from his Klamath shack and $1000 to Metzger — or Metzger's life |
| **Best at** | Repair 70%, Unarmed 52%, Small Guns 50%, Lockpick 50%; Energy Weapons climbs from 12% to 145% |
| **Weapons** | Knife, pistol, rifle and shotgun; also unarmed weapons, and thrown knives, rocks and grenades |
| **Levels** | 6 level-ups, one for every 4 of yours from your level 5 — see [how levelling works](/companions/overview) |

## Recruiting

### Reaching him

His door is locked. Try to open it where the guard can see you and he warns you off ("Hey! Hands
off."); try again and the slavers attack. Picking the lock while he can see you simply fails.

The guard lets you in if:

- you are a **Slaver**, or Metzger has given you permission (ask him "Can I see him?");
- your **Repair or Science is tagged or 60%+** ("Well, I'm fairly skilled in repairing things…");
- you promise "Please, he won't leave the room. I promise." and either have Repair tagged or pass a
  **Speech roll at −10**;
- you ask "Can't we work something out - financially maybe?", pass a **Barter roll at −20** and pay
  **$300**;
- you wear him down: after a second failed plea, or "Screw you, buddy!", he refuses three more times
  and gives in on the fourth;
- or you are a low-Intelligence character, who has a path of their own once Vic's name is known.

The [Den](/walkthrough/the-den) walkthrough covers the options in context.

### His radio

Vic owes Metzger, and the way out starts with **his own radio**: "There is an old radio in my shack
back in Klamath… You been to the Dunton's? My shack's... well, it was just east of their place."

<Item>Vic's Radio</Item> is in a bookcase in his [Klamath](/walkthrough/klamath) shack, behind a
locked and trapped door (Lockpick at −20). The dresser there holds **three** more of the
<Item name="Vic's Water Flask">water flasks</Item> like the one the Elder gave you. Keep at least one:
Ed needs to see it later.

Give Vic the radio ("Can you use this radio I found?") and he fixes Metzger's. There is no Repair roll
for you. An ordinary <Item>Radio</Item> only gets "Darn it. That's close, but I need the radio from my
shack in Klamath."

### Freeing him

Two things free him:

- **Pay Metzger.** Once the radio is fixed, go through "About Vic..." → "Why are you keeping him here?"
  → "Maybe some money might help clear things up?" He wants **$1000**. A female character with
  **Charisma 6+** can ask "Can't we work something out?" and get half off by sleeping with him.
  "About Vic..." needs Intelligence 4 or more.
- **Kill Metzger.** His death frees Vic, but it does not unlock the door: pick the lock out of the
  guard's sight, or once he is dead too.

Freeing Vic gives no XP or karma of its own; killing Metzger earns only what any kill of an evil
character does.

### Asking him to join

Once he is free he asks how he can repay you. "Come with me, I could use a hand." brings him along if
you have a free party slot. If you do not, he walks over to **Mom's** and waits there. If you buy
his freedom and leave without him, you find him at Mom's on your next visit; after Metzger's death he
only moves there once his door has been unlocked. At Charisma 1 (without
Magnetic Personality) your party limit is 0, so he can never join.

There are no karma, reputation or skill checks.

## Quests

- <Quest>Find Vic the Trader</Quest> is in your Pip-Boy from the start and completes the first time
  you talk to him. No XP.
- <Quest>Free Vic from his debt by getting his radio from his house in Klamath and paying Metzger</Quest>
  starts when Vic explains his problem and completes when Metzger is paid or dead. No XP.

### The water flask

Once he is free, ask him "Where did you get this water flask?" (also in his party menu under "I need
to ask you something."). The first time is worth **200 XP** and **+5 karma**, and puts
[Vault City](/walkthrough/vault-city) on your map.

In Vault City you can now ask **Ed** in the Courtyard "Do you know a trader named Vic?" (with Vic
along: "My friend Vic here says he bought a water flask from you."). With
<Item>Vic's Water Flask</Item> in your inventory he remembers where the flask came from. Broken Hills,
New Reno, Redding, the Den and Modoc go on your map as he talks, and hearing him out to the end is
worth **500 XP**.

### His daughter

**Valerie**, who works in the maintenance shed in Vault City's Downtown, is Vic's daughter. She will
not talk from 19:00 to 06:00, and is not there at all if you arrive between 02:00 and 07:00.

1. **Her tools.** <Quest>Deliver pliers (tools) and a wrench to Valerie</Quest>: bring a
   <Item>Wrench</Item> and a <Item>Tool</Item> — she takes them as soon as you talk about her tools,
   even before you have offered to fetch them. **250 XP**, **$50** and **+1 Vault City reputation**.
2. **Come back a day later** for a free <Item>Super Tool Kit</Item>. Only after that does she offer
   anything else, including her weapon upgrades.
3. **Bring Vic.** Talk to her with him in the party — at any point, even at night — and the two of
   them argue. No XP.
4. **Talk them round.** Ask her "What's the deal with you and your father?", then "Sounds like you and
   him have some things to work out." and "Are you sure?", and finally "Look, he was in a mess of
   trouble with slavers…", which needs **Charisma 8+ or Speech 75%+**: **250 XP** and **+3 karma**.
   Vic does not have to be with you for this step.

| Event | XP | Other |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Vic | — | — |
| Ask about the water flask | 200 | +5 karma; Vault City on the map |
| Ed remembers the flask | 500 | Five towns on the map |
| Valerie's tools | 250 | $50, +1 Vault City reputation |
| Reconcile Vic and Valerie | 250 | +3 karma |

:::danger[Never hurt Valerie while Vic is in the party]
Vault City turns hostile, you lose your citizenship, and Vic leaves and attacks you: "I'm going to rip
your darn head off! No one hurts my family!"

If he is **not** in the party at that moment — waiting somewhere, or never recruited — he does not
react, even if he watches you do it.
:::

RPU replaces four of his six stage templates, which raises his HP at stage 2 and his Repair at stages
4–6. <Vanilla>Stage 2 kept 78 HP, so he stalled for a level, and stages 4–6 had Repair 105, 112 and 130
— below his Small Guns at stages 4–5 and his Energy Weapons at stage 6.</Vanilla>

Throwing climbs from 24 to 65, Unarmed from 52 to 60 and Melee from 42 to 50. Science (28–32),
First Aid (26–32) and Doctor (18–21) stay low. Without armour his normal damage threshold and
resistance are 0.

If he is with you from your level 5, his first level-up comes between your levels 5 and 8 and his last
by level 28.

## Gear

He arrives with **nothing**. Everything he carries is what you give him.

### What he can wield

His art has animations for **knives, pistols, and rifles and shotguns**, plus throwing frames for
knives, rocks and grenades. Unarmed weapons need no animation. In his words: "I'm pretty good with
small arms. I can handle almost any rifle, shotgun, and most small-medium pistols. I 'can' use knives.
I've also got a pretty good throwing arm, but my aim's not the best in the world... actually, it's
pretty horrible."

He **cannot** use SMG-animation guns — the 10mm SMG, <Item>14mm Pistol</Item>,
<Item>.223 Pistol</Item>, H&K P90c, "Grease Gun", H&K G11 and G11E — nor laser, plasma or pulse
rifles, any big gun, clubs, hammers or spears. Energy **pistols** are fine: they use the pistol
animation.

### What to give him

Small Guns is his weapon skill from the start; Energy Weapons only reaches 100% at his fourth
level-up. Compare in full on [Weapons and Armour](/equipment).

| Weapon | Skill | Damage | AP | Min ST | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <Item>10mm Pistol</Item> | Small Guns | 5–12 | 5 | 3 | |
| <Item>Hunting Rifle</Item> | Small Guns | 8–20 | 5 | 5 | Long Range |
| <Item>.44 Magnum Revolver</Item> | Small Guns | 12–18 | 4 | 5 | |
| <Item>Combat Shotgun</Item> | Small Guns | 15–25 | 5, burst 6 | 5 | Accurate; 3-round burst |
| <Item>Sniper Rifle</Item> | Small Guns | 14–34 | 6 | 5 | Long Range; single shot only |
| <Item>Pancor Jackhammer</Item> | Small Guns | 18–29 | 5, burst 6 | 5 | Accurate; 5-round burst |
| <Item>PPK12 Gauss Pistol</Item> | Small Guns | 22–32 | 4 | 4 | Accurate; range 50 |
| <Item>M72 Gauss Rifle</Item> | Small Guns | 32–43 | 5 | 6 | Accurate; range 50 |
| <Item>YK32 Pulse Pistol</Item> | Energy | 32–46 | 4 | 3 | Penetrate |
| Alien Blaster | Energy | 30–90 | 4 | 2 | Accurate |

**Mind the AP cost.** He keeps using the same attack until he cannot afford another, so with his
starting 8 AP a 4-AP pistol fires twice a turn and a 5-AP gun only once — if he does not have to move.
At 10 AP (stages 4–5) the 5-AP guns fire twice, and at 12 AP (stage 6) the 4-AP pistols fire three
times.

**Mind his Strength.** It is 5 until his fourth level-up, then 6, then 7. Each point short costs 20% to
hit, so the M72 Gauss Rifle, <Item>H&K CAWS</Item> and <Item>Tommy Gun</Item> (all ST 6) cost him 20%
before then. <Item>Power Armor</Item> and Hardened Power Armor give a party member who wears them
**+3 Strength**, which settles it.

**Set Weapon Pref. to "Ranged"** if you give him a knife as a spare: on his default, "Ranged then
melee", he will fall back to it. Grenades are only thrown under "None", and even then any usable gun
he carries comes first.

Guns with a burst mode fire bursts whenever his chance to hit is 50% or better, and the Burst setting
offers him nothing stricter than "Be careful not to hit me". If he keeps catching you in the spray,
give him a single-shot gun, or switch his bursts off with Party Orders (see [the
overview](/companions/overview)).

With the NPC Armor mod bundled with RPU, his appearance follows his armour:

| He wears | He looks like |
| --- | --- |
| <Item>Leather Jacket</Item>, <Item>Combat Leather Jacket</Item> | Vic in a jacket |
| <Item>Leather Armor</Item>, <Item>Leather Armor Mark II</Item> | Vic in leather |
| <Item>Metal Armor</Item>, <Item>Metal Armor Mark II</Item>, <Item>Tesla Armor</Item> | Vic in metal |
| <Item>Combat Armor</Item>, <Item>Combat Armor Mark II</Item>, <Item>Brotherhood Armor</Item> | Vic in combat armour |
| <Item>Power Armor</Item>, Hardened Power Armor | Vic in power armour |
| <Item>Advanced Power Armor</Item>, <Item>Adv. Power Armor MKII</Item>, <Item>Robes</Item> | The player's own advanced power armour or robe |

His power-armour art has animations for every weapon type, but the mod still limits him to knives,
pistols and rifles; his other armoured looks have the same animations as his normal art. The mod hooks
into the engine through sfall script hooks that FOR:CE implements; that it works under FOR:CE has been
checked in the source, not in a running game.

He never changes armour on his own. Put armour in his pack and press **Use Best Armor** on his combat
control screen; his dialogue can take it off again.

## Repair

No script asks for Vic's Repair. What he does is engine behaviour: when you use a skill on something
from the **Skilldex**, the party member with the highest value in that skill does it instead of you —
provided it is that member's own best skill, and you are not standing next to the target. Ties go to
you.

Repair is Vic's best skill at every stage, so **whenever his Repair beats yours and every other
companion's, he does your Repair jobs.** Scripted Repair rolls made for you use his skill too: the
generator in the [Toxic Caves](/walkthrough/toxic-caves) (a Repair roll at −40) is one, and with it
the elevator. Checks that only look at your skill level, without rolling it, still use your own.

His Science (28–32) is never his best skill, so he never does Science for you.

## Using him

**Combat settings.** Open his combat control screen when he joins. Compared with the full menu, his
choices are limited:

| Setting | Default | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burst | Be careful not to hit me | Always!, Sometimes, Be careful not to hit me |
| Weapon Pref. | Ranged then melee | None, Ranged then melee, Ranged, Unarmed |
| Distance | Stay close to me | Stay close to me, Snipe the enemy, On your own, Stay where you are — no "Charge!" |
| Attack Who | Whoever is closest | Whoever is attacking me, The weakest, Whomever you want, Whoever is closest |
| Chem Use | Stimpaks when hurt a lot | Stimpaks when hurt a bit, Stimpaks when hurt a lot — no "I'm clean" |
| Run Away | Not feeling good | Abject coward, Your finger hurts, You're bleeding a bit, Not feeling good — no "Never!" |
| Disposition | Custom | Custom, Defensive, Aggressive |

:::caution[Leave Run Away alone]
As he comes, he runs only below 10 HP, or when crippled or blinded. Choosing a Run Away option
recalculates that from his maximum HP, and the bravest one he has, "Not feeling good", sends him
running below **40%**.
:::

**Healing.** Told "You should heal yourself now.", he uses a Super Stimpak, Stimpak, Healing Powder,
Doctor's Bag or First Aid Kit from his pack, in that order; with none, he rolls First Aid. Out of
combat he also treats himself from his pack, depending on his Chem Use setting.

**His menu.** "I need to ask you something." holds the water-flask question, and "Where is your friend
Ed, again?" until you have met Ed. Open his menu more than 50 times and you can ask "Would you stop
calling me Boss?" — he tries "Chief", then "Sport".

## Losing him

**He never leaves over karma or reputation.** What does turn him:

| What happens | Result |
| --- | --- |
| You hurt Valerie while he is in the party | Leaves and attacks, along with Vault City |
| You hurt him while he is **not** in your party (before you recruit him, or while he waits) | Attacks on sight — **for good** |
| You steal from him while he is out of the party | The same |

Hitting him while he is **in** your party — on purpose or with a stray burst — does not set him off,
and nor does taking things from him. Looting him while he lies unconscious runs the same script as
stealing, so that is safe too while he is in the party.

**Rejoining after "wait here"** fails, and he stays put, if your Charisma is 1 or the party is full
("Uh, Boss? You seem a bit crowded. I'll just keep waiting here for ya.").

**If he dies**, his gear stays on the body — until you leave the map or load a save, when a companion
killed in combat vanishes with everything he carried (see [Death](/companions/overview#death)).

**Selling him:** Metzger will not buy him back. **Mordino** in [New Reno](/walkthrough/new-reno) and
**Barkus** in [Vault City](/walkthrough/vault-city) each pay $100, or $200 if you haggle and pass a
Barter roll, but only when Miria, Davin and Sulik are not in the party — they take those first.
Mordino costs −3 karma for the offer, −3 for haggling and −10 for the sale, has him killed off-screen
and gives you his gear. Barkus costs −3 for considering it, −3 for haggling and −10 for the sale, and puts the gear in
his box.
