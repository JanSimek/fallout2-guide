# Marcus

> *"I can use just about any big gun, like rocket launchers or miniguns, and the big energy weapons.
> I ain't bad with my fists, and I'm a hell of a shot with grenades. I don't wear armor. They just
> don't make it my size."* — Marcus

Marcus is the super mutant sheriff of Broken Hills, a refugee from the Master's army. He costs nothing,
is worth **1000 XP** the first time he joins, starts with 130 HP and a minigun, and carries 275 lb.
Take him if you have a slot once Broken Hills is sorted out — but keep the party out of his line of
fire, because almost everything he can hold fires in bursts.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [Broken Hills](/walkthrough/broken-hills), on the Downtown map |
| **Joins if** | the air purifier is fixed, the conspiracy is exposed (or the NCR scientist reported), karma 0 or higher, IN 4+, a free slot |
| **Costs** | Nothing |
| **Best at** | Big Guns 115%, Unarmed 110%, Melee Weapons 110%, Energy Weapons 87% |
| **Weapons** | Big guns only — laser rifle class, minigun, rocket launcher — plus fists and throwing |
| **Levels** | 5 level-ups, one for every three of your level-ups from level 12 while he is with you ([how it works](/companions/overview)) |

## Recruiting

Ask him *"You wanna travel with me?"* — an IN 4 option. He agrees only when **all** of these hold:

1. **The purifier works.** <Quest>Fix the mine's air purifier</Quest> is done — repaired, or rewarded
   by Zaius.
2. **The trouble is over**, meaning you have told him at least one of:
   - where the missing people's bodies are (<Quest>Find the missing people for Marcus</Quest>);
   - that Jacob and Aileen asked you to break the convicts out or blow up the mine;
   - where the escaped convicts went;
   - about the NCR scientist, once Dr. Henry has sent you with
     <Quest>Test the mutagenic serum on a super-mutant. Try Broken Hills</Quest>.
3. **Karma 0 or higher.**
4. **A free party slot** — see [party size](/companions/overview).

His refusals tell you which one is missing: *"Ah, there's too much going on right now. When things calm
down, then I just might."* (quests), *"I thought you were one of the good ones. I ain't coming with
you."* (karma), *"Too many people. I've done my traveling with a horde."* (party full).

The first time he joins you get **1000 XP** and +5 Broken Hills reputation. There is no skill check and
no fee.

:::danger[Blowing up the purifier loses him]
If Jacob's conspiracy succeeds, Marcus disappears from Broken Hills for good.
:::

:::caution[Getting him to talk at all]
- Below **−10 karma** (−5 with the Karma Beacon) he only tells you to stay civilised.
- If Broken Hills is hostile to you, he attacks instead of talking.
- Rude answers lower his opinion of you by 10, once each: *"Bullshit. How could you have been around
  that long?"*, *"I'm a little busy."*, *"I don't have time."*, *"Damn right. Gonna do something about
  it, mutie?"*, *"Huh. You're a lot uglier this way. Bye."* and a failed Speech roll after *"I'm not afraid of
  you."*. Once his reaction is down to −25 or worse he brushes you off.
- A character with IN 3 or less cannot recruit him. Answering *"Uh!"* twice starts a fight.
:::

## Quests

Marcus hands out or closes most of the [Broken Hills](/walkthrough/broken-hills) quests. *"What's the
news?"* gives you, in order, the Chad job (unless he dislikes you), the mine, the escaped convicts and
the missing people — *"I'll do it for free."* pleases him and swaps the $500 reward for a Scoped
Hunting Rifle.

*"I have something I wanted to tell you."* is where you report back:

| You tell him | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Where the missing people are | $500, or the Scoped Hunting Rifle if you worked for free. **No XP.** The conspirators go to jail. |
| Jacob and Aileen's plot, or where the escaped convicts are | **2500 XP**, +20 karma, +10 Broken Hills reputation. The conspirators go to jail. |
| About the NCR scientist | **500 XP**, +5 karma, +5 Broken Hills reputation |
| Chad's skimming, once it is proven in Broken Hills and you have his briefcase — or after Chad has given himself up | **1500 XP**, +5 karma, +5 Broken Hills reputation |

That menu stays available while he is in your party, but only on the Broken Hills maps.

## Gear

He arrives with a <Item>Minigun</Item> in his hands, loaded with 120 rounds of <Item>5mm JHP</Item>, three
more packs of it, five <Item>Stimpak</Item>s, two <Item>Antidote</Item>s, a <Item>Flare</Item> and $140.
Take the Antidotes — companions never build up poison, so they do nothing for him.

### Weapons

His art only has big-gun frames, so no knives, clubs, spears, pistols, SMGs, rifles or shotguns. With ST
10 he meets every Strength requirement. Compare them on [Weapons and Armour](/equipment).

| Class | Big Guns | Energy Weapons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laser rifle class | <Item>Bozar</Item>, <Item>M60</Item>, <Item>Light Support Weapon</Item>, <Item>Flamer</Item>, Improved Flamer | <Item>Laser Rifle</Item>, <Item>Plasma Rifle</Item>, <Item>Turbo Plasma Rifle</Item>, <Item>YK42B Pulse Rifle</Item> |
| Minigun | Minigun, <Item>Avenger Minigun</Item>, <Item>Vindicator Minigun</Item> | <Item>Gatling Laser</Item> |
| Rocket launcher | <Item>Rocket Launcher</Item> | — |

He can also punch with a <Item>Power Fist</Item> or Mega Power Fist, and throw grenades.

- **Big Guns is his best skill.** The Minigun does 7–11 a round in 40-round bursts. The **Bozar** (25–35,
  15 rounds) and the **Avenger** (10–14, 40 rounds) are the upgrades; all of these cost 6 AP, so he
  fires once a turn until stage 4 gives him 12 AP.
- **Energy rifles are the safe choice.** They fire single shots for 5 AP — **two shots a turn from
  stage 1** (10 AP). The Turbo Plasma Rifle does 35–70; the YK42B Pulse Rifle 54–78 and cuts armour's
  DT to a fifth. His Energy Weapons skill is lower (87% → 118%), but still good.
- **The Rocket Launcher** (35–100, single shot) is an area weapon, but the AI's check is weak: it holds
  fire only if an ally within 2 hexes of the target would still be hurt after their armour. An ally with
  0% explosion resistance — an unarmoured character, for one — does not stop him.
- **Fists**: the Mega Power Fist does 20–40 for 3 AP, three punches a turn at 9 AP, and his melee
  damage adds to the top of the range. With his default weapon preference (None) he only reaches for it
  when his gun is out of action — unusable, empty with no ammunition to reload, or judged unsafe. Set his
  preference to Unarmed if that is the plan.
- **Grenades**: his Throwing is **32%**, whatever he says. He only considers them with his weapon
  preference on None, and then only when his gun is out of action.

:::danger[His burst weapons ignore the Burst setting]
The Burst setting (and the Party Orders **D** toggle) only decides whether a companion uses a weapon's
*second* firing mode. Every minigun, the Bozar, M60, Light Support Weapon and Gatling Laser have a burst
as their **only** mode, and the flamers a flame attack — so Marcus fires them that way every time.

What remains is the engine's safety check, which simulates **one** random spray and only objects if an
ally caught in it would take damage after their armour. An ally with 0% resistance to the damage type
always passes, and the real burst is a fresh roll. Keep the party out of his line of fire, or give him
an energy rifle.
:::

### Armour

**What follows assumes RPU's default `marcus_armor=1`** (in `mods/rpu.ini`).

**Bill**, the trader at the [Abbey](/walkthrough/abbey), has one set of Mutant Armor. Ask him *"Say, I've
noticed a lot of old equipment and items have been collected here at the Abbey…"* — he offers it **only
with Marcus in your party**, for **$3000**. Bill puts it on Marcus himself (*"Hey, this fits
nicely!"*). His look changes, and with it one thing he can do: the armoured art has no flame-attack
frames, so **he cannot use the flamers while wearing it** — unless you installed RPU's optional
*Extended flamer attack animations*, which adds them. Everything else in the table above still works.

| At stage 5 | AC | Normal | Laser | Fire | Plasma | Electrical | Explosion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marcus alone | 29 | 5/40% | 8/60% | 4/30% | 4/50% | 2/50% | 6/40% |
| **Marcus in Mutant Armor** | **44** | 6/40% | 18/80% | 4/30% | 10/60% | 12/60% | 8/50% |
| <Item>Combat Armor</Item> on its own | 20 | 5/40% | 8/60% | 4/30% | 4/50% | 2/50% | 6/40% |
| <Item>Power Armor</Item> on its own | 25 | 12/40% | 18/80% | 12/60% | 10/40% | 12/40% | 20/50% |

The armour adds a lot of AC and laser, plasma and electrical protection, but **only +1 DT against normal
damage and nothing at all against fire**, and it weighs 100 lb of his 275.

- *"I need to talk to you about your gear."* → *"I need you to remove the armor that you're wearing."*
  turns it into a 100-lb inventory item, and *"Can you put your armor on?"* puts it back. That line is
  the only way to re-equip it.
- If he dies, the armour is left on his body as that item — but a companion killed in combat vanishes
  with everything he carries the next time the map loads ([dead companions](/companions/overview)), so loot
  him before you leave.

**With `marcus_armor=0`**, Bill never offers it and Marcus has no armour at all.

Either way, nothing else fits him: the combat control screen's *Use Best Armor* skips Marcus, and the
bundled NPC Armor mod does not cover him.
<Vanilla>There is no armour for Marcus.</Vanilla>

## Using him

**Combat settings.** He allows only *Charge!* or *On your own* for Distance, and *You need a tourniquet*
or *Never!* for Run Away. His Burst choices are *Always!*, *Sometimes* and *Be careful not to hit me* —
which, as above, makes no difference to his burst-only weapons.

**Healing.** He has no automatic out-of-combat healing, unlike Lenny and Goris. In combat he takes
stimpaks below 30% HP on the default *Stimpaks when hurt a lot*. Out of combat, *"You should heal
yourself now."* uses a healing item he carries, or rolls his First Aid (30%) for 5–15 HP.

**Places that object to him:**

- **Vault City** shuts and locks its gate while he is in your party (*"That mutant had better step away
  from the gate. Now."*), unless you are Captain of the Guard or the Enclave is destroyed. Tell him to
  **wait** outside — a waiting companion is no longer in the party list, so the guard stops objecting.
- **Dr. Troy** shouts *"A mutant! Guards!"* and turns hostile if Marcus is with you, unless you are
  Captain of the Guard. (Talking to him also works once the Enclave is destroyed, but the check he repeats
  every 20–40 seconds ignores that.)
- **Lynette** is set against you from the start if you talk to her with him along, and two of the
  replies make her hostile.
- **Lou** and **Josh** in [Redding](/walkthrough/redding) only brush you off with a remark.

:::tip[Dr. Troy's ammunition]
As Captain of the Guard, let Dr. Troy heal an **injured** Marcus. Once, he hands over one pack each of
.223 FMJ (50), .45 (10), 10mm JHP (24), 9mm Ball (20), 5mm JHP (50) and 7.62mm (20), and two packs each
of .44 FMJ and .44 JHP (20 each).
:::

## Losing him

- **He never leaves on his own** while following you.
- **After "wait here"**, *"Let's go."* is refused, and he stays behind, if you have CH 1, the Slaver or
  Childkiller reputation, karma of −101 or lower (−51 with the Karma Beacon), or a full party.
- **He turns hostile only on the Broken Hills maps** — when the town is hostile to you, or when you stole
  from or insulted him — and only if he can see you.
  <Vanilla>An enemy of Broken Hills was attacked by him on any map.</Vanilla>
- **Hitting him in Broken Hills while he is not in your party** makes him a permanent enemy and turns the
  whole town hostile. Outside Broken Hills, or while he is with you, his script ignores it.
- **Being caught stealing** from him while he is not in your party makes him hostile.

If he dies, his armour stays on the body. He gets an [ending](/reference/endings) slide — travelling east
in search of other refugees from the Master's army — if he is alive at the end and has ever joined you, or
if you foiled the conspiracy with karma 0 or higher.
