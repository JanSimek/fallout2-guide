# Marcus

**Found in:** [Broken Hills](/walkthrough/broken-hills)

The super mutant sheriff, and a companion of the Master's army from Fallout 1. Joins after you finish
<Quest>Fix the mine's air purifier</Quest> and <Quest>Find the missing people for Marcus</Quest>:
**1000 XP**.

A mixed blessing. He is big, carries a great deal, and does enormous damage — and he is slow, cannot
wear armour, and favours weapons that kill your own party.

## Statistics

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Main skill** | Big Guns |
| **Also good at** | Energy Weapons, Unarmed |
| **Animations** | Throw, large rifle, minigun, rocket launcher |
| **AC/DT/DR** | 20/4/30 (lv 1) · 10/4/30 (lv 2–4) · 20/5/40 (lv 5–6) |
| **HP by level** | 130 · 145 · 165 · 175 · 190 · 205 |
| **AP by level** | 9 · 10 · 11 · 11 · 12 · 13 |

He is the **only companion who can use miniguns and large energy weapons**.

## Weapons

| Stage | Weapon |
| --- | --- |
| Broken Hills | Minigun |
| If you find one | Bozar — expect to scrape companions off walls |
| As soon as possible | **Turbo Plasma Rifle** |
| Late | Pulse Rifle |

A **Mega Power Fist** works well if he survives charging ahead with it.

:::danger[Set his combat options carefully]
Left to himself he will burst-fire a minigun through your entire party. This is the single most
common way to lose Sulik.
:::

## Armour

He cannot wear normal armour — but RPU adds an exception.

:::tip[Metal armour for Marcus]
With the **NPC Armor** mod installed, talk to **Trader Bill** at the [Abbey](/walkthrough/abbey) and
ask if he has found anything special. **$3000**, and Marcus must be in your party.

Tell Marcus to wear it **through dialogue**, not with the "wear best armor" button.

Fully levelled and armoured he is better protected than combat armor, though not quite power armor.
<Vanilla>There is no armour for Marcus at all.</Vanilla>
:::

## Where he cannot go

:::caution
**Vault City** will not admit Marcus unless you are Captain of the Guard, and bringing him in anyway
turns the city hostile. Do not talk to Lynette with him in your party under any circumstances.

**Lou** in Redding and **Josh** at the restaurant will not speak to you with him along.

**The Enclave** allows only one companion, and he is a reasonable choice for it.
:::

:::tip
As Captain of the Guard you can let **Dr. Troy** heal Marcus, which produces a windfall of ammunition:
20 7.62mm, 50 5mm JHP, 20 9mm ball, 24 10mm JHP, 10 .45, 40 .44 Magnum JHP, 40 .44 Magnum FMJ and 50
.223 FMJ.
:::

## Losing him

- **Will not join** below 0 karma
- **Will not rejoin** at karma −101 or lower, or as a Slaver or Childkiller
- **Attacks you anywhere** — not just in Broken Hills — if you are an enemy of Broken Hills and he is
  not in your party
- Attacking Marcus anywhere turns **Broken Hills** hostile
