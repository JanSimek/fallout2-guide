# Goris

**Found in:** [Vault 13](/walkthrough/vault-13)

An intelligent, scholarly deathclaw in a hooded robe. Gruthar points you at him with
<Quest>Talk to Goris</Quest>; ask him everything before asking him to join.

:::danger[Ask every question first]
Leave the conversation early and you can only ever ask him to join. The rest of his dialogue —
including the one that puts the **Military Base** on your map — is gone.
:::

He cannot wear armour, and the animation of him removing and putting on his robe is slow enough to be
genuinely irritating.

## Is he worth it?

He lands a high proportion of **critical hits** and carries a lot — he can take a floater down in one
or two turns. Against that: he arrives very late, his lack of combat options leaves him exposed, and a
fully equipped Sulik or Cassidy in Power Armor with a Mega Power Fist outclasses him.

:::caution[Where he cannot go]
- **The Enclave** — he simply dies there. Leave him behind.
- **Navarro** — impossible to keep alive.
- **Redding** — Lou and Josh will not talk to you with him along.

He walks into **Vault City** without comment, apparently on the strength of the cloak.
:::

## Quirks

:::note[The rejoin bug]
Try to recruit Goris after the Vault 13 massacre and he joins, then immediately leaves again — a
variable that never resets. It happens if he is in Vault 13 and you never went through the "pack is in
trouble" conversation.

**Fix:** save and reload, or leave Vault 13 so you are outside the vault door and come back.
:::

:::note
When Goris leaves your party he **tags along invisibly**, waiting to reappear in his usual spot when
you next pass Vault 13. In that state he does **not** count against your party limit — and he
**continues to gain levels** as if he were with you.
:::

## Losing him

- **Will not join** below 0 karma, with a negative Vault 13 reputation, or as a Berserker or
  Childkiller
- **Leaves** and returns to Vault 13 if your karma falls below 0, or you become a Berserker or
  Childkiller
- **Will not rejoin** if your Vault 13 reputation is Hated or worse

If he is with you at the time of the Vault 13 massacre he leaves to return there, and you can pick him
up again afterwards.
