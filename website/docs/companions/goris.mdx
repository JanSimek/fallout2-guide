# Goris

> *"I'm sorry to have deceived you, but humans have a habit of shooting deathclaws on sight."* — Goris

Goris is the scholar of the Vault 13 deathclaws, hiding his nature under a hooded robe. He fights only
with his claws, but his Unarmed skill runs from 110% to 170%, his claws cut through armour, and he ends
with 15 AP. He costs nothing and needs no equipment; the price is a long list of things that send him
home. His conversation is also how you find the Military Base.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [Vault 13](/walkthrough/vault-13), on level 3 of the vault |
| **Joins if** | karma 0 or higher, Vault 13 reputation 0 or higher, not a Childkiller or Berserker, a free slot — and, the first time, the right questions asked |
| **Costs** | Nothing; no XP either |
| **Best at** | Unarmed 110%, with melee damage 23 and 11 AP |
| **Weapons** | Unarmed only — his claws |
| **Levels** | 6 level-ups, one for every four of your level-ups from level 10 while he is with you ([how it works](/companions/overview)) |

## Recruiting

Gruthar's <Quest>Talk to Goris</Quest> (Gordon can start it too) points you at him; **any** conversation
with Goris completes it, for no XP.

### The first conversation

Only on the Vault 13 map, he opens with *"Hello, I'm Goris. I don't recognize you; are you new here?"*.
*"Can I ask you some questions?"* or *"Mind if I ask you some questions?"* leads to his question list —
and **this is the only conversation that has it**.

| Question | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| *"What can you tell me about the deathclaws here?"* | Opens the travel option. Read on through MORE and he mentions the Enclave — Dr. Henry and the slave overseer in NCR both check whether you have heard this. |
| *"Do you know what happened to the people who used to live here in the vault?"* | Also mentions the Enclave. Needed, with the deathclaw question, before you can ask him to join. |
| *"How long have you been here?"* | Needed for the Enclave question. |
| *"Do you have any information on this Enclave you spoke of?"* | **Marks the Military Base on your world map** (*"The Enclave showed an interest in some ruins to the west of here."*). Appears once he has mentioned the Enclave and you have asked how long he has been here. |
| *"You mentioned you wanted to do some traveling. How about joining me?"* | The join check below. Appears after the deathclaw and vault-people questions. |

The other questions (what he is doing here, whether he is a prisoner, Gruthar, the threat the deathclaws
pose, the virus) are conversation only.

:::danger[Ask about the Enclave before you leave]
Later conversations offer only *"Hey, I'm looking for a traveling companion. You up for it?"* and
goodbyes, so the Military Base question is gone once you walk away. The one way back to the list is being
turned down: *"I understand. Perhaps you'll reconsider later."* or *"Ok, I understand."* return you to
the questions.
:::

If you tell him you have had adventures (*"Yes, I've had some adventures…"*), he answers your questions
in exchange for your stories when you say goodbye: *"Gladly…"* passes three hours, *"Yeah, sure."* one,
and *"Promise? What promise?"* gets you out of it. There is no reward either way.

### The join check

He refuses — *"(Goris hesitates and you get the feeling you are being carefully scrutinized.) I'm sorry,
but I don't think that you and I share the same ideals…"* — if any of these apply:

- karma below 0;
- Vault 13 reputation below 0;
- the Childkiller or Berserker reputation (see [Reputation](/character/reputation)).

Otherwise he joins when the conversation ends. With a full party he says he will wait for a place to open.

**On a later visit** — or wherever you left him waiting — *"Hey, I'm looking for a traveling companion.
You up for it?"* goes straight to this check; no questions needed.

:::note[Low Intelligence]
With IN 3 or less, the first conversation runs *"Me [your name]. Me new here."* → *"Uh... 'seasoned'?"*
→ *"Food seasoned, not me."* → *"Uh... Okay."*, and he joins if he does not dislike you. That path only
checks your party size and whether Vault 13 hates you; with negative karma, or as a Childkiller or
Berserker, he walks off again on his next heartbeat (see [Losing him](#losing-him)). Later, *"You come
me?"* runs the full check. See [Low Intelligence](/systems/low-intelligence).
:::

## Gear

He has a **claw in each hand**: 3–4 damage, 3 AP a swipe, and the Penetrate perk, which cuts the target's
damage threshold to a fifth. His melee damage is added to the top of that range, so a swipe does 3–27 at
recruitment. With 11 AP that is three attacks a turn, four from stage 2, five at stage 6.

His art can **punch and nothing else** — no kick, no throw, no weapon of any class. He has no gear
menu, and nothing puts armour on him: *Use Best Armor* works only for two-legged companions, and the
bundled NPC Armor mod does not cover him.

The only items his art could use are punching weapons: <Item>Brass Knuckles</Item>,
<Item>Spiked Knuckles</Item>, <Item>Boxing Gloves</Item>, <Item>Plated Boxing Gloves</Item>, the
<Item>Power Fist</Item> (12–24) and the Mega Power Fist (20–40) — the last two with Penetrate, like his
claws.
<Unverified>FOR:CE explicitly lets Goris pick weapons from his inventory, and a punching weapon passes
every engine check on paper, but nobody has confirmed that he actually wields one in play.</Unverified>
Compare them on [Weapons and Armour](/equipment).

## The deathclaw underneath

When combat starts, FOR:CE plays his disrobing animation and turns him into a grey deathclaw; when it ends
he puts the robe back on. Examined mid-fight, he is *"a gray deathclaw. It's Goris."*

RPU can speed that animation up: `goris_derobing_speed` in `mods/upu.ini` is **50** frames per second by
default (16 is the original speed, 100 the maximum, 0 leaves it alone). **Under FOR:CE this setting does
nothing**: RPU applies it by rewriting the animation files with sfall's file functions (`fs_seek`,
`fs_write_short`), which FOR:CE does not implement. The installer's *Faster derobing for Goris* →
*Low FPS* option takes a different route — it installs a separate data file with the animation, loaded
like any other mod.

After your first map change together, his menu gains *"You're a deathclaw? Is there a reason you didn't
mention this to me before?"*, once. *"I understand why you felt it necessary to hide your identity…"*
keeps him; *"I'm sorry, but I don't like being deceived. You should leave."* only makes him **wait where
he is**.

## Using him

**Combat settings** are nearly fixed: Weapon Pref. is *Unarmed* only, Distance is *Charge!* only, and
there are no Burst options. Attack Who is open — the default is *Whoever is closest*; *Whoever is
attacking me* keeps him on the enemies going for you. Run Away offers *You need a tourniquet* (the
default) or *Never!*.

**Healing.** He **never uses stimpaks in combat** — the engine only lets two-legged critters take drugs
there, whatever his Chem Use says. Out of combat he uses carried healing items by himself when he is
down to 60% (*Stimpaks when hurt a bit*) or 30% (*hurt a lot*, the default). *"You should heal yourself
now."* uses an item, or rolls his First Aid for 5–15 HP. Party Orders' heal and loot orders skip him.

**Towns.** Vault City's gate guard has no objection to him. Lou and Josh in
[Redding](/walkthrough/redding) only brush you off with a remark while he is along.

:::caution[The Enclave and Navarro]
Goris is said to simply die at the Enclave, and to be impossible to keep alive in Navarro.
<Unverified>Both warnings are inherited. No Enclave or Navarro script treats Goris specially — the one
Enclave script that names him, the reactor, shocks you and every companion alike when it is blown
up.</Unverified>
:::

## Losing him

**He walks home** when, while he is in the party:

- your karma drops below 0, or you become a Childkiller or Berserker — *"Your not anyone I want to be
  around I am leaving."*;
- his pack is in trouble (below).

Walking home, he stays in your party list as an **invisible** member until you next enter Vault 13, where
he reappears. In that state he **does not count** against your party limit — and he **keeps levelling**
with you.

### The pack in trouble

Gruthar's reward for <Quest>Fix the Vault 13 computer</Quest> starts a two-week timer. After that, the
next time you are outside Vault 13 — provided Gruthar is alive and Dr. Schreber in
[Navarro](/walkthrough/navarro) is not dead — the Vault 13 massacre takes place. The next time Goris is
with you and visible, he tells you *"I suddenly sense that my pack is in trouble. I am sorry to leave
you, but I must check on my brothers."* and heads home (it waits until any fight is over).

In Vault 13 he then only says *"The security cameras recorded what has happened. You should go check
it."* Once you have watched the recording, *"I wish to avenge my brethren."* → *"All right. Let's do it."*
runs the normal join check, and the pack-in-trouble departure cannot happen afterwards — at worst his
party menu opens by itself once. If he is with you when the recording ends: *"Their blood will run like
the Nile!"*
<Vanilla>If he had not been through the pack-in-trouble talk, recruiting him after the massacre made him
leave again at once.</Vanilla>

### Other ways to lose him

- **Right after "wait here"**, *"Let's go."* is refused with a full party, or at Vault 13 reputation −15
  or worse (*"I think I will do better on my own."*). Talk to him later and it is the full join check
  again.
- **Hitting him while he is not in your party** (before he joins, or while he waits) makes him a
  permanent enemy who attacks on sight. While he is with you — visibly or walking home — the game never
  runs that check.
- **Being caught stealing** from him while he is not in your party makes him hostile. Looting him while
  he is in the party and unconscious does nothing.
- His script has a check meant to turn him on you when he targets a Vault 13 deathclaw; under FOR:CE it
  never fires.

If he dies, nothing special happens to his claws. He has no ending slide.
