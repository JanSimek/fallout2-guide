# Sulik

> *"We be good with fists, spear, big hammer or sub-machine gun. We be a handful."* — Sulik

A warrior from the [Umbra Tribe](/walkthrough/umbra-tribe) who came to Klamath looking for his sister
after slavers took her, got drunk, broke things, and now owes $350. Take him: he is available almost at once, his
Melee Weapons and Unarmed skills are the highest on his sheet at every stage, and bringing him along
is worth an extra 500 XP when you find his sister.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [Klamath](/walkthrough/klamath), Buckner House, a few steps from Maida |
| **Joins if** | His debt is settled, your Charisma is 2 or more, you have a free party slot, your karma is −100 or better, and you are not a Childkiller, Slaver or Berserker |
| **Costs** | $350, or a rescue reward from Maida or Ardin Buckner |
| **Best at** | Melee Weapons 95%, Unarmed 90%, Throwing 80%, Sneak 75%, Small Guns 70% |
| **Weapons** | Knife, hammer, spear and SMG; also unarmed weapons, and thrown knives, spears and grenades |
| **Levels** | 6 level-ups, one for every 3 of yours from your level 6 — see [how levelling works](/companions/overview) |

## Recruiting

He is standing in Buckner House. Whatever route you take, freeing him is worth **500 XP** and
**+10 Klamath reputation**, and you get it once:

| Route | Cost | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pay Sulik | $350 | Ask him "What are you doing here?", then "You have to pay for damages?", then "Here's the $350." |
| Pay Maida | $350 | Ask Maida "Tell me about Sulik." and work round to "What if someone else paid Sulik's damages?" Both ways of saying you will pay lower her opinion of you. |
| Maida's reward | Nothing | Once <Quest>Rescue Torr</Quest> has succeeded, ask Maida to free Sulik in return for finding Torr. |
| Ardin's reward | Nothing | When Ardin thanks you for finding Torr or Smiley (<Quest>Rescue Smiley the Trapper</Quest>) — each only if you took that job from her — ask her to "consider Sulik's debt paid" (**+75 karma**). Then **talk to Maida** — he is not free until you do, and she hands out the XP. |

The Ardin route is the best deal: no money, and the karma on top. Each rescue earns its own thank-you
from Ardin, so if you do both you can take Sulik's freedom for one and **$100** for the other.

Once Ardin has forgiven the debt, Sulik stops offering to take your money ("…I just now need to make
all the arrangements with Maida."), so you cannot collect the 500 XP twice.

:::caution[Low Intelligence]
With IQ 3 or less, paying Sulik himself is the only route: Maida has nothing to say about him, and
Ardin's reward never comes. His "stupid spirits" talk still leads to the payment: answer "Uh?", "Uh-huh!", "Huh?" and "Uh?",
then "Uh-huh!" to pay. It need not be your first meeting — later conversations usually offer "Uh?"
again.
:::

### Asking him to join

Once he is free, ask him along ("Now that you're free, why not travel with me?", or "That'd be
great." straight after paying him). He refuses a Charisma 1 character, and a full party gets "You
have your army. Don't need we and I for help now." There is no money, karma or skill check at the
join itself, but he turns away a character he objects to:

- **Slaver** reputation: at your first meeting he will not talk to you ("One of you took our sis. Bad
  karma... for you."); talk to him again and he turns on you for good.
- **Childkiller**: he refuses you for good.
- **Karma below −100**: he refuses until it recovers.
- **Berserker**: he talks and agrees, but walks out again at once.

Your very first conversation checks only for Slaver, so a Childkiller or a character with karma below
−100 can get his agreement then — and he leaves again straight away.

His sister's quest starts the moment he agrees to join.

## His sister

<Quest rpu>Find Sulik's missing sister</Quest> starts when Sulik joins, or when you meet his sister
Kurisu without him. She is caged in the [Slaver's Camp](/walkthrough/slavers-camp), which is not on
your map until someone tells you where it is — and every one of them wants the quest already active:

| Who | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| A slave you freed in [the Den](/walkthrough/the-den) | Ask "Wait, one more thing. I'm looking for someone specific…" |
| Metzger, the Den | **Charisma 6+ or Speech 51%+** to get him talking, then a Speech roll at −10. One try. |
| Vortis, [NCR](/walkthrough/ncr) | Ask to buy slaves; he sells the location for **$1000**, or **$2000** if your karma is over 500 (over 250 with Karma Beacon; never with Cult of Personality) |

The Umbra Tribe's chief only sends you to Metzger and the Den; he does not mark the camp.

**Bring Sulik to the camp.** With him in the party:

- talking to Kurisu pays **500 XP** on the spot and completes
  <Quest rpu>Reunite Sulik and his sister, Kurisu</Quest>;
- you can say "Alright Sulik, it's time for your plan." (or nod at him): he attacks the guard outside
  and the slavers attack you on sight.

The rest of the rescue — the key, picking the cage, slipping away — is on the
[Slaver's Camp](/walkthrough/slavers-camp) page. Leaving with Kurisu takes you to the Umbra Tribe (a
day by car, three on foot), where you get **1000 XP** and **+15 Umbra Tribe reputation**. The Pip-Boy
quest completes a moment later, when Kurisu starts her homecoming talk.

If you rescued her without Sulik, tell her "No problem, I'll bring him here." and bring him to the
village for the same 500 XP. He then leaves the party, as below.

| Event | XP | Other |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Sulik | 500 | +10 Klamath reputation |
| Ardin forgives the debt | — | +75 karma |
| Reunite him with Kurisu | 500 | — |
| Bring Kurisu home | 1000 | +15 Umbra Tribe reputation |

## Gear

He arrives with a <Item>Leather Jacket</Item> (AC 8, DR 20%) and a <Item>Sledgehammer</Item>, and
nothing else.

### What he can wield

His art has animations for **knives, hammers, spears and SMGs**, plus throwing frames for knives,
spears, rocks and grenades. Unarmed weapons need no animation. That leaves out clubs, pistols, rifles,
shotguns and every big gun — and every energy weapon, since none of them uses one of his animations.

The **14mm Pistol, .223 Pistol, H&K G11 and G11E** use the SMG animation, so he can use them.

### What to give him

His Melee Weapons is 25–50 points higher than his Small Guns at every stage, and his Strength (7, then
8) covers everything below. Every weapon's numbers are in the [Object Database](/database).

| Weapon | Skill | Damage | AP | Min ST | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <Item>Combat Knife</Item> | Melee | 3–10 | 3 | 2 | Three attacks at 9 AP |
| <Item>"Little Jesus"</Item> | Melee | 5–14 | 3 | 2 | Penetrate |
| <Item>Sharpened Spear</Item> | Melee | 4–12 | 4 | 4 | Reach of 2 hexes |
| <Item>Ripper</Item> | Melee | 15–32 | 4 | 4 | Penetrate; takes Small Energy Cells |
| <Item>Super Sledge</Item> | Melee | 18–36 | 3 swing, 4 thrust | 5 | Knockback |
| <Item>Power Fist</Item> / Mega Power Fist | Unarmed | 12–24 / 20–40 | 3 | 1 | Penetrate; Small Energy Cells; see below |
| <Item>10mm SMG</Item> | Small Guns | 5–12 | 5, burst 6 | 4 | 10-round burst |
| <Item>H&K P90c</Item> | Small Guns | 12–16 | 4, burst 5 | 4 | 12-round burst |
| <Item>.223 Pistol</Item> | Small Guns | 20–30 | 5 | 5 | Penetrate; single shot only |
| <Item>H&K G11E</Item> | Small Guns | 13–23 | 5, burst 6 | 6 | 7-round burst; 4.7mm caseless |

:::tip[Take the sledgehammer off him]
On his default settings he uses a melee weapon's **second** attack whenever his chance to hit is 50%
or better, and keeps using that same attack until he cannot afford another. The Sledgehammer's thrust
costs 5 AP, so at his starting 9 AP he makes **one** attack a turn (4–9 damage) where a Spear gets two
and a Combat Knife three. From his first level-up (10 AP) he manages two.
:::

:::caution[Power Fists need a different weapon preference]
A Power Fist punches, and punching is an **unarmed** attack. His default Weapon Pref., "Ranged then
melee", does not include unarmed, so he will never use one. Set Weapon Pref. to **"Unarmed"**. "None"
allows it too, but ranks any gun, thrown weapon or melee weapon he carries above the fist.
:::

Guns with a burst mode fire bursts whenever his chance to hit is 50% or better and the game's weak
safety check passes, and the Burst setting
offers him nothing stricter than "Be careful not to hit me". If he keeps catching you in the spray,
give him the single-shot .223 or <Item>14mm Pistol</Item>, or switch his bursts off with Party Orders
(see [the overview](/companions/overview)).

Grenades are only thrown under Weapon Pref. "None", which Sulik's settings do offer — and even then,
any usable gun he carries comes first.

### Armour

With the NPC Armor mod bundled with RPU, his appearance follows his armour:

| He wears | He looks like |
| --- | --- |
| <Item>Leather Jacket</Item>, <Item>Combat Leather Jacket</Item> | Sulik in a jacket |
| <Item>Leather Armor</Item>, <Item>Leather Armor Mark II</Item> | Sulik in leather |
| <Item>Metal Armor</Item>, <Item>Metal Armor Mark II</Item>, <Item>Tesla Armor</Item> | Sulik in metal |
| <Item>Combat Armor</Item>, <Item>Combat Armor Mark II</Item>, <Item>Brotherhood Armor</Item> | Sulik in combat armour |
| <Item>Power Armor</Item>, Hardened Power Armor | Sulik in power armour |
| <Item>Advanced Power Armor</Item>, <Item>Adv. Power Armor MKII</Item>, <Item>Robes</Item>, <Item>Bridgekeeper's Robes</Item> | The player's own advanced power armour or robe |

His own armoured looks have the same weapon animations as his normal art. The borrowed advanced power
armour and robe looks have every animation, but the mod still allows him only knives, hammers, spears
and SMGs, so armour never changes what he can wield. The mod hooks into the engine through sfall script hooks that FOR:CE
implements; that it works under FOR:CE has been checked in the source, not in a running game.

He never changes armour on his own. Put armour in his pack and press **Use Best Armor** on his combat
control screen; his dialogue can take it off again.

## Using him

**Combat settings.** Open his combat control screen when he joins. Compared with the full menu, his
choices are limited:

| Setting | Default | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burst | Be careful not to hit me | Always!, Sometimes, Be careful not to hit me |
| Weapon Pref. | Ranged then melee | None, Melee, Melee then ranged, Ranged then melee, Unarmed — **no "Ranged"** |
| Distance | Charge! | All five |
| Attack Who | The strongest | Whoever is attacking me, The strongest, Whomever you want, Whoever is closest |
| Chem Use | Stimpaks when hurt a lot | All five |
| Run Away | You need a tourniquet | You need a tourniquet, Never! |
| Disposition | Custom | Custom, Defensive, Aggressive, Berserk — no Coward |

On the default settings he also runs when crippled or blinded, or below 10 HP.

**Outdoorsman.** When the game checks whether you spot an encounter on the world map, it uses the best
Outdoorsman in the party. His goes from 40% to **90%** at his last stage.

**He never uses your skills for you.** A companion only takes over a skill when it is their own best
skill and higher than everyone else's. Sulik's best is always Melee Weapons.

**Healing.** Told "You should heal yourself now.", he uses a Super Stimpak, Stimpak, Healing Powder,
Doctor's Bag or First Aid Kit from his pack, in that order; with none, he rolls First Aid. Out of
combat he also treats himself from his pack, depending on his Chem Use setting.

**Keep your record clean.** He walks out over low karma and bad reputations, and over trouble in
Klamath — see [Losing him](#losing-him).

## Consulting the spirits

His menu has **"Sulik, can you consult the spirits for me?"** Each question you then ask makes a
**Luck roll** for you (a d10 against your Luck):

- **Success:** a remark about where you are, for most towns and sites in the game. Anywhere else, "They
  don't want to talk right now."
- **Failure:** one of ten generic riddles.

Both questions roll the same way, so at Luck 10 you always get the answer for where you are.

**"Where exactly is your tribe, Sulik?"** (or "Tell me about the Great Salt Water.") puts the
[Umbra Tribe](/walkthrough/umbra-tribe) on your world map.

## At the Umbra Tribe

If you bring him to his village before you have found his sister, he says "This here be our village.
We and I go speak with our friends." and **leaves the party** to wander the village. He does the same
after Kurisu's homecoming.

For two game hours after he leaves you, he only says "We do some byes and catch you up on the road
out." After that he asks to come back with you. Tell him to stay ("No, I think it is time we part our
ways…" or "No, I think it's time you looked for her yourself.") and say goodbye, and he **hands you his
entire inventory** and stays in the village; you can still ask him back later.

This homecoming is RPU's.
<Vanilla>The voiced lines for it are in the original game's files, but the original has no Umbra Tribe
map, so they never play.</Vanilla>

## Losing him

| What happens | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Karma drops below −100 (as he sees it: the Karma Beacon doubles it, Cult of Personality counts it as good) | Leaves; will rejoin once it recovers |
| You become a Berserker | Leaves, and walks out again if you take him back |
| You become a Childkiller | Leaves, never talks to you again |
| You become a Slaver | Leaves, never rejoins, and attacks you on sight |
| You are in Klamath while the town is hostile | Leaves and never rejoins. Out of the party, he attacks you there. |
| You are in the Umbra Tribe while it is hostile | Leaves and turns hostile |
| You hurt him while he is **not** in your party (before you recruit him, or while he waits) | Attacks you. Hurting him in downtown Klamath also turns the town hostile. |
| You steal from him while he is out of the party | Turns hostile |

Neither of the last two is permanent. After his first attack he calms down, and away from a hostile
Klamath he can be talked to and recruited again. Hitting him while he is **in** your party — on
purpose or with a stray burst — does not set him off at all.

**Rejoining after "wait here"** fails, and he stays put, if your Charisma is 1, the party is full, or
your **Klamath reputation is −15 or lower** — wherever you are, unless you have Cult of Personality.
The same check applies when you ask him back at the Umbra Tribe.

**If he dies**, his gear stays on the body — until you leave the map or load a save, when a companion
killed in combat vanishes with everything he carried (see [Death](/companions/overview#death)). Kurisu
and the Umbra chief mourn him.

**Selling him** is possible in three places, each for bad karma:

- **Metzger** ([the Den](/walkthrough/the-den)) pays 2 × 600 × (160 + your Barter) ÷ (160 + Metzger's
  Barter) and keeps his gear; −15 karma. Sulik ends up in the slave pen.
- **Mordino** ([New Reno](/walkthrough/new-reno)) says 100 chips but actually pays **$200**. Haggling
  (a Barter roll at +10) leaves it at $200 on a success and cuts it to $100 on a failure. −3 karma for
  the offer, −3 for haggling and −10 for the sale. Sulik is killed off-screen and his gear comes back
  to you.
- **Barkus** ([Vault City](/walkthrough/vault-city)) pays $100, or $200 if you haggle and pass a
  Barter roll; −3 karma for considering it, −3 for haggling, −10 for the sale. His gear goes into
  Barkus's box.

Mordino and Barkus take one companion at a time and pick Miria, then Davin, before Sulik.
