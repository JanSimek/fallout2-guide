# Sulik

**Found in:** [Klamath](/walkthrough/klamath), Buckner House

Your first companion, and he should be with you to the end. A tribal from the
[Umbra Tribe](/walkthrough/umbra-tribe) looking for his sister, working off a $350 bar tab.

See [Klamath](/walkthrough/klamath) for the three ways to free him — always talk to **Sulik**, not
Maida, for the 500 XP.

## Statistics

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Main skill** | Melee Weapons |
| **Also good at** | Small Guns, Unarmed, Throwing, **Outdoorsman** |
| **Animations** | Knife, sledgehammer, spear, SMG |
| **HP by level** | 85 · 93 · 103 · 113 · 123 · 134 |
| **AP by level** | 9 · 10 · 10 · 11 · 11 · 12 |

He reaches **Outdoorsman 80%** at his highest level, the best of any companion, and it applies
automatically on the world map.

## Weapons

:::tip Take the sledgehammer away immediately
He prefers the slow sledgehammer attack and gets **one** swing per round with it — against **two**
with a spear and **three** with a knife.
:::

| Stage | Weapon |
| --- | --- |
| Klamath | **Sharpened Spear** or **Combat Knife** |
| The Den / Vault City | 10mm SMG |
| NCR / Vault 15 | H&K P90c, or **(Mega) Power Fist** |
| San Francisco | H&K G11E, once you have caseless ammunition |

A **.223 Pistol** is the safer gun choice if you dislike being burst-fired through. The **Super
Sledge** is worth considering if you want him knocking enemies away so you can shoot them.

## Consulting the spirits

Sulik can "consult the spirits" for location-dependent hints. They are extremely vague and not worth
studying.

:::note
RPU unlocks Sulik's voiced dialogue about his sister. You will not notice until you reach the
[Umbra Tribe](/walkthrough/umbra-tribe).
<Vanilla>The lines exist in the files but are never triggered.</Vanilla>
:::

## Losing him

- **Leaves** if you become a Childkiller or Berserker, or your karma drops to −101 or lower
- **Attacks** if you become a Slaver, or cause trouble **while in Klamath**
- **Will not rejoin** if your Klamath reputation is Hated or worse
