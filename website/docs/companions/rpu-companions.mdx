# RPU Companions

:::note RPU only
Three companions sleep in hibernation chambers on the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa) **Blue Level**. None of
them exists in the unmodded game.
:::

:::danger You get exactly one
Extracting one permanently locks the other two chambers **and their personal lockers**, which cannot
be opened by any means afterwards. Choose deliberately.
:::

The computer inside the door describes all three and performs the extraction. It warns you that you
need a can of **ERSI** — go and make one at [Mr. Chemmie](/walkthrough/epa) on the Violet Level, where
the recipe becomes available only at this point. Or ignore the warning and see what happens.

---

## Kitsune

A Chinese spy who worked for the Americans.

**Recruiting:** needs **CH 5**, or **CH 2+ with Speech above 80%**.

:::danger One attempt
Fail the recruitment and she is gone permanently. The same applies if you leave her behind and later
try to pick her up while at **CH 1**.
:::

**Training:** ask about her past and follow the thread. Eventually she teaches you **+10% Sneak,
Lockpick and Steal**.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Main skill** | **Lockpick** |
| **Combat, best first** | Unarmed, Melee, Small Guns, Throwing |
| **Bad at** | Energy Weapons, Big Guns |
| **HP** | 80 → 130 |
| **AP** | 9 throughout |

She can equip **any weapon in the game**.

:::tip
As long as her Lockpick exceeds yours, she picks locks for you automatically — the way Vic handles
Repair. That is a real convenience if you did not tag Lockpick.
:::

---

## Cat Jules

Ex-army, reassigned to EPA guard duty, and volunteered for human/feline DNA experimentation. A tribute
to Cat from *Red Dwarf*.

**Training:** ask and he gives **+10% Small Guns, Big Guns and Energy Weapons**.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Combat, best first** | Small Guns, Energy Weapons, Unarmed, Melee |
| **Late game** | A passable Big Guns skill on his final level |
| **Bad at** | Throwing |
| **HP** | 80 → 130 |
| **AP** | 8 → 10 |

He can equip any weapon.

:::danger
Same as Kitsune: **CH 1** when recruiting or re-recruiting and he vanishes from the game.
:::

---

## Dex

A sociopathic killer, according to his file. Innocent, according to himself.

:::note
Play an **evil character** — negative karma — and he drops the pretence entirely. Ask for his life
story and he tells you all of it. Good characters never find out.
:::

**Training:** ask for tips and he teaches **+15% Speech and Barter**, the largest single training bonus
of the three.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Main skill** | Speech — which he cannot help you with |
| **Also good at** | Melee, Unarmed. Small Guns is adequate. |
| **Late game** | Passable Energy Weapons and Throwing |
| **Weapons** | **Unarmed weapons, knives, pistols and SMGs only** |
| **HP** | 80 → 130 |
| **AP** | 8, rising to 9 on his last level |

:::danger He angers easily
Pick your lines carefully before recruiting him or he turns hostile.

Female characters can sleep with him beforehand. **At 0 or positive karma he kills you afterwards.**
:::

:::note
Dex carries a **pack of cigarettes**, which you may not have seen before. Male characters can smoke
with a lighter in inventory.
:::

---

## Which to take

**Kitsune** for the automatic lockpicking and the thief-skill training, **Cat Jules** for the broadest
weapon training and the best all-round combat, **Dex** for the Speech and Barter bonus and the most
interesting dialogue on an evil playthrough.

Cat Jules is the safest pick. Kitsune is the most *useful* if you skipped Lockpick at character
creation.
