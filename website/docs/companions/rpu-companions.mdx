# RPU Companions

Kitsune, Cat Jules and Dex are frozen in hibernation chambers on the EPA's Blue Level, and **only one
of them can be woken alive**. Cat Jules is the easy pick and a good shot with anything. Kitsune fights
well unarmed and picks locks for you. Dex is good with a knife and teaches the biggest per-skill bonus.
<Vanilla>None of the three exists in the original game.</Vanilla>

## Waking them

### Reaching the chambers

The chambers are on the [Blue Level](/walkthrough/epa#blue-level). The automap calls it the Secret
Level. You get there by the service elevator on the Violet Level, and its door opens only once the
static hologram has given you the password, **FLOWERPOT**. The EPA page has the full route.

Between the elevator and the chambers are aliens: a Queen Alien, a dozen more of them and eleven
Wanamingo Eggs. A force field then blocks the way. Using **Repair** on its control panel is a roll at
**−80%**. You can retry as often as you like, and the first success gives **70 XP**. Damaging the panel
works too (an explosive next to it will do): the panel explodes, and shortly afterwards the field is
destroyed for good.

### The Hibernation Computer

The Hibernation Computer has three options:

- **"Subject history."** Their files: Subject A is Kitsune, B is Dex, C is Cat Jules. **Read Dex's
  file before you wake him**, because it changes what he lets you say.
- **"Retrieve a test subject."** It warns that the subject needs ERSI (Environmental Rejection Syndrome
  Inhibitors). With a canister in your inventory you get "Place ERSI canister into the dispenser." and
  the computer uses one canister. Without one you can pick "Proceed anyway."
- **"[DONE]"**

Once you pick a subject, their chamber is opened. Walk up to it and they start talking. If ERSI was
used, the conversation goes normally. **Without ERSI, the companion dies at the first line.**

### Only one ERSI canister

[Mr. Chemmie](/walkthrough/epa#mr-chemmie) on the Violet Level makes ERSI, and it is the only source
in the game. He has "just enough chemicals" for **one batch**, and the option appears only after the
Hibernation Computer has warned you. So do it in this order:

1. At the computer, pick **"Retrieve a test subject."**, read the warning and back out.
2. At Mr. Chemmie: **"More Options."** → **"Something Special"** → **"Environmental Rejection Syndrome
   Inhibitors."** → **"Create."** No ingredients or Science roll needed.
3. Pick the canister up from the machine ("Please collect the item from the machine!").
4. Go back to the computer with it and retrieve the companion you want.

:::danger[You get one]
The computer will let you retrieve the other two as well, but there is no second canister, so they die
on waking. Doing that is still worth something: **it unlocks their lockers.**
:::

### Their lockers

Each companion's belongings are in a locker on the same level:

| Locker of | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Kitsune | <Item>Necklace</Item> |
| Cat Jules | <Item>Leather Jacket</Item> |
| Dex | <Item>Knife</Item>, <Item>Lighter</Item>, <Item>Human Ear</Item>, <Item>Pack of Cigarettes</Item>, <Item>A watch</Item>, <Item>Fuzzy Painting</Item> |

Until its owner has been retrieved, a locker only answers "This locker is electronically locked. There
is no way to unlock it from here." After that it opens like any other container.

Recruiting a companion empties their locker into their own inventory, which is why Dex and Cat Jules
talk as though they have collected their things.

### What all three share

- **A free party slot.** Your limit is Charisma ÷ 2, plus one with Magnetic Personality, and never more
  than 5. It is checked again whenever you ask a waiting companion to rejoin. See
  [Companions](/companions/overview).
- **Intelligence 4 or more.** Every line in their dialogue needs it (Smooth Talker counts). A
  [low-Intelligence](/systems/low-intelligence) character gets no options at all and cannot recruit
  any of them.
- **No quests.** No XP or karma for recruiting them.
- **Five level-ups.** Their first stage comes at one of your levels 12–14 and their last at one of your
  levels 24–26, provided they are in the party the whole time. How this works is on the
  [overview](/companions/overview).
- **Strength 6 at every stage.** They meet every weapon's Strength requirement except the Strength 7
  guns (M60, Minigun, Avenger Minigun, Vindicator Minigun).
- **One lesson each.** Each lesson takes **2 hours**. A tagged skill gets the same bonus, except that
  Dex's +15 comes out as +14 on a tagged skill.
- **The same default combat settings**: Distance "Charge!", Attack Who "The strongest", Chem Use
  "Stimpaks when hurt a lot", and Burst "Be careful not to hit me". With that Burst setting they fire a
  burst only at 50% or better to hit, and only if the burst safety check passes.
- **They heal themselves.** Out of combat they use healing items from their own inventory once they
  are hurt badly enough (how badly depends on their Chem Use setting), so **keep a few
  <Item>Stimpak</Item>s on them**. Told to heal, they use items first and then their First Aid skill:
  Kitsune 48% (83% at her last stage), Dex 22%, Cat Jules 24%.
- **Metzger buys them.** [Metzger in the Den](/walkthrough/the-den) buys any of them, for −15 karma,
  and whatever they carry goes with them.
- **No consequences when they die.** Their scripts do nothing when they die.

## Kitsune

A Chinese spy who worked for the Americans. She is the best unarmed fighter of the three, has 9 AP
from the start, and **picks locks for you**. Take her if your own Lockpick is weak and you have the
Charisma or Speech to talk her round.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [EPA](/walkthrough/epa#blue-level), Blue Level, hibernation chambers |
| **Joins if** | Charisma 5 (7 for the blunter line), or Speech 81%+ as a second chance; a free slot |
| **Costs** | The ERSI canister |
| **Best at** | Lockpick 135%, Unarmed 125%, Melee 115%, Small Guns 107%, Throwing 102% |
| **Weapons** | Every class: knife, club, hammer, spear, pistol, SMG, rifle/shotgun, big guns (laser-rifle class, minigun, rocket launcher), plus the Lightsaber |
| **Levels** | 5 level-ups, one per three of your levels from level 12 ([levelling](/companions/overview)) |

### Recruiting

She talks as soon as she wakes. The conversation follows a fixed path to "What are you going to do
now?", where you have two lines to persuade her:

| Line | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| "I know it's a wasteland out there, but you've still got a whole life to live…" | Charisma 5 |
| "It doesn't look like you've got much of a choice…" | Charisma 7 |

If the check fails, she says "Forget it…" and you answer "I'll come back. Maybe you'll change your
mind." At **Speech 81% or higher** that leads to a second chance, and she comes along. Otherwise the
conversation ends with her striking out on her own, and **she is gone for good**.

:::tip[Charisma 4 is enough with shades]
The check uses your current Charisma, and a pair of <Item>Mirrored Shades</Item> **held in a hand
slot** adds 1. So does the Gain Charisma perk.
:::

If you pass and have room, she joins with "Don't make me regret this." If your party is full, she stays
where she is. Talk to her again when you have space, pick "You ready to take me with you?" and then
"Let's go." A Charisma 1 character can only get this far through the Speech route, and she walks out on
them for good ("Jesus, you look like the Grim Reaper.").

### Stats by level

AP 9, AC 8 and melee damage 9 at every stage.

| Stage | HP | Unarmed | Melee | Small Guns | Throwing | Energy, Big Guns | Lockpick | Critical chance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | 80 | 125% | 115% | 107% | 102% | 21% | 135% | 5% |
| 1 | 90 | 135% | 125% | 117% | 112% | 36% | 145% | 8% |
| 2 | 100 | 145% | 135% | 127% | 122% | 46% | 155% | 11% |
| 3 | 110 | 155% | 145% | 137% | 132% | 56% | 165% | 14% |
| 4 | 120 | 165% | 155% | 147% | 142% | 66% | 175% | 17% |
| 5 | 130 | 170% | 160% | 152% | 147% | 76% | 180% | 20% |

Her Sneak goes from 109% to 149% and her Steal from 104% to 144%.

### Gear

She can animate every weapon class, but **her default Weapon Pref. is "Unarmed"**, so she ignores guns
and melee weapons until you change it in combat control. She can choose None, Melee, Ranged, Ranged
then melee or Unarmed; "Melee then ranged" is not available to her.

- **Unarmed (her default and best combat skill).** Knuckles and the <Item>Power Fist</Item> count as
  unarmed, so she uses them without any setting change. The Power Fist does 12–24 for 3 AP, which
  means **three punches a turn**. <Item>Spiked Knuckles</Item> (4–10) do the same job early on.
- **Melee.** Set Weapon Pref. to "Melee" and give her a <Item>Super Sledge</Item>: 18–36, 3 AP a
  swing, Strength 5. That is also three swings a turn.
- **Small Guns.** Set it to "Ranged". The <Item>PPK12 Gauss Pistol</Item> (22–32, 4 AP, range 50)
  fires twice a turn with her 9 AP.

Weapon numbers are in the [Object Database](/database).

**Armour.** RPU bundles the NPC Armor mod, which changes how she looks in armour:

| She wears | She looks like |
| --- | --- |
| <Item>Leather Jacket</Item>, <Item>Combat Leather Jacket</Item> | herself |
| <Item>Leather Armor</Item>, <Item>Leather Armor Mark II</Item> | a woman in leather armour |
| <Item>Metal Armor</Item>, <Item>Metal Armor Mark II</Item>, <Item>Tesla Armor</Item> | a woman in metal armour |
| <Item>Combat Armor</Item>, <Item>Combat Armor Mark II</Item>, <Item>Brotherhood Armor</Item> | a woman in combat armour |
| Power armour of any kind | your own look in that armour (power or advanced power armour) |
| <Item>Robes</Item>, <Item>Bridgekeeper's Robes</Item> | your own look in robes |
| anything else | herself |

Every one of these looks has frames for every weapon class, the Lightsaber included, so armour never
limits her weapons.

### Picking locks

When you use Lockpick on something and **her Lockpick is higher than everyone else's in the party,
yours included**, she does the job instead of you. A tie goes to you. Lockpick is her best skill at
every stage, which this also requires. It does not work in combat, and the engine never does it for
Steal.

### Her past and her lesson

Once, from her party menu: "Kitsune, I'm pretty sure you wouldn't have been frozen in a cryo tank for no
good reason…". She tells you about her work as a spy and about the FEV. **Once the story starts, you
cannot leave it.** It ends with you asking what she can teach you, and she gives you **+10% Sneak,
Steal and Lockpick**. Two hours pass.

"About your eyes..." is flavour only.

### Losing her

- She never turns hostile.
- Failing the recruitment without Speech 81% loses her for good (above).
- **Rejoining at Charisma 1:** if you tell her to wait and come back with Charisma 1, she leaves and is
  **deleted along with everything she carries**. With a full party she just stays where she is.

## Cat Jules

Sergeant Christopher "Cat" Jules served in the 205th Airborne, then was assigned to EPA security and
became a subject of the "Human Reflex Enhancement Project". The feline DNA explains his teeth. He is
**the safe pick**: nothing to pass beyond Charisma 2, no way to make him hostile, every weapon class,
and the best Small Guns of the three.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [EPA](/walkthrough/epa#blue-level), Blue Level, hibernation chambers |
| **Joins if** | You ask him; Charisma 2+; a free slot |
| **Costs** | The ERSI canister |
| **Best at** | Small Guns 111%, Unarmed 110%, Melee 100%, Energy Weapons 98% |
| **Weapons** | Every class: knife, club, hammer, spear, pistol, SMG, rifle/shotgun, big guns (laser-rifle class, minigun, rocket launcher) |
| **Levels** | 5 level-ups, one per three of your levels from level 12 ([levelling](/companions/overview)) |

### Recruiting

The conversation is the same for male and female characters apart from some wording. When he asks
"What am I gonna to do now though?":

- **"Well, I could use a companion. Want to join me?"** He joins.
- "Well, I really must be going. Best of luck to you out there." He leaves and **is gone for good**.

With a full party he waits ("Come back when you got less people…"). Come back later and pick "You
ready to take me with you?" and then "Let's go." At Charisma 1 he calls you ugly and leaves for good.

### Stats by level

AC 9 at every stage. His melee damage drops from 7 to 6 at stage 5.

| Stage | HP | AP | Small Guns | Unarmed | Energy Weapons | Melee | Big Guns | Critical chance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | 80 | 8 | 111% | 110% | 98% | 100% | 58% | 5% |
| 1 | 90 | 9 | 121% | 115% | 108% | 105% | 68% | 6% |
| 2 | 100 | 9 | 131% | 125% | 118% | 115% | 78% | 7% |
| 3 | 110 | 9 | 141% | 135% | 128% | 125% | 88% | 8% |
| 4 | 120 | 9 | 151% | 145% | 138% | 135% | 98% | 9% |
| 5 | 130 | 10 | 166% | 155% | 153% | 145% | 108% | 10% |

Throwing is his weakest combat skill, rising from 51% to 76%.

### Gear

He can animate every weapon class. The Lightsaber is the exception: his own look and his leather,
metal and robe looks have no frames for it, but his combat-armour and power-armour looks do. His
default Weapon Pref. is **"Ranged then
melee"**, so he uses guns straight away. That setting ignores unarmed weapons, though. **To give him
the <Item>Power Fist</Item> he asks for, set Weapon Pref. to "Unarmed" or "None".** He can choose
the same options as Kitsune.

- **Small Guns.** The <Item>PPK12 Gauss Pistol</Item> (22–32, 4 AP) fires twice a turn even at 8 AP.
  The <Item>Pancor Jackhammer</Item> fires a 5-round burst of 18–29 for 6 AP. At stage 5, with 10 AP,
  he fires the <Item>M72 Gauss Rifle</Item> (32–43, 5 AP) twice a turn.
- **Energy Weapons**, which he says he prefers. The <Item>YK42B Pulse Rifle</Item> (54–78) and the
  <Item>Plasma Rifle</Item> (30–65) each cost 5 AP, so they fire once a turn until his 10 AP at stage
  5, then twice.
- **Big Guns** are only worth it late (108% at stage 5). The Minigun and Avenger Minigun need
  Strength 7, and each point short costs him 20% to hit.

Weapon numbers are in the [Object Database](/database).

**Armour.** NPC Armor treats his armour groups the same way as [Kitsune's](#gear): jackets keep his own
look, leather, metal and combat armour each get a sprite of their own, and power armour gives him
your own look in that armour. Robes get a robe sprite that the mod adds. His locker's Leather Jacket
leaves his look unchanged. Every one of these looks has frames for all the other weapon classes, so
armour never takes a gun out of his hands.

### His story and his lesson

Both once each, from his party menu:

- "So, how did you end up in that cryo chamber? And what happened to your teeth?" His backstory,
  flavour only.
- **"Since you were in the army, you think you might be able to teach me anything?"** gives you **+10%
  Small Guns, Energy Weapons and Big Guns**. Two hours pass.

### Losing him

- He never turns hostile.
- Choosing "I really must be going" at the chambers loses him for good.
- **Rejoining at Charisma 1:** if you tell him to wait and come back with Charisma 1, he leaves and is
  **deleted along with everything he carries**. With a full party he just stays where he is.

## Dex

Dexter "Dex" Patrick. His file calls him "a narcissistic, woman hating, sociopathic murderer" with 68
confirmed victims. To a character with karma 0 or above he insists he is innocent. He is the best melee fighter here but
**can use only knives, pistols, SMGs and his fists**, his look never changes with armour, and any
answer that does not take him along makes him attack. Take him for the lesson, or for the dialogue on
an evil character.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [EPA](/walkthrough/epa#blue-level), Blue Level, hibernation chambers |
| **Joins if** | You choose a line that takes him along; Charisma 2+; a free slot |
| **Costs** | The ERSI canister |
| **Best at** | Speech 145% (used for your Speech rolls), Melee 144%, Unarmed 114%, Small Guns 89% |
| **Weapons** | Knife, pistol and SMG only, plus unarmed |
| **Levels** | 5 level-ups, one per three of your levels from level 12 ([levelling](/companions/overview)) |

### Recruiting

What he says first depends on your sex and your karma. At **0 or above** he plays the innocent.
**Below 0** he makes no secret of what he is. If you read his file at the computer first, you get
different options throughout.

:::danger[Every refusal makes him hostile]
Any ending that does not take him along makes him attack. That includes the polite one, "The doc? Oh,
he's probably around here somewhere… Bye!". Pick a line that invites him, such as "I say you've got a
deal... partner.", "Sure, I suppose we could partner up. Welcome to the team, Dex.", "Fair enough. Let's
go." or "Hell, no need to try and convince me…".

If he does attack, he fights without his locker's knife, because of the same bug.
:::

:::danger[Female characters]
A female character is offered the line "Forget about names. How 'bout you take those clothes back off
and show me what pre-war men are made of."

- **Karma 0 or above:** he stabs you afterwards, and **you die.**
- **Karma below 0:** the scene ends with you catching him reaching for a knife, and he joins ("I... think I'm in love."). Game
  time passes.
:::

With a full party he stays ("It's a bit crowded. Let me know if things open up."). Come back later and
pick "You ready to take me with you?" and then "Let's go." At Charisma 1 he refuses ("No way in hell
I'm traveling with you.") but stays put rather than attacking.

### Stats by level

Melee damage 8 at every stage.

| Stage | HP | AP | AC | Melee | Unarmed | Small Guns | Energy Weapons | Critical chance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | 80 | 8 | 6 | 144% | 114% | 89% | 42% | 5% |
| 1 | 90 | 8 | 8 | 154% | 124% | 99% | 52% | 10% |
| 2 | 100 | 8 | 8 | 164% | 134% | 109% | 62% | 15% |
| 3 | 110 | 8 | 8 | 174% | 144% | 119% | 72% | 20% |
| 4 | 120 | 8 | 8 | 184% | 154% | 129% | 92% | 25% |
| 5 | 130 | 9 | 10 | 194% | 164% | 139% | 112% | 30% |

His Speech rises from 145% to 195%, and it is his best skill at every stage. While his Speech is higher
than yours, any Speech **roll** made for you uses his value. Dialogue checks that simply compare your
Speech with a number still use yours. Throwing rises from 54% to 104%.

### Gear

His sprite has only knife, pistol and SMG animations, so the engine will not let him use clubs,
spears, hammers, rifles, shotguns or big guns. His default Weapon Pref. is **"Ranged then melee"**,
which means he reaches for a gun before a knife even though his Melee is far higher. **Set it to
"Melee" or "Melee then ranged" once he has a good knife.** He can choose all six options, but under
Run Away only "You need a tourniquet" and "Never!".

- **Melee (knives).** The <Item>Ripper</Item> (15–32, 4 AP, Strength 4) gives him two swings a turn.
  <Item>"Little Jesus"</Item> (5–14, 3 AP) is the lighter choice.
- **Unarmed.** The <Item>Power Fist</Item> (12–24, 3 AP) gives him two punches a turn, and three at
  stage 5. It needs Weapon Pref. "Unarmed" or "None".
- **Small Guns.** The <Item>PPK12 Gauss Pistol</Item> (22–32, 4 AP) fires twice a turn. The
  <Item>H&K G11E</Item> uses his SMG animation, needs Strength 6 (which he has) and fires a 7-round
  burst of 13–23 for 6 AP.
- **Energy Weapons.** Pistols only, and worth it only late (112% at stage 5).

Weapon numbers are in the [Object Database](/database).

**Armour** protects him normally, but NPC Armor does not cover him, so **his look never changes.**

His locker holds a <Item>Pack of Cigarettes</Item> and a <Item>Lighter</Item>. With both in inventory, a
male character can smoke the pack: 10 uses, not in combat or in Advanced Power Armor, and only while
RPU's smoking option is on (the default).

### Talking to him

From his party menu:

- **"Any tips you might be able to give me?"** Once only. It gives you **+15% Speech and Barter**, and
  two hours pass.
- "So, what's your story?" Offered only **while your karma is below 0**, so a character who turns evil
  later still gets it. It is his life story, quick or detailed.
- "So, what can you tell me about the Vaults?" He has forgotten the first three times. Ask a fourth
  time and he remembers living by the Chicago Vault.
- "Is there anything useful you can tell me about the time before the Great Silence?" Six tall tales,
  with no effect.

### Losing him

- **Any refusal at the chambers** makes him attack, and he stays hostile on sight after that.
- A non-evil female character who takes his offer dies (above).
- Once he has joined, nothing in his script makes him leave or turn on you.
- Told to wait, he refuses to rejoin at Charisma 1 or with a full party, but he stays where he is. His
  script never deletes him.

## Which to take

**Cat Jules** is the one to take if you just want a fighter. He has no check to fail and no way to turn
on you. He can use any gun you find, has the best Small Guns and gets the most AP by the end.

**Kitsune** is the one to take for work outside combat. If you did not build for Lockpick, her
135%–180% opens things for you. Give her a Power Fist and she hits hard from the day she joins, but
you need Charisma 5 or 81% Speech to recruit her.

**Dex** gives the best lesson for a talker, makes your Speech rolls with his 145%–195% unless yours is higher, and an evil
character gets the most out of his dialogue. In
combat his weapon limits leave him behind the other two unless you give him a Ripper or a Power Fist.
Female characters with karma 0 or above must never pick the "clothes back off" line.
