# Dogmeat

**Found in:** the **Cafe of Broken Dreams** special encounter

The Vault Dweller's dog, and a Fallout 1 reference. Take him early for the novelty, or not at all.

## RPU additions

:::tip Kevlar armour
With the **NPC Armor** mod, talk to **Liz** in the [Broken Hills](/walkthrough/broken-hills) hardware
store and ask if she has anything special off the shelves. **$2000**, with Dogmeat in your party.
Talk to Dogmeat to put it on or take it off.

Fully levelled and armoured, he has roughly the protection of combat armor with better laser
resistance.
<Vanilla>There is no dog armour.</Vanilla>
:::

:::tip Dog food
The locked storage shed on the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa) surface contains **cans of dog food**. Use one
on Dogmeat and he is restored to full health.
<Vanilla>The EPA does not exist.</Vanilla>
:::

## Quirks

:::danger Mel
Hurt Dogmeat while he is **not** in your party and **Mel** shows up with an H&K G11E, and they both
attack. Early in the game that is very dangerous. Kill Dogmeat in a single round and Mel does not
appear.
:::

:::caution
When Dogmeat is not in your party the game considers him to be **on the NCR team**. Attack him in NCR
and the police open fire on you.
:::

:::note
Dogs cannot open doors, and cannot climb down the EPA elevator shaft.
:::

## The other dogs

**Laddie** (Modoc) joins over the party limit and leaves when Jonny is found. **Robodog** (NCR, from
Dr. Henry's mutagenic serum quest) is not worth taking. The **pariah dog** from a special encounter
joins over the limit, follows you forever, cannot be instructed, has **750 HP**, will not fight, and
gives you the **Jinxed** trait permanently. Kill it or leave the map.
