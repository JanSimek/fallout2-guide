# Dogmeat

A cameo from the first game, waiting in the Cafe of Broken Dreams special encounter. He can't use
weapons, armour or chems. He makes up for it with 15–17 Action Points, an Unarmed skill that
reaches 165%, and six level-ups. He takes a party slot like anyone else, so take him if you have one
to spare and like the idea of a dog that hits hard.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | The [Cafe of Broken Dreams](/systems/random-encounters#special-encounters) special encounter, from player level 6 |
| **Joins if** | You wear no armour (or Environmental Armor), **or** you feed him; and you have a free party slot |
| **Costs** | Nothing |
| **Best at** | Unarmed 91%, 15 AP, 98 HP at recruitment |
| **Weapons** | None. He always fights unarmed. |
| **Levels** | 6 level-ups: one in every three of your level-ups from your level 6 on — see [how levelling works](/companions/overview) |

## Recruiting

The cafe is a **special encounter** that can only happen **from level 6**, and only once. It is in
the desert encounter tables around Navarro, the EPA, San Francisco, New Reno, NCR and Vault 15, and on
the routes between them. The northern tables (Arroyo, Klamath, the Den, Modoc, Vault City, Gecko,
Redding, Broken Hills) don't have it.

Talk to him and he growls unless one of two things is true:

- **You wear no armour, or Environmental Armor.** The check turns away every other body armour in the
  game, the Bridgekeeper's Robes included. *"The dog gives a low whine when it sees the vault suit. It
  looks like it wants to come with you."*
  <Vanilla>The Bridgekeeper's Robes also pass the check.</Vanilla>
- **You have fed him.** Use an <Item>Iguana-on-a-stick</Item> or <Item>Dog Food</Item> on him. The
  food is used up and the join offer opens straight away, whatever you are wearing. There is a loose
  Iguana-on-a-stick on the cafe map, and a crate holding eight more.
  <Unverified>The Random Encounters page says recruiting him with food usually crashes the game. The
  script path is ordinary, but a crash can't be ruled out from source.</Unverified>

**He counts against your Charisma limit.** With a full party the only reply is *"I'll come back and get
you when I have some room in my party."* Turning him down costs nothing: he just wanders around near
you.

:::danger[Don't hit him first]
Damaging him while he is not in your party makes him permanently hostile and brings **Mel**.
See [Losing him](#losing-him).
:::

## Gear

He can't use any of it. His art has no weapon animations, the game only lets him fight unarmed, and
the engine never hands a dog a weapon from its inventory. *Use Best Armor* only works on two-legged
companions. So the [Object Database](/database) has nothing for him except the kevlar below.

He never takes chems either. Only two-legged companions use them in combat, and **"I'm clean"** is the
only Chem Use setting he has, so he never heals himself between fights. What heals him:

- **<Item>Dog Food</Item>**, used on him while he is in the party: a full heal. There is **one** in
  the whole game, in the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa#the-surface)'s storage room — the ground floor of
  the main building, behind a door the keys from the groundskeeper's quest open. The box it is in
  is not itself locked, unlike the locker beside it.
  <Vanilla>Dog Food does not exist.</Vanilla>
- Resting and world-map travel, which heal the whole party.
- A level-up, which is always a full heal.

An Iguana-on-a-stick used on him once he has joined is eaten (*"Woof! Woof!"*) and **heals nothing**.

## Kevlar armour

RPU adds a set of armour made for him, the **K-9 Kevlar**. **Liz** in
[Broken Hills](/walkthrough/broken-hills#dogmeats-armour) sells it:

- Ask her *"Do you have anything special you could set me up with that isn't on the shelves?"* The
  line needs **IQ 4**.
- She only offers it while **Dogmeat is in your party**. It costs **$2000** and she sells it **once**.
- It depends on `dogmeat_armor=1` in `mods/rpu.ini`, which is on by default. The NPC Armor mod plays no
  part in it.

To fit it, talk to Dogmeat with the kevlar in your inventory: *"Here boy, I got something for you!"*
He wears it, and his sprite changes to show it. *"Is the armor weighting you down, boy?"* takes it off
and gives it back to you. Both lines need IQ 4. It stays on through his level-ups, bonus and all. If he
dies wearing it, it drops next to his body.

| K-9 Kevlar alone | +20 | +1 / +1 / 0 / 0 / +1 / +2 | +5% / 0% / +15% / +25% / +40% / +10% |
| Dogmeat, recruited, with kevlar | 36 | 5 / 7 / 4 / 4 / 1 / 6 | 35% / 75% / 25% / 45% / 40% / 35% |

**Fully levelled and in the kevlar, his DT and DR match Combat Armor's own values**, with 80% laser
resistance instead of 60%, and his own AC on top.

## Using him

- **Speed is his strength.** 15–17 AP, an Unarmed skill of 91–165% and a critical chance of 27–43% make
  every turn count. His 75–80% laser resistance makes him a good choice against laser-armed enemies.
- **Combat Control** offers him only Custom, Aggressive and Berserk. Distance can be Stay close, Charge!,
  On your own or Stay where you are, and Run Away can be Not feeling good, You need a tourniquet or
  Never!. By default (Custom) he goes after whoever is attacking you, charges, and runs once he is below
  10 HP. An RPU script keeps him from ending up with the Coward or Defensive packet.
  <Vanilla>Changing his disposition could leave him on the wrong AI packet, because his packets are
  not in the order the engine expects.</Vanilla>
- **He can't open doors.**
- **He can't go down the EPA elevator shaft.** Using it tells him to wait at the top: *"Sorry my non
  two-legged friend. You're going to have to wait up here for me."* Waiting takes him out of the party,
  so taking him back needs a free slot. **Count your slots before you recruit anyone at the EPA.**

## Orders

His menu is short:

| IQ | Options |
| --- | --- |
| 4 or more | Follow distance (close, medium, far), *"Stay, Dogmeat. Stay!"*, fit or remove the kevlar, leave him alone |
| 3 or less | *"Uh-uh, doggie. Yu stay."*, or shamble off |

There is **no heal option, no gear option and no way to dismiss him**. To leave him somewhere, tell him
to stay. That takes him out of the party, so he stops using a slot. While he can see you, he howls
*"Awooooooooooo"* every 30–90 seconds. Talk to him to get the join offer again. **It checks your
Charisma limit again.**

## Losing him

:::danger[Mel]
Hit Dogmeat while he is **not** in your party (before you recruit him, or while he is told to stay) and
he becomes **permanently hostile**. Unless that hit kills him, combat ends and **Mel** appears beside
him, holding an <Item>H&K G11E</Item> with <Item>4.7mm Caseless</Item> to spare: *"Ey'e mate, you
screwen with me dog."* Then both of them attack. His script would react the same way to being stolen
from, but the game never lets you steal from (or loot) a dog.

**Kill him with the first hit and Mel never appears.** His script is removed when he dies, before it
can call Mel.
:::

- Stray hits while he is in your party, such as a companion's burst, don't count.
- Nothing ever calms him down again. Once hostile, he only growls when you talk to him.
- Killing him yourself costs **10 karma**.
- Nothing else in his script makes him leave.

Laddie and the pariah dog are on [Other Companions](/companions/other-companions), and K-9 has
[his own page](/companions/k9).
