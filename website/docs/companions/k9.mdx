# K-9

**Found in:** [Navarro](/walkthrough/navarro), Dr. Schreber's lab

A cybernetic dog, and the best of the game's several dog companions — which is a low bar. Formally the
game calls it "Cybernetic Dog".

Get the quest from **Dr. Schreber** (or from K-9 itself after you kill him), fetch the **K-9
Motivator** from the vertibird hangar, and use it: **3500 XP**, Science check required.

## Statistics

| | |

| **AP by level** | **14 · 15 · 16 · 17 · 19** |

Enormous Action Points, negligible HP growth.

:::note
K-9 keeps its innate **AC 8** across every level-up.
<Vanilla>The level-up protos dropped the AC bonus, so it vanished after the first one.</Vanilla>
:::

:::caution
K-9 counts as **organic** for healing, but has only **50% EMP resistance** — worth remembering before
you lob pulse grenades over a dog fighting robots.
:::

## Joining

With **karma −100 or higher** and no Childkiller status, K-9 joins immediately — **even over your
Charisma limit**. You can walk around Navarro with it and nobody reacts.

It is also the **only companion the guard outside the Navarro commander's office will overlook**.

:::danger[Losing K-9 is permanent]
It leaves if you become a Childkiller or drop to karma −101 or lower — and **once it has left this
way, you can never recruit it again**, even if your karma recovers.
:::

:::note
K-9 cannot climb down the [EPA](/walkthrough/epa) elevator shaft. It waits at the top.
:::
