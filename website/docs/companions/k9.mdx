# K-9

Dr. Schreber gave his dog a cybernetic brain. "It worked but he developed a bad case of morals", so
Schreber pulled its motivator and left it switched off by his lab door. Fixing it is worth **3500 XP**
whether or not it then joins you, and **it joins even if your party is full**. It fights only with its
teeth, but with 14–19 AP and AC 33 it far outclasses [Robodog](/companions/robodog).

| **Joins if** | Schreber is dead, you repair it with the K-9 Motivator (Repair roll at −10), your karma is −100 or higher and you are not a Childkiller |

## Recruiting

This is the quest <Quest>Fix K-9</Quest>. The order that works:

1. Ask Schreber about the dog.
2. Get Raul's permission and take the motivator from the locker.
3. Kill Schreber.
4. Repair the dog.

### Asking Schreber

Schreber is in his lab on the Underground level. He talks to you only if all of these hold:

- the base is not on alert;
- you wear power armour;
- you are alone. K-9 does not count as company. With any other companion he calls for the guard
  ("Intruders! Call the guard!" or similar), and the base can go on alert.

Ask "What's with the dog statue by the door?" and then "Can he be fixed?". He tells you he gave its
motivator circuit to **Raul up in maintenance**. That starts the quest.

If Schreber is already dead, the dog itself can start the quest instead (below).

### The motivator

The <Item>K-9 Motivator</Item> is in the locker next to **Raul**, in the vertibird hangar on the
Vertibird Base level. Once the quest is active, put on power armour (without it Raul only floats a line
at you) and tell him "The doc wants to fix his dog. He sent me for the part." He answers "it's in the
locker over there. Take it and tell the doc Raul says hi." From then on he ignores the locker.

Without his permission, Raul watches the locker whenever he can see you:

- he sees you unless you are sneaking and either have Sneak 90% or more, or have Sneak 40–89% and pass
  a Sneak roll at −10;
- the first time you tamper with the locker he warns you;
- the second time he puts the base on alert and attacks.

He also stops watching it while he is fighting Quincy.

Picking the motivator up gives no XP.

### Schreber must die

**The dog does nothing for you while Schreber lives.** Until then it only says "You are not my master.
I cannot speak to you. Good bye.", and using a skill or an item on it just opens that line. It does not
matter who kills him. If you do it yourself, you get **1000 XP and +20 karma**.

### The repair

With Schreber dead, use your **Repair** skill on the dog while carrying the motivator, or use the
motivator on it. Either way you make a **Repair roll at −10**. A companion such as Vic makes the roll
for you if their Repair is the party's highest and it is their own best skill.

- **Success:** the motivator is used up, you get **3500 XP**, the quest is complete, and K-9 is
  switched on.
- **Failure:** "That is not where that goes, master. Are you sure you know what you are doing?" You
  keep the motivator and can try again.

Using Repair without the motivator gives "You seem to be missing a part."

Talking to the dog first is optional. After Schreber's death it asks "The life functions of my master
have terminated. Are you my new master?". Saying yes starts the quest if Schreber had not, which is
what Raul's permission needs, but it changes nothing about the repair. Saying no has no lasting effect.

### Joining

The moment it is switched on, K-9 decides whether to join. This check happens **only once**:

- **karma below −100** or the **Childkiller** reputation: "You are a bigger asshole than Schreber. I
  will not join you." It refuses **for good**, but you keep the 3500 XP.
- otherwise: "I appear to be fully functional now, master. Shall I join you?" and it joins
  immediately, **with no party-size or Charisma check**.

After it has joined, your karma and reputation never matter to it again.

:::note[Navarro does not count K-9 as company]
The shared "are you alone?" test in Navarro's scripts ignores K-9, so with only K-9 along you still
count as alone. That includes the commander's door guard. Power armour checks still apply.
:::

## Gear

**There is nothing to equip.** Its art has no weapon animations, and FOR:CE never has a dog pick a
weapon from its inventory. Asked what it can use, it says "Teeth, master. The shiny white things
installed in my mouth."

**No armour either.** FOR:CE does not let four-legged companions wear armour, and the NPC Armor mod does
not cover K-9. The K-9 Kevlar sold in [Broken Hills](/walkthrough/broken-hills) is scripted for Dogmeat
only.

**You cannot give it anything.** It has no Trade screen ("This critter can't carry anything.") and
cannot be stolen from, so it never carries healing items. "You should heal yourself now." therefore
always falls back on its own First Aid (30%, 36% at the last stage): 5–15 HP on a success, and 15
minutes pass. It does not take chems in combat.

**Combat settings:** Weapon Pref. is locked to "Unarmed", and there is no "Snipe" distance and no burst
setting. K-9 shares its default custom combat settings with Robodog, so a change made for one applies
to both.

## Using it

- **It bites often.** An attack with no weapon costs 3 AP, so it bites four times a turn at 14 AP and
  six times at 19 AP. Its Melee Damage is added to the maximum damage of every bite.
- **It is a free extra slot.** It joins over your Charisma limit. That lasts only until you tell it to
  wait, because getting it back goes through the normal party-size check.
- **Heal it like a living creature.** Stimpaks, First Aid and Doctor all work. Repair does not: the
  engine refuses to repair anything that isn't a robot. The doctors in Broken Hills and Vault City
  (Dr. Troy and Andy), and Lenny in Gecko, heal it along with the rest of the party. Dr. Johnson in
  Redding treats one patient per visit: you if you are hurt, otherwise the first companion on his list
  who is with you, and K-9 is near the end of that list.
- **Don't hit it while it waits.** A stray burst on a K-9 that follows you only costs it HP, but a
  waiting K-9 attacks you for it (below).
- **Check your karma before the repair**, not after.

## Orders

K-9's menu opens with "Yes, master?":

- "You should heal yourself now." (only when it is hurt).
- "We need to change our distance from one another.": close, medium or far ("I will follow at your
  heel, master.").
- "I need to talk to you about your gear.": "I have fur, master. I carry no other gear.", and the
  weapons question.
- "I need you to wait here until I come back.": "I will await your return, master."

There is no order to dismiss it for good.

## Losing it

- **Hurting it:** while K-9 is in your party, nothing happens beyond the damage: the game does not run
  its "you hurt me" script when one party member hits another, deliberately or with a stray burst.
  Only while it waits does your attack count. It then attacks you the next time it sees you, though
  its script attacks only that once. <Unverified>Whether it stays hostile after that fight was not
  traced.</Unverified> It still offers its menu afterwards, and "Let's go." still works. Your
  companions' attacks never count.
- **Rejoining:** while it waits, "Let's go." brings it back, unless you have Charisma 1 ("You are a
  cretin. I will not go anywhere with you. Go away.") or a full party ("I will wait here until a
  position opens."). Either way it keeps waiting, and you can ask again later.
- **Karma and Childkiller** matter only at the moment of the repair (see above).
- **The EPA:** it cannot follow you down the broken elevator shaft. It leaves the party and waits at the
  top.
- **Stealing from it** is impossible: the game does not let you steal from K-9.
- **Death:** killing it costs no karma.
