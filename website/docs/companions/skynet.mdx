# Skynet

**Found in:** [Sierra Army Depot](/walkthrough/sierra-army-depot), level 3

Officially the "Brain Bot". **Skynet** properly refers to the version built with the Cybernetic
Brain — the only one that can level up.

See [Sierra Army Depot](/walkthrough/sierra-army-depot) for assembly: a **Robot Motivator**, a
**Bio Med Gel**, and a **brain**.

## Which brain

| Brain | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Abnormal | Pack mule. You have to **kill** it to get rid of it. |
| Chimpanzee | Pack mule, but you can tell it to wait. |
| Human | Mediocre sniper. **Cannot level up.** |
| **Cybernetic** | The real Skynet: auto-repair, levels up, genuinely useful. |

**Science 121%** is needed to retrieve the Cybernetic Brain from level 4 storage.

## Weapons

**.223 Pistol** → **Pancor Jackhammer** or **H&K P90c** → **Gauss Rifle**. An **H&K G11E** works if
you do not mind expensive ammunition being sprayed around. It can use a **Power Fist** at high levels,
though not as well as a gun.

## Quirks

:::note
Skynet is the **only companion that cannot be healed** with First Aid, Doctor or chems. It repairs
itself to full HP on command using its internal repair systems.

You can also use **Repair** on it for a few points, three times a day — but not tools.
:::

:::caution
It starts with **no EMP protection whatsoever**. One level-up gives it full protection.
:::

**It is painfully slow.** And because you may not reach the SAD until quite late, Vic and Cassidy may
already have Gauss Rifles, in which case you do not need a third sniper.

:::danger Do not anger Skynet first
Tell the terminal you are "Dixon", or be sarcastic, and then build the bot with the Cybernetic Brain,
and the moment you leave the depot it **instantly kills one of your other companions** — not Marcus,
Goris or a dog — and attacks you.

**Assemble the bot before talking to Skynet at all** and it is friendly regardless.
:::

:::note
Skynet **cannot climb down the EPA elevator shaft**. It waits at the top.
:::
