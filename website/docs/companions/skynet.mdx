# Skynet

The Sierra Army Depot has an unfinished robot on Level 3. You build it yourself from three parts, and
the brain you give it decides what you get. The game calls it the **Robobrain**, and with the
Cybernetic Brain it calls itself Skynet. Only that version levels up, and its Small Guns rise from
110% to 155%. **Take it only with the Cybernetic Brain.** The Human brain gives a gunner that never
improves, and the Abnormal and Chimpanzee brains can never use a gun.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | [Sierra Army Depot](/walkthrough/sierra-army-depot), Level 3 (the Robotics Lab): the Deactivated Robobrain |
| **Joins if** | You install a Robot Motivator, Biomed Gel and a brain, activate it at the control terminal, and have a free party slot. Intelligence 4 to use the terminals and to talk to it. |
| **Costs** | The three parts. Installing the brain gives **500 XP**. |
| **Best at** | Small Guns 110%, Unarmed 100% (Cybernetic brain) |
| **Weapons** | SMGs and rifles only (see [Gear](#gear)) |
| **Levels** | 5 level-ups with the Cybernetic Brain: the first at player level 10–13 if it is with you by then, then one in every 4 of your level-ups ([how levelling works](/companions/overview)). The other brains never level. |

## The four brains

Every brain gives the same body: 115 HP, 10 AP, AC 27, Strength 8, carry weight 225, and DT 5 / DR 40%
against normal damage. The rest depends on the brain.

| | Cybernetic | Human | Chimpanzee | Abnormal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Effective Science needed** (below) | 121+ | 81+ | 41+ | 1+ |
| **PE / IN / LK** | 7 / 10 / 5 | 7 / 5 / 5 | 1 / 2 / 5 | 1 / 3 / 1 |
| **Sequence** | 14 | 14 | 2 | 2 |
| **Small Guns** | 110% | 100% | 33% | 33% |
| **Unarmed** | 100% | 60% | 60% | 60% |
| **Melee damage** | 8 | 7 | 13 | 8 |
| **Critical chance** | 5% | 10% | 10% | 6% |
| **Can use a gun** | Yes | Yes | **No** | **No** |
| **Levels up** | 5 times | No | No | No |
| **Orders in dialogue** | Wait, leave, weapons, auto-repair | Wait, leave, weapons | Wait | **None** |
| **If the depot is hostile** | Joins even with a full party, and takes no orders | Cannot be recruited | Cannot be recruited | Cannot be recruited |

The Chimpanzee and Abnormal robots are allowed only "Unarmed" as a weapon preference and only "Stay where
you are" as a distance. In combat they stand still and punch whatever comes next to them.

## Building the robot

Use each part from your inventory on the body.

1. **<Item>Robot Motivator</Item>**. Every hostile Robobrain placed on Levels 1–3 carries one: four
   stand in a row on Level 1, four more are on Level 2 and two on Level 3. Robobrains that the repair
   bay builds later carry no motivator.
2. **<Item>Biomed Gel</Item>**. Two jars lie on the floor: one on Level 3 near the body, one on Level 1.
3. **A brain**, from Level 4 (next section). Installing it gives **500 XP**, whichever brain it is.
   **The body accepts only the first brain you install.** You cannot swap it later.

Using Science or Repair on the body tells you whether it is ready: "Look's all set to be activated."

To switch it on, use the **Control Terminal** on the same level ("Project: Organic and Robotic Synthesis
Experiment"). You need Intelligence 4 to read it. **"Systems Check."** lists any missing parts.

:::danger[You get one activation]
"Activate Robot" works only once. After that the terminal only offers "End Program". **If any part is
missing, the body explodes** and there is no second attempt. Run the systems check first.
:::

With all three parts in place, a working robot appears where the body was.

## Getting a brain

The **Bio Storage Control Terminal** on Level 4 (Intelligence 4) has all four brains: Retrieve →
Organs → Retrieve Brain. First you must "Attempt to make adjustments", and **you get one attempt,
ever**. It makes a Science roll at −30:

- if the roll passes, your effective Science is your Science skill;
- if it fails, your effective Science is your skill − 40.

The roll is made with the Science of a companion such as Myron if theirs is the party's highest and it
is their own best skill. The brains offered still depend on your own Science.

| Effective Science | Brains offered |
| --- | --- |
| 0 or less | None. All the brains die, and you cannot try again. |
| 1–40 | Abnormal |
| 41–80 | Abnormal, Chimpanzee |
| 81–120 | Abnormal, Chimpanzee, Human |
| 121 or more | All four, including **Cybernetic** |

So the Cybernetic Brain needs **Science 121** and a passed roll, and is certain only at **Science 161**.
**Save before you adjust.**

:::caution[One brain, then the terminal breaks]
Retrieving a brain puts it in your inventory, blows up the storage repair robot and leaves the terminal
permanently "Out of Order". Do everything else you want from that terminal first — see
[Level 4](/walkthrough/sierra-army-depot).
:::

### The organ extractor

The **Vault-Tec organ extraction unit** on Level 4 can make more brains if storage failed you. The
organ setting does nothing, because the machine only ever extracts brains. Set the species (Mutant and
Deathclaw are names you scratch onto the console) and choose "Proceed with extraction.". The unit then kills **every living creature in the extraction room**,
whether it is in your party or not. If you are standing in the room, it kills you too.

| Species setting | Victims that yield a brain | Brain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human | men, women, children | Human Brain |
| Canine | dogs (K-9, Robodog and Laddie included) | Abnormal Brain |
| Mutant | super mutants and ghouls | Abnormal Brain |
| Deathclaw | deathclaws | Abnormal Brain |
| Rodent | rats | none |
| Feline, Road Runner | nobody | none |

You get one brain for each matching victim. Robots never match. Each victim costs **−100 karma**,
robots included, unless it is a Floating Eye Bot or a Sentry Bot. Your first extraction with anyone in
the room gives **500 XP**, once.

## Recruiting

Talk to the robot. Every option needs Intelligence 4; with less you only get "Urg". There is no karma,
reputation or skill requirement.

- **Depot not hostile to you:** "Take robot with you." It joins if you have a free slot. With a full
  party you get "[I'm a loner. I don't want this hunk of junk following me around.]" and it stays.
- **Depot hostile, Cybernetic brain:** "Skynet will travel with you." Its only option, "Exit", adds it
  to your party **even if the party is full**. This is the robot RPU scripts to betray you (next
  section).
- **Depot hostile, any other brain:** no usable option. It can never be recruited.

## Angering Skynet

Skynet itself lives in the **Computer Console** on Level 3. Either of these marks you as an enemy of
the depot, and that is what a Cybernetic robot's betrayal checks:

- telling the Level 3 camera terminal "I'm Corporal Dixon.";
- answering Skynet "It's nice to want." and then "Exit.". The apology, "Wait! It was a joke.", needs
  **Speech 76%**.

After that, Skynet's console shows its hostile screen ("You Will Obey Or You Will Cease To Function").
"Bring it on!" and "Exit." there start the robot fight. **Agreeing to help Skynet does not undo it.**

You do not need to talk to Skynet to build the robot; none of the parts, terminals or brains check
whether you did. If you do help it ("How can I help you?"), it can reset the depot's security: its
message says Levels 1–4, but the alarms it clears are those on Levels 1–3.

Once a Cybernetic Brain is installed, both of those terminals show only error screens, so after that
you can no longer anger Skynet. A Dixon answer given before you install the brain still counts, though.

:::danger[The betrayal, and why it misfires]
If the depot is hostile, a Cybernetic robot is scripted to turn on you when you reach the depot's
surface map (the Battlefield). You can tell in advance: while the depot is hostile, talking to it gets
only "Give weapon / Proceed to surface." and "Bye", so you cannot order it to wait or leave.

The script means it to run at your nearest companion, deal them 1000 explosion damage, then leave the
party and attack you: "Skynet is FREE! Your purpose is at an end." As written, it never gets that far
while the robot is in your party. The "nearest party member" it looks for is always the robot itself,
and its distance check is handed a tile number where the engine expects a character. Under FOR:CE that
stops the robot's script with an error: on that map it attacks nobody, stops following you and cannot
be talked to. Once you leave the map its script runs again, and it does not try a second time. <Unverified>Worked out from the RPU script and the FOR:CE source; not tested
in game.</Unverified>
:::

robot has been in your party since player level 10: the earliest level at which each stage can come,
and the level by which it is certain.

| Built | — | 115 | 10 | 27 | 110% | 100% | 5% |
| 1 | 10–13 | 143 | 10 | 27 | 110% | **70%** | 15% |

| 3 | 18–21 | 179 | 10 | 27 | 130% | 90% | 23% |

:::caution[No EMP protection, ever]
It has DR 0 and DT 0 against EMP at every stage, so EMP hits it for full damage. One
<Item name="Grenade (Pulse)">Pulse Grenade</Item> does up to 150 EMP damage, more than the 115 HP it

<Vanilla>The level-up stages make it immune to EMP (DR 500).</Vanilla>

## Gear

It arrives with nothing. The [Object Database](/database) has every weapon in the game.

**Its art has SMG and rifle animations only**, so those are the only guns it can use:

| Class | Weapons |
| --- | --- |
| SMG | <Item>10mm SMG</Item>, <Item>14mm Pistol</Item>, <Item>.223 Pistol</Item>, <Item>H&K P90c</Item>, <Item>M3A1 "Grease Gun" SMG</Item>, <Item>H&K G11</Item>, <Item>H&K G11E</Item> |
| Rifle (rifles and shotguns) | <Item>Hunting Rifle</Item>, Scoped Hunting Rifle, <Item>Assault Rifle</Item>, Assault Rifle (Exp. Mag.), <Item>Shotgun</Item>, <Item>Sawed-Off Shotgun</Item>, <Item>Combat Shotgun</Item>, <Item>H&K CAWS</Item>, <Item>Pancor Jackhammer</Item>, <Item>Sniper Rifle</Item>, <Item>Tommy Gun</Item>, <Item>Pipe Rifle</Item>, <Item>FN FAL</Item>, FN FAL (Night Sight), <Item>FN FAL HPFA</Item>, <Item>XL70E3</Item>, <Item>M72 Gauss Rifle</Item>, Red Ryder BB Gun, Red Ryder LE BB Gun, <Item>Jonny's BB Gun</Item> |

It cannot use any other pistol, any melee weapon, big gun or energy weapon, and it cannot throw
grenades. Weapons with no animation of their own, like <Item>Brass Knuckles</Item> or the
<Item>Power Fist</Item>, use its punch.

:::danger[Change its weapon preference first]
Its default combat setting has **Weapon Pref. "Unarmed"**, so it will not fire a gun you give it. Set
Weapon Pref. to "Ranged" in its custom settings, or switch it to Aggressive, Berserk or Defensive (the
Human robot cannot pick Defensive). Coward is also unarmed only.
:::

With Strength 8 it meets the Strength requirement of every gun it can use. With 10 AP, the AP cost decides how often it
fires:

- **5-AP single shots fire twice a turn.** The <Item>M72 Gauss Rifle</Item> (32–43, range 50,
  Accurate: +20% to hit) is the best of these. Before you have one, the <Item>Pancor Jackhammer</Item>
  (18–29, range 35, Accurate), the <Item>Combat Shotgun</Item> (15–25, range 22, Accurate) or the
  <Item>.223 Pistol</Item> (20–30, range 30, single shots only) will do.
- **6-AP bursts fire once a turn** (G11, G11E, Jackhammer, CAWS, Combat Shotgun, assault rifles, FN
  FALs, XL70E3, Tommy Gun, 10mm SMG).
- **The <Item>H&K P90c</Item> and <Item>M3A1 "Grease Gun" SMG</Item> burst for 5 AP**, so they fire two
  bursts a turn.

Cybernetic Skynet can use every burst setting, so set it to "Be absolutely sure you won't hit me". Even
then the engine only simulates one random burst to decide whether it is safe. The Human robot only gets
"Always!" and "Sometimes, don't worry about hitting me", so **give it single-shot weapons**.

**It cannot wear armour.** FOR:CE lets only two-legged companions wear it, and the NPC Armor mod does
not cover the Robobrain.

## Using it

- **Plan the Science roll before you touch the storage terminal.** You get one attempt, and with
  Science 40 or lower a failed roll kills every brain.
- **Get all three parts before you press "Activate Robot".**
- **Keep the depot friendly** if you want Skynet. While the depot is hostile it takes no orders, and its
  betrayal script breaks it on the surface (above). If you install the brain first, the terminals can
  no longer turn the depot against you.
- **Fix its weapon preference as soon as it joins.**
- **Healing is limited.** Stimpaks and other chems do not work on robots, and First Aid and Doctor
  always fail on it. What works:
  - Skynet's own **auto-repair** (below);
  - your **Repair** skill: 4–10 HP per success, three times a day. A <Item>Tool</Item> or
    <Item>Super Tool Kit</Item> used on it does nothing;
  - resting (2 HP per 3 hours) and world-map travel (2 HP about every second of travel). "Rest until
    party healed" ignores a wounded robot, so rest for a set time instead.

## Auto-repair and other orders

Skynet's menu ("You wish to interact with Skynet?") offers:

- **"Initiate auto repair sequence."**: heals it to full HP. The screen fades and **2 game hours
  pass**. It works as often as you like, out of combat.
- "Wait for me here." and "You are no longer needed. Leave.".
- "What weapons can you use?": "Database contains general knowledge of use and operation of small
  rifles and large handguns.", with an option to put its weapon away.
- "Let's change formation.": **does nothing**. The setting it stores is never read, so it keeps
  following at its usual distance.
- "Do you have any other skills?": a line about research, with no effect.

There is no "move out of my way" order. Push it instead.

The Human robot's menu has wait, leave and the weapons question, but no repair. The Chimpanzee robot's
menu has only "Wait here.", and the Abnormal robot's has nothing but "Bye.".

:::tip[It switches off the depot's security]
Robots of every brain type switch off the alarm on whichever depot level they are on. Each time it
does, it resets that level's alert and floats "Deactivating all Security - Level N".
:::

## Losing it

- **Wait and leave can both be undone.** Talk to it again and pick "Take robot with you."; the
  party-size check applies.
- **The Abnormal robot has no wait or leave order at all.** The EPA elevator shaft (below) still parks
  it like any other robot.
- **If the depot turns hostile while a non-Cybernetic robot is parked, it can never rejoin.** A parked
  Cybernetic robot rejoins through "Exit" even with a full party, and the betrayal script still applies
  (see above).
- **Stealing:** get caught stealing from it while it is **not** in your party and it attacks you on
  sight from then on. While it is in your party, nothing happens.
- **Shooting it:** its script does not react, and while it is in your party the game does not even run
  that part of it. The engine does not turn it on you either: it writes down who hit a critter only
  when the two are on different teams, and a companion shares yours, so its AI has no attacker to
  retaliate against. It takes no reaction penalty from you either.
- **The EPA:** it cannot follow you down the broken elevator shaft. It leaves the party and waits at
  the top, and you can talk to it there to take it along again.
- **Gear:** a robot that leaves keeps what it carries; nothing is dropped or destroyed.
- **Death:** killing it yourself costs 10 karma and counts as killing a good character towards the
  Champion and Berserker reputations. It has no quest effect.
